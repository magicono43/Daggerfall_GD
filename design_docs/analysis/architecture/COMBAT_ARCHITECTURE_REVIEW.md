# COMBAT architecture review

This review covers COMBAT-001 through COMBAT-021 in the [feature catalog](../systems/COMBAT_FEATURE_CATALOG.md). Evidence comes from static inspection of the local DFU 1.1.1 Release snapshot. Paths are relative to Assets/Scripts. The exact commit is unavailable in the [repository record](../00_REPOSITORY_OVERVIEW.md). Runtime reachability, balance, performance and classic parity remain open.

## Existing architecture

### Player readiness, attack commitment and contact · COMBAT-001–008

Game/WeaponManager.cs owns sheathing, hand selection, attack input, equipment-to-weapon presentation, animation progress and hit delivery. Game/FPSWeapon.cs exposes current and hit frames and handles weapon presentation. Update gates impact with isDamageFinished and an exact current-frame comparison. It handles melee contact or bow release at that point, then applies fatigue and conditionally tallies practice. This distinguishes attempted attacks from hits and synchronizes impact with the visible swing. A change to how frames advance or interruptions clear state can change whether the impact is delivered. Frame skipping under load needs a fixture.

MeleeDamage uses the view and physics contact to choose a target, while WeaponEnvDamage handles environmental strikes and delegates some exterior door bashing to PlayerActivate. Preserve distinct outcomes for an entity, a door/mechanism and a miss. The runtime ordering and collider mapping need geometry tests. Attack action rules and world fixture policy intersect through Unity transforms and hit data. See Game/WeaponManager.cs, Game/PlayerActivate.cs and Internal/DaggerfallAction.cs.

Bow draw, release and cooldown use weapon state and FormulaHelper.GetBowCooldownTime. The release path consumes one arrow, copies its summoned flag into the missile and stores the bow in a manager field. Recovery rules differ by target and source. Player hits can add a recoverable arrow to an enemy before the final health change, while enemy BowDamage adds an arrow after its damage route. The rules for summoned and quest ammunition are worth making explicit. The release code dereferences the arrow returned by the inventory lookup without a local null check. Whether an arrow can disappear between draw eligibility and release needs runtime validation.

### Projectiles and magic delivery · COMBAT-009, 014, 017

Game/DaggerfallMissile.cs combines flight, collider/rigidbody behavior, target gathering, impact visuals, sounds and payload dispatch. Caster and arrow flags travel on the projectile. DoCollision checks impactDetected and changes collider/rigidbody state. AssignBowDamageToTarget reads WeaponManager.LastBowUsed at player impact. That field is overwritten on release. An earlier projectile can therefore resolve with a later bow if such shots coexist. Enemy arrows compare the first hit entity with the caster's current EnemySenses.Target, then EnemyAttack.BowDamage uses current target and equipment. Retargeting or equipment changes in flight can affect resolution. These dependencies are confirmed. Exact in-play exposure depends on flight time and encounter behavior.

Touch and area delivery reuse DaggerfallMissile targeting with different spatial checks and deliver effect payloads to entity behaviors. DoAreaOfEffect uses Physics.OverlapSphere and a temporary list. Its duplicate check consults the preexisting target list, then appends the temporary list after the loop. Multiple colliders resolving to the same newly found entity can therefore append it more than once. The reviewed check alone does not prevent duplicate payload entries. Verify collider configurations and downstream behavior before calling this a shipped combat bug. Allocation and search costs are unmeasured. No in-flight projectile persistence contract was established in the inspected save paths.

### Hit resolution, intake and feedback · COMBAT-010–014

Game/Formulas/FormulaHelper.cs CalculateAttackDamage shares attack routing, hit/damage calculations, poison, wear and other callbacks across player and enemy attacks. It returns a number after making some mutations. A top-level override replaces this path, including default side effects. WeaponManager.WeaponDamage and EnemyAttack.ApplyDamageToPlayer/ApplyDamageToNonPlayer then handle additional consequences. Player hits can break concealment, recover arrows, trigger sound/blood and knockback, apply Strikes enchantments and item callbacks, reduce health, attribute the attack and notify racial effects. Enemy paths arrange their consequences around a different set of target and presentation concerns. Shared calculations and extension seams are strengths. Their placement across callers makes order harder to verify and creates drift risk. Do not assume the two routes should produce identical feedback or practice.

EnemyAttack.SendDamageToPlayer uses Unity SendMessage with the string RemoveHealth. PlayerHealth.RemoveHealth and PlayerEntity.SetHealth contribute to intake and death. PlayerFootsteps also exposes RemoveHealth. A string message gives no compile-time recipient contract and can reach several components. Trace scene bindings and each receiver's role before changing it. Preserve CHAR ownership of health/death policy, including prevention, and keep feedback distinct from committed gameplay effects. Relevant paths include Game/PlayerHealth.cs, Game/PlayerFootsteps.cs, Game/Entities/PlayerEntity.cs, Game/EnemyBlood.cs, Game/EnemySounds.cs and Game/MagicAndEffects/EntityEffectManager.cs. See also [RULE's attack resolution proposal](RULE_ARCHITECTURE_REVIEW.md) and [MOVE's landing proposal](MOVE_ARCHITECTURE_REVIEW.md).

### Enemy cadence and legal escalation · COMBAT-015–019

Game/EnemyAttack.cs FixedUpdate uses GameManager.ClassicUpdate, live speed and melee timer to attempt attack animation. Update consumes mobile DoMeleeDamage or ShootArrow flags. MeleeDamage evaluates reach, facing and context at commitment. Enemy attacks can bash doors, and bow impact can use a non-player target path. Separating attack selection from commitment allows the target to move or become invalid during wind-up. The two update clocks, animation flags and shared random draws complicate deterministic tests. ResetMeleeTimer also folds in level and reflexes. Comments document intentional deviations from classic, so the fixture target needs to be chosen explicitly.

WeaponManager routes civilian hits into guard escalation through PlayerEntity. A guard hit can lower reputation and present a surrender dialogue before health application. EnemyAttack stores damage in an instance field, and the deferred refusal callback calls SendDamageToPlayer using that field. This confirms the callback reads mutable state. Overlap or subsequent attack resolution before refusal could change the damage. UI gating may prevent it in normal play. Other effects and reputation can already have been applied before the prompt. LAW should own surrender and court policy, with combat retaining the exact pending hit context. See EnemyAttack.ApplyDamageToPlayer and SurrenderToGuardsDialogue_OnButtonClick and PlayerEntity.SurrenderToCityGuards.

### Enemy death, quest state and corpse ownership · COMBAT-020–021

Game/EnemyDeath.cs subscribes to entity death and defers CompleteDeath to Update. Quest-linked enemies wait for QuestResourceBehaviour.IsFoeDead. Finalization deactivates the actor without destroying the saved enemy, creates a corpse with a new UID and optional quest UID, unequips and transfers the enemy items, plays feedback and publishes OnEnemyDeath. Preserving dead actor identity and transferring quest items with the inventory are valuable behaviors. Deactivation precedes corpse creation and transfer. A failure after deactivation could interrupt completion without an explicit retry contract. The quest gate also has no local recovery for a missing acknowledgment. No runtime failure was reproduced. Inspect Utility/GameObjectHelper.cs CreateLootableCorpseMarker, Internal/DaggerfallLoot.cs and Game/Questing/Components/QuestResourceBehaviour.cs for the connected contracts.

## Comparison

| Concern | DFU | Proposed Godot design |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Attack commitment | Weapon and mobile frames with manager flags | Explicit attack phases and one commit contract |
| Projectiles | Payload and caster travel with the shot, while some attribution is resolved through live manager state | Per-shot source and targeting policy with deduplicated impact targets |
| Damage and effects | Shared calculation with mutations, plus route-specific follow-up | Ordered combat operation with rule, inventory, effect and health ownership |
| Surrender and death | Deferred UI callback and quest-gated corpse creation | Captured pending hit and recoverable death finalization |

Verify skipped/interrupted attack frames, changed ammo during draw, multiple arrows in flight, retargeting, multi-collider area hits and effects that change damage mid-resolution. Exercise guard refusal and forced surrender, then quest death and corpse transfer across save/load. Profile projectile and collision workloads before changing query allocation. No runtime, profiling or classic parity tests were performed.
