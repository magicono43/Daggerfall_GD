# ECON architecture review

Scope covers ECON-001 through ECON-019 in the [feature catalog](../systems/ECON_FEATURE_CATALOG.md). Evidence comes from static inspection of the local DFU 1.1.1 Release snapshot. The exact commit is unavailable in the [repository overview](../00_REPOSITORY_OVERVIEW.md). Source paths are relative to Assets/Scripts. Runtime reachability, performance and classic parity remain open.

## Existing architecture

### Access, stock and quotes · ECON-001–003

Game/PlayerActivate.cs checks building access. DaggerfallMerchantServicePopupWindow routes by merchant context and Game/Guilds/Services.cs supports custom registrations. Internal/DaggerfallLoot.cs StockShopShelf supplies physical stock. DaggerfallTradeWindow filters local and remote collections, while DaggerfallGuildServicePopupWindow.GetMerchantMagicItems provides a specialized source. These boundaries distinguish location access, available services and item eligibility. The window still coordinates stock, selection and transaction state. Validate custom merchant eligibility and restocking separately from display filters.

DaggerfallTradeWindow.GetTradePrice and UpdateCostAndGold calculate basket totals and refresh affordability. Game/Formulas/FormulaHelper.cs CalculateTradePrice provides reusable arithmetic, with regional pricing context shared with FACT and TIME. The confirmation handler recomputes the price. Repricing can be useful, but the quote shown and the amount settled can differ if inputs change. Whether ordinary modal flow permits such a change requires a runtime fixture. Preserve integer rounding and per-mode pricing boundaries before extracting a calculator.

### Settlement, shop theft and item services · ECON-004–008

Game/UserInterfaceWindows/DaggerfallTradeWindow.cs stages selections and commits through ConfirmTrade_OnButtonClick. Sales add coin when carry weight permits and otherwise create a letter of credit, then notify trade subscribers before clearing the sold collection. Purchases call PlayerEntity.DeductGoldAmount, notify, then transfer the basket. Repair and identify also call DeductGoldAmount before applying the service. The return value is a remaining unpaid amount, which these branches ignore. If a recomputed price cannot be covered at confirmation, the callback can still deliver goods or services. The missing check is confirmed. Normal reachability depends on earlier affordability checks and state changes while the prompt is active. Subscriber failure or reentry before collection changes is a separate ordering risk.

DoSteal reuses the selected basket but evaluates shoplifting and can dispatch guard consequences. Retain the separation between paid settlement and theft while letting LAW own the legal outcome. Repair mode estimates and commits batch times through UpdateRepairTimes, preserves longer previously assigned durations, and supports instant repair and interrupted collection. ItemRepairData owns individual job fields. Identification modifies item state through IdentifyItem. These operations have different eligibility and fulfillment rules but share the same UI payment pattern. The batch loops and collection transfers have no measured performance issue. See the [ITEM review](ITEM_ARCHITECTURE_REVIEW.md) for transfer failure and repair-time arithmetic.

### Bank accounts, credit and loans · ECON-009–012

Game/DaggerfallBankManager.cs owns static regional accounts and routes operations through MakeTransaction and TransactionResult events. DepositGold accepts carried and wagon coin, while WithdrawGold checks carry weight. DepositAll_LOC converts credit items into account funds. Withdraw_LOC checks a commission and minimum, then debits the account before creating and inserting the credit item. ItemCollection.AddItem provides no success result. This is a confirmed ordering limitation. A failed output operation could leave the debit committed. Preserve the distinction between coin, account balances and credit instruments.

The public DepositGold and WithdrawGold methods check affordability or capacity but contain no local positive-amount guard. A negative amount passed directly can reverse the intended balance changes. This is a code-level boundary weakness. UI exposure is unverified. Region bounds also need checking wherever callers bypass the validated dispatch path.

BorrowLoan credits principal, adds formula-derived repayment and assigns a due date. RepayLoan can use account funds alone or the remainder after carried funds and credit are applied. LoanChecker attempts overdue repayment and marks the default before applying the LAW penalty. The default flag guards repeat penalties. The order also means a failure applying the penalty could leave the flag set. Preserve reminder, partial repayment and default as distinct outcomes. Verify timing at the deadline and repeated checks after partial repayment. See Game/LoanChecker.cs and Game/Entities/PlayerEntity.cs DeductGoldAmount.

### Deeds, rentals and specialized services · ECON-013–018

DaggerfallBankManager.PurchaseHouse and PurchaseShip use carried funds or credit first and the account for the remainder, then grant ownership. AllocateHouseToPlayer also supports knightly order rewards. AssignShipToPlayer marks ship scenes permanent through SAVE. TransportManager consumes ship ownership. Preserve grant and purchase as distinct operations on the same entitlement. The payment and entitlement steps cross owners without a single completion contract, so test partial changes if placement, grant or cache updates fail.

DaggerfallTavernWindow.RentRoom creates or extends a rental keyed by map and building context. PlayerEntity removes expired rentals. Confirmation handles the price, while the window also supports free service paths. Absolute expiration is useful for time skips and restoration. Define extension behavior for a rental that expires before confirmation and keep room access distinct from payment.

DaggerfallGuildServicePopupWindow.DoGuildService routes training, donation, cure, potion and spell commerce, item making, teleport, summoning, rewards and quest offers. FACT owns rank and eligibility, while service windows and destination systems own many mutations. Keep focused per-service logic. Shared UI routing should not force quest offers, free rewards and charged transactions into one settlement model. See Game/UserInterfaceWindows/DaggerfallGuildServiceTraining.cs, DaggerfallGuildServiceDonation.cs, DaggerfallGuildServiceCureDisease.cs and DaggerfallDaedraSummonedWindow.cs. Pricing and eligibility are candidates for authored data where multiple merchant types use the same policy.

### Persistence and session lifecycle · ECON-019

StartGameBehaviour sets up accounts and houses for a new character, and the classic path reads bank records through ReadNativeBankData. Native SAVE restores regional bank records, deeds and houses. SaveLoadManager also restores house data early on an interior load path before reconstruction. This is a useful ordering requirement. Rental and repair records belong to the relevant player and item state. Static bank ownership makes session reset and restore order important, especially on retry or missing data. Preserve durable account, debt, entitlement and expiry state, and derive scene/cache registration from it. Compatibility with modified house keys, missing service providers and old saves needs fixtures.

## Comparison

| Concern | DFU | Proposed Godot design |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Merchant settlement | Windows stage baskets, quote and apply transactions | Explicit quote and commit with owner-validated payment and fulfillment |
| Banks and credit | Static regional accounts and operation-specific updates | Session-owned accounts with validated amounts and committed results |
| Deeds and timed services | Purchase and grant paths also update runtime state | Stable entitlements and deadlines restored before their consumers |
| Specialized services | Guild and merchant windows orchestrate multiple systems | FACT provides entitlement and service owners fulfill typed requests |

Verify changed quotes, insufficient funds on confirmation, failed item delivery, credit payout weight, invalid direct bank inputs, partial/defaulted loans, deed grants, room expiry and repair interruptions. Exercise classic and native restore before making compatibility decisions. No runtime, profiling or classic parity tests were performed.
