# LOC-007 — Interior people and service placement

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-14 with “I approve”.

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

Find occupants and service providers in the rooms they serve. Waiting inside a building updates who is present and which services are available. Quest substitutions preserve the shop's obligations and each person's history.

## Confirmed requirements

- **LOC-007-D01.** Use stable interior-scoped placements with explicit actor assignments and validated authored alternatives. If a required occupant has no usable placement, fail preparation under LOC-005. An optional occupant stays absent.
- **LOC-007-D02.** Reevaluate occupants individually when authored conditions change, including after rest and time skips. Routine arrivals and departures avoid visible popping and defer during an active interaction with the actor. Evaluate service availability immediately under ECON rules.
- **LOC-007-D03.** A quest substitution explicitly assigns the replacement and declares the original occupant's disposition. Allow at most one active occupant per placement. A substitute providing a service requires a separate authorized provider binding.

See the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#loc-007-interview-decisions).

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

Static inspection of the local DFU 1.1.1 snapshot. `Assets/Scripts/Internal/DaggerfallInterior.cs#AddPeople` instantiates actors from RMB people records and passes the record and building key to `Game/StaticNPC.cs#SetLayoutData`. It can hide occupants based on opening hours, guild access or membership, or player home ownership. It runs quest setup for those left active. `UpdateNpcPresence` can enable the people group on reopening after rest or idle without repeating the per-occupant assembly conditions.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

1. Resolve each placement in the active interior and layout variant. Bind its stable placement ID to the intended stable actor ID. Validate the primary position and applicable authored alternatives for the actor and current geometry. Use only eligible authored positions. During preparation, report a required placement failure through LOC-005. An optional actor with no usable position remains absent.
2. Reevaluate individual authored presence conditions when relevant game time, ownership, faction, quest or other declared inputs change. Apply current conditions during preparation and after rest or time skips. For routine live changes, defer while the player is interacting with the actor or the appearance or disappearance would be visible. Revalidate the pending change against current eligibility and placement before applying it.
3. Evaluate physical presence separately from service eligibility and dialogue availability. ECON evaluates current schedule, authorization and provider context immediately at the relevant checks. An interaction awaiting confirmation revalidates its original actor and service. A merchant may remain present after trading closes. Apply each service route's own provider requirements, including shelf routes governed by ECON-001.
4. Resolve quest substitutions through QUEST and NPC. Validate the substitute, the original occupant's disposition, placement availability and any affected provider assignment before applying the change. Preserve one active occupant per placement and each actor's independent history. A valid service reassignment preserves establishment-owned stock and obligations under their owners. Pending interactions cannot transfer to a new actor.

## Formulas, parameters and configuration

Authored content supplies placement assignments, eligible alternatives, required or optional status, presence conditions and supported substitutions. Service hours, eligibility and transaction rules remain with ECON and their other owners. Visibility checks, position tolerances and reevaluation timing remain open. No new balance formula is selected.

## Required data

Stable interior, placement, actor and applicable establishment and service IDs. Authored poses and alternatives, current layout version and activation, presence conditions, required status, actor and quest state, original occupant disposition and authorized provider bindings.

## Inputs, outputs and events

- **Inputs** — Resolved layout, actor and service assignments, current conditions and interaction state, placement results and quest substitution requests.
- **Outputs** — Placed, absent, pending or failed occupant results and current placement and provider context for consumers.
- **Events** — Publish committed occupant or provider changes with their scoped identities. Reevaluate affected interactions under their owners. Readiness alone produces no transaction or quest outcome.

## Cross-system interactions

- **LOC-005/006, DATA and MOD** provide the resolved interior and validated placement definitions.
- **NPC-004** owns actor identity and service attachment rules. **TIME, FACT and property owners** supply current presence inputs.
- **ECON-001 and MOVE-023** own service access and interaction revalidation. **QUEST-013/015** own quest placement and substitution. **SAVE** coordinates durable reconstruction.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- An occupied or unsuitable pose may use an eligible authored alternative. An exhausted optional placement stays absent. A required failure during entry uses the interior recovery flow.
- Rest may cross several schedule boundaries. Resolve current conditions and discard obsolete deferred changes. Reopening cannot activate an occupant still excluded by another current condition.
- A visible actor or an actor in conversation can remain present while service checks deny a closed or expired offer.
- Two substitution requests cannot create two active occupants for one placement. An absent or unauthorized substitute cannot become the provider through placement alone.

## Save/load considerations

Restore applicable durable actor, quest and provider assignments through their owners before exposing required occupants. Rebuild placements against the current compatible layout and reconcile current presence conditions. Preserve actor history and establishment state across visits and substitutions. Validate changed references with SAVE and use applicable migration or recovery. Restoration creates no duplicate actor, service or quest outcome.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Use scoped identities across shared templates, repeated activation and provider changes. Bind live references to the active interior. Scene representation, visibility tests and update scheduling remain open.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Reused layouts place distinct occupants. A blocked primary pose uses only an eligible authored alternative. Exhausted required and optional placements follow their respective outcomes.
2. Rest and time skips reevaluate occupants individually. Routine changes defer while visible or in active interaction, while service availability uses current ECON conditions.
3. An authored quest substitute occupies the intended placement once, with a declared original occupant disposition and independent history. It can provide the service only with an authorized binding.
4. Revisit and restoration preserve owner state and avoid duplicate occupants or stale interaction retargeting.
