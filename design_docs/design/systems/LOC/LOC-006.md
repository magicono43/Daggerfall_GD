# LOC-006 — Interior markers and exit matching

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-14 with “I approve”.

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

Make each doorway lead to its intended landing and each exit lead to its intended exterior doorway. Keep rental, rest and guard placement associated with the right interior and role. A temporary obstruction should preserve the player's intended doorway and produce a clear outcome.

## Confirmed requirements

- **LOC-006-D01.** Give markers stable interior-scoped IDs and explicit roles, including entry, rest or rental, guard spawning and other supported gameplay uses. Allow explicitly declared multiple roles. Each usable interior exit links to its intended exterior doorway.
- **LOC-006-D02.** Resolve missing classic links during content preparation using verified source marker and door geometry. A classic rest marker can serve entry only when the import mapping verifies that use. Missing or ambiguous matches require a validated override or explicit failure for the affected entrance.
- **LOC-006-D03.** Keep the selected doorway and marker binding when placement is blocked. Allow MOVE-027 bounded corrections within the intended space and on the intended side. If no permitted placement works, apply the approved failure or recovery flow. ECON and LAW retain their own candidate selection and failure rules.

See the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#loc-006-interview-decisions).

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

Static trace of DFU 1.1.1. `Assets/Scripts/Internal/DaggerfallInterior.cs#FindClosestEnterMarker` searches enter and rest markers by distance. `Game/PlayerEnterExit.cs#TransitionInterior` starts from the exterior door position, uses the closest eligible marker as a search origin when available, and tries the closest interior door. When that fails it can place the player above a marker. `DaggerfallInterior.cs#FindMarkers` lists markers by type. `Game/UserInterfaceWindows/DaggerfallTavernWindow.cs#RentRoom` uses rest-marker candidates, and `Game/Entities/PlayerEntity.cs#SpawnCityGuards` uses the lowest outer interior door in eligible buildings.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

1. Resolve a marker by stable interior identity and local marker ID. Each record declares its roles and its placement relative to the resolved interior. Role queries return candidates within the requested interior. Consumers apply their own eligibility and selection rules.
2. Resolve the exterior entrance's intended interior landing through the validated LOC-003 and LOC-005 bindings. Resolve an activated interior exit to that exit's linked exterior doorway. Separate authored front and back exits can lead to different exterior doorways even if entry was through only one of them. Preserve the selected link through preparation and commitment.
3. During classic content preparation, use verified source marker roles and door geometry to establish stable mappings for layouts without explicit links. Retain the original marker role and declare an additional entry role only where verified. Require a validated override or fail the affected entrance when the required match cannot be established. Entering the interior uses the resolved mapping.
4. Hand off the resolved marker pose and doorway context to MOVE. Validate the current placement against collision, occupants and the intended doorway side. A blocked pose can use only permitted bounded local corrections within the intended space. If these fail, return an explicit failure to the approved transition or recovery flow.
5. Offer rest or rental and guard spawn markers to ECON and LAW under their own eligibility, placement and failure rules. Marker availability alone grants no room access, creates no guard and commits no gameplay action.

## Formulas, parameters and configuration

Marker roles, poses, supported role combinations, and entrance and exit links are authored or imported content. Use MOVE-027 bounds for corrections. Classic matching transforms, tolerances and validation fixtures remain open. No new distances or gameplay formulas are selected.

## Required data

Stable interior, marker and doorway references, resolved layout or variant and version, roles, local pose and facing as required, intended doorway side and space, validated links, import or override provenance, current frame and applicable placement request context.

## Inputs, outputs and events

- **Inputs** — Validated interior layout, marker and doorway records, selected link or role query, and current placement context.
- **Outputs** — Resolved link and marker pose, scoped role candidates, or an explicit unavailable or invalid result. MOVE supplies validated placement outcomes.
- **Events** — Identify required mapping failures by affected entrance when available. MOVE, ECON and LAW publish their committed outcomes.

## Cross-system interactions

- **LOC-003/005 and DATA-003/005** supply scoped links, resolved geometry, classic marker inputs and validated overrides.
- **MOVE-026/027 and CORE** own transition, placement correction and recovery.
- **ECON-015, LAW-007 and QUEST** consume eligible scoped markers under their own rules. **SAVE** coordinates compatibility and restore.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- Check equidistant, coincident or missing classic candidates and nearby markers on different floors. An unresolved match needs explicit resolution or failure.
- Check a marker at the local origin, shared templates and changed record order. Identity and lookup remain scoped to the interior.
- Check a valid marker occupied by an actor or blocked by a mechanism. Corrections stay within the intended space and on the intended side.
- Validate removed or changed markers and stale preparations before applying a placement result.

## Save/load considerations

Persist committed marker or doorway references where an owner needs them for durable state. Validate the restored layout and references, and use explicit migration or approved recovery for required incompatibilities. Restore a committed pose through SAVE and MOVE rules without replaying doorway traversal, rental or spawning.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Keep marker identity independent of enumeration order and scene handles. Resolve poses from the active layout and frame. Matching tooling, tolerance values and the concrete data schema remain open.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Two exits serving one interior resolve their own exterior doorways regardless of entry door. Marker role queries are scoped and support explicitly declared multiple roles.
2. Classic fixtures verify entry and exit mappings, including a verified rest-as-entry case. A missing or ambiguous required match uses a validated override or fails explicitly.
3. Occupied or obstructed landing poses retain their binding and permit only bounded corrections on the intended side. Exhausted correction uses approved failure or recovery.
4. ECON and LAW receive relevant role candidates and retain their own placement choices and outcomes. Restoring committed context produces no duplicate gameplay action.
