# LOC-016 — Moving geometry, traps and puzzles

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-14 with “I approve”.

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

Make lifts, traps and puzzles respond predictably to their authored conditions. A linked gate can wait for a lift to arrive. A valid answer advances its puzzle, and a trap or teleport produces the outcome its target and current context allow.

## Confirmed requirements

- **LOC-016-D01.** Support reusable actions for motion, text and answers, teleport, spells, damage and resource drains. Allow authored targets, conditions and puzzle answer aliases. Support explicit validated location-specific rules where reusable actions are insufficient.
- **LOC-016-D02.** Ordinary moving geometry stops at its last valid position on obstruction and waits for clearance under a declared policy. Carrying, pushing and crushing follow MOVE-008. Compression damage requires an explicitly configured crush hazard. A motion completion link fires only on reaching the intended endpoint.
- **LOC-016-D03.** Validate targets and commit effects through their owners once per authorized occurrence. Teleport success requires committed placement. An incorrect answer leaves success links inactive. Retries and penalties require authoring. Restore supported progress on native load and reset ordinary mechanism state on successful reentry under LOC-013 while preserving quest outcomes.

See the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#loc-016-interview-decisions).

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

Static inspection of DFU 1.1.1. `Assets/Scripts/Internal/DaggerfallAction.cs#Move` selects forward or reverse tweens from the current endpoint. `ShowTextWithInput` resolves accepted strings and `UserInputHandler` advances the chain on a case-insensitive match. `Teleport` uses the linked object's transform. `CastSpell`, `DrainHealth` and `DrainMagicka` handle other effects. `Poison` has no effect in the inspected handler.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

1. Resolve the action definition, scoped targets and applicable conditions from validated content and the LOC-015 activation context. Reusable effects and supported site rules declare required references and owner contracts. Preserve verified classic behavior through explicit mappings.
2. Run mechanism motion toward the authored endpoint through coordinated MOVE physics. Apply declared carriage, pushing and crush policies under MOVE-008. Ordinary obstructed geometry stops at its last valid pose and waits for clearance under its declared policy. Revalidate clearance before resuming. An obstruction or cancellation cannot satisfy an endpoint completion link. Report terminal failure under the applicable chain and owner rules.
3. Present authored puzzle text and evaluate the submitted answer against the active puzzle's validated accepted strings and aliases. A correct answer can satisfy its own success condition. An incorrect answer leaves success links inactive. Apply only authored retry or penalty rules. Tie delayed input to the original activation and puzzle state.
4. Pass teleport intent to WORLD-003 and MOVE for destination validation and commitment. Pass spell, damage and drain effects to MAGIC, COMBAT or the applicable resource owner. Revalidate targets in the active context and retain committed outcomes so duplicate delivery or retry cannot repeat an effect. Report each result to LOC-015 for its declared link timing.
5. Follow MOVE-021 pause and restoration rules. On successful stock dungeon reset, initialize ordinary motion, traps and puzzles to their authored fresh-visit state and retire obsolete pending work. Preserve quest progress and required owner state under LOC-013.

## Formulas, parameters and configuration

Author poses, motion timing, obstruction policies, supported carrying and hazard profiles, teleport targets, puzzle text and answers, and any retry or penalty rules. Effect magnitudes and resource calculations follow their owners. Exact motion tolerances, wait parameters and answer normalization remain open. No new universal damage formula is selected.

## Required data

Scoped mechanism, action, target and link IDs, layout and visit identity, authorized occurrence, motion endpoints and progress, obstruction and collision policy, puzzle text and answer definitions, attempt state, effect parameters and committed owner outcomes.

## Inputs, outputs and events

- **Inputs** — Authorized action, resolved content and targets, current motion and collision context, puzzle input, and owner validation results.
- **Outputs** — Applied, waiting, completed or failed mechanism outcomes, with validated owner operations and state.
- **Events** — Report action success and completion milestones to LOC-015. Effect owners publish committed teleport, damage, spell and quest outcomes once.

## Cross-system interactions

- **LOC-009/013/015 and DATA-005/MOD** provide layout, reset, scoped triggers and validated content.
- **MOVE-004/008/021/027 and WORLD-003** own physics, carriage, crush rules, pause and placement. **LOC-017** specifies action doors.
- **COMBAT, MAGIC, CHAR and QUEST** own gameplay effects and progression. **UI and AV** present prompts, motion and feedback. **SAVE and CORE** coordinate recovery and supported restoration.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- Check a blocked lift with a waiting gate, a rider under a low ceiling and an explicitly authored crush hazard.
- Check an incorrect answer, a stale answer submission, a duplicate trap trigger and a teleport target that becomes blocked.
- Preserve previously committed effects if a later linked action fails. A failed or cancelled reentry leaves the current visit state intact.

## Save/load considerations

Persist supported motion progress, target state, attachment context, puzzle progress, pending timers and committed effects through their owners. Restore geometry before validating dependent attachments and positions. Resume supported progress without replaying damage, activation or committed outcomes. Apply approved save restrictions when in-flight state cannot be restored. Successful stock reentry reset initializes ordinary mechanism state.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Keep authoritative motion and effect state coherent with physics and active layout. Scope delayed results to their occurrence and visit. Presentation and content schema choices remain open.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Validated reusable effects and site rules resolve their intended targets. Unsupported required effects follow content recovery.
2. An ordinary blocked mechanism waits safely and never advances its completion link before reaching its endpoint. An enabled crush hazard follows MOVE-008.
3. A correct answer can advance its puzzle. Wrong and stale answers cannot commit success. Teleport and trap outcomes commit only through their owners and are deduplicated.
4. Native load resumes supported mechanism state without replay. Successful stock reset restores ordinary mechanism defaults and preserves quest outcomes.
