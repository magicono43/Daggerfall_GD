# LOC-015 — Action trigger acceptance and chaining

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-14 with “I approve”.

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

Dungeon mechanisms respond to their authored triggers and run linked effects at predictable points. A switch can start several effects immediately, a gate can wait for a platform to arrive, and a puzzle can advance only after the correct answer. Repeated inputs and restoration preserve deliberate activations without duplicating effects.

## Confirmed requirements

- **LOC-015-D01.** Check external triggers against each action's accepted types. A validated link can activate an enabled downstream action independently of its external trigger flags. Carry activation identity through the chain to prevent duplicate effects.
- **LOC-015-D02.** Declare link timing explicitly. Native links default to dispatch after source success, with explicit dispatch at activation or after completion supported. Verified classic mappings can retain their observed timing and conditions, including correct-answer gates.
- **LOC-015-D03.** Reject unsupported busy retriggers. Waiting and repetition require explicit bounded policies. Missing required links fail preparation. A required link that becomes unavailable during play stops that path and reports failure without replaying committed effects. Intentional timed loops require explicit schedules and stop conditions.

See the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#loc-015-interview-decisions).

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

Static inspection of DFU 1.1.1. `Assets/Scripts/Internal/DaggerfallAction.cs#Receive` rejects inactive, disabled or busy actions and checks external trigger flags. Linked `ActionObject` dispatch bypasses that flag check. `Play` dispatches the next action before its own effect for most action flags. `UserInputHandler` advances the chain after a matching answer. `IsPlaying` follows successor links recursively while the current action is idle, with no cycle guard in that path. `Utility/RDBLayout.cs#LinkActionNodes` resolves action links. `Internal/DaggerfallActionCollision.cs` can forward collision triggers to `Receive`.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

1. Validate action definitions and link targets in their authored placement scope. Preserve supported classic trigger types, links and timing through verified mappings. An independent external activation requires an enabled action and a matching trigger type. A linked request must come from a validated link in the current activation and must pass target availability and repeat rules.
2. Deduplicate repeated delivery of the same authorized activation and effect occurrence. Preserve distinct authored activations and scheduled repetitions. Door-specific and generic dispatch share the applicable activation identity under MOVE-018/021. Repeated block placements resolve independent state.
3. Dispatch each link at its resolved timing. Activation dispatch runs when the source accepts activation. Success dispatch waits for the action owner's successful outcome. Completion dispatch waits for the declared completion milestone, such as the end of motion. Apply any authored outcome condition, including correct puzzle input, at that milestone. Honor validated authored order where multiple links are eligible.
4. Apply the declared one-shot, repeatable and busy policies. Reject a busy trigger unless an explicit bounded wait or repeat rule permits it. Validate intentional cyclic behavior with a schedule, bounded execution and stop conditions. An unsupported cycle cannot recurse or repeatedly apply effects.
5. Missing required links fail preparation under DATA-005 and the applicable layout owner. A required link that becomes unavailable during execution stops the affected path and reports failure to its owner. Retain committed effects and their completion records. Any recovery must respect those outcomes.
6. Pause freezes gameplay-linked timers under MOVE-021. On a successful stock dungeon reset, restore ordinary mechanisms to their authored initial trigger and chain state and retire pending work from the prior visit. Preserve quest progress and owner-governed state under LOC-013.

## Formulas, parameters and configuration

Content declares trigger types, link timing and conditions, authored dispatch order, repeat and busy policies, and intentional schedule bounds and stop conditions. Native link timing defaults to source success. Exact wait bounds, chain budgets and supported scheduling parameters remain open.

## Required data

Stable scoped action and link IDs, trigger and activation identity, effect occurrence identity, link timing and conditions, repeat policy, pending progress and timers, committed outcomes, active layout and visit identity, and classic or mod provenance.

## Inputs, outputs and events

- **Inputs** — Authorized external or linked trigger, current action state, validated link data, owner outcome or completion, time and lifecycle context.
- **Outputs** — Accepted, rejected, waiting, completed or failed results, and authorized link dispatches.
- **Events** — Preserve activation provenance across acceptance, dispatch, completion and failure. Effect owners publish committed outcomes once.

## Cross-system interactions

- **DATA-005 and LOC-009** provide validated links, placement scope and preparation readiness.
- **MOVE-018/021** govern interaction routing, trigger composition and pause. **LOC-016/017** define mechanism effects and door behavior. **COMBAT, MAGIC and QUEST** own their applicable outcomes.
- **LOC-013** governs dungeon resets. **SAVE and CORE** govern supported in-flight restoration and recovery.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- A direct interaction and a door callback delivering the same activation cannot duplicate an effect. A later authorized trigger can run a repeatable action.
- A wrong puzzle answer leaves its gated link undispatched. Delayed motion cannot satisfy a completion link before its declared milestone.
- A missing required target fails preparation. A target that becomes unavailable after an earlier effect commits stops the affected path and preserves the committed outcome.
- Check an idle cycle, a bounded scheduled loop, and a busy target whose wait bound expires. Apply explicit failure or stop behavior.

## Save/load considerations

Persist supported mechanism state, pending link progress, timer context and committed outcomes under MOVE-021 and SAVE. Restore against validated scoped references without firing links as fresh triggers or replaying effects. Apply approved save restrictions to unsupported in-flight cases. Restore within a visit preserves chain state. Successful reset initializes ordinary chains under LOC-013.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Bound execution and wait work. Validate layout, visit and activation identity before accepting delayed results. Execution representation, scheduling and serialization formats remain open.

## Acceptance criteria

1. External triggers respect authored types. Valid links can activate downstream actions eligible for linked dispatch. Duplicate delivery produces one effect occurrence.
2. Native success dispatch and explicit activation and completion dispatch run at their declared milestones. Verified classic timing and correct-answer conditions are preserved.
3. Unsupported busy triggers reject. Allowed waits and loops obey authored bounds and stop conditions. Required link failures preserve already committed effects.
4. Save/load restores supported progress without replay. Successful stock reset initializes ordinary mechanisms and prevents obsolete work from the previous visit.

## Outstanding questions

- Verify classic trigger flags, link timing, cycles and exceptional mechanisms against representative fixtures.
- Define completion milestones, execution and wait bounds, and migration of supported pending chains with effect owners and SAVE.
