# LOC-017 — Action door locks and motion

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-14 with “I approve”.

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

Operate ordinary action doors without a moving door narrowing a passage. Locks, failed attempts and first-use interactions remain consistent throughout a visit. Returning after a stock dungeon reset restores ordinary doors to their authored starting state.

## Confirmed requirements

- **LOC-017-D01.** Retain MOVE-021's ordinary door behavior. Ordinary doors are nonblocking while opening, closing or open. Fully closed doors restore blocking only when solid occupants are clear. The exact visual behavior while blocking is pending remains open under MOVE-021.
- **LOC-017-D02.** Within a visit, save and restore locks, magical holds, failed-pick skill, first-use text state and supported motion or pending closure. Successful stock dungeon reentry reset initializes ordinary doors from authored defaults and clears failed-pick history, subject to quest state and validated mod policies.
- **LOC-017-D03.** Allow explicitly authored first-use actions to display text and defer opening. Recheck current lock and access on the next valid attempt. Other effects follow LOC-015 timing. NPC activation cannot consume a player-only prompt.

See the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#loc-017-interview-decisions).

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

Static inspection of DFU 1.1.1. `Assets/Scripts/Internal/DaggerfallActionDoor.cs#ToggleDoor` ignores moving-door toggles. `AttemptLockpicking` can clear a lock and request opening. `Open` can dispatch a first-use DoorText action and defer opening, and opening motion can switch the collider to a trigger. `Assets/Scripts/Game/Serialization/SerializableActionDoor.cs#RestoreSaveData` restarts motion from saved progress. The source door field marks failed-pick skill persistence as a TODO.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

1. Resolve requests against the exact scoped door, current state and authorized interaction. Apply MOVE-021 for toggles and locked feedback. Reject ordinary toggles during motion. Route picking through RULE-009 and MOVE-021. After failure, retry requires strictly higher live skill. Route deliberate bash and magical actions through their approved owners. A successful pick clears the lock and requests opening.
2. Ordinary open and moving doors permit player and applicable projectile passage under MOVE-021. Retain interaction targetability under existing dispatch rules. Apply blocking only after the door is fully closed and clearance is validated, including after restoration. Treat occupancy as pending blocking, without crushing or pushing. Validated special mechanisms follow their authored policies and MOVE-008.
3. Resolve a player-only first-use action through the original door activation. Where authored, show its text and defer opening on that activation. Keep its consumed state within the visit. A later valid attempt checks current access and locks. NPC actions leave player-only prompt state intact.
4. Dispatch authorized attached actions once at their declared LOC-015 milestone. Evaluate completion against the milestone the link declares. A link that requires a closed, blocking door waits for both conditions. Opening, motion restore and collision updates preserve the source activation context.
5. On successful stock reentry under LOC-013, restore ordinary locks, door poses and first-use state from the authored initial state and clear failed-pick history. Retire old pending door work. Honor quest state and approved modded reset policy. A failed reentry leaves the reset due.

## Formulas, parameters and configuration

Use RULE-009 for picking and COMBAT-006/016 for eligible bashing. Configure authored lock state, motion, collision and target policies, first-use actions and reset overrides through validated content. Numeric motion values, masks and closure presentation require fixtures.

## Required data

Stable scoped door and action IDs, active layout and visit, authored lock and pose defaults, current physical lock and magical hold, failed skill, first-use state, motion direction and progress, pending blocking, activation identity and applicable owner results.

## Inputs, outputs and events

- **Inputs** — Authorized interaction, linked action, owner lock or bash result, current motion and occupancy, or restore and reset context.
- **Outputs** — Lock and motion state, interaction and blocking context, and checked success, rejection or pending results.
- **Events** — Publish motion and blocking milestones to their consumers. LOC-015 dispatches authorized links under the original activation. Owners publish outcomes once.

## Cross-system interactions

MOVE-018/021 govern dispatch and ordinary door behavior. MOVE-008 governs explicit crush hazards. RULE-009, CHAR, COMBAT, MAGIC and NPC-014 govern attempts and outcomes. LOC-013/015 supply reset and chain semantics. DATA, MOD, QUEST, CORE and SAVE govern compatible content, retained outcomes and restoration.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- Test closing with a solid occupant in the doorway. Passage stays nonblocking until safe closed blocking can be established.
- Test equal and lower skill after a failed pick, magical hold, and a stale bash against a moving or opened door.
- Test NPC use before the player's first-use prompt and duplicate delivery from generic and door-specific dispatch.
- Check a stale motion completion after restore or reset. It cannot replay an old trigger or impose blocking on the current doorway.

## Save/load considerations

Restore supported door state and applicable chain progress against stable compatible layout references before play resumes. Verify occupancy before enabling blocking. Preserve within-visit failed skill and first-use state without repeating prompts, effects, rolls or practice. Unsupported in-flight states follow approved save restrictions and required incompatibilities follow recovery.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Track physical blocking, projectile response, interaction targetability and motion state coherently. Validate current layout, visit and activation before applying delayed updates. Scene representation and serialization format remain open.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Ordinary open, opening and closing doors permit passage. Blocking resumes only at a fully closed and clear doorway. Applicable projectile and interaction rules hold.
2. Restore a locked or moving door within a visit with its failed-pick skill, prompt state and supported progress intact. No effect or attempt replays.
3. Stock reentry restores ordinary authored defaults and clears failed-pick history. Quest retention and validated mod policies apply.
4. A player-only first-use prompt can defer opening once per authored state. NPC use cannot consume it. Subsequent attempts and links obey current eligibility and declared timing.
