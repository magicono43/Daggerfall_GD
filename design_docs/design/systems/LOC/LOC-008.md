# LOC-008 — Furniture and property containers

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-14 with “I approve”.

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

Make furniture interactions predictable. A shop shelf exposes its assigned stock, a reading fixture offers books, and designated home storage keeps the player's belongings across visits and compatible layout edits.

## Confirmed requirements

- **LOC-008-D01.** Author explicit fixture roles for decoration, readable bookshelves, shop stock, and household or player storage. Import classic roles from verified model and building context. Allow validated mod overrides.
- **LOC-008-D02.** Use stable interior-scoped fixture IDs. Compatible moves, rotations and appearance changes can preserve fixture identity and contents. Reused layouts have independent state per interior. Replacement or removal of a fixture with durable contents requires explicit owner-approved disposition.
- **LOC-008-D03.** Property acquisition changes access and stock policies only for designated fixtures. Eligible player storage has no automatic household restock. Existing contents follow their approved disposition rules. Decorative and reading roles persist unless an explicit property rule changes them.

See the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#loc-008-interview-decisions).

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

Static inspection of the local DFU 1.1.1 snapshot. `Assets/Scripts/Internal/DaggerfallInterior.cs#AddFurnitureAction` classifies selected shelf models using the building type and player ownership. It attaches shop stock, bookshelf behavior or house storage as appropriate. It also recognizes generic house container models. `MakeHouseContainer` adds a loot container and can register it for serialization. The load ID packs the building key and position data. `Game/PlayerActivate.cs#ActivateLaddersAndShelves` handles bookshelf activation, while `ActivateLootContainer` handles container interactions.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

1. Resolve the fixture role and its target binding from the validated interior definition. Classic import translates supported model and building-context combinations into explicit roles. Native and modded layouts can author roles directly. A compatible appearance override can reuse the resolved role. Validate required bindings against the active layout and report any required failure through LOC-005.
2. Resolve a placed fixture by stable interior and local fixture ID. Bind functional fixtures to their declared reading, stock or storage context. Geometry, placement and role compatibility remain independently verifiable. Identical models and shared templates can have distinct fixture records and owner state.
3. Pass the exact fixture and context to the interaction owner. Reading follows MOVE-021 and grants no implicit item ownership. Shop and storage interactions follow MOVE-022, ECON-001 and ITEM-021/022. Each transfer checks current target, reach and eligibility. If the original fixture moves out of reach, unloads or is replaced, pending transfers require a fresh interaction under ITEM-022.
4. On property acquisition, apply the applicable ECON-013 and ITEM-021 disposition and access rules to the designated fixtures. Preserve provenance for generated stock, deposits and protected contents. Expose eligible player storage without automatic household refresh. Apply explicit authored role changes through validated property rules.
5. Preserve fixture identity through compatible edits. When a fixture with durable contents is replaced or removed, validate the owner-approved custody or recovery outcome before applying the change to that state. Do not infer continuity from nearby furniture or the same mesh.

## Formulas, parameters and configuration

Configure fixture roles, property designations, applicable reading or stock references and explicit overrides. Stock refresh, storage admission and transfer parameters remain with their owners. No new stock or capacity formula is selected. Classic classification coverage, fixture ID encoding and migration representation remain open.

## Required data

Stable interior and fixture IDs, placement and resolved layout version, role and source provenance, applicable property, reading, stock or container references, ownership and access context, and content disposition or compatibility data where required.

## Inputs, outputs and events

- **Inputs** — Resolved layout, fixture definitions and bindings, current ownership and owner state, and validated content edits.
- **Outputs** — Resolved fixture and role, usable owner binding, or explicit unavailable or failed binding.
- **Events** — Interaction and property owners publish committed outcomes. Fixture assembly does not itself generate transfers, reading rewards or ownership changes.

## Cross-system interactions

- **LOC-005 and DATA-003/005/021** provide interior context, classic data and validated overrides or appearance substitutions.
- **MOVE-018/021/022** own dispatch, reading and container interaction. **ECON-001/013** own shop and property access. **ITEM-021/022** own stock, contents and transfers. **QUEST and LAW** apply their applicable protected-item and property rules. **SAVE** coordinates restoration and recovery.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- Check identical shelves with different roles, repeated interiors, and a moved or reskinned storage fixture. Resolve each intended role and state.
- Check acquisition with household stock, player deposits and protected contents present. Validate custody and access under the applicable owner rules.
- Check removal or incompatible replacement of nonempty storage. An unresolved disposition requires approved recovery.
- Check empty persistent storage and a pending interaction during unload or replacement. Follow ITEM-022's persistence and revalidation rules.

## Save/load considerations

Persist owner-held contents and durable state against stable fixture and container references. Rebuild live bindings from the validated layout, checking compatibility independently from ID continuity. Apply validated migration or approved recovery for incompatible references and contents. Routine layout reconstruction does not refresh stock or replay transactions.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Keep fixture identity independent of position, visual asset and live node identity. Retain scoped and versioned bindings through activation. Scene representation and fixture authoring format remain open.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Identical models can resolve different authored roles. Representative classic fixtures and validated overrides resolve their intended roles.
2. A compatible fixture move or appearance edit preserves stored contents. Reused interiors keep independent state. A removed or replaced fixture with durable contents follows an explicit disposition.
3. Property acquisition enables designated storage without household refresh. Existing contents follow approved disposition, and reading and decorative fixtures retain their roles unless explicitly changed.
4. Restoring an interior rebinds the correct contents and does not replay stock or item transactions. A stale pending transfer cannot act on a different fixture.
