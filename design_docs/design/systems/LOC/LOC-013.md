# LOC-013 — Fixed and random enemy placement

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-14 with “I approve”.

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

Populate dungeons with recognizable fixed encounters and context-appropriate random enemies. By default, returning after a committed departure starts a fresh dungeon visit. Quests retain their assignments and earned progress.

## Confirmed requirements

- **LOC-013-D01.** Use authored enemy types for fixed slots and verified classic dungeon-specific selection for imported random slots by default. Support authored alternate profiles, including level-based selection. Settle assignments once per authorized population and reuse them on retries and reloads.
- **LOC-013-D02.** By default, reset the dungeon on successful reentry after a committed departure, including teleport out. Failed exits, block changes and native load inside the dungeon preserve the current visit. Reset ordinary enemies, corpses, loot and mechanisms to fresh visit state and delete ordinary player-dropped items left in the dungeon. Make reset behavior moddable.
- **LOC-013-D03.** Preserve quest assignments and progress. A living quest enemy returns to its assigned spawn. A defeated quest enemy stays defeated and any item still required from its body remains recoverable under quest custody rules.
- **LOC-013-D04.** Check spawn suitability for the assigned enemy. Use only eligible authored alternatives for that slot and retain the chosen enemy on retry. An exhausted required slot fails preparation. An exhausted optional slot stays unfilled.

See the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#loc-013-interview-decisions).

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

Static inspection of DFU 1.1.1. `Assets/Scripts/Utility/RDBLayout.cs#AddFixedEnemies` and `AddRandomEnemies` process separate RDB enemy records. The classic random path seeds separate land and water candidate lists from the dungeon record's location ID. The alternate path uses supplied monster power and variance. `Internal/DaggerfallDungeon.cs#LayoutDungeon` supplies a tick-derived seed and clamps player level divided by 20 to the range 0–1 for monster power. The target reset policy is the confirmed design decision above.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

1. Resolve slots by dungeon, block placement and local record identity. Resolve quest claims and retained obligations before ordinary population. A claimed slot cannot also publish an ordinary occupant.
2. For a fresh population, use fixed archetypes and applicable random profiles. Settle selection under RULE-002/003 as applicable, and construct and restore actors through NPC-005. A new authorized population can capture fresh construction inputs. The same classic selection inputs can reproduce the same enemy types on later visits.
3. Validate authored poses for the assigned actor's size, locomotion and water context. Check eligible authored alternatives within the slot. Reuse the retained assignment across retries. Required placement failure follows LOC-009 readiness and MOVE recovery.
4. A committed exit makes the stock reset due at the next successful entry. Prepare the fresh visit and its owner dispositions before committing entry. Discard failed or cancelled preparation without consuming the reset or deleting existing custody. Retries reuse settled results for the same pending population. Clear ordinary visit state through its owners once per successful reset. Restore the ordinary layout, enemy population, loot and mechanisms to their fresh-visit policies. Retire ordinary corpses and their remaining ordinary contents and delete ordinary player drops still in dungeon custody. Detailed loot and mechanism initialization remain with their respective features.
5. Carry active quest placement claims and objective outcomes into the fresh visit. Return living quest enemies to their assigned spawn under their existing identity. Preserve committed defeat and keep any outstanding quest item recoverable through its owner. Protected items and outstanding obligations follow ITEM, QUEST and other applicable owner rules before cleanup. Modded reset policies may change triggers and retention through validated definitions subject to those owner rules.

## Formulas, parameters and configuration

Configure fixed archetypes, random profiles, spawn alternatives, required slots and reset triggers and retention. Stock reset triggers on reentry after committed departure. DFU's monster power formula is a source reference for the alternate path. Exact imported tables and optional profile weighting require verification.

## Required data

Stable dungeon, placement, slot and occupant IDs, resolved content and profile versions, spawn poses and requirements, settled population results, visit and departure state, applicable quest claims, protected custody obligations, and reset policy.

## Inputs, outputs and events

- **Inputs** — Validated layout and profiles, visit and transition context, owner state, placement results and quest claims.
- **Outputs** — Resolved occupants or empty slots, staged reset and disposition results, and explicit preparation failures.
- **Events** — Bind reset commitment to successful reentry. Publish owner outcomes once. Reset cleanup produces no new kill, acquisition or quest credit.

## Cross-system interactions

- **LOC-009/012** supply layout readiness and environmental context. **DATA and MOD** validate slot definitions, profiles and reset policies.
- **NPC-005/007 and RULE-002/003** own construction, placement checks and selection sources. **MOVE-026/027 and WORLD** supply committed departure and arrival context.
- **QUEST-013, COMBAT-021 and ITEM-022** own claims, defeat, corpse and item custody. **LOC-014 through LOC-017** specify fresh treasure and mechanism state. **SAVE** coordinates durable restoration.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- Failed exit, internal traversal or load inside preserves the visit. Teleport to a different context counts only on successful departure.
- A failed reentry or required spawn must preserve protected custody and pending reset eligibility.
- A moved living quest foe returns to its assigned spawn. A defeated quest foe stays defeated, including when an outstanding quest item must remain recoverable.
- Check ordinary and protected items sharing one pile. Apply disposition to each item under its custody rules.

## Save/load considerations

Save committed visit and reset eligibility, population assignments, and owner state. A save inside restores that visit, including applicable defeat and drop state. A save outside retains any reset due on reentry. Validate changed slot mappings and layout compatibility under SAVE.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Keep slot and visit state independent of scene activation. Coordinate coherent reset and transition commitment so failures or stale completions cannot delete or duplicate state.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Fixed and random slots select as configured. A blocked slot uses only valid authored alternatives without rerolling.
2. Ordinary dungeon state refreshes and ordinary drops are deleted after committed departure and successful reentry. Failed transitions, internal movement and load inside preserve the current visit.
3. Living quest enemies return to their assigned spawn. Defeated quest enemies remain defeated and required items remain recoverable.
4. An approved mod profile can change the reset behavior while honoring protected custody and quest obligations.
