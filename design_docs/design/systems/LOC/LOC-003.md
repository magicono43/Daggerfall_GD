# LOC-003 — Doorway and building hit metadata

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-14 with “I approve”.

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

Let the player deliberately target a specific entrance, even when several doors share a facade or collider. Keep inspection, access attempts and traversal associated with the intended building and entrance as geometry changes or streams.

## Confirmed requirements

- **LOC-003-D01.** Give each entrance a stable ID scoped to its building, with validated destination and return doorway links. A move or mesh change can preserve the same entrance ID when its binding remains compatible. A replacement gets a new ID and requires explicit migration where continuity is intended.
- **LOC-003-D02.** Resolve hits through explicit collider bindings or authored hit regions. Require an explicit validated selection rule when regions overlap. An ambiguous live hit produces no door action.
- **LOC-003-D03.** Derive classic hit regions from verified source metadata where possible. Allow explicit mod bindings and overrides. Validate alignment and reachability of required regions after transforms or mesh changes. A required entrance that cannot be bound fails preparation for the affected area through existing recovery.

See the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#loc-003-interview-decisions).

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

Static inspection of the local DFU 1.1.1 snapshot. In `Assets/Scripts/Utility/RMBLayout.cs`, `AddStaticDoors` and `AddStaticBuildings` attach metadata to exterior objects. In `Game/PlayerActivate.cs`, `GetDoors` and `GetBuildings` look on the hit transform and its ancestors. `ActivateStaticDoor` checks custom doors before classic bounds. `Internal/DaggerfallStaticDoors.cs#HasHit` tests the hit point against world-space collider bounds and generally keeps the last match, stopping early for a dungeon exit.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

1. Bind the location-scoped building record to its local entrance IDs and validated destination and return links. Keep the exact entrance identity in the handoff to MOVE. Building inspection can resolve a building on a facade hit under MOVE-019 and applicable disclosure rules. A building hit alone grants no door action.
2. Resolve the hit against the active layout and the bindings for the hit geometry. Shared meshes and combined colliders can expose several entrances through their authored regions. Evaluate the resolved transform consistently with the placed geometry. Apply the validated selection rule when several door regions match. If the live result is ambiguous, dispatch no door action.
3. Build supported classic regions from verified source records. Validate explicit mod bindings and overrides in the active content. Check ownership, transform alignment, destination and return references, and interaction reach under authored access and traversal conditions. Send an unbound required entrance through WORLD-004/006 preparation failure and applicable DATA-003 or CORE recovery.
4. Pass the resolved entrance and current binding context to MOVE for interaction and access checks. Revalidate deferred attempts against their original identity and context before commitment under MOVE-020/026. Unload or replacement can invalidate the live binding. Access, locks, discovery and transition commitment use their respective owner rules.

## Formulas, parameters and configuration

Hit geometry, collider associations, overlap selection and supported links are content data. Coordinate conversion, region shape, tolerances and overlap rule representation remain open. Use MOVE interaction range and eligibility rules. No new range or hit tolerance is selected here.

## Required data

Stable location, building and entrance IDs, owning placement and source record, resolved transforms and frame, collider or region bindings, overlap selection rule where needed, destination and return references, layout and activation identity, and content provenance.

## Inputs, outputs and events

- **Inputs** — Current hit geometry and point, active layout and frame, and validated entrance and building bindings.
- **Outputs** — Exact resolved building or entrance and binding context, or an applicable miss, ambiguous or unavailable outcome.
- **Events** — Report required preparation failures with the affected building and entrance where available. MOVE and gameplay owners publish committed actions and outcomes.

## Cross-system interactions

- **LOC-001/002** supply placed geometry, scoped building records and readiness dependencies. Interior features supply destination placement and return binding data.
- **DATA-003/005/021 and MOD** govern active definitions, classic structural inputs and replacement geometry.
- **WORLD-004/006** gate readiness and retire stale bindings. **MOVE-018/019/020/026** own dispatch, information, access and traversal. **WORLD-015, QUEST and LAW** apply discovery, quest and legal consequences under their own rules.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- Check adjacent and overlapping entrances on one facade, a shared collider, and rotated or repeated placements. Hits must resolve within the owning placement.
- Check a wall hit that permits inspection, an entrance behind blocking geometry, and a visually relocated modded doorway with stale interaction geometry.
- Unload or replace a binding during a deferred attempt. An outdated hit or intent cannot redirect the action to another entrance.
- A required broken link or unusable hit binding fails explicit preparation. Distinguish this from an ordinary miss or an otherwise valid locked door.

## Save/load considerations

Persist committed stable doorway context through SAVE and MOVE-026. Reconstruct live bindings from validated content. Check link and placement compatibility independently from ID continuity and use validated migration or explicit recovery for incompatible required references. Restore committed context without replaying interaction or entry.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Keep hit resolution coherent with the active frame, resolved transform and activation. Preserve exact entrance identity regardless of collider grouping. Collider implementation, region representation and fixture tolerances remain open.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Two entrances on a shared collider resolve to their own IDs and validated destination and return links. An inspectable facade hit alone initiates no door action.
2. Authored overlap follows the validated selection rule. An ambiguous live hit produces no access attempt or transition.
3. Classic and modded fixtures demonstrate aligned, reachable bindings after supported transforms. A missing required binding fails affected-area preparation explicitly.
4. A compatible edit retains entrance identity. Replacements use new IDs, and unresolved stale attempts never retarget. Incompatible committed references follow migration or recovery.
