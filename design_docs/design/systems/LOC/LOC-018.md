# LOC-018 — Interior and dungeon automap discovery

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-14 with “I approve”.

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

Build a map through exploration. Players can use remembered routes on a return visit and distinguish what they have explored during the current visit. Older dungeon maps can be forgotten as new ones are explored, while the player's notes remain available when their locations are rediscovered.

## Confirmed requirements

- **LOC-018-D01.** Reveal the occupied segment and nearby segments in direct sight within a bounded range. Solid floors, walls and closed doors block sight-based discovery. Record discovery during exploration even with the map closed.
- **LOC-018-D02.** By default, preserve earned discovery through the stock dungeon reentry reset and start fresh current-visit highlighting. Loading inside preserves that visit's highlighting. Distinguish prior and current discovery with styling readable without color. Retention and highlighting carryover are moddable.
- **LOC-018-D03.** Scope stable segment IDs to the interior or dungeon. Carry discovery through compatible segments or validated mappings. New or unmatched geometry starts hidden.
- **LOC-018-D04.** Remember five dungeon maps by default, with a configurable cap and an unlimited option. Evict the least recently visited dungeon when admitting a map above the cap. Revisiting updates recency. Save/load preserves the order. Mods can configure retention.
- **LOC-018-D05.** Eviction forgets explored dungeon geometry and retains player notes. Hide a note in forgotten geometry until its location is rediscovered. Quest progress and independently authorized marks follow their owners.

See the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#loc-018-interview-decisions).

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

Static inspection of DFU 1.1.1. `Assets/Scripts/Game/Automap.cs` uses periodic downward and view-direction raycasts for discovery. It tracks discovered and current-run geometry and uses grayscale for previously discovered geometry. `SaveStateAutomapDungeon` stores geometry, notes and discovered teleporter connections and prunes records by last-visited game time. `Assets/Resources/defaults.ini.txt` sets the default retention count to five.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

1. Derive segments and map geometry from the effective layout and variant under LOC-005 and LOC-009. Simplification preserves meaningful spatial relationships. Resolve discovery in the active layout using occupied space and bounded direct sight. Intervening gameplay geometry governs occlusion. Map opening, map camera movement and rebuilding the map representation create no discoveries.
2. Record durable discovery and current-visit highlighting as separate facts. Reentry under LOC-013 retains remembered geometry and starts a new set of visit highlights by default. Native restoration within the visit restores both sets. Expose validated mod policies for carryover.
3. Track dungeon map memory per character. A committed visit updates the dungeon's recency, and admitting a dungeon map over the configured cap removes the least recently visited remembered dungeon's geometry discovery and related highlighting. Maintain a deterministic recency order even when visits share the same game-time stamp. Opening the map and restoring a save do not count as visits. The cap applies to dungeon maps. Unlimited retention skips capacity eviction.
4. Keep player-authored notes through eviction. Apply discovery visibility to the note's compatible location. Rediscovery can reveal the note again. Independently authorized quest marks follow their own disclosure rules and cannot reveal hidden geometry by implication.
5. Validate saved segment and note bindings against the active layout. Carry progress only through compatible identity or a validated mapping. Newly added or unmatched geometry starts hidden. An unresolved note remains hidden pending compatible resolution. Apply approved recovery where required by a dependent owner.

## Formulas, parameters and configuration

Default dungeon memory cap is five. Support validated cap overrides and unlimited retention. Configurable carryover applies to earned discovery and visit highlighting. Discovery range, scan timing, segment granularity, boundary handling and visual styling require fixture review. No DFU raycast constants are adopted as target values.

## Required data

Character and scoped site and segment IDs, effective layout version and transforms, map and gameplay occlusion geometry, committed pose and view context, durable discovery, current visit identity and highlights, ordered dungeon recency, retention policy and note references.

## Inputs, outputs and events

- **Inputs** — Validated layout, current exploration context, committed visit changes, retention configuration, note references and authorized mark data.
- **Outputs** — Disclosed geometry and marks, prior and current visit styles, and explicit compatibility or unavailable results.
- **Events** — Commit discoveries from exploration and eviction from retention policy. Refresh affected map views without emitting gameplay entry or quest outcomes.

## Cross-system interactions

LOC-005/009 supply resolved layouts. LOC-013 supplies the dungeon reset boundary. MOVE supplies the authoritative pose and view. LOC-019, UI and AV govern annotations and presentation. QUEST owns independently authorized marks and progress. DATA, MOD and SAVE govern configuration, compatibility and restoration.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- Test stacked rooms, a closed door, a newly opened view and near geometry hidden behind a wall.
- Test a sixth dungeon at the default cap after revisiting an older one. Evict by actual visit order, including when game-time stamps tie.
- Test an evicted dungeon with notes and a quest mark. Rediscovering a compatible segment can reveal its note. Quest disclosure remains owner-controlled.
- Test renamed sites, changed geometry and stale exploration results after transition. Unmatched new geometry stays hidden.

## Save/load considerations

Persist durable discovery, current-visit highlighting, dungeon recency and note references with layout compatibility context. Capture committed state without treating save or load as exploration or a visit. Restore the same order and apply validated compatibility or recovery. The retention policy does not erase notes or quest progress.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Keep authoritative discovery independent of material state and live scene handles. Scope queries to the active layout and visit. Map simplification, scan scheduling and storage format remain open.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Occupied and visible nearby segments reveal with the map closed. Occluded geometry and other floors remain hidden until eligible.
2. Stock reentry retains remembered geometry and starts fresh highlighting. Native load inside restores highlighting. Styles are distinguishable without relying on color.
3. The default cap remembers five dungeons. A sixth evicts the least recently visited, with revisits and save/load preserving order. A validated unlimited configuration performs no capacity eviction.
4. Evicted geometry hides its notes until rediscovery, and quest progress and authorized marks follow their owners.
5. A compatible edit retains mapped discovery. Newly added and unmatched geometry stays hidden.
