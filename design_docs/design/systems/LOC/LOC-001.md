# LOC-001 — Location layout and block placement

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-14 with “I approve”.

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

Make settlement exteriors coherent places to explore. Streets and entrances should connect to usable routes, and maps, quests and interactions should resolve the same placed buildings and landmarks. Familiar classic layouts retain supported spatial details. Mod authors can build both regular towns and irregular settlements that fit their terrain.

## Confirmed requirements

- **LOC-001-D01.** Preserve supported classic placements on import. Support snapped blocks and free placement with rotation in native layouts.
- **LOC-001-D02.** Allow shared settlement boundaries. Intentional footprint overlap requires an explicit authored selection rule wherever a consumer needs one settlement. Reject ambiguous overlaps during validation.
- **LOC-001-D03.** Support authored terrain shaping for streets and entrances. Validate that required routes and doors are reachable before making the affected area playable.

See the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#loc-001-interview-decisions) for the interview confirmation.

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

In the local DFU 1.1.1 snapshot, `Assets/Scripts/Internal/DaggerfallLocation.cs#LayoutLocation` walks the exterior width and height and places RMB blocks on a rectangular grid. `Terrain/StreamingWorld.cs#UpdateLocation` adds a terrain-relative settlement origin, feeds each block to city navigation, and can yield after placing each block. `Utility/RMBLayout.cs#CreateBaseGameObject` creates models and props and derives building and door keys from grid coordinates and record index. `Utility/GameObjectHelper.cs#CreateRMBBlockGameObject` adds sprites and lights and can add a block ground plane. The streaming caller disables the separate block plane. This is a static source trace.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

1. Resolve the active validated exterior definition and its geographic origin. Preserve supported classic placement, orientation and gameplay records through coordinate conversion. Native layouts can combine snapped and freely positioned, rotated pieces. Apply the same resolved placement to geometry, collision and placement-derived references used by navigation, doors, markers and maps.
2. Scope each placement to its location and each local record to its placement. Repeated uses of a block may share definitions and assets. Each occurrence has its own identity and owner-held mutable state. Reordering or changing a layout requires compatibility validation for affected references.
3. Validate ordinary settlement footprints independently of the block grid and map pixels. Shared edges and intentional overlaps must produce deterministic membership and single-settlement selection where required. Reject an overlap that leaves a required selection unresolved. Optional law, ambient and discovery areas follow their respective owner policies. WORLD-013 publishes committed membership changes.
4. Apply authored terrain shaping as part of preparing the affected exterior. Validate the required connections between terrain, walkable surfaces and supported entrances for the current layout. Reachability is evaluated for the authored access and traversal context so intentional locks and restrictions retain their gameplay behavior. Keep the affected area behind the WORLD-004 readiness gate until its required collision, routes, door bindings and other gameplay dependencies are usable.
5. Fail required preparation explicitly when a required block or binding is missing, malformed or unsupported. Report the affected location, placement or record and failure cause when available. Apply DATA-003 and WORLD-004 recovery as appropriate. Optional decoration follows WORLD-006 readiness rules.

## Formulas, parameters and configuration

The DFU reference grid places a block at local horizontal offset `(x × RMB side, y × RMB side)`, where RMB side is 4,096 classic units before conversion. Native content defines snapped or free transforms, footprints, overlap rules and authored terrain shaping. Godot unit conversion, snap increments, boundary inclusion and tolerances, terrain authoring format and reachability checks require fixture validation. No new numeric defaults are proposed.

## Required data

Stable location, block definition, placement and scoped record IDs. World origin and coordinate convention. Resolved transforms, footprint and overlap rules, terrain shaping and binding references, gameplay-critical geometry and collision, required route and entrance references, and active layout version and mod provenance.

## Inputs, outputs and events

- **Inputs** — Validated classic or native definitions, world and terrain context, requested area, and owner readiness results.
- **Outputs** — Resolved placements, scoped records, footprint and selection inputs for WORLD, geometry and collision for assembly, and explicit pending, usable or failed results.
- **Events** — Assembly readiness and failures identify the applicable area, layout and activation. WORLD and gameplay owners publish committed arrival, entry and discovery.

## Cross-system interactions

- **DATA-003/004/005/021** govern active content, geographic identity, block definitions and compatible geometry.
- **WORLD-002/003/004/006/013** govern coordinate publication, arrival, readiness, activation and committed membership. **WORLD-015/016** consume stable layout records with disclosure controls.
- **MOVE and LOC entrance features** use validated door and route bindings. **QUEST, NPC, ITEM and SAVE** use scoped records and retain owner-held state. **AV** uses placement and presentation inputs.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- Validate two towns in one pixel, a footprint across a pixel seam, a shared boundary and an intentional overlap. Check selection at the edge and inside the overlap. An overlap rule cannot independently grant discovery or jurisdiction.
- Verify that a freely rotated repeated block keeps its door, marker, collision and map representations aligned and resolves to its own records.
- Check shaped terrain at seams and entrances for gaps, steps and blocked required routes. A validated record can exist while its live route is still preparing.
- Discard obsolete readiness results after a layout or activation change. Repeated activation must preserve owner state and avoid duplicate interactions.

## Save/load considerations

Rebuild the exterior from validated content and rebind durable owner state through stable scoped references. Check layout compatibility independently of identity. Changed or removed required references need validated migration or explicit recovery. Revalidate the saved pose and quest bindings independently where a placement or terrain shape changes. Recompute membership without replaying entry, discovery or quest outcomes.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Validate conversion with representative classic layouts and validate native layouts against the same gameplay-facing geometry and reference requirements. Keep definition lookup independent of live activation, and publish coherent results in the active world frame. Scene representation, terrain tooling and validation algorithms remain open.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A representative classic exterior retains supported block positions, orientations and gameplay references across geometry, navigation and map data.
2. A native exterior combines snapped and freely rotated pieces. Repeated pieces resolve independent records and mutable state through reload and reactivation.
3. Shared edges and intentional overlaps resolve deterministically under validated rules, including within one map pixel. Ambiguous overlap fails validation.
4. With authored terrain shaping, required streets and entrances are usable under their intended access rules before the affected area becomes playable. Unfinished dependencies use the streaming hold. Invalid required content yields explicit failure.
5. An incompatible change to a saved or quest-referenced placement requires validated migration or explicit recovery. Compatible reassembly preserves owner state without replaying gameplay events.
