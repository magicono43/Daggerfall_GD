# LOC-012 — Water and special block context

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-14 with “I approve”.

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

Water contact follows the spaces the player actually enters, including pools that span blocks and dry floors above or below them. Crossing an invisible block seam in one pool preserves the swimming and breathing context. A palace hall and the dungeon beneath it can have distinct atmosphere and gameplay rules.

## Confirmed requirements

- **LOC-012-D01.** Convert verified classic water heights into depth-enabled areas with validated extents. Allow native bounded water volumes that span blocks. Resolve water data independently of arrival-marker selection.
- **LOC-012-D02.** Treat connected water at the same height as continuous across blocks. Preserve differing authored surfaces and resolve contact from actual position. Require a validated rule for conflicting volumes. Seam crossings and restoration preserve breath state. Splash feedback requires genuine gameplay entry into water.
- **LOC-012-D03.** Use explicit spatial areas with declared uses. Import verified classic castle and treasure-room coverage and allow mods to author areas across blocks or confined to one floor. Resolve membership from committed position. Each owner applies its own rules.

See the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#loc-012-interview-decisions).

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

Static inspection of DFU 1.1.1. `Assets/Scripts/Internal/DaggerfallDungeon.cs#FindMarkers` reads water height and castle metadata from the first Start marker and assigns water level 10000 when no Start marker exists. `Utility/RDBLayout.cs#AddWater` places a block-sized plane for wet blocks when its prefab is available. `DaggerfallDungeon.cs#GetPlayerBlockIndex` uses horizontal bounds. `Game/PlayerEnterExit.cs#Update` updates water and castle context on block changes and clears those facts when outside any block. `SpecialAreaCheck` recognizes Daggerfall's treasure room by the block name `S0000161.RDB`. Lighting, music and enemy behavior consume castle context.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

1. Resolve validated water and special-area definitions with the effective dungeon layout and transforms. Translate classic environmental metadata through verified mappings, retaining source provenance. A shared source marker can supply environmental and arrival data through their respective contracts.
2. Evaluate water using its actual extent and surface and the current pose and intervening solid geometry. Keep floors, bridges and dry spaces distinct from valid water contact. Use continuous water context across connected equal-height seams. Keep physically separate pools distinct even if their surfaces have the same height. Preserve authored height differences. Require a validated selection rule wherever conflicting volumes would make the result ambiguous.
3. Provide water geometry and context to MOVE-014. MOVE evaluates body contact, swimming and breathing-point submersion from its respective checks. AV uses the applicable context for presentation. Genuine committed entry can produce splash feedback once. Rebase, continuous seams and native restoration produce no synthetic entry or breath reset.
4. Resolve special-area membership against the committed position and declared spatial scope. Areas may span placements or be confined vertically. Supply memberships to the declared consumers. Lighting, music, hostility and other outcomes follow their owner policies. Membership alone grants no access, immunity or automatic change in hostility.
5. Report an explicit absence when a valid query falls outside all applicable definitions. Use approved content and readiness recovery for invalid or unavailable required definitions. Retire stale context when the layout or activation changes.

## Formulas, parameters and configuration

DFU converts the native water height to a local vertical offset using `-nativeBlockWaterLevel × MeshReader.GlobalScale`. Its 10000 sentinel is translated as absence in the classic adapter. Validated native content defines surfaces, extents, connected water, conflict rules and special-area scopes and uses. Exact fit tolerances, shape formats and boundary conventions remain open. Movement and breathing thresholds follow MOVE-014.

## Required data

Stable dungeon, placement, water and area references as applicable, resolved layout and transforms, source metadata and provenance, water surface and depth-enabled extent, connection and overlap rules, special-area scope and consumer uses, committed pose and activation context.

## Inputs, outputs and events

- **Inputs** — Validated layout and environmental definitions, committed position and frame, and geometry and occupancy context required by the consumer.
- **Outputs** — Resolved water geometry or absence, special-area memberships, or an applicable unavailable or failed result.
- **Events** — Publish coherent context changes with active layout and scope. MOVE and other owners publish physical transitions, presentation and gameplay outcomes.

## Cross-system interactions

- **LOC-009/011, DATA-005 and MOD** provide the resolved layout and verified structural metadata. **MOVE-004/014/027 and CHAR** consume physical water context for movement, placement and breath under their own rules.
- **AV and LOC-018** use validated environmental data for presentation and applicable map depiction. **NPC, COMBAT, LAW and QUEST** apply only their declared special-area policies. **SAVE** coordinates compatible reconstruction.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- Test a connected pool crossing several blocks, independent pools at the same height, a height step, and an unresolved overlap.
- Test dry floors, bridges and stacked rooms that share horizontal coordinates with water or a special area. Use the actual spatial context.
- Test leaving all defined areas, a current pose restored underwater, and frame changes at a seam. Do not carry stale memberships or reset breath.
- Invalid required environment definitions follow explicit preparation failure and recovery. Optional presentation failure cannot change authoritative physical context.

## Save/load considerations

Reconstruct water and special areas from the compatible resolved layout. Restore committed pose, breath and durable gameplay state under their owners. Recompute current contact and memberships against validated geometry before play resumes. Required pose or layout incompatibility uses approved migration or recovery. Restore emits no synthetic splash or area entry.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Use consistent transforms for environmental queries and resolved gameplay geometry. Keep environmental context distinct from its rendered representation and from owner-specific effects. Volume construction, spatial indexing and boundary algorithms remain open.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A representative classic flooded block resolves its verified water height and usable extent. A dry floor or bridge in the same horizontal footprint does not cause false immersion.
2. A continuous equal-height seam preserves contact and breath without a duplicate splash. Differing surfaces and conflicting volumes follow validated content rules.
3. A special area may span blocks or occupy one floor. Membership changes with committed position and drives only declared owner uses.
4. Restore reconstructs validated context and preserves breath without replaying splash or entry. Invalid required environment data follows approved recovery.
