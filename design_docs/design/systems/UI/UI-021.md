# UI-021 — Court and incarceration presentation

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-16 with “I approve”. Updated 2026-11-16.

## Gameplay purpose and intended experience

Understand the charge and possible penalties, make a deliberate plea or defense, and read the resolved outcome before continuing. During prison presentation, hold a control to accelerate the countdown.

## Confirmed requirements

- **UI-021-D01.** Present explicit plea and defense choices as applicable, followed by a verdict and sentence summary with one Continue acknowledgment.
- **UI-021-D02.** Use hold-to-speed-up for the stock prison countdown. Support modded input, pacing and presentation.

See [gameplay decisions](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#ui-021-interview-decisions).

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

In the local DFU 1.1.1 snapshot, `Assets/Scripts/Game/UserInterfaceWindows/DaggerfallCourtWindow.cs#HandleCourtLogic` handles court stages, `#DebateLie_OnButtonClick` handles a defense response, and `#UpdatePrisonScreen` drives the countdown. `Update` allows held Back to accelerate the countdown while in prison. The court window also participates in advancing time and releasing the player. Findings are from static inspection.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

1. Render the authoritative case and charge under LAW-011. Before plea, show the disclosed ordinary pre-plea fine and prison ranges, their shared units, applicable unpaid-fine rule and whether severe punishment is possible. Keep the retained exact base result internal until its owner authorizes disclosure. Display only the applicable choices at each stage, including the owner's rescue or severe-outcome route where relevant.
2. Require explicit selection of a plea or applicable defense. Capture case, stage and choice identity and submit through UI-002. The LAW owner revalidates and resolves the outcome. Focus and navigation alone do not plead, choose a defense or advance the case. Back and Return to Game cannot dismiss a required decision.
3. Show one verdict and sentence summary with the owner-disclosed disposition and applicable obligations. Continue acknowledges the displayed result and advances the owner's presentation flow. It cannot change the verdict or repeat the calculation, payment or penalties. Required resolution and recovery remain with LAW. Acquittal, rescue and severe outcomes use their applicable owner routes. Open a prison countdown only for a positive final ordinary sentence under LAW-015.
4. Show a **Hold Back to Speed Up** hint using the active device's binding label. Require a fresh press in the countdown scope. While held, accelerate presentation and return to normal pacing on release. Holding can advance presentation to its end early. Consume the activation through release so it cannot dismiss the next view or act in gameplay. Provide an equivalent reachable control on supported devices. Mods may use different bindings, speeds, visuals and skip styles.
5. Countdown pacing changes only presentation. LAW/TIME commit the same prison interval, release processing and required consequences through the approved once-only flow. Keep input gated until release placement and required settlement are ready. Show brief release feedback after a confirmed return, including applicable disclosed obligations, without another mandatory acknowledgment. If release is blocked or fails, show truthful owner recovery and do not report freedom or resume gameplay early.
6. Support targeted and whole-view modding under UI-003. Mods preserve required disclosures, explicit choices and owner scope. Presentation callbacks cannot recalculate a case, collect payment again, skip a sentence consequence or restore old gameplay input.

## Parameters, required data and configuration

LAW-015 defines the prison interval as the committed sentence days multiplied by 1,440 game minutes, with separate release processing under its policy. UI introduces no sentence formula. Required data includes case, session and stage identity, charges, disclosed ranges and risks, available choices, verdict and obligations, countdown state, binding, release readiness and owner recovery. Exact visuals, baseline countdown speed and acceleration remain open. Input and presentation profiles are moddable.

## Inputs, outputs and events

Inputs include case presentation, explicit choice, Continue, hold/release, owner settlement, countdown completion and release outcome. Outputs include disclosed court stages, scoped responses, acknowledged outcome, accelerated presentation and owner-confirmed release or recovery feedback. UI-001/002/005 govern handoffs, command identity and explicit responses.

## Cross-system interactions and consistency

Reviewed against LAW-011–016 for disclosure, rescue, plea, verdict, severe outcomes, prison and save boundaries, TIME for committed intervals, and CORE-016 and MOVE-001 for scope and input. UI-003/004 govern modding and controls. The countdown hold differs from cinematic any-key skip under UI-008. LAW and TIME own the sentence and settlement.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- A stale or duplicate choice cannot resolve a replacement case. A held Continue cannot accelerate a newly opened countdown.
- Fast and normal countdowns produce identical authoritative time and consequences. A held acceleration input cannot leak through release.
- No positive prison term means no countdown. Acquittal, rescue and severe outcomes retain their established routes.
- Technical release failure leaves controls gated and uses the relevant recovery. An old callback cannot present release for another session.

## Save/load considerations

LAW-016 governs save admission. Owners persist eligible case, committed obligations and settlement progress. Restore supported presentation from owner state under CORE/SAVE. Runtime focus and held input are transient. Loading cannot re-plead, collect a fine, replay time or manufacture release.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Separate presentation pacing from time advancement. Bind input and completion to the active stage and make required actions reachable across supported devices. Keep exact timing, layout and mod schemas open.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Court presents the approved pre-plea disclosures and only owner-approved choices. A plea or defense commits through explicit input once.
2. The verdict shows an accurate summary and one Continue. The acknowledgment cannot repeat settlement.
3. Stock hold speeds the countdown and release restores normal pace if still active. Both speeds yield identical time and consequences. Input cannot leak through the handoff.
4. Release feedback follows ready owner completion and technical failures retain recovery. Mods can customize presentation and acceleration while preserving required outcomes.
