# UI-016 — Quest offers, journal and notebook

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-16 with “I approve”. Updated 2026-11-16.

## Gameplay purpose and intended experience

Read quest offers and make deliberate choices. Find current objectives, revisit earned clues and outcomes, and keep personal notes. Use the journal to locate places when the game has authorized that information.

## Confirmed requirements

- **UI-016-D01.** Open on Active Quests, with separate History and Notebook views. Stock search covers visible quest titles, earned journal text and personal notes. Quest records are read-only. Players can add, edit and delete personal notes.
- **UI-016-D02.** Offer Show on Map for references the player is authorized to locate. Preserve vague clues as written. Where a previously valid destination is unavailable, show the reason where known and keep the entry readable.

See the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#ui-016-interview-decisions).

## Detailed rules and behaviors

1. Render quest offers and prompts from owner-supplied localized content and stable choice identities. Present blocking prompts one at a time in authored order. Apply UI-005 focus and input rules within the prompt's authored cancellation and deferral policy. Decline is an explicit choice. QUEST/NPC validate deadlines, offer lifetime, prerequisites and response identity before committing. Supported once-per-quest presentation receipts are recorded only after successful required presentation.
2. Active Quests shows disclosed current objectives and chronological earned entries. History shows retained terminal outcomes and earned entries in committed order under QUEST-018. Reopening, switching locale or searching shows only authorized records and never replays a reveal, branch or acknowledgment. Preserve historical facts under QUEST-007. Authored records, including authored notebook entries where provided, remain read-only.
3. Notebook offers Add, Edit and Delete for player-authored notes. Use UI-005 validation and cancellation for text input. Require explicit delete confirmation with the note bound by stable ID. Invalid or cancelled edits leave the prior note intact. Successful edits do not modify quest facts, grant discovery or change an authored note. New text follows the approved localized text-entry and label rendering rules while remaining player-authored content.
4. Provide one search across the visible records in Active Quests, History and Notebook, with each result identifying its source view. Match the current rendered quest title and earned text, including approved readable fallbacks, plus personal note text. Use case-insensitive literal matching. Reveal no hidden branches, undisclosed labels or internal metadata through results, counts or suggestions. Selecting a result navigates to its record without triggering an action. Clear search returns to browsing. Fresh entry opens Active Quests with no search query.
5. Bind Show on Map to the explicit place reference and owner-authorized disclosure. An ordinary vague clue remains readable without a precise marker. Revalidate the referenced target and disclosure at activation. If an authorized reference cannot currently resolve, display the known reason beside the entry and keep the original text. A historical place link remains bound to its recorded place and cannot silently jump to a replacement, namesake or relocated objective. WORLD owns current discovery and any expressly authorized map changes.
6. Open the map through its owner and preserve the originating journal view, search and eligible focus while covered. Returning restores the valid context under UI-001. Scope changes or invalidation cannot reopen retired prompts or action targets. UI-003 permits targeted or whole-view customization while preserving disclosure, required prompt handling and accessible controls.

## Parameters, required data and configuration

No gameplay formula or balancing value applies. Required data includes quest, prompt, retained variant and choice identities, authored closure policy, disclosed objective/history records and readable fallback, stable note ownership and identity, authorized place reference and availability, active view, focus and search query. Owners define content limits, deadlines, disclosure and supported recovery. Exact search layout, note length constraints and mod schema remain open.

## Inputs, outputs and events

Inputs include quest presentation requests, explicit choice or acknowledgment, journal open, tab navigation, search, note edits, Show on Map and owner results. Outputs include presentation and response outcomes, visible results, committed note changes, scoped map requests and availability feedback. UI-002 governs command identity. Search, focus and refreshing alone emit no gameplay mutation.

## Cross-system interactions and consistency

Reviewed against QUEST-007/010/011/018 for localization, deadlines, history and prompts, NPC-022/023 for disclosure and offer lifetime, WORLD-015 for authorized discovery, and UI-001 through UI-005 for navigation, routing, customization, focus and confirmations. CORE-012 retains ordered opening presentation. CORE-013 and QUEST/SAVE own supported prompt restoration. Journal links and search display existing knowledge within owner permissions.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- Equal timestamps follow committed quest order. Rewritten objectives retain earned history and its original facts.
- An author or player can use the same visible title for separate records. Search results and editing stay bound to stable identity.
- A place can become unavailable between display and activation. Keep the entry readable and report the owner outcome.
- Missing essential prompt text follows approved localization fallback or owner recovery. A deadline, duplicate click or stale response cannot bypass current validation.

## Save/load considerations

Persist personal notes and their stable identities through SAVE. QUEST owns earned records, receipts and supported pending prompts and preserves their identities and order. Search, focus and current tab are runtime state. Rebuild from current authorized records on load and reject old-scope commands. Loading never repeats a choice, note edit or map disclosure.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Derive search results exclusively from authorized rendered content. Separate editable note state from authored records. Use scoped identity for prompts, record actions and map links. Exact indexing, controls and persistence representation remain implementation choices.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Stock journal opens on Active Quests and exposes History and Notebook. Active and historical records preserve owner-approved facts and order.
2. Search finds disclosed titles, earned text and personal notes across the views without exposing hidden data. Opening a result performs navigation only.
3. Players can add, edit and delete personal notes. Cancellation leaves the prior state intact. Authored records remain read-only.
4. Show on Map resolves the authorized place only. Vague clues remain vague and unavailable places leave readable entries with status feedback.
5. Prompt choices respect authored order, closure policy, validation and scope. Restore, redraw and stale callbacks cannot replay choices or disclosures.
