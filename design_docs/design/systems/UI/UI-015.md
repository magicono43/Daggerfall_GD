# UI-015 — Talk topics and service menus

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-16 with “I approve”. Updated 2026-11-16.

## Gameplay purpose and intended experience

Talk to the intended NPC, browse relevant subjects and services, and reach the correct interaction without accidentally asking a question or making a purchase. Finishing a service normally returns to play.

## Confirmed requirements

- **UI-015-D01.** Keep stock dialogue and service screens separate. An NPC who offers both can show a small entry menu with Talk and applicable services. A dialogue-only NPC can open Talk directly. Mods may rearrange or combine the screens.
- **UI-015-D02.** Default to returning straight to gameplay after finishing a service. Make the return behavior moddable.

See the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#ui-015-interview-decisions).

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

In the local DFU 1.1.1 snapshot, `Assets/Scripts/Game/UserInterfaceWindows/DaggerfallTalkWindow.cs` presents topics and Polite, Normal and Blunt tone choices. `Game/TalkManager.cs#StartNewConversation` prepares conversation state, and `#TalkToStaticNPC` can route to a quest offer before ordinary dialogue. `DaggerfallGuildServicePopupWindow.cs#DoGuildService` and `DaggerfallMerchantServicePopupWindow.cs` route separate service interactions. Findings are from static inspection.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

1. Use the NPC-017 entry decision and scoped actor identity. When both Talk and services apply, present a compact entry menu where the owning context permits it. A dialogue-only interaction can go directly to Talk. A service-only context exposes the applicable service route. An authorized quest interception or refusal takes its owning path before ordinary entry.
2. Present stock DFU-style topic categories and tones through NPC-018/019. Show only authorized labels and contextual disambiguation. Selection, focus, tone changes and list refreshes do not submit a question. An explicit submission captures the conversation, speaker, topic and chosen tone. The NPC owner validates it, resolves the answer and owns allowances, reactions and practice.
3. Present declared services with their labels and applicable access state. Hide options the owner does not disclose. Where an unavailable option is disclosed, show its reason without allowing an invalid request. Explicit service selection captures the NPC, service and interaction context. Revalidate access before opening or committing. Opening a service does not itself authorize a charge or a quest decision.
4. When a service reports that its interaction has finished, close its service and NPC entry layers and return to gameplay under UI-001. A successful transaction inside an ongoing service does not automatically finish the whole service. Release only the closing scopes' ownership. Consume the closing input and let CORE determine when gameplay input and simulation are eligible.
5. Back or a cancelled child prompt before service completion returns one layer under UI-001 when that context is still valid. Recoverable service errors keep the relevant view and valid selection. For quest prompts, follow the QUEST owner's continuation, including any interception that requires exiting or opening another view. If the interrupted interaction is still valid and the owner specifies a return, restore eligible focus and refresh available topics or services. Never synthesize an answer or reissue an offer on return.
6. Allow mods to combine or replace views, change presentation and choose a return-to-NPC-menu policy on service completion through supported contracts. Before restoring that menu, revalidate the speaker and interaction scope, refresh options and restore eligible focus. If a normal or modded return target has expired, fall back to the valid gameplay or owner recovery destination with concise feedback where applicable. Keep required quest decisions and transaction recovery reachable.

## Parameters, data and configuration

No new gameplay formula applies. Required data includes actor and conversation identity, topic and tone IDs, authorized labels, service and entry context, owner eligibility and outcome, return policy and focus state. NPC, QUEST, FACT and ECON own disclosure, availability and outcomes. UI/MOD define supported layout, routing and return overrides.

## Inputs, outputs and events

Inputs include NPC entry, focus and category changes, tone selection, topic and service activation, Back, cancellation, completion, quest continuation and scope invalidation. Outputs include menu or talk view, scoped topic/service requests, owner response, validity feedback and committed navigation. Route explicit requests through UI-002 and scope outcomes to the initiating interaction.

## Cross-system interactions and consistency

Reviewed against NPC-017 conversation lifecycle and modded pause, NPC-018/019 question and disclosure rules, FACT-014 and ECON-001 service entry, UI-001/002 navigation and request identity, UI-003/004 customization and focus, and UI-005 choice outcomes. QUEST owns interruptions and continuation. Service screens retain their own transaction and confirmation policies. Ordinary menu completion cannot release independent CORE suspension.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- Identical topic labels remain distinct by ID. Refreshing a list or returning from a child cannot ask a question or consume an allowance.
- An actor, quest state or service permission can change while a live modded menu is open. Revalidation prevents routing to a replacement speaker or charging for unavailable work.
- A completed transaction inside an ongoing window and final service exit are separate outcomes. Duplicate completion cannot close a later conversation.
- If a permitted return target becomes stale, use a valid destination without reviving the conversation or replaying its greeting, action or quest offer.

## Save/load considerations

Ordinary dialogue returns to gameplay on load under NPC-017. Topic focus, entry menu and return paths are transient. Durable knowledge, quest effects and service transactions remain with their owners. A supported restored activity reconstructs its own view from authoritative state. Load cannot replay dialogue or service outcomes.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Bind routes to stable actor, conversation and service identities. Keep owner operations separate from presentation and make navigation outcomes scope-safe. Preserve keyboard/controller access in stock and modded views. Exact layouts, mod schemas and routing APIs remain implementation choices.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Stock entry separates dialogue from service views and routes dialogue-only NPCs appropriately. An eligible quest interception takes the owner path.
2. Categories and tones are accessible. Only an explicit topic submission can resolve an answer or consume an allowance.
3. Service selection validates its intended actor and service. Disclosed restrictions show reasons and stale targets cannot commit.
4. Completed stock services return to gameplay. A supported mod policy returns to a still-valid, refreshed NPC menu.
5. Cancellation and recoverable failures preserve applicable prior context. Quest prompts follow owner continuation. Expired targets and duplicate callbacks cannot replay actions.

## Outstanding questions and approval

Exact menu layout, service-specific completion signals and mod schema remain owner dependencies.
