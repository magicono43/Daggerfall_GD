# UI-020 — Rest, travel and transport dialogs

**Approval status — Behavioral baseline approved and finalized as v1.** Travel presentation remains provisional pending playtesting and revision. Updated 2026-11-16.

## Gameplay purpose and intended experience

Choose how to rest, travel or change transport. Understand why an option is unavailable and what happened once an activity begins. Use playtesting to refine the feel and pacing of travel feedback.

## Confirmed requirements

- **UI-020-D01.** Keep familiar choices visible with owner-provided restriction reasons. Keep eligible alternatives selectable. A failed confirmation retains valid selections and shows the updated reason or quote.
- **UI-020-D02.** Show actual elapsed game time and a reachable Stop action during rest. Use a short travel progress view driven by committed progress, with truthful arrival, interruption or recovery feedback. Show a travel stop control only under an owner-supported policy. Make layouts moddable. Playtest and revisit the presentation before treating its pacing and visual design as settled.

See [gameplay decisions](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#ui-020-interview-decisions).

In the local DFU 1.1.1 snapshot, `Assets/Scripts/Game/UserInterfaceWindows/DaggerfallRestWindow.cs#Update` drives the rest presentation and its paced activity. `DaggerfallTravelPopUp.cs#UpdateLabels` updates estimated trip time and cost, and `#BeginButtonOnClickHandler` handles departure. `DaggerfallTransportWindow.cs` provides a separate local transport selector. ## Detailed rules and behaviors

1. Present separate rest, trip setup and local transport dialogs. List familiar options the owner permits disclosing and annotate blocked choices with an actionable reason where available. Each alternative has its own admission check. For example, blocked sleep does not block independently permitted loitering.
2. Rest offers the TIME-010 modes and their supported duration controls. Show actual committed elapsed game time once active. Provide a reachable Stop control that submits a stop request to TIME. Display the owner's stopped, completed or interrupted outcome, preserving committed time and recovery. A stop press cannot activate gameplay during the handoff.
3. Trip setup uses the authorized destination and owner-provided options, duration estimate, charges, tender and warnings under TIME-012–014. Confirm the exact current quote and its applicable disclosed risks. A new material quote uses a short change notice and fresh acceptance under the UI-017 pattern. The owner revalidates before departure. Preserve eligible selections on rejection while the view remains valid. Hidden conditions are disclosed only under the owner's rules.
4. The travel progress view reflects committed progression and never advances time or calculates arrival on its own. The draft proposes showing departure, elapsed route progress where meaningful, and arrival preparation as distinct phases. When progress cannot be expressed truthfully as a fraction, use an indeterminate or phase-only presentation. On interrupted travel show the actual outcome and disclose placement only as authorized. Show arrival only after TIME-015 confirms settlement and ready placement. A technical failure uses owner recovery. No stock travel Stop is introduced absent an approved owner operation.
5. Local transport shows current mode and applicable choices. Changing focus or inspecting a mode performs no switch. Deliberate selection revalidates with MOVE before changing mode. If denied, show the owner reason and keep eligible selection. On success, use the owner's committed mode and applicable navigation handoff.
6. Scope callbacks and requests to their active operation and session. UI can refresh from committed progress while gameplay input is gated. Preserve independent suspension and apply UI-001, UI-002 and UI-005 for input, prompts and handoff. Modded views under UI-003 retain owner validation, reachable controls and truthful progress. Treat the short travel presentation as a baseline for playtesting. Review legibility, pace and interruption feedback in a prototype before settling exact visuals or timing.

## Parameters, required data and configuration

No new timing, fare or balance formula applies. Required data includes mode and option IDs, owner eligibility and reasons, duration request or estimate, authorized destination and quote, warnings, committed elapsed time and route progress, operation scope, stop support and terminal outcome. TIME and MOVE define valid parameters and mutation. UI/MOD define supported presentation and controls. Exact progress layout, animation and pacing remain open for testing.

## Inputs, outputs and events

Inputs include opening a dialog, option and duration edits, destination quote, Confirm, Back, available Stop, and owner progress or outcomes. Outputs include eligibility feedback, current quote, scoped request, elapsed progress, completed/interrupted result or recovery navigation. Display never commits elapsed time or resources.

## Cross-system interactions and consistency

Reviewed against TIME-010 rest, TIME-012–015 selection, quotes and journey progression, MOVE-016 transport, CORE-016 suspension, and UI-001/002/003/004/005 for navigation, routing, customization, access and confirmation. UI-019 retains valid map view state on return. TIME owns stopping, committed time and interruption effects.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- A danger or eligibility change can invalidate an option after display. Revalidate at commitment and explain the denial.
- A stopped or interrupted activity preserves elapsed effects. Travel interruption cannot show successful arrival or imply a refund.
- Updated terms and newly applicable disclosed warnings require owner-approved renewed acceptance. Old quotes and callbacks cannot activate the new request.
- Fast completion, slow scene preparation and technical recovery must remain distinguishable during playtesting. Progress must not claim elapsed game time based on wall time.

## Save/load considerations

Uncommitted dialog state, focus and presentation timers are transient. TIME/MOVE/SAVE own durable outcomes and supported activity snapshots. Restore a supported operation from committed owner state without repeating elapsed time, charges or effects. Invalidate old UI commands.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Keep simulated progress and presentation pacing independent. Map UI requests to explicit owner operations and use accessible control and progress feedback. No prototype or runtime playtest was performed in this specification pass.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Familiar options show actionable blocked reasons while independent alternatives remain selectable.
2. Rest shows actual elapsed game time and supports reachable Stop, with committed consequences preserved.
3. Travel setup shows authorized terms and obtains required acceptance. Rejection preserves valid selections. Changed terms require fresh confirmation.
4. Travel feedback reflects real owner progress and distinguishes arrival, interruption and recovery. Stop is available only where the owner supports it.
5. Local transport changes only on a valid explicit request. Stale callbacks or loading cannot repeat an activity.
6. Before closing presentation design, playtest stock and supported custom layouts for progress legibility, perceived pacing, prompt handoff and interruption/recovery feedback, and revise as needed.

## Outstanding questions and approval

Exact visuals, phase labels, minimum display duration and any animation or fade treatment depend on playtesting.
