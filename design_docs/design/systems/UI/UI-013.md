# UI-013 — Inventory panes and contextual actions

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-16 with “I approve”. Updated 2026-11-16.

## Gameplay purpose and intended experience

Browse carried items and accessible remote inventories, inspect equipment on the paper doll, and act through a clear selected mode. Move a whole stack with an ordinary transfer and choose a precise quantity through a separate action.

## Confirmed requirements

- **UI-013-D01.** Retain visible DFU-style action modes and a paper doll in the stock inventory, with mouse, keyboard and controller access. Support modded layouts and interaction styles.
- **UI-013-D02.** Use a separate Split/Move Quantity action. Ordinary transfer attempts the whole selected stack.

See the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#ui-013-interview-decisions).

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

In the local DFU 1.1.1 snapshot, `Assets/Scripts/Game/UserInterfaceWindows/DaggerfallInventoryWindow.cs` filters local and remote panes by category and uses `SelectActionMode` to route actions. Paper-doll and accessory controls have distinct click handlers. `TransferItem` checks both left and right Ctrl during transfer. It can open the split dialog when Ctrl is held or when the whole stack cannot fit, provided splitting is allowed and the item is a stack. `SplitStackPopup_OnGotUserInput` handles the entered count. Findings are from static inspection.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

1. Stock inventory presents carried items, an accessible remote pane when applicable, category tabs, the paper doll and accessory controls, and visible Info, Equip, Remove/Transfer and Use modes as applicable. Display the active mode and pane context. Screen-owner rules govern specialized selection flows and the remote destinations they expose. Use UI-003 customization for alternative layouts or per-item menus and UI-004 for reachable mode and item navigation.
2. Selecting or focusing a row does not mutate inventory. An eligible activation uses the active mode and the selected item. Info shows disclosed item information. Equip, unequip, transfer and use route to their owners. A paper-doll hit or focus target identifies the whole equipped item through stable item/slot identity, including multi-slot equipment. Show committed outcomes and actionable owner rejection reasons.
3. An ordinary transfer requests the full selected stack or single item. The request is all-or-nothing. If the complete quantity cannot move, explain the reason and offer the separate quantity action where supported. Do not open the picker automatically or silently take a partial amount.
4. Expose a labeled Split/Move Quantity action for eligible transfer contexts, including keyboard and controller access. Ctrl+left-click on a stack opens that action while in a transfer context. Use UI-005 for quantity entry and validation. Exact Quantity and All submit explicit amounts. Take What Fits is a separate explicit choice when supported, with the moved quantity and remainder shown. Cancel leaves custody and quantity unchanged.
5. Capture stable item and stack identity, source, destination and applicable context at invocation. Before committing, the owner rechecks accessibility, custody, quantity, capacity and relevant restrictions. A stale or changed selection cannot transfer a replacement stack or commit to another remote inventory. Refresh from committed results, retaining valid selection and focus where possible. Use a safe fallback focus when the original item no longer exists.
6. Stock inventory follows UI-001 pause and input ownership, including supported live-UI mods. Modded input routes and buttons retain ITEM validation, approved disclosure and explicit quantity semantics. A poke at a preview, a pane refresh or a mode change cannot repeat an operation.

## Parameters, required data and configuration

Required data includes active mode and filter, pane/source and destination identities, stable item/stack and equipment-slot identity, displayed count and disclosed properties, access and capacity outcomes, and selected quantity policy. No extra capacity formula applies. ITEM-008 owns fitting quantity and all-or-nothing versus partial admission. Layout, exposed modes, controls and shortcut mappings use the applicable UI/MOD/settings contracts.

## Inputs, outputs and events

Inputs include pane/tab and mode changes, item selection and activation, quantity picker open/submit/cancel, remote access changes and committed owner outcomes. Outputs include inventory and equipment presentation, scoped item-action requests, rejection or success feedback, and updated selection. UI-002 handles command identity and stale responses.

## Cross-system interactions and consistency

Reviewed against ITEM-005–008 custody and quantity rules, ITEM-011 equipment identity and disclosure, ITEM-015 item action routing, MOVE-022 wagon access, and UI-001 through UI-005 for context, routing, customization, focus and prompts. Merchant and other specialized flows retain their owning transaction rules. Stock pause behavior and live UI profiles follow CORE-016 and UI-001.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- Quantity can change or the remote source can become unavailable while a picker is open. Revalidate and report the current outcome without moving a different stack.
- Exact or whole transfer cannot partially succeed. Explicit Take What Fits can move the maximum eligible quantity and reports what remains.
- Closing or cancelling a quantity prompt returns to the same valid inventory context. A replacement item in the same row or equipment slot is a different action target.
- An illegal or unavailable action explains the rejection. Mode switching, hover and inspection do not expose undisclosed properties or activate item effects.

## Save/load considerations

Inventory, equipment and custody are durable under ITEM/SAVE. Pane state, active mode, quantity drafts and focus are scoped UI state. Fresh entry uses stock or configured context defaults. A load cannot replay pending requests. Supported restored views use authoritative state under UI-001.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Render owner-provided item data and route explicit operations through scoped requests. Keep a quantity prompt bound to the original stack and transfer context. Exact layout, other input mappings, and view APIs remain implementation choices.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Stock inventory offers visible action modes, local and eligible remote panes, category tabs and navigable equipment targets.
2. Ordinary transfer attempts the full quantity without implicit splitting. A failed whole transfer leaves items unchanged and explains why.
3. Split/Move Quantity is separately reachable by mouse, keyboard and controller. Ctrl+left-click opens it for an eligible transfer stack.
4. Exact and All remain atomic. Take What Fits is explicit, reports moved and remaining quantities, and obeys owner policy.
5. Refresh, cancellation, stale selections and duplicate callbacks cannot redirect or repeat an item action. Paper-doll actions resolve the intended equipped item.
6. Modded views and input routes preserve owner checks and remain usable under the applicable input and pause policies.
