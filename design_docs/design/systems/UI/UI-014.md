# UI-014 — Reading and spell selection

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-16 with “I approve”. Updated 2026-11-16.

## Gameplay purpose and intended experience

Read and revisit books, including illustrated custom editions. Browse learned spells and eligible magic items without activating them by highlighting a row. Get a clear reason when an action is refused.

## Confirmed requirements

- **UI-014-D01.** Use a DFU-style paged reader with Previous, Next and a page indicator. Reopen at the remembered passage using approved anchor rules. Close back to the originating view. Support moddable presentation and images in custom books.
- **UI-014-D02.** Use dedicated stock spell and magic-item selectors. Highlighting shows details. Refused actions keep the selector and valid selection available with a reason. Successful spell readiness returns to gameplay for normal cast input. Successful magic-item actions follow their owning flow.

See the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#ui-014-interview-decisions).

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

Static inspection of the local DFU 1.1.1 snapshot shows that `Assets/Scripts/Game/UserInterfaceWindows/DaggerfallInventoryWindow.cs#UseItem` opens ordinary books through the reader. `DaggerfallBookReaderWindow.cs` provides page navigation and scroll-based page updates. `DaggerfallSpellBookWindow.cs` populates learned spells and separates inspection from readying. `DaggerfallUseMagicItemWindow.cs` builds a separate list of usable magic items and routes explicit selection to item use.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

1. Resolve the current readable edition through DATA-018. Show its title, body and available author information. Present Previous and Next and the current page relative to the current pagination. Reflow for language, viewport and UI size. Allow supported alternate presentations, including scrolling. Closing restores the originating view and valid focus under UI-001. If no usable edition exists, show the owner reason while preserving the item.
2. Remember one reading position per work for each character using a verified passage anchor and edition context. Track the passage as the player navigates. On reopening or reflow, restore the corresponding anchor under DATA-018. If correspondence fails after an edition change, open at the beginning with a notice. A fallback display alone does not replace the saved anchor. Record a new reading position when the player deliberately moves within the available edition. Item ownership, reading outcomes and rewards retain ITEM-016 rules.
3. Let custom editions reference packaged illustration assets at declared points in the body, with optional captions and text alternatives. Support inline and standalone page illustrations, preserving image proportions within the reader. Illustrations participate in pagination but are not navigation commands or gameplay triggers. Where an edition declares an image optional, a missing asset uses its text alternative or a neutral placeholder. Missing required presentation assets follow DATA-018 edition usability and whole-edition fallback. Structural contribution errors follow DATA-003. Do not borrow images from unrelated editions to fill missing references.
4. Show learned spells with owner-provided names, details and current cost. Preserve inspectable temporarily blocked entries with reasons under MAGIC-011, including missing spellbook, resource or state restrictions. Expose only authorized magic-item options and disclosed details. Separate current cast cost from any service price. Opening the screen, highlighting and sorting perform no readiness, item use or purchase.
5. Provide explicit Ready or Use actions. Capture the stable spell, variant or item identity and source context. Revalidate through MAGIC or ITEM before committing. If refused, keep the selector open while its scope remains valid, preserve the selection if eligible for inspection, and show the reason. If the selection was removed, use safe focus without silently picking a different action target. Use a clear empty state and a reachable Back action when the list has no entries.
6. On successful spell readiness, return through UI-001 to the gameplay input context when eligible. Consume the selection input and require normal cast input under MAGIC-003/004 and CORE-016. A successful magic-item use follows its owner's completion, pending or follow-up prompt route, including any item spell-readiness step. A follow-up rejection or stale callback cannot consume another item or reroute to another spell. Preserve independent suspension and owner confirmation.
7. Support UI-003 targeted and whole-window changes, approved sorting and display options, and UI-004 scaling and focus. Custom layouts preserve selection identity, explicit actions, owner disclosure, and reachable cancel and recovery. Books and selectors use applicable UI-001 pause and live-UI profiles.

## Parameters, required data and configuration

No new gameplay formula or cost is introduced. Required data includes work and edition identities, passage anchors, formatted body, illustration references and required/optional status, caption or equivalent text where supplied, page state, origin scope, stable spell/item identity, undisclosed and disclosed state boundaries, current cost, access and owner outcomes. DATA/MOD define illustration schemas and supported assets. MAGIC, ITEM and RULE own readiness, charges, costs and effects.

## Inputs, outputs and events

Inputs include reader open, Previous/Next, reflow, close, selector open, highlight, explicit Ready or Use, and owner outcomes. Outputs include rendered pages and images, position updates, selection details, scoped owner requests, rejection feedback and committed navigation. Read, ready, use and cast outcomes remain distinct. Request and scope identity follow UI-002.

## Cross-system interactions and consistency

Reviewed against DATA-018 edition and anchor rules, ITEM-015/016 item use and books, MAGIC-003/004 readiness/casting, MAGIC-011 learned access and blocked visibility, CORE-016 input handoff, and UI-001 through UI-005 for navigation, modding, focus and prompts. The reader presentation does not change book identity or value. The stock selectors use owner checks and preserve distinct learned and item-backed sources.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- A missing optional illustration leaves readable text and a clear fallback. Missing required content uses the owner edition policy.
- Reflow or localization can change page count without losing a verified passage. An unsupported anchor uses the beginning-and-notice fallback.
- A spell or item can become unavailable while selected. Revalidation prevents casting, spending or consuming a substitute.
- Rapid presses and delayed callbacks cannot use an item twice or turn a successful readiness press into a cast.

## Save/load considerations

Persist the remembered position through UI/SAVE by character, work, and verified edition/anchor context. Keep page index, images in memory, focus and selector state transient. ITEM/MAGIC own durable items and known spells. Restoration cannot replay reading, readiness or use outcomes. Supported restored activities use existing owner rules.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Resolve illustrations through supported packaged content references and render them as presentation. Keep layout independent of stable reading position. Route spell and item actions through owner models and UI-002. Exact image formats, layout schema, controls and persistence representation remain implementation choices.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A readable book opens with paged navigation, restores a verified passage, and closes to the originating context. Reflow preserves the passage under the approved rules.
2. A custom edition can include inline and standalone illustrations. Optional image failure uses a placeholder or text alternative. Required failure follows edition policy.
3. Highlighting exposes only permitted details and performs no action. Temporarily unusable learned spells remain inspectable with reasons.
4. A refused explicit action keeps the valid selection and explains failure. An empty or stale selection cannot act on a substitute.
5. Successful readiness returns to eligible gameplay without casting. Magic-item use follows its owner flow and cannot duplicate an effect.

## Outstanding questions and approval

Exact formats, layout and reader/selector mod schemas remain owner dependencies.
