# UI-006 — Vitals and effects display

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-16 with “I approve”. Updated 2026-11-16.

## Gameplay purpose and intended experience

Let players read their reserves and recognize impending loss of an active effect at a glance. Provide a steady stock display and let mods change individual indicators or the full presentation.

## Confirmed requirements

- **UI-006-D01.** Stock compact HUD keeps health, fatigue and magicka visible at full capacity and below. Show breath during active breath-holding and while a gradual-recovery ruleset leaves the reserve partly depleted. Keep exact values available in the status view. Make vital appearance, shape, display format and position moddable, including numbers-only displays.
- **UI-006-D02.** Stock compact effects prioritize soon-expiring effects that currently affect the player, use a steady warning marker, and expose other disclosed effects through overflow and details. Make icons, display behavior, layout and placement moddable.

See the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#ui-006-interview-decisions).

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

Static inspection of the local DFU 1.1.1 snapshot shows that `Assets/Scripts/Game/UserInterface/HUDVitals.cs` computes health, fatigue and magicka fill from current divided by maximum and supports gain/loss presentation. `HUDBreathBar.cs#UpdateBreathBar` reads the breath ratio and checks a low-breath threshold. `HUDActiveSpells.cs#UpdateIcons` selects visible effect bundles, refreshes on lifecycle and magic-round events, and flags expiry when the longest remaining component has fewer than two rounds. Expiring non-item icons can blink. `Game/UserInterfaceWindows/DaggerfallHUD.cs` composes these views.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

1. Stock compact mode uses steady health, fatigue and magicka bars whenever the applicable HUD is visible. For a positive maximum, display fraction is current divided by maximum, clamped to 0–1 for rendering. A valid zero maximum displays an empty bar, and the status view can report 0/0. Use the committed values and effective maximum from CHAR-018. Presentation never clamps or changes gameplay state.
2. Show stock breath while the owner reports an active breath-holding activity, including at zero reserve. Also show it during owner-reported gradual recovery below maximum. Hide it when normal breathing resumes under instant recovery, or when gradual recovery completes. Suppression of reserve consumption and water-breathing eligibility come from the owning systems.
3. Render only effects disclosed for the current player. Group related effects by spell or source under MAGIC-010. A group earns a steady expiry marker when at least one disclosed, currently effective contribution has an owner-supported impending-expiry warning. Respect timing profiles and component lifetimes. Where only part of a group is expiring, details identify which contribution. Indefinite or equipment-bound effects display the applicable lifetime description.
4. Give warned groups first claim to compact slots. Within each tier, keep currently visible groups in stable order where possible and use stable group identity to break ties when rebuilding. Keep other eligible groups accessible through a `+N` overflow indicator and a reachable effect details view. Count omitted, compact-eligible groups. Suppressed contributions may appear in details under MAGIC-010. Hidden effects do not affect visible counts. A missing cosmetic icon uses a generic fallback.
5. Expose targeted HUD edits and supported whole-view replacement under UI-003. Mods can change textures, shapes, labels, anchoring, position, indicator format, visibility rules, icon presentation, grouping layout, sort and overflow presentation. A replacement may use numeric vitals or represent effects with text. Visible effect data and warnings continue to respect owner disclosure, and a modded presentation preserves access to disclosed overflow and details under MAGIC-010. Follow UI-004 scaling and focus rules for interactive inspection. Passive HUD indicators retain UI-001 input and pause behavior.
6. Refresh on committed changes and current-session binding. CORE-016 owns effective HUD visibility and pause. A visible HUD can reflect committed changes during pause, while gameplay-time effect durations and breath follow their owners' clocks. Rebind from current state on reveal or load, with no old-player values or replayed effect application notices.

## Parameters, data and configuration

Required inputs include current/max health, fatigue, magicka and breath, breath activity and recovery state, and the disclosed effect groups and contributions. Effect display also needs allowed source labels, timing or lifetime descriptors, effective/suppressed status, and warning eligibility. UI data includes effective HUD visibility, available compact capacity, modded layout and display settings. Compact capacity affects presentation only. Use owner-supported expiry information. Exact stock capacity, warning thresholds by eligible timing profile, artwork and geometry remain open.

## Inputs, outputs and events

Inputs include committed vital, breath and effect changes, session bind/teardown, visibility and layout changes, and optional inspect interaction. Outputs include vital indicators, effect summaries, expiry markers, overflow and accessible details. If inspection opens UI, it follows UI-001/002. Display updates produce no gameplay mutations or additional effect events.

## Cross-system interactions and consistency

Reviewed against CHAR-018 for vital ownership and valid zero maxima, MOVE-014 for underwater context, MAGIC-010 for grouping, overflow, timing, warnings and restoration, CORE-013/016 for readiness and visibility, and UI-001/003/004 for input ownership, extensibility and scaling. Disease and other hidden conditions follow owner disclosure. HUD appearance and placement change presentation while read models continue to follow those contracts.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- A zero maximum produces no division error. A capacity change updates the display from the new committed current and maximum together.
- An empty air reserve remains visible during breath-holding. Recovery presentation follows the configured activity instead of estimating remaining breath locally.
- Overlapping contributions can warn independently without using one misleading countdown. Hidden contributors do not leak through an icon, warning or overflow count.
- A narrow or enlarged layout with many effects still gives access to details. A missing cosmetic asset uses fallback. Missing required gameplay effect data follows the existing load policy.
- Hiding the HUD, replacing a modded widget, or ending a session cannot mutate pools or effect state.

## Save/load considerations

Save durable pools and effects through their owners. Rebuild indicators, warning state and overflow from the restored authoritative model before gameplay readiness as required by MAGIC-010. UI instances and transient presentation state are runtime state. Settings and mod configuration follow CORE and DATA policies.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Supply presentation with current-session, read-only models scoped by ownership and disclosure. Publish stable HUD customization targets and a supported replacement contract. Ensure changing UI scale or using a custom indicator preserves matching input regions where interaction is offered. Exact node, refresh and styling implementation remains open.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Stock health, fatigue and magicka remain visible when the HUD is enabled and shown, including at full capacity. Exact values are available in status and zero maximum renders safely.
2. Breath shows during holding and eligible incomplete gradual recovery. No visual transition changes reserve state or advances its clock.
3. Soon-expiring effective disclosed groups get compact priority and a steady marker. Overlapping lifetimes remain inspectable. Overflow is reachable and excludes undisclosed groups.
4. A mod can reposition vitals and replace bars with different shapes or numbers. A mod can independently change effect icons, layout and placement, or supply a custom rendering with continued access to effect details.
5. Pause, HUD hiding, session replacement and load show coherent current state without gameplay mutations, stale values or replayed notifications.
