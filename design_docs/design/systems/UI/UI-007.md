# UI-007 — Navigation, interaction and temporary notices

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-16 with “I approve”. Updated 2026-11-16.

## Gameplay purpose and intended experience

Give players a reliable sense of heading, aim and current interaction mode. Make recent actions and restrictions easy to read during play, even when several notices arrive together.

## Confirmed requirements

- **UI-007-D01.** Show the compass and crosshair by default during ordinary gameplay, with independent visibility settings. Show the current interaction-mode icon when modes apply and brief feedback when the mode actually changes. Mods can change cue appearance, placement and display behavior.
- **UI-007-D02.** Queue distinct routine notices briefly and merge repeated notices about the same outcome. A newer mid-screen notice can replace a routine notice it makes obsolete. Decisions and recovery remain available through their owning prompt or screen. Make notice behavior and presentation moddable.

See the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#ui-007-interview-decisions).

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

Static inspection of the local DFU 1.1.1 snapshot shows that `Assets/Scripts/Game/UserInterfaceWindows/DaggerfallHUD.cs` composes compass, crosshair, interaction-mode and notification components and applies layout and visibility rules, including large-HUD variants. `Game/DaggerfallUI.cs#AddHUDText` sends strings or formatted tokens to `Game/UserInterface/PopupText.cs`. `DaggerfallHUD.cs#SetMidScreenText` provides a timed mid-screen channel.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

1. The stock compass follows the active gameplay view's heading. Show only directional detection information authorized by MAGIC-025 and other approved owners. Crosshair placement follows the effective gameplay viewport and aim reference supplied by the owner. It grants no target identity, range or eligibility information. Stock interaction-mode cues reflect the committed mode under MOVE-019 and do not add target highlights. Selecting the same mode again emits no mode-change notice.
2. Effective HUD visibility follows CORE-016 and valid session scope. Independent compass and crosshair preferences govern those cues when the HUD is shown. Keep cue presentation passive. Covered HUD elements cannot receive input through an active modal. Rebuild cues from current committed state on reveal, view change or session restore.
3. Use an ordered, bounded routine notice queue. Merge repeats that share an owner-declared semantic key within the same scope and short presentation window, including any relevant target or outcome. Localized text alone does not establish equivalence. Replace the oldest queued routine notice when the queue is full so feedback stays current. Presentation merging and expiry do not alter underlying command outcomes.
4. Use the mid-screen channel for brief announcements meant to draw attention. A newer routine announcement replaces the current one only when its declared replacement group and relevance say the old information has been superseded. Other routine announcements follow bounded queue rules. Required replies, recovery steps and durable information use their owning surfaces. Any optional reminder shares the same authoritative outcome and cannot close or resolve those surfaces.
5. Use real-time presentation timing for temporary notices. In the stock policy, HUD notices may expire while covered or hidden, and expired notices do not replay on reveal. Notices are scoped to the session or relevant view. Old callbacks and notices cannot leak into the next session. Showing a reminder or changing notice timing cannot advance a gameplay timer or extend an owner deadline.
6. Expose targeted and whole-view customization through UI-003. Support cue assets, layout, position and visibility behavior, and notice styling, channels, durations, queue limits, merging and replacement policies. Custom layouts follow UI-004 and respect approved disclosure, scope, required feedback and recovery routes. Presentation rules do not invent a new detection capability or replace an owner's decision surface.

## Parameters, required data and configuration

Inputs include current view heading and aim reference, committed interaction mode, authorized indicators, HUD and cue preferences, and notice descriptors with message data, scope, priority/channel, timing and semantic keys. Outputs include cues, displayed notices and presentation outcomes such as merged, superseded or expired. Owners emit committed outcomes, and UI renders localized feedback. Exact stock durations, queue sizes, positions and visual assets remain open. No gameplay formula or numerical balance parameter applies.

## Cross-system interactions and consistency

Reviewed against CORE-016 HUD visibility and real-time presentation, CORE-018 shortcut feedback and recovery, UI-001/002 context ownership and command outcomes, UI-003/004 customization and scaling, UI-005 required prompts, MOVE-019 mode feedback, MAGIC-025 compass disclosure and WORLD-015 discovery. WORLD and MAGIC own information authorization. A heading cue or detection marker does not grant map discovery. DATA supplies localized text and AV supplies presentation assets and viewport integration.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- Similar-looking feedback for different targets stays distinct when their owner keys differ. A repeat burst cannot fill the queue with copies or erase a required owner decision.
- A late notice from a retired session or view cannot appear in the next scope. Fades and hidden layouts cannot claim expired information is newly committed.
- Compass and crosshair align to current committed view geometry, including after resizing. Missing optional cue artwork may use a fallback with the same disclosure policy.
- Hiding the HUD or choosing a custom notice policy cannot remove the existing validation, required prompt or recovery path.

## Save/load considerations

Temporary notices and their queues are runtime presentation state. Reconstruct persistent cue state from approved preferences and restored owners. Supported durable information and restorable prompts follow their owner contracts. Loading does not replay prior routine notices.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Keep notice presentation independent of authoritative command delivery. Use consistent viewport coordinates for world-facing cues and publish stable mod targets and recognized notice policy fields. Exact layout nodes, timing mechanism and configuration schema remain implementation choices.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Stock compass and crosshair default on and can be hidden independently. The mode icon reflects the current eligible mode and only an actual mode change emits feedback.
2. Stock cues respect valid viewport geometry, HUD visibility and approved detection and interaction disclosure.
3. Routine notices queue in order, eligible repeats merge, and saturation follows the declared routine overflow policy. Mid-screen replacement requires explicit supersession.
4. Required decisions and recovery remain available when temporary notices merge, expire or are hidden.
5. Mods can change cue appearance and placement and customize notice display, timing and delivery policy within the documented owner constraints.
6. Pause, fades and session replacement preserve correct scope and timing. Restored cues reflect current owners without replaying stale notices.
