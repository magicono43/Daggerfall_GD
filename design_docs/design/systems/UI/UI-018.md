# UI-018 — Magic crafting screens

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-16 with “I approve”. Updated 2026-11-16.

## Gameplay purpose and intended experience

Compose a spell, brew a potion or enchant an item. See the known restrictions and costs while editing, then deliberately approve the exact craft. An experiment can remain uncertain even when the UI explains its risks.

## Confirmed requirements

- **UI-018-D01.** Keep distinct stock editors with live updates for disclosed costs and restrictions. Show casting cost separately from the spellmaking fee. Show enchanting capacity, credits, required traits and price. Show ingredients, quantities and only authorized outcomes in Potion Maker. Make layouts moddable.
- **UI-018-D02.** Require an explicit review of charges, consumed inputs, expected output and disclosed risk. Warn that an unknown experiment can waste ingredients without giving away its outcome. Back retains the draft. Rejection explains the reason and retains the draft, with feedback beside affected selections where possible.

See [gameplay decisions](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#ui-018-interview-decisions).

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

The local DFU 1.1.1 snapshot has separate `DaggerfallSpellMakerWindow.cs`, `DaggerfallPotionMakerWindow.cs` and `DaggerfallItemMakerWindow.cs` under `Assets/Scripts/Game/UserInterfaceWindows/`. `DaggerfallEffectSettingsEditorWindow.cs` provides effect editing. `MixCauldron` handles potion mixing and `EnchantButton_OnMouseClick` checks the item, entries, funds and capacity before applying the enchantment. Findings are from static inspection.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

1. Spellmaker shows the selected effects and setting values, applicable restrictions, current casting cost and a separately labeled service fee. Item Maker shows the exact target, chosen powers and drawbacks, required dependent entries, occupied capacity and credits, and separate gold price. Dependent entries show why they are required and follow MAGIC-015 removal rules. Potion Maker shows selected ingredient quantities and approved recipe information. Ordinary experimentation does not reveal an undisclosed match through output labels, errors, preview appearance or enabled state.
2. Refresh owner-supplied previews after relevant draft and context changes. Rendering, browsing and previewing cannot spend gold, reserve inputs, advance RNG, use items or grant anything. A child editor returns to the same scoped draft. Invalid selections remain available to correct with an owner reason. The associated crafting specification governs exit, discard and persistence.
3. Show one explicit review before commitment. Display applicable charges, consumed items and quantities, or the target modified by the action. Describe the expected output where disclosed. For an unknown experiment, describe only the permitted uncertainty, including possible ingredient loss and no potion. Back or decline returns to the draft under UI-005.
4. Bind acceptance to the exact draft revision, sources, provider context, quote and relevant material terms. Owners revalidate before committing. If terms change, show a short notice and updated review for fresh acceptance, following the UI-017 pattern. For an invalid selection, return to the active editor and mark the affected selection where possible. If the underlying service scope is no longer valid, use its exit or recovery policy and preserve drafts as their owners require.
5. Show pending during admitted work and prevent duplicate confirmation. Publish success and refresh resources only from the committed owner result. A resolved unsuccessful experiment is an owner outcome that can legitimately consume ingredients. A technical failure follows the owner's unchanged or explicit recovery result. Display these outcomes with distinct feedback. UI-015 governs leaving a completed service.
6. Support targeted edits and whole-view replacement under UI-003. Custom layouts retain required terms, disclosure, exact request identity and reachable Back and Confirm controls. The same presentation contracts support validated mod crafting policies under MAGIC/DATA.

## Parameters, required data and configuration

No new cost, capacity or recipe formula applies. Required data includes draft and revision IDs, service identity, effects and settings, source and target IDs, quantities, capacity and dependency breakdown, owner quotes and restrictions, disclosed risk/outcome, and operation/recovery state. MAGIC, RULE and ECON supply calculations and disclosure policy. UI/MOD define layout and allowed presentation extensions.

## Inputs, outputs and events

Inputs include opening a service, selection/edit/removal, owner preview changes, review, Confirm, Back, discard and owner results. Outputs include refreshed builder state, review and scoped acceptance, actionable rejection, committed feedback or recovery. UI-002 governs request identity and stale events.

## Cross-system interactions and consistency

Reviewed against MAGIC-012/013/015 for spellmaking, potionmaking and enchanting, applicable ITEM and ECON transfer and payment contracts, UI-001 through UI-005 for navigation, routing, customization and confirmation, and UI-015/017 for service completion and revised terms. MAGIC owns effect validation and crafting results. UI never uses a preview to decide stock recipe success.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- A target, ingredient stack, supplier or quote can change between preview and acceptance. Reject stale targets and require fresh acceptance for revised material terms.
- A missing provider or invalid combination shows an owner error. Required dependencies cannot disappear while a parent still needs them.
- An unknown recipe cannot be inferred from preview availability. A legitimate unsuccessful experiment and a technical failure produce distinct owner outcomes.
- Late, duplicated or retired callbacks cannot consume inputs, grant a second result or overwrite a newer draft.

## Save/load considerations

Follow each crafting owner's draft persistence policy. MAGIC-015 retains enchanting drafts across service exit and native saves. Preserve other drafts only under their approved owner contracts. Revalidate restored draft data and current access without committing it. Focus, live quotes and pending UI confirmations are transient. Crafted outputs and durable recovery belong to their owners.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Use scoped read-only previews and explicit owner operations. Keep secret recipe outcomes outside the visible preview model. Ensure required details are readable across scale settings and available to keyboard/controller users. Exact widgets and schema remain implementation choices.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Stock screens remain distinct and show live permitted previews, including separate spell costs and enchanting capacity/credits/price.
2. Unknown experiments disclose the risk of loss while preserving the unknown output. Preview does not commit or leak it.
3. Confirmation presents the exact current intent. Back preserves the draft, and rejection shows the applicable reason.
4. New material terms require fresh acceptance. Repeated or stale callbacks cannot duplicate effects or redirect the craft.
5. Supported modded layouts preserve review, disclosure and accessible actions. Restored drafts cannot replay crafting.

## Outstanding questions and approval

Exact layouts, supported mod schemas and owner-specific draft controls remain dependencies.
