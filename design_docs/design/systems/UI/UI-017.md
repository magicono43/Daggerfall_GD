# UI-017 — Merchant, banking and property screens

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-16 with “I approve”. Updated 2026-11-16.

## Gameplay purpose and intended experience

Review what a transaction costs and delivers, then deliberately accept it. If the terms change, notice the change and review the updated quote. Return from a declined confirmation with eligible work intact.

## Confirmed requirements

- **UI-017-D01.** Keep distinct stock merchant, bank and property screens. Use a consistent review layout wherever the owner requires confirmation. Show what is given and received, the total, the payment or payout form, and applicable terms such as loan due dates. Allow modded layouts with required terms and actions accessible.
- **UI-017-D02.** When terms change, show a simple notice followed by the new quote and require fresh acceptance.

See the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#ui-017-interview-decisions).

## Detailed rules and behaviors

1. Use distinct shop, bank and property views. Show only owner-disclosed actions, with reasons for visible restrictions when available. Shop intent preserves the ECON-003 basket and its source, direction and quantities. Bank and property screens collect the input required for their applicable ECON owner operations.
2. Use a shared review structure for transactions requiring owner confirmation. Show the action, what leaves and what is received, applicable quantities and totals, payment or payout instrument and destination, and any required fees, resulting terms or dates supplied by the owner. Show zero proceeds explicitly where offered. Supply owner-provided breakdowns for inspection where required. Apply UI-005 safe focus and cancellation policies. Review cannot commit an action by itself.
3. Confirm only the captured owner quote and exact operation identity, including selection revision, provider, affected account or property and applicable terms. The owner revalidates authorization, availability and current terms. Disable repeat submission while settlement is pending. A stale callback cannot use the newly highlighted offer, account or property.
4. If the owner returns changed material terms, show a concise change notice on the refreshed confirmation and present the new quote. The updated quote requires a fresh deliberate acceptance. Revalidation and reconfirmation continue to apply if terms change again. A prior acceptance cannot settle revised terms.
5. If a transaction is unavailable, show the relevant owner reason. Return to the editor or service screen when its context remains valid and mark the affected line or action. In trade, retain invalid lines and intended quantities until the player edits or removes them. No or Back from trade confirmation retains the basket. Clear or leaving trade discards it under ECON-003. Keep bank or property draft input where owner rules allow. If the route is stale, use the applicable exit or recovery path. A rejection cannot implicitly settle a subset or initiate another action.
6. Show success from committed owner outcomes and refresh from authoritative balances, custody, stock and ownership. Stay in an ongoing service unless the owner ends it or the player leaves. UI-015 controls return after service completion. Route required recovery through the owner. Custom views and targeted edits under UI-003 preserve readable review terms, identity, accessible actions and owner outcomes.

## Parameters, required data and configuration

No new price, interest, transfer or balance formula applies. Required data includes operation/request and quote identities, provider and session scope, transaction mode, target and revision, disclosed line items and amounts, instruments and destinations, applicable terms and deadlines, eligibility, and settlement outcome. ECON/RULE supply pricing, fees, terms and validation. UI/MOD supply presentation and supported extension points.

## Inputs, outputs and events

Inputs include service entry, selection and edits, review, Confirm, Back, Clear, quote refresh, and owner settlement results. Outputs include a visible quote, scoped acceptance, changed-terms notice, line/action rejection, committed success or recovery. UI-002 governs request outcomes. Only owners publish committed transaction events and effects.

## Cross-system interactions and consistency

Reviewed against ECON-001/003–005 for routes, baskets and settlement, ECON-009/013 and applicable bank and property owners, RULE-021 pricing, ITEM custody and capacity, and UI-001 through UI-005 for navigation, routing, customization, focus and confirmation. UI-015 governs service exit. SAVE/CORE own durable operations and recovery. The shared review pattern follows each owner's confirmation policy.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- A changed payout form, fee or other material term requires a fresh quote even when the total is unchanged.
- An unavailable line remains in the trade basket with its requested quantity and known reason. Owner rejection cannot charge, transfer or silently reduce the basket.
- An interrupted provider or retired scope invalidates the old confirmation. Late responses cannot settle twice or show success for a newer interaction.
- A modded layout must keep all required terms readable and Confirm and Back reachable where allowed.

## Save/load considerations

Baskets, quote confirmations and view drafts are session-only. SAVE and transaction owners persist committed balances, goods, deeds and supported recovery state. Reconstruct a supported service only under its owner contract. Load invalidates old confirmations and cannot repeat settlement.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Keep quote presentation and state mutation separate. Bind responses to the exact owner intent and use current validation at settlement. Provide responsive, accessible controls and documented mod targets. Exact layout, widgets and operation representation remain implementation choices.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Merchant, banking and property screens remain distinct and show a consistent review wherever confirmation is required.
2. Review shows the required items or effects, totals, payment or payout form and applicable terms. Modded views preserve those fields and required controls.
3. A changed material term replaces the quote, displays a brief notice and requires fresh acceptance. Earlier confirmation cannot settle new terms.
4. Unavailable trade lines remain in the editable basket with reasons. Cancellation, Clear and service exit follow owner rules.
5. Repetition, stale callbacks and load cannot redirect or duplicate settlement. Success follows committed results, and service completion follows UI-015.
