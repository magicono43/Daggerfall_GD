# UI-019 — Exterior, dungeon and travel maps

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-16 with “I approve”. Updated 2026-11-16.

## Gameplay purpose and intended experience

Orient locally, inspect explored spaces and find known travel destinations. Use familiar controls across maps and retain the current view when backing out of a child prompt.

## Confirmed requirements

- **UI-019-D01.** Share Pan, Zoom, Recenter, Inspect and Back where applicable, with mouse, keyboard and controller access. Keep 3D rotation and height controls on the dungeon map and search and destination filters on the travel map. Make controls and layouts moddable.
- **UI-019-D02.** Fresh local maps center on the player. An authorized journal link centers on its place. Returning from a child view restores valid pan, zoom and selection. A fully closed map reopens at its default view.

See [gameplay decisions](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#ui-019-interview-decisions).

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

In the local DFU 1.1.1 snapshot, `Assets/Scripts/Game/UserInterfaceWindows/DaggerfallAutomapWindow.cs` supports zoom, rotation, top and 3D view controls, and reset. `DaggerfallExteriorAutomapWindow.cs` presents the settlement layout. `DaggerfallTravelMapWindow.cs#HandleLocationPickEvent` handles destination selection and `DaggerfallTravelPopUp.cs` provides the trip setup and confirmation surface. Findings are from static inspection.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

1. Open the applicable local map for the committed gameplay context. Exterior maps show authorized settlement geometry and marks under WORLD-015/016. Interior and dungeon maps use the resolved layout and disclosed geometry under LOC-005/018. Show current player location and facing where supported. Map preparation cannot discover areas or instantiate gameplay objects.
2. Share action names and interaction patterns for Pan, Zoom, Recenter, Inspect and Back. Show relevant navigation controls and support keyboard/controller focus as well as pointer controls. A map can opt into controller pointing under UI-004 and provide a route back to ordinary focus navigation. Keep dungeon rotation and height view controls distinct. Preserve LOC-019 note and learned connection behaviors. Offer travel search and type filters only in their applicable map context.
3. A fresh local map centers on the current player with its default zoom and orientation. A fresh ordinary travel map opens in the current region when applicable, in its default regional view. An authorized journal shortcut opens the relevant map and focuses the referenced place at the authorized precision. Recenter restores the applicable default viewpoint, using the current player position for local maps. Explicit Inspect shows authorized information only.
4. Keep the same map view when opening and returning from a child, including current pan, zoom, rotation or height where applicable, and eligible selection and focus. Revalidate selection and map context on return. If a target is no longer valid, clear it and keep the view where safe. If the map's context changed, show a reason when applicable and use the valid current default or the owning exit route. A full close resets transient camera and selection state. Saved filters, map notes and discovery follow their existing owner policies.
5. Travel maps follow TIME-012's region-scoped stock search, filters, unambiguous selection and disclosure. Authorized journal shortcuts use stable place identity and may temporarily show an exact selection outside a manual filter when allowed. Partial disclosure cannot produce an exact pin or trip preview. Inspecting, selecting and previewing never advances time, pays for travel or moves the player. A distinct confirmation goes through TIME revalidation.
6. Apply UI-003 to supported map views, including control bindings and optional detail layers. Modded displays respect disclosure checks in markers, notes, search, selection and result counts. Adapt rendering and hit testing coherently under UI-004. Stock map pause and supported live profiles follow UI-001 and CORE-016.

## Parameters, required data and configuration

No new gameplay formula applies. Required data includes map type and context, authorized geometry and place IDs, player pose, disclosure, selected region/site and filters, source link and precision, and transient camera, selection and focus. WORLD/LOC/TIME own geometry and disclosure policy. Exact stock zoom ranges, rotation/height ranges, default orientations and binding choices remain owner dependencies.

## Inputs, outputs and events

Inputs include map open, authorized journal links, Pan, Zoom, Recenter, Inspect, Back, dungeon controls, search/filter and explicit destination selection. Outputs include scoped map presentation, visible details, position reset or restoration, unavailable feedback and owner-authorized trip preview requests. UI-002 governs request identity and stale results.

## Cross-system interactions and consistency

Reviewed against WORLD-015/016 for settlement maps, LOC-005/018/019 for geometry, discovery and notes, TIME-012 for search and selection, UI-016 for authorized journal links, and UI-001 through UI-005 for navigation, modding, focus and confirmation. Discovery, notes and travel admission remain with their owners. UI zoom, rotation and filters affect presentation.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- Several disclosed locations share a pixel or name. Use the owner disambiguation flow and stable identity.
- A journal link may lose its authorization or refer to a missing place. Keep feedback truthful and expose only permitted precision.
- A scene, variant or layout can change while the map is covered or live. Do not show or act on geometry from a retired scope.
- Recenter, resize and changes in input device cannot accidentally inspect, commit travel or alter map knowledge.

## Save/load considerations

Map camera transforms, focus and selection are runtime state. WORLD/LOC/SAVE persist authorized discovery and notes. Approved preferences and filters use owner storage. Reconstruct maps from current authoritative data and reject old-scope callbacks and selections. Load cannot repeat trip confirmation or disclosure.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Use consistent world-to-map and pointer transforms for the effective layout. Keep available controls distinct from disclosed data and validate modded view routes. Exact renderer, controls and geometry representation remain implementation choices.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Each map provides applicable shared controls across supported devices and its specialized tools.
2. Fresh local maps center on the player. An authorized journal link focuses the permitted location without increasing disclosure. Fresh ordinary travel uses its applicable regional default.
3. Returning from a child restores valid camera and selection. Closing fully resets transient view state and leaves durable map data intact.
4. Search, inspection and markers obey the same disclosure rules, including under mods. Destination preview is distinct from travel confirmation.
5. Old context and stale destinations cannot expose geometry or redirect a request. Resize and input handoffs preserve correct targeting.
