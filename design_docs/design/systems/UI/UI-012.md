# UI-012 — Character sheet and progression prompts

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-16 with “I approve”. Updated 2026-11-16.

## Gameplay purpose and intended experience

Inspect the character's current attributes and skills, understand disclosed modifiers, and review character history. Opening the sheet at a safe moment begins allocation when a reward is pending. The player can see what they are allocating and when those changes become permanent.

## Confirmed requirements

- **UI-012-D01.** Stock character-sheet opening automatically begins pending reward allocation when safe. Make the allocation trigger moddable.
- **UI-012-D02.** Use a clean DFU-style overview with current effective attributes and skills. Provide separate details for permanent values and disclosed modifiers where they differ. Make History and Rename accessible from the sheet, and support modded presentation and layout.

See the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#ui-012-interview-decisions).

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

In the local DFU 1.1.1 snapshot, `Assets/Scripts/Game/UserInterfaceWindows/DaggerfallCharacterSheetWindow.cs#UpdatePlayerValues` refreshes character values and starts allocation when `ReadyToLevelUp` is set. For ordinary progression it increases level and maximum health before allocation finishes. `#CheckIfDoneLeveling` blocks closing while spendable points remain and commits working attributes when complete. The sheet offers grouped skill dialogs and opens `DaggerfallPlayerHistoryWindow.cs` separately. Findings are from static inspection.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

1. On opening, show committed effective attributes and skills, the character's current actual level and relevant owner-supplied identity and progression information. Provide details showing permanent values and disclosed modifiers where applicable. Keep raw skill-practice counters out of the ordinary sheet under CHAR-012. Provide History with existing biography/history content and Rename using CHAR-007 validation and UI-005 text entry. Renaming does not change character identity, earned progress or established history.
2. After a deliberate stock sheet open, request automatic allocation of the next pending or deferred reward under CHAR-015 if the current context is eligible. Evaluate safe entry using owner state before treating the sheet's pause as evidence of safety. Revalidate on admission. A passive refresh, panel return, restore or stale callback cannot independently trigger an allocation. An approved restore of an already-active allocation reconstructs that same activity.
3. If safe entry is refused, keep the ordinary sheet available with a concise reason and a pending reward indicator. Offer a deliberate Claim Reward retry from the sheet, also available as an alternative trigger for mods. Re-evaluate through the owner on retry. No automatic start merely because safety changes while the sheet is open.
4. Use a distinct allocation surface or mode. Show reward type, starting permanent attributes, working values, remaining points, proposed level and maximum-health gain where applicable, and eligible controls. Keep preview values separate from committed character stats. When every attribute is capped, show the exact unusable remainder and require explicit confirmation before discarding it. CHAR-015 controls preparation, caps, spend/refund rules, ordering and durable results.
5. Stock allocation holds its approved suspension and blocks return to play until a valid confirmation. Only the owner atomically commits the reward. Show success after the owner confirms it, update the sheet from committed state and leave current health unchanged by the ordinary maximum-health grant. Return to the sheet after each completed reward, show any remaining backlog and allow a deliberate next claim. A later claim cannot overtake an active or deferred reward.
6. Provide supported mod overrides for the opening trigger and view layout, including a manual-claim profile and UI-003 targeted or whole-view replacement. CHAR-015 governs mod-only deferral, disabled by default. A deferred draft retains its prepared roll and partial allocation, grants no benefits and revalidates on resume. Mods and the no-UI-pause profile cannot bypass the approved safety and suspension of mandatory allocation. Commit, deferral and close use UI-001/002 outcomes and scoped handoffs.

## Parameters, required data and configuration

No additional progression formula applies. CHAR-015 owns pool size, maximum-health computation and resolved attribute caps. UI consumes committed identity, attributes and skills, disclosed modifiers/history, pending reward count, eligibility and reason, reward kind and identity, prepared pool and health result, working allocation, and applicable trigger/deferral profiles. Exact safe-entry threat criteria remain a CHAR/COMBAT integration dependency. Controls, layout and presentation are moddable under UI-003.

## Inputs, outputs and events

Inputs include sheet open, details, History, Rename, claim/retry, spend/refund, Confirm, supported defer and owner outcomes. Outputs include character details, pending or blocked feedback, allocation preview, scoped owner requests and committed reward feedback. Use request and reward identity for deferred work. Display or navigation alone never grants progression benefits.

## Cross-system interactions and consistency

Reviewed against CHAR-007 rename/history, CHAR-011/012 for attributes and skill disclosure, CHAR-013/014 for eligibility and backlog, CHAR-015 for claims, CHAR-018 for health and CORE-016/018 for suspension and shortcut eligibility. UI-001/002 govern navigation and routing, UI-003/004 govern customization and access, and UI-005 governs confirmation and text input. The stock automatic trigger follows a deliberate sheet open and the existing safe-entry requirement. The owner remains responsible for reward commitment.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- Opening during an unsafe state leaves the reward pending. An ordinary pause or repeated opening cannot bypass admission.
- Temporary modifiers cannot create permanent spendable points or change the prepared health gain. A failed commit retains the prepared reward without another roll.
- At all caps, the exact remainder is visible and discard requires confirmation. Escape or Return to Game cannot bypass mandatory allocation.
- Stale responses after load or session replacement cannot allocate to a different character. A rename rejection or cancellation preserves the current name.

## Save/load considerations

CHAR/SAVE own durable pending rewards, prepared outcomes and supported allocations. UI restores a supported active allocation from authoritative current-session state before releasing applicable load suspension. Runtime focus, view objects and request handles are reconstructed. Restore cannot reroll, start a different reward or grant benefits before confirmation.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Read committed character state through owner models and route mutations through scoped owner requests. Preserve accessible history, rename and claim navigation with keyboard/controller and responsive sizing. Exact view layout, request API and mod schemas remain implementation choices.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The sheet shows effective attributes and skills, offers permanent/disclosed modifier details, and provides History and Rename.
2. A deliberate safe stock opening with a pending reward begins exactly one allocation. Unsafe entry leaves the reward pending and supports later owner-validated claim. Refresh and stale callbacks cannot begin allocation.
3. The allocation shows truthful working and capped values. No benefit applies before owner confirmation, and an ordinary max-health gain leaves current health unchanged.
4. Stock allocation blocks return to gameplay. Modded deferral preserves the same reward and gives no early benefit. A manual-claim profile leaves ordinary sheet open independent of claim.
5. Confirmation, restore and retry cannot duplicate or reroll a reward. A completed allocation returns to the sheet with the remaining backlog visible where applicable.
