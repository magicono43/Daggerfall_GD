# NPC-018 — Tone, reaction and greetings

Approval status **Approved — finalized v1**

The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-11 with “I approve”. See NPC-018-A01 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-018-interview-decisions).

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

An NPC's reputation context determines their welcome, while the player's tone and social skills influence their answers. The same speaker remembers a recent refusal. The player can improve their standing, wait for a temporary refusal to clear, or use an authored quest exception when available.

## Confirmed requirements

NPC-018-D01–D03 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-018-interview-decisions) confirm DFU-style reception and answer reactions, one game day of temporary refusal with relevant change-based recovery, explicit quest exceptions, and moddable practice for each eligible option used in a skill-linked tone.

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

In the local 1.1.1 snapshot, `Assets/Scripts/Game/TalkManager.cs#GetReactionToPlayer` combines faction reputation, biography modifier, resolved social-group reaction modifier and applicable group reputation. `TalkToNpc` handles suppression, base refusal, prior refusal, quest greeting and ordinary greeting selection. `GetNPCGreetingRecord` can record a faction greeting refusal. `GetReactionToPlayer_0_1_2` uses Personality, question type, tone and social group. It tallies a tone skill while the cached reaction for that tone equals zero and uses the cached total when it is nonzero. The source can draw again even when it reuses that total. Per-option practice below is the chosen recreation policy.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

- Bind reception to the resolved actor and context under NPC-017. Apply dialogue capability, availability and suppression checks. An explicit quest exception can override ordinary refusal within its declared scope. After admission, an applicable authored quest greeting takes priority over ordinary faction or generic greetings.
- Use DFU-style faction greeting categories and generic reaction bands. If the base reaction is below the refusal threshold, refuse while that predicate remains true. A temporary faction refusal displays its applicable rejection and records the actor, applicable context and time. Repeat attempts during the refusal use a short response and retain the settled decision.
- Clear temporary refusal on a relevant reputation or quest change or after one game day. Count only changes to the inputs that govern that actor's admission. Reevaluate current standing when reconsidering. Another conversation, UI redraw or reload alone does not clear the record.
- Normal tone uses Personality and the question modifier. Polite tone adds the social group's polite modifier and an Etiquette skill outcome. Blunt tone adds the group's blunt modifier and a Streetwise skill outcome. Resolve one retained skill roll per linked tone per conversation on its first eligible use. Apply each question's own modifier and current applicable context to its answer reaction.
- Award one use of the linked skill per committed eligible dialogue option in that tone, whether the skill roll passed or failed and whether the NPC's answer was favorable. Another genuine eligible question can award another use, even when the roll is reused. Selecting a tone, previewing, redraw and repeated delivery do not award practice. Normal tone has no linked practice award. CHAR-012 owns acceptance.
- Apply practice to eligible question submissions, including a repeated genuine submission if permitted by topic policy. Disabled choices and consequence-free UI navigation remain outside eligibility. Rate limits and amounts can be modded.

## Formulas and parameters

- Stock opening reaction is faction reputation plus applicable biography and social reaction modifiers and social-group reputation. The source hard refusal threshold is strictly below −20, with generic greeting bands starting at 0, 10 and 30. Use these DFU values as defaults. For the applicable faction greeting refusal branch, compare the greeting-specific resolved reputation with a uniform integer from −10 through 5. Lower reputation refuses.
- Stock answer score is `trunc(live Personality / 5) + question modifier + tone modifier`. Polite and blunt modifiers include the social-group entry and +5 on linked skill success or −10 on failure. Use the approved percentile contract. Normal tone has zero tone modifier.
- Use three response bands under the DFU comparison: below threshold, from threshold to below threshold + 20, and at or above threshold + 20. The reference threshold is a uniform integer from 0 through 20 derived consistently for the NPC. Use a stable actor-scoped threshold independent of scene object and shared random state. Stock group and question modifiers come from DFU tables.
- Temporary refusal lasts one game day. Each eligible polite or blunt option awards one linked skill use by default.

## Required data, inputs, outputs and events

Inputs include actor and conversation IDs, faction and social-group context, biography and reputation modifiers, quest and refusal state, game time, question and tone, live Personality and skill, and validated greeting and response tables. Outputs include admission or refusal, localized greeting, response band, retained tone result and deduplicated practice report.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- A temporary refusal can expire while the base reaction still blocks dialogue. A change affecting an unrelated faction does not automatically clear it.
- A quest exception overrides only its declared ordinary refusal rule. An unavailable actor still follows NPC-017 and MOVE-023.
- A legitimate repeat question may award practice under the stock cadence, so frequent permitted questions can train the skill. The topic owner governs whether the question can be asked again. Retry of the same option commitment cannot award twice.
- Missing required context uses the owning system's recovery. Failed presentation cannot reopen dialogue or replay practice.

## Configuration and balancing

Support moddable reaction thresholds, social and question modifiers, tone roll and practice cadence, refusal duration and relevant reset inputs, quest exceptions and feedback. Validate tone and group mappings and use explicit owner policies for added groups or tones.

## Save/load considerations

Persist applicable actor refusal records and committed practice. Reconcile refusal against restored game time and relevant owner facts. Ordinary conversation closes on load under NPC-017. Transient tone rolls are scoped to that conversation. Restore cannot clear durable refusal by itself, replay a greeting decision or duplicate a practice award.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Separate admission, greeting choice, tone checks, answer generation and practice. Use stable actor and option commitment identity and purpose-owned randomness. Exact table mappings, localized records and persistence interfaces remain integration work.

## Acceptance criteria

1. DFU-style reputation inputs and bands govern ordinary reception. Applicable authored quest greetings take priority after admission, and explicit quest exceptions can admit an otherwise refused conversation.
2. Temporary refusal survives other conversations and reload, clears after the approved duration or a relevant change, and still respects current low reputation.
3. Polite and blunt use their linked skills and group modifiers. Normal uses neither. Each question applies its own modifier even when it reuses a tone skill result.
4. Each committed eligible polite or blunt option awards one use regardless of outcome. Repeated callback delivery and tone selection alone grant none. Mods can change practice cadence.
5. Stable actor identity preserves response threshold and refusal state through scene reconstruction.
