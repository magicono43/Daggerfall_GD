# NPC-019 — Topic lists and NPC knowledge

Approval status **Approved — finalized v1**

The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-11 with “I approve”. See NPC-019-A01 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-019-interview-decisions).

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

Let players ask about their surroundings, people and relevant quest subjects. Speakers give consistent answers about what they know, while local context and special roles make some NPCs better sources. A speaker can know a subject and still be unwilling to provide another useful answer in that conversation.

## Confirmed requirements

NPC-019-D01–D03 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-019-interview-decisions) confirm moddable DFU-style categories, stable actor-and-topic knowledge with relevant updates, and a shared moddable allowance of one useful Tell Me About or rumor answer per conversation.

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

In the local 1.1.1 snapshot, `Assets/Scripts/Game/TalkManager.cs#AssembleTopicLists` assembles general, location, person and thing lists. General topics include Any News, Where Am I and registered quest subjects. `CheckNPCcanKnowAboutTellMeAboutTopic` filters out-of-region quest persons and places. `GetNPCKnowledgeAboutItem` checks same-building context, spymaster status and related organizations before the table-based roll. `ResetNPCKnowledge` clears per-list caches. The source defaults to one Tell Me About or rumor answer and has scoped exceptions. The Where Is Thing path is marked unused in the feature catalog.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

- Build moddable categories from general prompts, relevant local places and people, and subjects exposed by their owning quest. Display only authorized topic labels and context. Use stable topic and subject IDs so two identically named subjects remain distinct. Add available contextual labels when needed to disambiguate them.
- List eligibility, speaker knowledge, and willingness to answer are separate. An eligible question may return unknown. Apply quest disclosure and region constraints before answering, including for specialists. Apply configured knowledge rules for same-building subjects, related organizations and spymasters within their allowed scope. Resolve other cases using the DFU-style table.
- Keep knowledge stable per actor and topic through conversation changes, list rebuilds, streaming and reload. Retain the underlying table draw for the same actor and topic. Reevaluate applicable rules when relevant world, role or quest facts change. Preserve unaffected knowledge. A retired actor's knowledge cannot pass to a replacement.
- After knowledge, apply NPC-018 tone and answer handling. The default shared allowance is one useful Tell Me About or rumor answer per conversation. Unknown, unhelpful or refused answers leave the allowance available. Apply spymaster and qualifying same-building exceptions in their respective contexts. Exempt answers leave the ordinary allowance available. Directions and other categories follow their own owner policies.
- Consume the allowance once when a qualifying useful answer commits. A duplicate submission, UI redraw or retry cannot charge it again. A genuine new conversation resets the allowance under NPC-017. A permitted repeat question can still follow NPC-018 practice rules.

## Formulas and parameters

For stock table knowledge, compare a uniform integer from 1 through 20 with `10 + knowledge_modifier[question type, social group]`. The speaker knows the subject when the roll is at or below the threshold. Apply explicit eligibility and guaranteed-knowledge rules first. Default shared allowance is one useful answer. Exact table mappings and answer classifications require content verification.

## Required data, inputs, outputs and events

Inputs include actor, conversation and topic IDs, subject provenance and authorized label context, region and building, social group and faction relationship, specialist role, quest exposure and disclosure, and retained knowledge and allowance state. Outputs include eligible topics, known or unknown result, applicable answer route, and committed allowance use. Inspection and list rebuilds perform no committed answer or practice.

## Cross-system interactions

NPC-017 owns dialogue lifetime and actor context, and NPC-018 owns tone and practice. NPC-020 owns directions and map disclosure. NPC-021 owns rumor selection. FACT supplies group and organization context, LOC/WORLD supply scoped subject locations, and QUEST owns authorized exposure and expiry. DATA/UI supply localized lists and answers. RULE-003, CORE/SAVE and DATA/MOD govern retained results, restoration and validated extensions.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- Expired, removed or moved quest subjects are reevaluated against their current identity and disclosure rules. A stale option cannot expose an unauthorized subject or redirect to a similarly named one.
- Duplicate labels can use public place, role or other authorized context. A disambiguation label cannot reveal secret quest information.
- A relevant move can grant or remove same-building knowledge. A UI refresh or unrelated change leaves the settled table draw intact.
- If a required answer fails before commitment, the allowance remains available. An accepted answer cannot consume it twice when delivery is retried.

## Configuration and balancing

Support validated categories, list visibility, geographic and role filters, table modifiers, stable knowledge policy, answer allowance and exception scopes. Exact content and which responses count as useful are coordinated with the relevant answer owner.

## Save/load considerations

Preserve applicable actor/topic knowledge or enough stable state to reproduce it, plus durable quest exposure and learned facts. Reconcile changed subjects under owner rules. Ordinary dialogue closes on reload under NPC-017, so its current allowance ends with that session. Reload grants no new knowledge roll for the same unchanged pair.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Scope knowledge and questions to stable identities. Keep captions, scene references and UI list indexes out of authoritative identity. Exact retention schema, change notifications and mod interfaces remain integration choices.

## Acceptance criteria

1. General, local and authorized quest topics appear as configured. Same-name subjects remain distinct and contextual labels reveal only authorized information.
2. A visible question can receive an unknown answer. Applicable geographic, quest, specialist and relationship rules run before the stock table result.
3. Knowledge survives intervening conversations and reload. A relevant world or quest change can change the result without rerolling unaffected topics.
4. One qualifying useful answer consumes the shared Tell Me About and rumor allowance. Unknown, unhelpful and exempt answers preserve it. Duplicate delivery cannot consume it twice, and a genuine new conversation resets it.
5. Mods can change supported topics, knowledge and allowance policies. Stale subjects and recycled actor representations cannot redirect answers.
