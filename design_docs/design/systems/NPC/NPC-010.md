# NPC-010 — Stealth and illusion checks

Approval status **Approved — finalized v1**

The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-11 with “I approve”. See NPC-010-A01 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-010-interview-decisions).

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

Let slow movement and Stealth delay detection and let concealment spells interfere with otherwise eligible senses. An enemy who has already encountered the player can more readily track fast movement when they have a valid clue. A player can use solid cover to interrupt tracking. Stealth practice reflects genuine checks.

## Confirmed requirements

NPC-010-D01–D03 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-010-interview-decisions) confirm the game-minute Stealth cadence, the fast-movement bypass under eligible conditions, nominal 62.5 ms illusion checks, and one Stealth use per game minute across observers for an actual player roll. The cadence, movement threshold and practice policy are moddable.

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

In the local 1.1.1 snapshot, `Assets/Scripts/Game/EnemySenses.cs#FixedUpdate` evaluates illusion on classic updates. If the illusion gate permits contact, sight or eligible hearing can establish or refresh detection. Otherwise the ordinary fallback checks Stealth, with the alternate callback evaluated separately. `StealthCheck` excludes enemies outside classic activation scope, targets beyond `1024 × MeshReader.GlobalScale`, and nonhostile observers in dungeon castles. It reuses detection within a game minute, skips odd-minute player rolls below half speed, and can succeed without a roll at higher speed after a prior encounter. It tallies player Stealth at most once per minute across observers. `BlockedByIllusionEffect` gives precedence to a see-through counter, then Invisibility, Chameleon and Shadow. `Assets/Scripts/Game/GameManager.cs` defines the nominal classic interval as 0.0625 seconds.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

- Resolve the candidate and applicable sensory opportunities through NPC-008 and NPC-009. Apply MAGIC-025 concealment to ordinary channels. When the gate passes, accepted sight or eligible hearing takes precedence over a fallback Stealth check. Explicit alternate senses declare their own authorized concealment policy.
- Evaluate Chameleon or Shadow at most once per observer–target pair per eligible illusion interval. Roll only when an ordinary sensory or Stealth opportunity could use the result. Reuse the result for that interval. Paused gameplay, repeated queries and missed intervals do not create extra draws. Apply current categorical barriers such as Invisibility and configured counters before accepting observations.
- Use the Stealth fallback for eligible nearby candidates when sight and hearing have not established current detection. Use DFU's reference range and activation prerequisites, with native scope and distance mapping. An actual check resolves at most once per observer–target pair per game minute. For player movement below half speed, roll on even minutes only. Within the retained result's valid scope, a skipped minute or repeated evaluation can reuse the prior detection outcome. Recheck current geometry and observation eligibility before using it.
- At half speed or faster, a player previously encountered by that observer can bypass the fallback Stealth roll when an eligible observation exists. Solid cover blocks this shortcut from tracking through the obstruction. Require a usable sensory or fallback clue to support any fallback position. A fallback outcome preserves the clue's original precision and cannot supply fresh sight or an exact live position by itself.
- On an actual player Stealth roll, report one use at most once per player game minute across all observers, whether the roll succeeds or fails. Result reuse, movement bypasses and illusion-only checks award no use. CHAR-012 owns acceptance and advancement.

## Formulas and parameters

- Stealth uses [RULE-011](../RULE/RULE-011.md). With separation `d`, classic scale `g` and resolved live skill `S`, `D = trunc(d/g)` and the remaining-hidden chance is `clamp(2 × floor(D × S / 1024), 0, 100)` percent. The detection side uses the complement of the same roll.
- Illusion uses [MAGIC-025](../MAGIC/MAGIC-025.md). Absent an applicable counter, Invisibility blocks the gate, Chameleon passes on 8%, and Shadow passes on 4%. Use the approved family priority and source composition. These are per-check chances.
- The default illusion interval is 0.0625 seconds of eligible active simulation. The default Stealth movement threshold is half speed, and the practice award is one use per eligible game minute.

## Required data, inputs, outputs and events

Inputs include stable observer and target IDs, scope, game minute and eligible simulation interval, candidate eligibility and geometry, prior encounter state, movement-speed classification, resolved live Stealth, concealment families and applicable counters, and available observations or clues. Outputs include retained gate or Stealth result, accepted observation or detection failure, and a deduplicated player practice report. NPC-011 consumes the observation's source, time and precision.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- An illusion counter still requires ordinary geometry and sense eligibility. Concurrent concealment sources use the approved family priority without granting extra rolls.
- Changing target, moving behind cover or losing valid scope requires observation revalidation. A reused result cannot refresh a position without new supporting information.
- A missed or blocked check, failed provider, stale callback or missing actor cannot fabricate detection or practice. A resolved failed Stealth roll still qualifies for practice under the once-per-minute cap.
- Ordinary hearing cannot acquire an undetected target under NPC-009. The fast-movement rule requires a prior encounter and an eligible clue.

## Configuration and balancing

Support validated changes to check cadence, range, movement threshold, eligible fallback conditions, practice amount and cadence, and approved illusion profiles and counters. Evaluate balance at the configured interval because repeated 8% or 4% checks can yield detection quickly while a sensory opportunity persists. Keep cadence independent of frame rate and rendering settings.

## Save/load considerations

Restore supported prior encounter state, retained check windows and practice deduplication state with the current actor IDs. Rebuild live sensory eligibility after load. Loading cannot award another practice use or manufacture extra draws for a retained window. Historical clues retain their original time and precision.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Use explicit clock domains, scope-bound check identities and the appropriate owned random sources. UI queries are read-only. Exact world scale, activation-scope mapping, movement classifier and persistence schema remain integration choices.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Eligible below-half-speed player checks roll only on even game minutes, once per observer and minute. Repeated evaluation reuses the settled result while eligible.
2. A previously encountered player at or above half speed can bypass the fallback roll with an eligible clue. Solid cover prevents the shortcut from tracking through a wall.
3. Stock Chameleon and Shadow use the approved odds at the 62.5 ms nominal active interval, independent of rendering. Invisibility and counters obey MAGIC-025. Mods can change supported timing.
4. An actual player Stealth roll awards one use per minute across observers, regardless of result. Skips, repeated delivery, movement bypasses and reload award no extra use.
5. NPC-009 hearing remains approximate and cannot initiate ordinary contact. Fallback detection and restored clues preserve their supported precision.
