# NPC-003 — Civilian navigation and idling

Approval status **Approved — finalized v1**

The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-11 with “I approve”. See NPC-003-A01 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-003-interview-decisions).

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

Keep settlement streets active with pedestrians who tend to follow streets and can be approached for conversation. Pedestrians negotiate temporary obstructions and can wait visibly when no safe route is available.

## Confirmed requirements

NPC-003-D01–D03 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-003-interview-decisions) confirm DFU-style local wandering with moddable pace and preferences, calm approach idling with line of sight and a dialogue movement hold, and local rerouting and retry before routine retirement.

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

`Assets/Scripts/Game/MobilePersonMotor.cs` uses four cardinal neighbors and weighted navigation tiles. `SeekingTile` favors continued movement and higher-weight surfaces, with a 2.5% chance per seek to discard the forward candidate and sample a direction and a 20% chance to skip the lower-weight correction branch. `SetTargetPosition` reserves the target tile. `IsDirectionClear` probes geometry. `MovingForward` advances at 1.3 Unity units per second. `Update` pauses movement when paused or paralyzed and idles for a stationary, visible, non-beast player with weapons sheathed within 2.5 units and no nearby enemies. `FloatingOrigin_OnPositionUpdate` recomputes the scene target. `Assets/Scripts/Game/Utility/PopulationManager.cs#UpdateMobiles` queues recycling when seek count exceeds four.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

- Choose short traversable steps through the loaded settlement. Prefer continuing forward and staying on street surfaces, with occasional changes of direction and permission to use other valid ground. Check usable clearance and contention before starting a step.
- Enter approach idle when the nearby player is stationary, visible, outside beast form, has weapons sheathed, has clear line of sight to the civilian, and the nearby enemy check is clear. Re-evaluate when those conditions change. Resume from the current position after checking the route.
- Hold movement through active dialogue. Paused simulation and authoritative immobilization stop locomotion. Resume only when applicable holds have cleared and the route remains valid. Reactions and dialogue eligibility are owned by their respective features.
- When blocked, seek a safe nearby alternative. If the blockage may clear, wait briefly and retry. Report persistent failure to NPC-001 for routine retirement. If the civilian must stay because of visibility or conversation, hold safely and allow recovery if a route opens.
- Release stale movement claims when a step is abandoned or the actor retires. Preserve the actor's world position through coordinate rebasing. A routine recovery cannot visibly relocate a pedestrian to bypass an obstruction.

## Formulas and parameters

Expose walking speed, approach distance, street and direction preferences, turn frequency, retry interval and persistent-failure threshold. The reference values above describe DFU. Godot units, probabilities and the retry budget require tuning. Count blocked time or retries during eligible simulation, with the exact measurement to be selected during integration.

## Required data, inputs, outputs and events

Inputs include the actor ID, current placement, navigable surfaces and their preferences, loaded geometry, nearby actors and occupancy, player movement and visibility, weapon and form state, nearby-enemy eligibility, conversation state, simulation eligibility and immobilization. Outputs include movement intent, facing, locomotion or idle state, and a routine retirement request on persistent failure. Movement animation follows the resulting locomotion state.

## Cross-system interactions

NPC-001 owns admission and retirement, including visibility and conversation gates. NPC-002 owns actor identity across restore. WORLD/LOC own navigation readiness, street data and coordinate transforms. MOVE, COMBAT and MAGIC supply player or actor state. NPC dialogue owns conversation lifetime. CORE controls simulation eligibility, SAVE coordinates placement recovery, and AV presents movement.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- At a grid or streaming boundary, wait or turn while the next surface is unavailable. Avoid advancing through blocked geometry or committing two actors to an incompatible passage.
- A player across a wall does not trigger approach idle. Resume from a fresh clearance check if dialogue ends or an obstacle enters the old route during a hold.
- An actor held by dialogue, pause or immobilization does not accumulate navigation failure solely from standing still. If the civilian dies or retires, release its navigation claims.
- On a world-origin change, rebuild transient targets from authoritative world placement and recheck claims as needed. A recovered or retired actor cannot leave a stale reservation that blocks another civilian.

## Configuration and balancing

Profiles may tune pace, approach distance, route preferences, local variation and retry behavior. Use validated, bounded values. Avoid crowd stalls from overly persistent claims and repeated retirement requests from transient doorway congestion.

## Save/load considerations

Restore present civilians under their NPC-002 identities. Restore usable world placement and facing, then rebuild transient route choices and reservations against ready navigation. Restore any supported conversation or immobilization through its owner. If placement cannot be validated, defer movement and use owner-supported recovery while preserving the actor identity and NPC-001 visit state.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. The navigation representation and exact movement solver remain implementation choices. Preserve street bias, local variation and safe recovery with whichever representation is chosen. Keep reservations scoped to the current actor and navigation context.

## Acceptance criteria

1. In a representative settlement, pedestrians tend to continue forward and favor streets, sometimes turn, and can use valid alternate ground. A mod can change pace and route preferences.
2. Approach idle occurs only when all calm approach conditions are met, including clear line of sight. Active dialogue holds movement and ordinary locomotion resumes after its holds clear and the route is safe.
3. A temporary obstruction can clear after waiting or a local detour. Persistent blockage requests NPC-001 retirement, which respects visibility and active conversations. A visible stuck civilian waits safely.
4. Pause and paralysis stop locomotion. Navigation claims are cleared on abandonment or retirement. Stream boundaries and origin changes preserve valid placement and prevent movement through unavailable space.
5. Save/load restores the present actor under the same identity and rebuilds route and reservation state against current navigation.
