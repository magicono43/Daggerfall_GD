# NPC-017 — Conversation entry and actor context

Approval status **Approved — finalized v1**

The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-11 with “I approve”. See NPC-017-A01 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-017-interview-decisions).

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

Open a conversation with the intended person and the right local, faction and service context. Ordinary dialogue pauses gameplay. A repeat conversation has a fresh set of questions and answer allowances while the same speaker retains applicable knowledge and refusal history.

## Confirmed requirements

NPC-017-D01–D03 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-017-interview-decisions) confirm session reset, actor-scoped continuity, default paused dialogue with mod support for non-pausing dialogue, and return to gameplay on load with durable consequences preserved.

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

In the local 1.1.1 snapshot, `Assets/Scripts/Game/TalkManager.cs#TalkToMobileNPC` uses the regional People faction. `TalkToStaticNPC` resolves faction and building context and can route to a quest offer before ordinary dialogue. Both reset the successful-answer count. `SetTargetNPC` uses the previous component reference to recognize the last speaker. `TalkToNpc` handles suppression, refusal, greetings and dialogue window entry. `StartNewConversation` resets question state and prepares topics and rumors.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

- Accept an eligible Talk interaction or explicit Talk choice from a service interface under MOVE-023. Check the intended actor's availability, reach and obstruction at entry, along with dialogue capability, applicable hostility or suppression, and scoped quest routing. Use the approved owner contracts for service and quest choices.
- Bind the conversation to a stable actor ID and current gameplay scope. Resolve the speaker's settled name and portrait, actor type, location, region and faction context, and the interaction origin and applicable service role. Use the regional People faction as the ordinary mobile baseline and actor/building faction context for placed actors. FACT owns exact faction resolution. A service-origin Talk action remains bound to its originating provider.
- A genuine new conversation resets question count, current selection and answer allowance. Preserve speaker knowledge and applicable refusal records by actor ID across intervening conversations. NPC-018 owns refusal recovery. Topic and answer features own exact knowledge and allowance policies. Redraw or duplicate entry delivery does not create another conversation.
- Ordinary dialogue acquires a scoped gameplay pause under CORE-016. Release its pause on close or teardown. Support a modded non-pausing conversation under the same entry and validity rules. Hold a wandering speaker during active dialogue under NPC-001 and NPC-003, including when dialogue does not pause gameplay.
- Tolerate minor positional changes after admission. End the session if the actor dies, is removed or leaves the interaction area, the player leaves the area, or an applicable hostility or suppression rule makes dialogue unavailable. Provide a short reason when appropriate. Revalidate the session and actor before consequential choices and decline stale input without redirecting it.

## Formulas and parameters

Entry reach follows MOVE-023 and its dependencies. No new distance or reaction threshold is selected. Parameters include pause policy, ongoing interaction area and supported reason presentation. Exact tolerance and reaction policies remain owner work.

## Required data, inputs, outputs and events

Inputs include stable actor and session IDs, current scope, entry origin, capability and availability, geometry, settled presentation, region and faction context, active service or quest routing and suppression. Outputs include opened dialogue or a checked alternate route, refusal or unavailable result, pause and speaker hold ownership, and session close or invalidation. Capture the resolved actor for each consequential choice.

## Cross-system interactions

MOVE-023 owns activation and routing. NPC-001/003 hold wandering actors and NPC-002/004 provide identity. NPC-018 and subsequent dialogue features own reaction, greetings and content. FACT owns faction and reputation. QUEST and ECON own consequential choices and service actions. UI presents dialogue, CORE-016 owns suspension, and CORE-013/SAVE govern restore.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- Recycling a scene object cannot redirect an existing conversation or transfer knowledge or refusal state to the next actor.
- If a provider is replaced while a service screen is open, an old Talk choice cannot open dialogue with the replacement. An authorized new interaction may target them.
- Closing dialogue releases its own pause and speaker hold. Other active suspensions and owner obligations retain their own lifetimes.
- Missing required context or a failed quest route uses owner recovery. A stale response cannot duplicate an accepted choice or practice report.

## Configuration and balancing

Expose approved pause and non-pause profiles, dialogue availability and context mappings, and supported feedback. Exact topic rules, answer limits, faction relationships and refusal recovery belong to their owning features.

## Save/load considerations

Load into gameplay without reopening ordinary dialogue. Discard transient selections, question counters and presentation state. Restore durable quest choices, learned facts, applicable actor records and committed practice through their owners. Pending required quest or service activities follow CORE-013 and their own save rules. Loading neither accepts a pending choice nor refreshes durable refusal history.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Scope conversation state and lifetime to stable actor and session identity. Keep UI redraw separate from entry, pause ownership and consequential decisions. Exact context schema, save format and mod interfaces remain integration choices.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A mobile or placed NPC opens the intended route with the correct actor and context. Service or quest routes honor explicit ownership.
2. Reopening dialogue resets session counters and selections while the speaker retains applicable knowledge and refusal state even after another conversation.
3. Ordinary dialogue pauses gameplay. A mod can allow non-pausing dialogue while still holding the wandering speaker. Closing releases the dialogue-owned pause and hold.
4. Minor positional changes preserve dialogue. Invalid actor or context ends it safely, and stale input cannot redirect a choice.
5. Reload returns to gameplay, preserves durable consequences and does not replay a choice or reopen ordinary dialogue.
