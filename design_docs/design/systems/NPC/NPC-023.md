# NPC-023 — NPC quest-offer identity

Approval status **Approved — finalized v1**

The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-11 with “I approve”. See NPC-023-A01 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-023-interview-decisions).

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

Let players hear who has work and find the right person. A stock offer is a one-shot opportunity once shown. Authored offers can give players time to decide and let them return to the same offer later.

## Confirmed requirements

NPC-023-D01–D03 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-023-interview-decisions) confirm actor-bound personal work, explicit organization ownership, moddable visit-scoped eligibility, and one-shot offers with an authored deferral option.

It excludes children and requires a named building for the ordinary candidate pool. `SetRandomQuestor` selects a candidate for a referral and the pool is keyed by name seed. `IsCastleNpcOfferingQuest` uses a separate 25% first-talk check. `Assets/Scripts/Game/UserInterfaceWindows/DaggerfallQuestOfferWindow.cs` removes an ordinary candidate when its offer window opens. Original Daggerfall odds are unverified.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

- Bind a personal offer to the placed actor's stable ID. The person's deliberate relocation preserves the binding, subject to current offer eligibility. Organization or establishment offers can be explicitly owned by that organization or establishment and presented by currently authorized actors. Provider substitution requires a fresh valid interaction under NPC-004 and MOVE-023.
- Build the ordinary work pool from the applicable settlement's eligible placed actors. Stock criteria use named buildings and eligible merchant, commoner and noble roles, excluding children. Apply one retained 25% eligibility check per eligible ordinary actor per visit. For eligible castle actors, resolve the separate retained 25% check at first qualifying conversation in the visit. Ordinary eligibility takes precedence for actors who qualify for both, giving each actor one stock check per visit.
- Retain assignments and settled checks across building transitions and reload. Leaving and returning to the settlement allows fresh eligibility checks. NPC-001's building-exit crowd refill does not refresh the work pool. Open offers and accepted quests follow their owner lifecycle when a new visit starts.
- A work referral identifies an eligible provider by stable ID and gives its authorized current name and location. Stock selection is random among eligible candidates. A referral itself neither spends the candidate nor accepts a quest. Revalidate the provider on approach and report unavailability if eligibility changed. Never silently redirect the referral to a similarly named actor.
- A stock offer is spent for repeat presentation once successfully shown. The displayed offer still permits acceptance or decline. Closing without deciding also ends the stock opportunity. A failed presentation preserves the opportunity and retries present the same offer.
- Explicit quest or mod policy can retain a pending offer after closing and allow later acceptance or decline, including after reload when supported. Keep the same offer and resolved terms. Honor authored expiry and eligibility and revalidate at consequential choice. An existing active quest role uses its declared reservation and cannot be overwritten by ordinary work.

## Formulas and parameters

Default ordinary and castle eligibility odds are each 1/4 per applicable actor and visit. Selection from the eligible referral pool is uniform by default. Eligibility odds, refresh boundaries, role filters, referral choice and offer lifetime are moddable. Quest generation, rewards and explicit expiry remain QUEST policy.

## Required data, inputs, outputs and events

Inputs include stable actor, organization, establishment, settlement and visit IDs, candidate context and role, reservations, retained eligibility outcome, offer ID and settled terms, presentation acknowledgment and authored lifetime. Outputs include eligible candidate, scoped referral, presented or deferred offer, and accepted, declined, expired, or spent state. Retain operation identity for duplicate handling.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- A removed provider, changed authorization or conflicting reservation invalidates stale presentation or choices under owner rules. A new provider for an organization requires a fresh binding.
- A duplicate open, redraw or callback cannot generate another offer or spend a second opportunity. An offer already spent for repeat presentation can still accept a choice from its original valid open session.
- If a settled offer becomes invalid before commitment, explain unavailability and use owner recovery. Never assign an unrelated quest or actor as a silent replacement.
- Returning to a settlement does not duplicate a still-pending authored offer or an accepted quest.

## Configuration and balancing

Support validated authoring for eligible roles and owner types, odds, visit boundaries, provider binding, one-shot or deferable offers, expiry and referral selection. Deferrable offers should declare when they remain valid and which later interactions can resume them.

## Save/load considerations

Persist the visit, retained eligibility, spent status and supported pending offers with stable identity and settled terms. Restore accepted quest and owner receipts before accepting choices. Ordinary dialogue closes on load under NPC-017. Stock offers already shown remain spent, while supported deferred offers can be reopened under their policy.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Keep provider identity, offer owner, eligibility, presentation and choice commitment explicit. Stable IDs carry authoritative references. Exact interfaces, visit boundaries and save schemas remain integration choices.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Personal and organization offers retain the correct owner and provider across supported changes. A referral resolves the intended actor and authorized location.
2. Each applicable stock eligibility check uses 25% odds and is retained during the visit. Building entry and reload preserve it. Settlement return can refresh it.
3. Showing a stock offer spends repeat presentation. The open session can still accept or decline. Closing it does not permit reopening. A supported authored offer can be deferred and later reopened with the same terms.
4. Invalid, repeated or stale operations cannot duplicate an offer, accept a quest twice or bind a different actor.
5. Mods can change eligibility, refresh, referral selection and offer lifetime within supported contracts.
