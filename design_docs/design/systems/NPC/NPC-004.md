# NPC-004 — Placed NPCs and service identity

Approval status **Approved — finalized v1**

The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-11 with “I approve”. See NPC-004-A01 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-004-interview-decisions).

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

Give authored people a consistent identity across visits and let players return to the right person or establishment for a service or quest. A new clerk can continue an establishment's work. An independent trader can carry their own service and stock between supported locations.

## Confirmed requirements

NPC-004-D01–D03 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-004-interview-decisions) confirm stable placed identity, establishment-owned service state by default, mod support for actor-owned services and stock, and clear feedback when an approachable provider cannot offer an expected service.

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

In the local 1.1.1 snapshot, `Assets/Scripts/Game/StaticNPC.cs` uses layout or quest Person records and runtime location context. It derives a layout position hash, stores faction and billboard information, and uses a fixed faction name for individual factions or a seeded name from the region bank. `AssignQuestResourceBehaviour` checks for an active questor match. `Assets/Scripts/Game/PlayerActivate.cs#StaticNPCClick` checks quest handling and faction listeners before selecting services or dialogue using faction and building context. These are source observations. See the [feature catalog](../../../analysis/systems/NPC_FEATURE_CATALOG.md#npc-004--placed-npcs-and-service-identity).

## Detailed rules and behaviors

- Assign each authored person a stable actor ID. Reused building or dungeon templates have separately scoped occupants. Scene reconstruction, appearance changes and explicitly authored moves preserve identity. Replacing a person creates a new actor ID. A replacement can inherit a service assignment through an explicit provider binding. Personal and quest history remains with the original actor.
- Use authored names where supplied and settled generated names for configured regional naming through DATA-017. Preserve a generated name across reconstruction. An explicitly authored rename can change the displayed name while preserving actor identity.
- A service definition declares its capabilities and owner. Establishment-owned stock and obligations persist through provider changes. A valid provider binding identifies which person can act for the service under current location and access rules.
- Support actor-owned service state and stock through the same service interface. An independent merchant or trainer can have an explicit service in an inn, outdoors or another valid location without faction affiliation. The service follows the actor within its declared operating context. ECON and other service owners determine stock rules and which obligations they support.
- Resolve dialogue and service choices using current actor availability, capabilities and context. An explicit actor-scoped quest reservation may replace an interaction or observe it under MOVE-023. Release the reservation when its owner ends it. Other NPCs in the same faction retain independent availability.
- If an approachable provider temporarily cannot offer an expected service, give a short reason and allow eligible ordinary dialogue. An absent provider leaves the service unavailable until a valid provider is available. Confirmations revalidate the original actor, service, context and permissions. A provider change requires a new interaction with the authorized provider.

## Formulas and parameters

No numeric formula is introduced. Prices, stock quantities, replenishment, service reach, quest priority and faction access use their owning specifications. Configurable fields include actor identity and placement, service owner type, provider bindings, operating contexts and supported availability reasons.

## Required data, inputs, outputs and events

Inputs include stable actor, placement, establishment and service IDs, source provenance, region and faction context where applicable, naming and presentation data, provider bindings, availability and quest reservations. Outputs include a ready actor, eligible interaction choices or an unavailable reason, and a scoped interaction target for the owning system. Actor movement, provider changes, reservation changes and teardown invalidate affected choices. Service mutations use the owner contract.

## Cross-system interactions

LOC/WORLD resolve placement and establishment context. DATA-005 provides layout record context. DATA-017 supplies naming. MOVE-023 owns activation and revalidation. ECON, ITEM and FACT own service state, custody, permissions and transactions. QUEST owns actor-scoped reservations and quest roles. CORE/SAVE coordinate reconstruction and DATA/MOD validate definitions and migrations.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- Reject ambiguous IDs and bindings during content validation. If unresolved data reaches runtime, defer the affected interaction and preserve its records for owner recovery. Shared coordinates, names, factions and layouts cannot silently redirect a reference.
- Relocating the provider leaves establishment service state with its owner. The actor's personal service may operate at the new location if its configured rules allow it.
- If a provider changes while a menu is open, cancel stale confirmation with an explanation. Committed purchases, repair custody and rewards retain their owner receipts.
- If a quest actor is unavailable or a mod removes a placement, use owner-supported recovery or migration. Do not transfer a quest role or stock by proximity or name matching.

## Configuration and balancing

Mods can author placements, names, capabilities, authorized providers, operating contexts and establishment- or actor-owned service profiles. Definitions validate stable IDs, ownership and references. Access, stock and service cadence remain owner policy.

## Save/load considerations

Restore persistent actor facts, placement changes, active reservations, service ownership and supported provider bindings against stable IDs. Rebuild scene actors and validate bindings before enabling interactions. Persist generated names under DATA-017 and stock and obligations through their service owners. Pending transactions follow the owner's recovery rules. Use explicit migrations for changed authored keys and preserve unresolved records for recovery where the owner supports it.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Keep actor identity, placement, establishment identity and service ownership explicit. A scene instance, coordinate hash or display name alone cannot identify a persistent actor or service. Classic record references may support import and compatibility mappings. Independent services require no synthetic faction or building.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Reload and deliberate relocation preserve the same placed actor and their personal records. Two instances of the same layout resolve distinct occupants.
2. A replacement receives a new actor identity. An authorized successor can provide the establishment service with existing stock and obligations intact.
3. An independent modded actor can own and retain stock and provide a supported service outside faction or building ownership.
4. An actor-scoped quest reservation affects the intended interaction and releases with its owner. An approachable unavailable provider gives a reason and can still converse if eligible.
5. A stale menu cannot confirm against an unavailable or changed provider. Missing or conflicting identity data cannot redirect an interaction or duplicate a committed transaction.

## Compatibility review and outstanding questions

Placed actors have an authored lifecycle alongside the ambient policies in [NPC-001](NPC-001.md) and [NPC-002](NPC-002.md).
