# NPC-022 — Quest topics, links and post-quest responses

Approval status **Approved — finalized v1**

The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-11 with “I approve”. See NPC-022-A01 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-022-interview-decisions).

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

Let players follow quest clues into new conversation topics. Active questions retire when their quest ends, while earned discoveries remain available under their own rules. A questor can acknowledge a completed or failed quest the next time the player speaks with them.

## Confirmed requirements

NPC-022-D01–D03 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-022-interview-decisions) confirm authored reveals, terminal topic retirement, and once-per-outcome follow-up greetings with latest-first ordering.

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

In the local 1.1.1 snapshot, `Assets/Scripts/Game/TalkManager.cs#AddQuestTopicWithInfoAndRumors` registers quest resources and their text. `DialogLinkForQuestInfoResource` hides entries and can associate people, places or things. `GetNPCQuestGreeting` checks registered post-quest messages for a matching questor and expands text with link reveal enabled. `Assets/Scripts/Game/Questing/Quest.cs` registers authored success or failure rumors and questor messages, removes active topics and rumors, and processes temporary quest residences. The recreation uses explicit reveal operations under DATA-016.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

- Use quest-instance and resource IDs for authored topics and links. Keep hidden subjects unavailable to dialogue lists until an authorized quest event or presentation of an authored clue reveals them. A visible label does not authorize disclosing additional facts about its subject.
- By default, showing an authorized clue unlocks its linked topics without requiring a second action. Commit the reveal through an explicit owner operation on successful presentation of the bound clue. Preview, translation and redraw remain read-only. Mods may require acknowledgment or another declared action. After reveal, apply NPC-019 topic and knowledge eligibility.
- Retire active topics, links and associated active rumors on applicable completion, failure, abandonment or declined offer. Preserve legitimate journal entries and discoveries subject to their owners' explicit temporary-record policies. Mods may preserve selected topics with declared post-quest scope. Retirement revalidates any open choices.
- Register authored success or failure follow-ups for the intended questor by stable actor ID. After ordinary admission under NPC-017/018, show the latest eligible pending greeting first. Consume it after successful presentation and leave older ones pending for later conversations. Use committed outcome order to resolve identical timestamps. Without an eligible follow-up, use the applicable ordinary greeting rules.
- Follow-up text can authorize a new explicit reveal under its own post-quest policy. Active quest topics remain retired unless the quest or mod explicitly authorizes a post-quest topic. Post-quest rumors follow NPC-021 eligibility and lifecycle.

## Formulas and parameters

Default follow-up budget is one greeting per eligible conversation and one successful presentation per quest outcome and intended recipient. Order pending greetings by most recent committed outcome first. No random roll or calendar expiry is required by default. Configurable policies include reveal trigger, topic retention, greeting repetition, expiration and dedicated follow-up topics.

## Required data, inputs, outputs and events

Inputs include quest instance, resource and link IDs, authored eligibility and reveal events, bound message and presentation result, terminal quest outcome, questor identity, and follow-up eligibility and completion. Outputs include authorized topic exposure, retirement, greeting presentation and consumption, and owner updates. Record stable operation identities for duplicate handling.

## Cross-system interactions

QUEST owns clue authorization, outcome and retirement. NPC-017 binds the speaker, NPC-018 owns greeting admission, NPC-019 builds topics, NPC-020 owns location disclosure, and NPC-021 owns rumors. DATA-016 renders messages without effects. MOVE-023 governs scoped interactions, and CORE/SAVE and DATA/MOD handle restoration and validated extensions.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- Identically named subjects from different quests remain distinct. Missing links or questors use owner recovery and cannot bind by name to another subject.
- A stale option or delayed presentation cannot reveal a retired clue. A committed reveal cannot be repeated by reopening its text.
- An unavailable or refused questor leaves the greeting pending. A failed display does not consume it. Retrying a successful display cannot consume another greeting or repeat its consequences.
- A newer outcome queues ahead of older eligible outcomes. A terminal quest result registers only the follow-up content authorized for that outcome.

## Configuration and balancing

Allow validated reveal and acknowledgment policies, post-quest retention, greeting priority, repeat and expiry, and alternate presentation as a topic. Preserve disclosure scope and committed outcome identity across overrides.

## Save/load considerations

Persist authorized exposures and retirements, pending and consumed follow-ups, and committed disclosure receipts through their owners. Preserve sufficient settled message context for eligible post-quest responses after active quest teardown. Revalidate on load. Ordinary dialogue does not reopen, and restore does not itself consume a greeting or disclose a clue.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Separate presentation from checked quest mutations. Keep actor, quest and resource identity stable and scope-bound. Exact owner recovery, presentation acknowledgment and save schemas remain integration choices.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Hidden subjects stay unavailable until an authorized event or successfully shown clue reveals them. Redraw and preview produce no reveal. Mods can require acknowledgment.
2. Ending a quest or declining an offer retires applicable active topics and clues. Earned journal and discovery records follow their owner rules. Explicitly retained topics remain within their declared scope.
3. The intended questor gives the latest pending eligible greeting once, leaving older outcomes for later conversations. Failure to present leaves the greeting pending.
4. Reload preserves pending and consumed greetings and revealed and retired topics. Duplicate delivery cannot reveal, consume or redirect a different resource.
