# NPC-008 — Target acquisition and allegiance

Approval status **Approved — finalized v1**

The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-11 with “I approve”. See NPC-008-A01 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-008-interview-decisions).

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

Enemies choose opponents from current relationships and available observations. By default, ordinary hostile enemies focus on the player, with player allies able to engage eligible hostile foes. Turning on enemy infighting allows eligible different-team enemies to fight one another. A hidden attacker can draw attention without giving the victim exact knowledge of their position.

## Confirmed requirements

NPC-008-D01–D03 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-008-interview-decisions) confirm a player-facing infighting toggle defaulting to off, DFU-style target scoring, and perception-limited local retaliation. Infighting, selection and retaliation are moddable.

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

`Assets/Scripts/Game/EnemySenses.cs#GetTargets` filters eligible player, monster and class actors using hostility, current and definition team values, infighting flags and quest restrictions. It scores candidates using visibility, proximity and whether the candidate already targets someone. Periodic target refresh can also assign a reciprocal target to an enemy with none. `Assets/Scripts/Game/EnemyMotor.cs#MakeEnemyHostileToAttacker` may redirect the victim if its existing target is absent, unseen or more than two Unity units away, and copies the attacker's exact position into target memory. A player attack can set hostility and restore a former ally's archetype team. These are observations from the local 1.1.1 snapshot.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

- Resolve candidates in the current simulation scope. Exclude self, dead, unavailable and otherwise invalid actors. Use current team and owner-provided hostility, ally, quest and protected-actor relationships. Recheck eligibility before an attack begins.
- With enemy infighting off, ordinary enemies do not proactively acquire unrelated nonplayer enemies. Player allies can engage eligible hostile foes, and those foes can respond through local retaliation. With it on, permit proactive acquisition between eligible different-team enemies. Same-team actors are excluded from default proactive infighting. Respect suppression on either participant and actor-scoped quest restrictions.
- Keep team membership, relationship to a particular actor and current target as separate state. Apply MAGIC-026 protection to the protected actor, leaving unrelated combat relationships available. Quest constraints and supported overrides use explicit ownership and precedence. The infighting setting does not itself change team membership, create hostility or erase an existing owner-authorized relationship.
- Periodically rescore eligible candidates using DFU-style priority and select the highest score. Invalidate a dead or ineligible target when the invalidation is known. Keep the current eligible target on an exact score tie and use a stable ordering if needed. A higher score can switch the target on reassessment. Candidate selection alone does not establish detection or authorize an attack.
- Consume a settled, attributed hostile outcome for retaliation. When the responsible actor is perceived, the victim may retarget under the retaliation policy. Otherwise, investigate an available clue from contact or impact and use perception to locate the attacker. A single friendly-fire hit permits local response without automatically changing team membership or escalating to nearby teammates. Apply MAGIC-026 break predicates independently.
- Target changes feed fresh AI decisions. Cancel unfinished attacks when required by the approved combat or pacification contract. Released attacks retain their original source and delivery rules.

## Formulas and parameters

The default DFU score is `max(0, R − d) + 10 × visible + 5 × untargeted_enemy`, where flags are 0 or 1, `d` is candidate distance and `R` corresponds to the reference value of 30 Unity units. The untargeted bonus applies to eligible enemy candidates without a current target. Exact Godot distance mapping, search range and reassessment cadence require tuning. Supported mods can change weights, eligibility, cadence and target retention.

## Required data, inputs, outputs and events

Inputs include stable actor and scope IDs, availability, team and relationship facts, quest permissions, infighting setting, candidate distance and perception observations, current target, and attributed hostile outcomes with available clues. Outputs include eligible candidates, selected or invalidated target, investigation intent, and explicit relationship changes when authorized. Target queries and diagnostics do not mutate relationships or consume combat actions.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- Changing the infighting setting triggers reevaluation. Drop targets whose only eligibility came from the disabled rule. Existing projectiles retain their approved delivery behavior.
- A hidden attacker, unknown source or unavailable actor cannot supply an exact current position by retaliation alone. Investigate a usable clue when available. Revalidate delayed outcomes against actor identity and scope.
- A quest lock, accepted pacification or target loss during attack preparation follows approved cancellation and owner precedence. A different target requires fresh intent.
- Recheck eligibility for any reciprocal response. A target choice cannot itself force an otherwise ineligible victim to attack.

## Configuration and balancing

Expose the enemy infighting toggle in settings, default off. Support validated mod rules for eligible teams and actors, exceptions, target scoring and timing, retaliation triggers and clue handling. Team changes or broader hostility require explicit owner-authorized policy.

## Save/load considerations

Persist the player setting through settings ownership. Preserve durable team, allegiance, protection and quest facts through their owners. Revalidate restored targets or select afresh from current scope and observations. Restore supported investigation state without replaying the original hostile outcome or attaching a reference to a reused actor representation.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Separate eligibility, perception, ranking, and attack authorization. Use stable actor identity and checked relationship changes. Exact update cadence, registry strategy, and state schema remain integration choices.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Enemy infighting is off by default and has a player-facing toggle. Enabling it permits eligible different-team enemies to engage, subject to actor suppression and quest constraints.
2. DFU-style score favors visible, nearby candidates and applies the untargeted-enemy bonus. Reassessment can switch to a higher-scored opponent. An invalid target is dropped.
3. A hostile outcome can prompt local retaliation. A hidden attacker provides only supported clues. A single friendly-fire hit does not automatically break an alliance or escalate to a team.
4. Pacification protects the applicable actor while unrelated combat remains possible. Target changes and setting changes respect approved attack cancellation and committed delivery.
5. Mods can change supported infighting, scoring and retaliation policies. Restore preserves durable relationships and validates target references before use.
