# NPC-024 — Conversation and actor state restoration

Approval status **Approved — finalized v1**

The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-11 with “I approve”. See NPC-024-A01 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-024-interview-decisions).

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

Resume the same world after loading. People retain their identities and settled state, enemies can resume a search from what they knew, and quest and dialogue consequences remain recorded. Ordinary dialogue closes on load and can be opened again through normal interaction.

## Confirmed requirements

NPC-024-D01–D03 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-024-interview-decisions) confirm required-actor recovery, retained AI history with rebuilt live state, and coherent saves of settled outcomes and supported outstanding work.

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

In the local 1.1.1 snapshot, `Assets/Scripts/Game/TalkManager.cs#GetConversationSaveData` and `RestoreConversationData` persist quest topics, rumors, post-quest messages, work candidates and castle NPCs already spoken to. `Assets/Scripts/Game/Serialization/SerializableEnemy.cs#GetSaveData` stores enemy identity, pose, vitals, hostility, prior encounter, team, inventory, equipment, effects and quest binding. `RestoreSaveData` reapplies those values after any required enemy setup. The source can increment a colliding load ID during registration. Native saves for the recreation follow CORE-013 and the approved actor and dialogue contracts.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

- Restore authoritative actor and owner records by stable identity. For civilians and placed actors, honor NPC-001/002/004 visit, vacancy, identity and interaction state. For enemies, restore assigned NPC-005 setup and current state. Preserve deaths, converted watch identity, custody, effects and quest bindings through their owners. Rebuild representations without generating replacement identities or loot.
- Retain valid references to actors whose areas are unloaded. A missing or conflicting required actor reference needs an explicit migration or owner-supported recovery. If unavailable, fail load with an explanation under CORE-013. Optional unresolved records can remain inactive with their original IDs and provenance. Recover them through an explicit validated mapping.
- Restore applicable knowledge and refusal records, learned facts, hint and map discovery state, board and rumor state, authorized and retired quest topics, pending and consumed follow-ups, and visit-scoped work offers and supported deferrals. Apply the respective owner lifecycle. Close ordinary dialogue on load. A supported ongoing quest or service activity follows its own restore contract.
- Preserve NPC-011 historical observations with their scope, time and precision, pursuit time, lifetime prior encounter and dungeon alert contributors. Restore NPC-010 retained checks and practice boundaries where supported. Reconcile alert against restored game time. Rebuild routes, live observations and prediction against ready geometry. An eligible enemy may resume searching from the old clue. COMBAT-015 cancels unfinished attacks under its load rules and preserves committed attack facts and intervals.
- Persist completed effects and any owner-supported outstanding steps of consequential interactions. If a coherent save cannot yet be taken, defer until the next supported safe point and show feedback while waiting. If the save cannot reach a supported boundary, cancel it or report explicit failure. Preserve the last valid save. Resume only outstanding authorized work.
- Validate the save and required content, then resolve authoritative actor and world records. Bind dependent owner state, rebuild ready representations and live eligibility, and publish availability with restoration origin. Only the references needed for current readiness must be live. Scoped records for unloaded areas can bind when their area is ready.

## Formulas and parameters

No new gameplay formula is introduced. Preserve each owner's approved clocks, durations, caps and retained outcomes. Save deferral and retry follow SAVE and the responsible owner. Exact timing and UI behavior remain integration details.

## Required data, inputs, outputs and events

Inputs include native save version, content and provider provenance, stable actor, scope and operation IDs, actor state, owner references, pending and settled steps, observations and timers, geometry readiness and migration rules. Outputs include restored actor availability, validated inactive references, resumed outstanding work, or explicit recovery or load failure. Publish restore origin without emitting a fresh encounter or conversation.

## Cross-system interactions

SAVE and CORE-013 own native save and load lifecycle. WORLD/LOC own residency, placement and readiness. NPC-001–023 supply their state and lifetimes. ITEM-025 owns custody, MAGIC owns effect state, COMBAT owns interruptions and committed attacks, QUEST and FACT own authoritative outcomes and relationships, and TIME provides the restored clocks. DATA/MOD own validated content and migrations. UI reports save deferral and restore results.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- Two records with one actor ID require explicit reconciliation. A repeated name, coordinate or recycled scene object cannot substitute for identity.
- A valid unloaded actor can retain quests and dialogue references. Interactions requiring the actor wait until readiness. If a required actor later fails to bind, use explicit owner recovery.
- A stale route or target must be revalidated. A historical visual observation grants no fresh sight. Expired alert or pursuit cannot be renewed by load.
- An accepted quest, committed disclosure, consumed greeting, earned practice or spent offer cannot repeat because an optional presentation step failed or the save is reopened.

## Configuration and balancing

Support versioned owner declarations for persistent fields, required and optional references, supported activity resumption, and validated mod migrations. Preserve approved actor and encounter behavior through compatible content changes. Exact schemas and deferral policies remain owner work.

## Save/load considerations

Use native saves and coherent owner boundaries. Restore only the state required by each feature and the remaining steps of explicitly supported operations. Reconstruct current scenes and UI from authoritative state. Preserve ordinary dialogue closure under NPC-017, deferred offer rules under NPC-023 and restoration-origin notifications under CORE-020.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Keep runtime handles, derived observations and raw UI objects out of durable identity. Use stable references, checked restoration, explicit migrations and retained operation receipts. Save schema and runtime ordering mechanisms remain integration choices.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Reload retains supported actor identities, positions and assigned state, conversation facts, work offers, quest records and committed outcomes. Ordinary dialogue stays closed.
2. Required missing or conflicting references recover through supported policy or fail with explanation. Optional records can remain inactive, and valid unloaded references resolve when ready.
3. Pursuit resumes only from valid historical clues within remaining time. Routes and live observations rebuild. Alert retains its original expiry. Unfinished attacks obey combat restore rules.
4. A save either captures settled outcomes and supported outstanding steps or waits for a coherent boundary. Retrying or loading cannot replay committed outcomes.
5. Availability uses restoration origin. No fresh encounter, actor generation, clue reveal, greeting consumption or quest acceptance occurs solely from reconstruction.
