# NPC-005 — Enemy archetype setup

Approval status **Approved — finalized v1**

The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-11 with “I approve”. See NPC-005-A01 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-005-interview-decisions).

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

Give enemy types recognizable combat capabilities. Monsters retain their authored strength, while fresh class enemies generally track player progression. Equipment and spell variation can make two enemies of the same type play differently. An enemy already encountered retains its assigned setup through save/load and area reconstruction.

## Confirmed requirements

NPC-005-D01–D03 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-005-interview-decisions) confirm DFU-style level and equipment inputs, baseline skills and spell assignments, and independently configurable reaction, team and spells. Existing enemies keep their assigned level. [RULE-019](../RULE/RULE-019.md) already governs enemy-class health assignment.

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

`Assets/Scripts/Game/Entities/EnemyEntity.cs#SetEnemyCareer` reads the monster level, health range and base armor from its mobile definition. Stock class enemies use player level, with an inclusive 3–6 level increase for the stock city watch class. Career stats, baseline skills, loot, equipment and applicable spells are assigned in the setup path. Custom careers have a separate branch. `SetEnemyEquipment` delegates to `ItemHelper.AssignEnemyStartingEquipment`, whose starting item-level input is player level. `SetEnemySpells` assigns casting skills and magicka, converts stock spell records, and logs and skips records it cannot resolve. `Assets/Scripts/Game/EnemyMotor.cs#Start` derives initial hostility from the mobile's reaction. These observations come from the local 1.1.1 source snapshot.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

- Resolve the archetype and an authorized creation context. Classify its setup explicitly as monster, enemy class or a supported custom profile. Capture player level once for construction inputs. Resolve authored career and combat capabilities, then assign the actor's level, stats, health, skills, inventory, equipment and spells before making the actor available.
- Stock monsters use authored level and base armor and roll health within their inclusive authored bounds. Stock class enemies use captured player level and the approved RULE-019 health calculation. Apply the stock city watch level bonus when generating a fresh watch combatant under that policy.
- Generate stock starting equipment using captured player level as its starting quality input and the archetype's loadout rules. Mods can select enemy-level quality or explicit gear. Resolve supported loot through ITEM and the configured tables. Equipped items and other carried loot belong to the resulting actor. Pickup, transfer and death use the owning custody rules.
- Assign stock spell lists for configured monsters and stock tiers for casting class enemies. A spell list, team and initial reaction can each be overridden independently. Initial reaction seeds actor behavior, while later hostility changes belong to NPC behavior and related owners.
- Use one settled construction result per authorized fresh spawn. Retries reuse settled rolls and already assigned items. Restoring an actor uses its saved results. Converting a peaceful watch actor uses the COMBAT-018 identity-preserving adapter and retains existing vital state and custody.

## Formulas and parameters

- Fresh stock monster level is the authored level. Fresh stock class level is captured player level, plus a uniform inclusive integer draw of 3–6 for stock city watch. Modified profiles may declare supported fixed levels, ranges or bounded scaling.
- For a stock class at level `L` with career hit points per level `H`, max health is `10 + sum(L independent uniform integers in 1..H)` under RULE-019. Stock monster max health is a uniform inclusive draw within validated authored bounds.
- Stock baseline skill is `min(100, 5 × L + 30)`. Casting class spell tier is `min(6, floor(L / 3))` using assigned enemy level. Monsters with stock spells use their assigned archetype list. Stock spell setup uses six magic-school skill overrides of 80 and max magicka of `10 × L + 100`.

## Required data, inputs, outputs and events

Inputs include the archetype ID and provider version, authorized operation and actor identity, captured player level, career and setup profile, initial team and reaction, approved random source, and validated equipment, loot and spell references. Outputs include an initialized actor with settled level, stats, health, skills, gear, loot, spell list, and initial behavior context. Publish availability after required setup succeeds. Construction itself grants no kill or loot credit.

## Cross-system interactions

NPC-006 and NPC-007 select encounters and arrange placement. DATA/MOD own archetype and reference validation. CHAR and RULE-019 supply career stats and class health. RULE-003 governs owned generation draws. ITEM owns generated items, equipment and custody. MAGIC validates spells and supported casting setup. COMBAT consumes the resulting attack and defense profiles and owns contact and death outcomes. QUEST and LAW can supply authorized context and guard policies. CORE/SAVE coordinate construction and restoration.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- Reject invalid bounds and unsupported profiles before publishing. Missing optional entries may be skipped with diagnostics. Missing required entries block the affected setup. Apply the content owner's recovery policy.
- Interrupted setup must keep unpublished actors out of interaction. A retry cannot duplicate item creation, reroll a resolved health outcome or publish two enemies.
- Player level changes during setup affect future authorized creations. Existing enemies retain their assigned level, health roll and generated gear.
- A noncasting enemy can have an explicitly empty spell list. A neutral caster can have a spell list and neutral initial reaction. Combat tactics determine later casting opportunities.

## Configuration and balancing

Expose supported policies for level, skills, health profile, equipment quality source, fixed gear, loot table, spell assignments, team and initial reaction. Validate ranges and dependencies. The stock item, spell and loot tables require content verification. This feature leaves encounter frequency, corpse transfer, and casting tactics to their owning specifications.

## Save/load considerations

Restore the same actor identity and assigned level, max and current vitals, skills, inventory, equipment, spells and current reaction state under their owner contracts. Changes to player level or archetype defaults affect fresh construction. Restoring a settled actor preserves its assigned results or applies explicit migration. Persist or recover in-flight construction only where supported by CORE/SAVE without replaying settled generation or custody changes.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Keep shared archetype data and assigned actor state separate. Use stable references for content and actor identity, validate supported ranges before draws, and require explicit construction, restoration and conversion entry points. The schema and actor assembly sequence remain integration choices.

## Acceptance criteria

1. At the same player level, a stock monster keeps its authored level, a fresh class enemy matches the player, and a fresh stock city watch combatant adds 3–6 levels. Mods can select another supported scaling policy.
2. Monster health stays within its authored range. Class health follows RULE-019 at the assigned level. Retry and reload preserve the assigned result.
3. Stock generated equipment begins from captured player level, while supported overrides can use enemy level or fixed gear. Level changes and retries do not refresh existing gear or duplicate loot.
4. Baseline skills, stock spell eligibility and tiering follow the selected profile. A mod can vary team, reaction and spells independently. Invalid required data cannot publish an incomplete actor.
5. Restore preserves the same actor and state. Peaceful watch conversion preserves identity, applicable vitals, custody and pending attack handling under COMBAT-018.
