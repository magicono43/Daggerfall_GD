# NPC-021 — Rumor pool and bulletin-board output

Approval status **Approved — finalized v1**

The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-11 with “I approve”. See NPC-021-A01 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-021-interview-decisions).

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

Let players hear local news and find active quest clues by asking NPCs. Bulletin boards show stable public notices that change when the news changes. Repeated conversations can yield familiar rumors.

## Confirmed requirements

NPC-021-D01–D03 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-021-interview-decisions) confirm moddable 50:1 quest weighting for speech, stable board notices with optional rotation, and retained repeat answers without duplicate discovery or quest progress.

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

In the local 1.1.1 snapshot, `Assets/Scripts/Game/TalkManager.cs#GetNewsOrRumors` checks conversation allowance and selects from eligible entries with `WeightedRandomRumor`. The weight is 1 for ambient entries and the configured `QuestRumorWeight` for quest-linked entries. `Assets/Resources/defaults.ini.txt` sets the default to 50. `GetValidRumors` filters region and channel and can suppress common rumors for flagged factions on a 75% roll. `GetNewsOrRumorsForBulletinBoard` selects the first eligible common entry with bulletin-compatible text and uses its first variant.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

- Keep rumor entries with stable identities and declared source, channel, scope, eligibility and lifecycle. Include eligible public or ambient material and authorized quest material. Filter by current actor or board context, declared region, faction and quest conditions, and expiry. Use the board's actual location for board queries.
- On an eligible committed Any News question, choose one spoken rumor from the filtered pool by stock weights. Resolve and retain its variant and authorized context for the conversation. A repeat request reuses that result subject to NPC-019's shared useful-answer allowance. A new conversation can select again and may repeat a familiar rumor.
- If there is no eligible spoken answer, give the applicable no-news response. Useful answers consume the allowance once, with NPC-019 exceptions. Apply the stock faction suppression chance during a new spoken selection opportunity and retain the outcome for that opportunity.
- On a genuine bulletin-board read, present its current eligible public notice. By default, select the first eligible common notice in stable authored order and use its first variant. Retain the selected notice and variant while eligible. Replace it on expiry, withdrawal or explicit supersession. A mod may declare a scheduled rotation. A board with no eligible notice can show an empty or no-news state.
- Reading a notice does not accept a quest or advance time. Key authorized one-time disclosures and quest progress to their owner-defined facts so repeating a rumor across speakers or channels cannot replay them. Text preview, rerender and translation cannot commit those effects.

## Formulas and parameters

Default spoken weight is 50 for each eligible quest-linked entry and 1 for each ambient entry. With `Q` quest and `A` ambient entries after filtering, the total quest probability is `50Q / (50Q + A)` for a nonempty stock pool. Apply one unbiased weighted selection with retained resolution. Stock spoken selection uses DFU's 75% suppression chance for applicable common rumors involving flagged factions. Boards use stable order by default and have no automatic calendar rotation.

## Required data, inputs, outputs and events

Inputs include rumor and variant IDs, source and owner, channel, region and faction context, quest eligibility, posting and expiry rules, actor or board scope, conversation allowance and time. Outputs include eligible pool, retained answer or notice, no-news state, allowance use and any authorized owner disclosure. Presentation receives the settled content and context.

## Cross-system interactions

NPC-017 owns dialogue scope, NPC-019 owns shared answer allowance, and MOVE-023 owns board activation. QUEST and FACT provide authorized rumors and current facts. LOC/WORLD supply board and actor context. TIME supplies expiry and modded rotation boundaries. DATA-016 and UI handle text and display. RULE-003 owns retained draws, and CORE/SAVE and DATA/MOD own restoration and validation.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- An expired or withdrawn entry cannot be newly disclosed. Revalidate before returning a retained answer on a new submission. A previously shown message remains historical and cannot cause a new side effect.
- If an entry loses eligibility during a conversation, return no current news for that settled request. A genuine new conversation can select from the revised pool.
- Missing text or invalid required context uses owner recovery. Unknown or unhelpful results leave the allowance available. A failed display cannot repeat a committed discovery or acceptance.
- Region changes and similarly named boards cannot redirect the bound context. A repeat notification cannot advance rotation or replay quest progress.

## Configuration and balancing

Expose weights, supported eligibility and channel rules, faction suppression, board ordering and refresh policy, and fallback text. Validate explicit authored supersession and rotation. High quest weights can crowd out ambient news when several quest entries are eligible.

## Save/load considerations

Restore authoritative entries, applicable board assignment and rotation state, expiry and committed owner disclosures. Reconcile notices against restored time and eligibility. Ordinary dialogue closes under NPC-017. Restored content does not replay discovery or consume a new rumor-selection draw by itself.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Keep filtering, selection, rendering and disclosure commits explicit. Use scoped actor and board identity and purpose-owned randomness. Exact board ordering metadata, provider hooks and save schema remain integration work.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Spoken news uses eligible entries with default weights 50 and 1. Mods can change weights and supported filters.
2. A board shows eligible public content from its own context and retains it until expiry, withdrawal or supersession. A mod can enable calendar rotation.
3. A repeat question within one conversation respects the shared allowance and uses its settled result while eligible. Genuine new conversations can repeat rumors. Repeat presentation cannot duplicate quest progress or disclosure.
4. Empty or invalid pools produce an appropriate unavailable result. Board reads cannot accept quests or advance time.
5. Reload restores eligible board assignments and committed owner facts without replay.
