# NPC-020 — Directions and map disclosure

Approval status **Approved — finalized v1**

The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-11 with “I approve”. See NPC-020-A01 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-020-interview-decisions).

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

Let knowledgeable speakers guide the player to people, places and services. Some directions point the player toward a building, while others mark it on the local map. Asking another person may improve the answer. Regional service directions help the player choose a settlement to visit.

## Confirmed requirements

NPC-020-D01–D03 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-020-interview-decisions) confirm DFU-style local hints, retained disclosure decisions, and moddable regional service selection. The stock regional policy randomly selects an eligible matching service record and names its settlement.

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

In the local 1.1.1 snapshot, `Assets/Scripts/Game/TalkManager.cs#GetKeySubjectBuildingHint` gives a direction indoors and uses a 35% map-reveal chance outdoors. The source comment leaves classic odds uncertain. `GetAnswerWhereIs` checks knowledge and handles same-building cases. `MarkKeySubjectLocationOnMap` discovers the building and updates applicable quest hint state. `GetAnswerWhereIsRegionalBuilding` names a settlement found by selecting from current-region matching building records.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

- Resolve the authorized topic, speaker knowledge and subject under NPC-017 and NPC-019. Query the current scoped location of a building or the currently authorized location of a quest person or place. Use stable subject and location IDs.
- If the player is already in the target building, give the applicable already-here answer. For a known local destination, use a compass hint indoors. An eligible outdoor building hint has a 35% chance of granting an exact local map mark and otherwise gives a compass hint. Explicit quest policy can require a hint or authorize an exact mark within its declared disclosure scope.
- Retain each eligible speaker's hint-versus-mark outcome for the same subject and applicable facts. A repeat conversation, reload or redraw uses that result. Different speakers can have different results. Indoor or outdoor context determines whether an ordinary mark can be disclosed while preserving the retained outcome. Moving subjects or materially changed quest disclosure are reevaluated under owner policy.
- For ordinary regional service questions, use eligible matches in the current region. Stock selection is random over eligible matching service records, reporting the associated settlement. Exact remote building disclosure requires an explicit policy. Mods can select the nearest eligible settlement or use another supported ranking without changing the question or display flow.
- Commit a map discovery only when the authorized answer grants it. Link the committed answer and discovery to the resolved subject so rendering or retrying the message cannot change the destination or duplicate discovery. Record quest hint or mark progress through the quest owner where applicable.

## Formulas and parameters

Stock outdoor exact mark probability is 35% per eligible retained decision under approved percentile and randomness contracts. Compass output uses the bearing from the player's applicable location to the subject, with sector boundaries and same-position tolerance to be tuned. Regional stock selection is uniform over eligible matching records. A nearest-selection mod declares its distance metric and tie policy.

## Required data, inputs, outputs and events

Inputs include conversation and speaker IDs, topic and subject IDs, knowledge and disclosure eligibility, player position and interior context, stable location and building references, quest subject position, and validated regional matches and policy. Outputs include unknown or unavailable answer, already-here answer, compass hint, regional settlement answer or committed map discovery. Carry the settled subject and authorized precision to text presentation.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- Missing, ambiguous or stale subject location yields an unavailable or unknown answer under the owner contract. Never mark an unrelated match by name.
- A newly moved quest subject uses its authorized current location. A prior historical discovery does not disclose a new location automatically.
- Indoors, an ordinary answer can provide directions even if the retained outdoor decision would grant a mark. Existing discoveries remain owner state.
- No eligible regional match yields an appropriate no-known-location answer. A modded selection policy must return an eligible result from its declared scope.
- Preview, translation, redraw and retry cannot commit an extra map discovery or reroll a settled result.

## Configuration and balancing

Support validated local mark odds, disclosure rules, retention policy, regional service filtering and selection, and authorized quest exceptions. Regional alternatives may rank by current location, travel distance or another declared metric. Preserve information permissions and stable subject identity across extensions.

## Save/load considerations

Restore applicable retained hint outcomes, committed discoveries and quest hint progress by stable IDs. Ordinary conversation closes on load under NPC-017. Reconcile changed subjects and mod definitions through owner policy. Restore cannot reveal a new location through text rendering.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Separate topic lookup, knowledge, disclosure selection, text rendering and authoritative map mutation. Keep captured IDs and precision stable across retry. Exact compass boundaries, selection hooks and save schemas remain integration work.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Eligible local answers can report already here, give directions indoors, and choose a map mark outdoors at the configured stock 35% chance. Authorized quest policy can set the applicable disclosure.
2. An unchanged speaker and subject retain the hint-versus-mark outcome through repeated conversations and reload. A second speaker can resolve independently.
3. A regional service answer can name an eligible matching settlement. A mod can select the closest eligible settlement. Ordinary regional answers reveal the settlement under stock policy.
4. A committed mark updates the correct building and applicable quest progress once. Text previews, duplicate delivery and missing locations cannot reveal unrelated map data.
