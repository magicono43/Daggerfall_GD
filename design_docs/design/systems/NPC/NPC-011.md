# NPC-011 — Pursuit memory, prediction and alert

Approval status **Approved — finalized v1**

The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-11 with “I approve”. See NPC-011-A01 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-011-interview-decisions).

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

Enemies pursue a useful last known destination after losing contact, then give up. Enhanced Combat AI can briefly anticipate where a moving target might go. A dungeon remains alerted while it has a valid alert contributor, affecting the eligibility of encounters during rest.

## Confirmed requirements

NPC-011-D01–D03 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-011-interview-decisions) confirm the 12.5-second default pursuit timeout, lifetime prior-encounter fact, brief enhanced prediction after sight loss, and per-enemy alert with eight game hours of decay. Pursuit timing, prediction and alert policy are moddable.

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

In the local 1.1.1 snapshot, `Assets/Scripts/Game/EnemySenses.cs#FixedUpdate` stores last known position, gives recent sight or hearing precedence over the Stealth fallback, and derives movement from consecutive sight samples. `PredictNextTargetPos` calculates an intercept. `Assets/Scripts/Game/EnemyMotor.cs` resets the give-up counter to 200 on detection and decrements it on classic updates while undetected. At the nominal 62.5 ms classic interval that is 12.5 seconds. `Game/Entities/PlayerEntity.cs` raises or refreshes a shared alert timestamp when requested and expires it after more than eight game hours. `Game/EnemyDeath.cs` clears the shared flag when an enemy targeting the player dies. Other enemies can raise it again.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

- Keep accepted observations by observer and target, with sense, precision, time and scope. Retain exact visual history alongside approximate hearing. Pursue the last sighting or investigate a newer usable hearing area. A presence-only observation provides no destination. Fallback and retaliation clues retain their authorized precision.
- Refresh the pursuit timer on fresh accepted positional contact. During loss of contact, count eligible active simulation time and pursue or search the retained destination. At timeout, end active pursuit and invalidate its destination. A fresh eligible observation can start pursuit again. Loss of target eligibility ends pursuit under NPC-008.
- Preserve an observer's prior encounter fact for that actor's lifetime, independently of the pursuit timer. NPC-010 can use it for fast-movement eligibility. The fact carries no position and is never inherited by a replacement actor.
- With Enhanced Combat AI enabled, derive velocity from consecutive accepted visual samples for the same target. Use a valid intercept while supported by those observations. After sight loss, allow only a brief, bounded extrapolation using the last accepted samples. Then fall back to retained observation data. Enhanced prediction supplies a destination or lead estimate, while navigation and attack authorization remain with their owners.
- Create or refresh dungeon alert contributions only when a living eligible hostile observer targeting the player has an accepted sight observation in that dungeon. Track each contributor independently. Alert is active for that dungeon while at least one unexpired contribution exists. Defeat removes that actor's contribution immediately, including when others remain.
- Retain contributions across streaming and return to the same dungeon until they expire or their owner confirms removal. Alert queries use the current dungeon. Resolve expiry and contributor changes before the applicable rest or encounter eligibility check. Alert enables the NPC-006 gate when its other conditions pass. Actual rest blockers are evaluated under CORE-019.

## Formulas and parameters

- Default pursuit budget is 12.5 seconds of eligible active simulation after the last accepted positional contact.
- For prediction, estimate velocity as displacement divided by elapsed sample time. An intercept can use the earliest feasible positive time at which the predicted target position can be reached at the pursuer's or projectile's applicable speed. Fall back to retained observations if samples or intercept are invalid or exceed the configured limits. Exact projection duration and distance bounds remain tuning parameters.
- Default alert decay uses DFU's strict boundary. Each contribution expires when more than 480 game minutes have elapsed since its last qualifying visual refresh.

## Required data, inputs, outputs and events

Inputs include observer, target and dungeon IDs, accepted observations, simulation elapsed time, game clock, eligibility and death or removal outcomes, Enhanced Combat AI setting, and validated profile. Outputs include pursuit destination or search area, prediction with its source and age, pursuit expiry, prior encounter fact, and dungeon alert state or transitions.

## Cross-system interactions

NPC-008 supplies target eligibility and retaliation clues. NPC-009 and NPC-010 supply accepted observations and precision. Navigation consumes pursuit destinations. COMBAT consumes authorized aim estimates and owns delivery and defeat. TIME supplies game time and active simulation clocks. NPC-006 and RULE-020 consume alert for eligible dungeon-rest opportunities, and CORE-019 handles actual rest eligibility. WORLD/LOC own dungeon scope. CORE/SAVE and DATA/MOD govern reconstruction and policy validation.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- An unreachable observation may guide a search only within reachable space. A prediction beyond a wall or movement constraint does not authorize a route or attack through it.
- Switching targets, teleporting or invalid samples invalidate prediction history for the affected track. Hearing can guide an area search without supplying exact velocity.
- Leaving the dungeon prevents its alert from affecting another dungeon. Streaming and repeated notifications do not multiply contributions. Defeat of one contributor cannot clear another's alert.
- Paused simulation does not consume pursuit time. Explicit game-time advancement can expire alert according to the committed calendar clock.

## Configuration and balancing

Expose validated pursuit duration, prediction policy and bounds, and alert triggers, decay and removal behavior. Keep pursuit time and calendar alert decay independently configurable. Excessive prediction or pursuit can undermine escape around cover, while long alert decay increases the time during which dungeon rest checks remain eligible.

## Save/load considerations

Restore supported historical observations, remaining pursuit time, prior encounter facts and dungeon alert contributors by stable IDs. Revalidate scope and actor eligibility and reconcile alert against restored calendar time before use. Rebuild live perception and prediction from fresh samples. Restore does not refresh alert, grant a new sighting or replay defeat.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Separate exact observations, approximate clues, derived predictions and calendar alert. Use scoped identities and explicit clock domains. Concrete data schema and navigation and projectile interfaces remain integration choices.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Without fresh positional contact, ordinary pursuit expires after 12.5 eligible active seconds. A newer hearing clue guides an approximate search and does not overwrite the last exact sighting.
2. Enhanced prediction uses consecutive accepted visual samples and expires after its configured post-sight bound. It never samples hidden live movement. With Enhanced Combat AI disabled, pursuit uses accepted observations.
3. Prior encounter survives pursuit expiry and reload for the same enemy. It never provides a target position by itself.
4. Two enemies can contribute dungeon alert independently. Defeating one leaves the other's contribution, defeating the last clears alert, and unrefreshed contributions expire on game time. Other dungeons are unaffected.
5. Save/load preserves original expiry and contributor identity. Mods can change supported pursuit, prediction and alert policies.
