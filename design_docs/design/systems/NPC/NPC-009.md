# NPC-009 — Sight, hearing and alternate detection

Approval status **Approved — finalized v1**

The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-11 with “I approve”. See NPC-009-A01 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-009-interview-decisions).

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

Let enemies see exposed targets and follow approximate clues after losing sight. A player can use solid cover to break visual contact, and a wall can block hearing. An ordinary closed door can conceal the player visually while still allowing a pursuing enemy to hear them nearby.

## Confirmed requirements

NPC-009-D01–D03 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-009-interview-decisions) confirm DFU-style sight with a moddable 180-degree baseline, approximate hearing after loss of sight, and no first detection by ordinary hearing or loud actions by default. Mods can enable noise-based acquisition and alternate senses with declared precision.

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

In the local 1.1.1 snapshot, `Assets/Scripts/Game/EnemySenses.cs#CanSeeTarget` checks distance and a forward cone, then casts an eye-to-eye ray. The ray can identify an intervening action door. `CanHearTarget` checks distance and static geometry, permitting sound through ordinary doors and other non-static objects. `FixedUpdate` tests ordinary hearing only for a previously detected target currently out of sight. When the illusion gate permits detection, sight or hearing writes the target's exact position. `CanDetectOtherwiseHandler` defaults to false and its stock call site can mark detection without writing a position.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

- Evaluate an eligible target within the observer's current gameplay scope. Sight uses the observer's configured range and forward cone and a clear line from the observer's eye to an eligible sight point on the target. Solid geometry and closed solid doors occlude sight.
- A successful visual observation supplies an exact observed position at the time of the check, subject to applicable stealth and illusion rules. A raw geometry result can be used by target ranking under NPC-008. Accepted detection and pursuit information must respect the applicable concealment and perception rules.
- Evaluate stock hearing only to maintain established contact with a target the observer currently cannot see. Use the configured range and a path test for acoustic obstruction. Solid walls block stock hearing. Ordinary doors can pass it. Stock hearing uses a distance check. Loud actions do not enable first acquisition through ordinary hearing.
- Accepted hearing supplies a bounded search area with a sample time. Preserve the last exact visual observation separately. The hearing result carries approximate position precision and acoustic contact.
- Mods may add first detection by noise or alternate senses. Each supported sense declares eligibility, geometry and concealment interactions, and whether it reports presence, an approximate location, or an exact observation. The stock alternate-sense hook adds no detection by default.

## Formulas and parameters

Stock sight uses a full cone of 180 degrees. The target must fall within the cone's 90-degree half-angle and within the effective sight range. Use the DFU sight range of `4096 × MeshReader.GlobalScale` and hearing range of 25 Unity units as native-scale references, with enemy-specific modifiers. The source uses strict angular and distance boundaries and caps its sight ray at the unmodified base radius. Test occlusion to the eligible target point within effective range so the configured modifier affects the usable range. Exact Godot distances, sample cadence and hearing uncertainty bounds remain tuning work.

## Required data, inputs, outputs and events

Inputs include stable observer and target IDs, gameplay scope, candidate eligibility, observer pose and eye point, target sight point, sense profile, current contact and concealment state, and sight and acoustic blockers. Outputs distinguish visual observation, approximate hearing observation, alternate-sense observation and no current contact. Carry source sense, scope, observation time and position precision. A door obstruction may be reported to the movement owner.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- A target behind a closed ordinary door can be heard only if the observer already has established contact and the rest of the hearing check passes. Solid walls block hearing. A target crossing behind cover ends the current visual observation.
- Presence-only information cannot supply a pursuit position by itself. Approximate information cannot overwrite the last exact visual observation as though the target were seen.
- An illusion counter removes the applicable illusion barrier while geometry and ordinary sense requirements still apply. Retaliation clues follow NPC-008 and do not grant sight.
- Invalid actor references, unavailable geometry and failed sense providers cannot publish a fresh observation. Pursuit memory and expiration remain with NPC-011.

## Configuration and balancing

Support per-enemy range and cone tuning and validated rules for occluders, hearing precision and alternate sensors. Mods may explicitly enable noise-based acquisition. Exact noise emitters and sound propagation for modded sensing remain extension design work.

## Save/load considerations

Recompute live observations after actors and geometry are ready. Restore supported retained contact and memory through NPC-011, preserving their original source and precision. A restored position is historical until a fresh sensory check accepts it.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Keep geometry tests, concealment results and accepted observations distinct. Scope observations to current actor identities. Rendering settings and query frequency cannot change authoritative concealment odds under MAGIC-025. Concrete collision masks, sample points and sense update cadence remain integration choices.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A target inside the configured cone and range can be seen when the view is unobstructed and applicable concealment allows it. A solid wall or closed solid door blocks sight.
2. An observer with established contact can hear a target after losing sight within hearing range and through an ordinary door. A solid wall blocks that result. Hearing gives an approximate location and preserves the previous exact sight observation as history.
3. An undetected target cannot start ordinary hearing contact by making noise under the stock policy. A supported mod can enable noise-based acquisition or declare a new sense and its precision.
4. An illusion counter still requires ordinary geometry and sense eligibility. Approximate and presence-only observations do not authorize attacks that require visual contact.
5. Restore retains supported historical information with its original precision and rebuilds current sensory state.
