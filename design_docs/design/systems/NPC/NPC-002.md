# NPC-002 — Civilian identity generation

Approval status **Approved — finalized v1**

The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-11 with “I approve”. See NPC-002-A01 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-002-interview-decisions).

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

Give outdoor pedestrians names, appearances and roles appropriate to the settlement. A person remains recognizable while present and after a save and reload. A replacement pedestrian is a new person, even when the game reuses the same scene object or generates the same name.

## Confirmed requirements

See NPC-002-D01–D04 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-002-interview-decisions). Keep each present civilian's identity stable, generate fresh identities for replacements, and preserve durable references to the original actor. Use regional names and appearances and moddable population weights. The default city watch roll is 1 in 32, and stock generated watch members are male. Restore present pedestrians and their actor-specific state. Any pickpocket-attempt restriction tracks the actor, with its gameplay rules owned by the pickpocket feature.

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

In the local 1.1.1 snapshot, `Assets/Scripts/Game/Utility/PopulationManager.cs` (`Start`, `GetEntityRace`, `UpdateMobiles`) derives a race from local climate and invokes the stock randomizer or a custom generator on activation. `Assets/Scripts/Game/MobilePersonNPC.cs` (`RandomiseNPC`, `SetPerson`, `SetRace`) selects a male watch member in outfit variant 0 on a 1-in-32 roll. The ordinary branch rolls male or female evenly and samples four outfit variants. Portrait choice samples 24 face variants. Naming uses the region's name bank with a Breton default when no region is available. The stock randomizer leaves the object's pickpocket-attempt flag untouched. `Assets/Scripts/Game/TalkManager.cs` (`SetTargetNPC`) can reuse conversation state based on the component reference. These behaviors come from static source inspection.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

- For a fresh NPC-001 admission, resolve a validated population profile, select the civilian or watch role, and resolve a compatible ancestry, gender, outfit, world appearance, portrait and regional name. Publish the initialized person with a fresh actor ID. Use the settled name and appearance for subsequent interactions.
- A scene representation belongs to one current actor. Reassignment clears the previous actor's interaction state and callbacks. A stale dialogue or gameplay reference cannot resolve to the next occupant. Two civilians may share a displayed name and still be distinct actors.
- Ordinary retirement ends the actor's street presence. Preserve durable quest, legal, combat or other owner records under their original actor ID. Explicitly durable actors and their obligations follow their owning feature's lifecycle. Preserve a watch actor's identity through an approved guard representation conversion.
- Keep actor-specific state, including an attempted pickpocket where applicable, with the actor. An independently generated pedestrian starts with fresh state. Encountering more pedestrians may create more potential pickpocket targets. Attempt limits, reward rates and any broader anti-farming rules belong to the pickpocket feature.

## Formulas and balancing parameters

The default chance of a city watch role is `1/32` for each fresh eligible identity generated by the stock profile, with male-only selection for that role. This is a probability per generation, so the watch count in any particular crowd can vary. Restoring a saved actor uses the recorded role. Mods may change role probability and ancestry, gender and appearance weights. The ordinary gender ratio and exact outfit and portrait pools are content tuning details.

## Required data, inputs, outputs and events

- **Inputs and data** include a stable settlement and region context, validated profile and provider IDs, supported ancestry/gender/role and appearance combinations, compatible name-bank mapping, actor ID allocation, and optional saved identity and owner state.
- **Outputs** include a settled actor ID, display name, ancestry, gender, role, world appearance and portrait references, and initialized actor availability. Retirement or representation conversion identifies the same actor for downstream systems. A restored actor follows a restore path with no new generation or replay of interaction outcomes.

## Cross-system interactions

NPC-001 owns admissions, retirement timing and death vacancies. DATA-017 owns validated name banks and mappings. DATA/MOD govern profile registration and content validation. Dialogue reads the current actor and its settled presentation. AV resolves world and portrait assets. COMBAT-018 owns civilian outcomes and guard conversion requirements. LAW, FACT and QUEST retain actor-scoped facts or obligations. CORE/SAVE coordinate reconstruction before gameplay resumes.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- A missing or incompatible profile, name bank or presentation mapping must resolve through a configured, validated fallback or defer publication. An incomplete actor cannot enter interaction. Fallback and migration policy is an integration detail.
- An interrupted generation cannot expose a partially initialized actor. A delayed callback from a retired actor cannot affect a replacement.
- A death, ordinary retirement or scene rebuild cannot transfer a person's interaction state to a replacement. Guard conversion preserves the actor link. A removed mod or missing saved appearance requires owner-supported compatibility handling while retaining the original actor ID and settled name.

## Save/load considerations

Persist the IDs, settled names, appearances, roles and relevant actor-specific interaction state of pedestrians present at save. Restore them under the same identities before filling available capacity with new pedestrians. Preserve NPC-001's death vacancies and leave retired pedestrians retired. Retain durable owner references to retired or dead actors. Saved names use the settled result under DATA-017, so changes to banks or generation weights apply to future assignments. Coordinate restore with NPC-001 and NPC-003 for street presence and placement.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Keep actor identity independent of displayed names, name seeds and scene objects. Use validated population profiles and publish identity only after initialization. The actor ID format, randomness implementation, persistence schema and presentation compatibility rules remain integration choices.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A fresh civilian uses the settlement profile and a compatible regional name and appearance. The stock role selector has a 1-in-32 watch branch with male-only watch selection. Supported mods can change the role and demographic rules.
2. Repeated interaction with the same civilian uses the same identity and presentation. A replacement using the same scene object gets a distinct actor ID and fresh state, even if their names match.
3. Reload restores present actors with the same names, appearances, roles and interaction state. Retired actors stay retired, and durable references and reserved death vacancies survive.
4. If pickpocket rules limit attempts, the same restored actor retains their attempted state and the new actor starts fresh.
5. Guard conversion preserves actor identity. Invalid content, interrupted initialization or stale interaction callbacks cannot publish an incomplete person or affect a replacement.

## Compatibility review and outstanding questions

The specification follows [NPC-001](NPC-001.md) fresh replacement and vacancy policy, [DATA-017](../DATA/DATA-017.md) name-bank and persistent assignment contracts, and [COMBAT-018](../COMBAT/COMBAT-018.md) durable death and guard identity rules. Restoring pedestrians already present preserves continuity under [MAGIC-029](../MAGIC/MAGIC-029.md) beast-form suppression of fresh admissions.

Open integration details include regional ancestry and appearance tables, compatible asset and naming fallback rules, exact ordinary demographic weights, actor ID allocation, and save and placement recovery.
