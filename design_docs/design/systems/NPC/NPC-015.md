# NPC-015 — Melee positioning and movement interruption

Approval status **Approved — finalized v1**

The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-11 with “I approve”. See NPC-015-A01 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-015-interview-decisions).

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

Enemies close to melee range, face their opponent, and can strafe or back away to manage spacing. Some swings allow movement, so the player must judge the enemy's approach and actual reach. Paralysis and knockback interrupt voluntary positioning and force the enemy to reassess.

## Confirmed requirements

NPC-015-D01–D03 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-015-interview-decisions) confirm Enhanced Combat AI on by default, moddable DFU-style moving swings, and distinct handling for pause, paralysis and knockback.

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

In the local 1.1.1 snapshot, `Assets/Scripts/Game/EnemyMotor.cs` uses classic close-and-face behavior without Enhanced Combat AI. Its enhanced behavior can approach, strafe, backstep or retreat. `StrafeDecision` rolls for strafing and chooses a side and interval. `EvaluateMoveInForAttack` uses relative level and conditions favoring advance. The motor reduces speed during enhanced one-shot animations and honors one-shot movement holds. `HandleParalysis` and `KnockbackMovement` control forced states independently of attack commitment.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

- Enhanced Combat AI is enabled by default. Use enhanced melee positioning when enabled and classic closing and facing when disabled. Respect NPC-011 prediction behavior under the setting and NPC-014's independently enabled door-bashing policy.
- Approach a reachable opponent, face them and seek usable melee spacing. Enhanced behavior can strafe, backstep when crowded, or retreat based on the DFU-style level-biased decision. The stock decision favors advancing when the target is unseen, paralyzed or facing away, or when the visible player is using a bow or has their weapon put away. Maneuvers obey NPC-013 clearance and footing.
- During admitted melee attacks, use the attack profile's voluntary movement hold or permitted reduced movement. An allowed step preserves the captured attack target and timing. Revalidate reach, facing and obstruction at commitment through COMBAT-016.
- Pause suspends the maneuver and its timers. On resume, revalidate its scope, target and next step. Paralysis discards the current voluntary maneuver and prevents voluntary movement. Knockback discards the voluntary maneuver and uses the authorized COMBAT-012 displacement. When movement is available again, choose a fresh maneuver from the current pose.
- Apply attack interruption separately under COMBAT-015. Paralysis cancels unfinished work and freezes its attack interval. Knockback blocks new attack starts and progresses the interval. An attack already underway continues if no separate interruption applies. Death ends voluntary action.
- Retain each settled tactical choice for its decision window, including through technical retry. Recheck short movement steps before execution. A blocked strafe or backstep waits or triggers reassessment at the applicable decision boundary.

## Formulas and parameters

- DFU strafe chance is `1/4` at a strafe decision, with an interval sampled from 1–2 active seconds and an even side choice. Reference strafe speed is one quarter of approach speed and backstep speed is one half.
- In the stock level-biased decision, `m = clamp(trunc((enemy_level − target_level)/2), −4, 4)`. Draw an integer from `m` through `m + 9`. Advance on a result greater than 4. For a retreat result `r`, the reference retreat distance multiplier is `2.25 × (1 + 0.25 × (2 − r))`. Evaluate the applicable forced-advance conditions first.
- Expose spacing bands, maneuver speeds and timing, and attack-profile movement multipliers. Exact Godot spacing, decision timing and animation integration remain tuning work.

## Required data, inputs, outputs and events

Inputs include actor and target IDs, accepted observation and pursuit intent, range and facing, level, relevant observed target state, movement profile, current geometry, Enhanced Combat AI setting, attack movement permission, and pause, paralysis or knockback state. Outputs include advance, face, hold, strafe, retreat, movement suspension or forced-movement handoff, with settled tactical choices scoped to the current actor and target.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- A side or backstep cannot cross a wall, unsafe drop or unsupported medium. When blocked, hold or reevaluate from safe space.
- A target switch, altered geometry or displaced actor invalidates any affected maneuver before movement. Existing attack intent follows its combat owner.
- If paralysis and knockback overlap, voluntary movement remains blocked. Forced displacement follows COMBAT-012 eligibility and approved effect handling.
- A resumed movement choice grants no extra combat action or timing draw. A missed moving swing follows ordinary combat commitment.

## Configuration and balancing

Support the Enhanced Combat AI setting and validated per-archetype policies for approach, strafe, retreat, reaction delays and movement during attacks. Preserve geometry and combat contracts. Faster decisions or retreat can make close combat harder, so test blocked passages and ledges alongside open rooms.

## Save/load considerations

Restore settings, actor placement, supported forced-state effects and pursuit facts through their owners. Rebuild voluntary maneuvers from restored current conditions. Keep COMBAT-015's load handling for unfinished attacks and retained intervals and COMBAT-012's knockback restore policy.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Keep tactical decisions, voluntary locomotion, forced displacement and attack commitments separate. Scope timing and decisions to eligible simulation and stable identities. Exact scheduler and profile interfaces remain integration choices.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Enhanced Combat AI starts enabled and gives eligible enemies enhanced positioning. Turning it off uses classic approach and facing. Mods can vary positioning by archetype.
2. Enhanced enemies can strafe and backstep with safe clearance. Relative level influences eligible advance or retreat decisions.
3. A stock attack that allows movement can advance at reduced speed. A hold profile stops voluntary movement. Both retain commitment-time geometry checks.
4. Pause suspends and revalidates the maneuver. Paralysis or knockback discards it. Once able to move, the enemy reassesses from its actual position.
5. Knockback and paralysis preserve their approved attack interruption and interval rules. Reload and callback retries cannot duplicate a committed outcome.
