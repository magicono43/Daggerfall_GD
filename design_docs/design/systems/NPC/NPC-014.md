# NPC-014 — Door opening and bashing

Approval status **Approved — finalized v1**

The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-11 with “I approve”. See NPC-014-A01 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-014-interview-decisions).

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

Doors can delay pursuit. Capable enemies open unlocked doors or deliberately try to force eligible locks while they have reason to pursue. Stock tall enemies can fit through ordinary low doorways using a temporary traversal adjustment. Mods can make a creature's size an obstacle to passage.

## Confirmed requirements

NPC-014-D01–D03 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-014-interview-decisions) confirm the DFU Enhanced Combat AI bash behavior as the default for stock eligible enemies, repeated attempts under melee cadence, and moddable automatic clearance adjustment.

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

In the local 1.1.1 snapshot, `Assets/Scripts/Game/EnemyMotor.cs#OpenDoors` uses the mobile's `CanOpenDoors` flag and opens a nearby remembered closed unlocked door. With Enhanced Combat AI, the same capability can set bashing during pursuit when the target is out of sight and the locked door is in melee range. `HandleBashing` uses a separate loop, and `Game/EnemyAttack.cs#MeleeDamage` can fall back to a door bash when the actor hit check fails. `EnemyMotor.HeightAdjust` temporarily shortens tall controllers after side collisions. Stock capability assignments come from `Assets/Scripts/Utility/EnemyBasics.cs`. The recreation follows approved COMBAT-016 for deliberate bash intent and cadence.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

- Use the stock DFU opening assignments and corresponding enhanced bash eligibility. Support independent opening and bashing capabilities. Enable the enhanced door-bashing policy by default for eligible enemies, independently of the Enhanced Combat AI prediction option.
- When an eligible nearby closed unlocked door blocks a useful route, a capable enemy requests opening through the door owner. Revalidate identity, reach, motion and lock state. An already open or moving door calls for waiting, passage or reevaluation under the door and movement rules.
- When pursuing a target that is out of sight and an eligible locked door blocks a useful route, a capable enemy can submit a deliberate bash against that door. Use COMBAT-015 and COMBAT-016 for opportunity, timing, interruption and commitment. A failed actor attack cannot become a door bash. Ordinary opening and bashing do not grant exterior scene-transition authority.
- Retry while pursuit and door eligibility remain and the shared cadence permits a new attempt. Default to no separate retry cap. If the door is ineligible, a modded retry limit is reached, or pursuit ends, use an eligible detour or stop. A failed attempt does not refresh pursuit, weaken the lock or reset the combat interval.
- Use DFU-style automatic temporary reduction for tall enemies when an obstruction requires extra height clearance. Apply the configured traversal shape only when it can pass through the opening. Restore normal height when expansion is clear. Mods can disable the adjustment or change the reduced profile per archetype, including large enemies that cannot fit.

## Formulas and parameters

Use approved COMBAT-006/016 bash rules. Eligible locked interior doors use raw chance `20 − lock value`, and expressly supported exterior doors use `25 − lock value`, with RULE-001 resolution and limits. COMBAT-015 owns the attempt interval. DFU reference opening distance is 2 Unity units and shortened height is 1.65 Unity units. Exact Godot equivalents and movement tolerances remain integration work.

## Required data, inputs, outputs and events

Inputs include enemy and door identity, pursuit and visibility, current pose and reach, route obstruction, opening and bashing capabilities, lock and magical hold, motion and collision, authored links and clearance profile. Outputs include authorized open request, explicit bash intent, door-owner result, route reevaluation and temporary traversal shape. Publish committed bash noise through the approved owner contract.

## Cross-system interactions

NPC-011 owns pursuit lifetime and NPC-013 owns routing and locomotion. MOVE-021 and LOC own door state, motion, links and occupancy. COMBAT-006/015/016 and RULE-001 govern bash resolution and cadence. MAGIC owns magical holds and relevant actor effects. LAW and NPC consume applicable noise or incident facts. CORE/SAVE and DATA/MOD own restore boundaries and validated capabilities.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- If a door opens or moves during bash preparation, skip at commitment while preserving the admitted attack interval. Never close it or substitute another target.
- A magically held or otherwise ineligible door follows the approved bash rules. A blocked doorway or unsafe resize keeps the actor in a safe pose and triggers route reevaluation.
- Use MOVE-021 occupancy rules when closing. Actor resizing or opening cannot push, crush or teleport an occupant.
- Several enemies can encounter one door. Revalidate every authorized operation against current door state. Repeated callbacks cannot repeat unlock, motion, linked actions or noise.

## Configuration and balancing

Expose stock and modded opening and bashing assignments, eligibility, optional retry limits and traversal profiles. Support modified size behavior and explicit exclusions. Preserve shared combat cadence, door state and occupancy unless an approved owner extension changes the applicable policy.

## Save/load considerations

Restore committed door state, locks and completed effects through door and combat owners. COMBAT-015 cancels unfinished enemy wind-up on load and retains the applicable interval and committed facts. Recheck route and clearance before moving or starting a new action. A remembered door or restored traversal pose does not authorize a fresh bash.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Keep door observation, route decisions, traversal shape, open requests and bash commitments distinct. Use stable door identity and authoritative geometry. Exact shape and projectile collision interactions during clearance adjustment need native fixtures.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Stock door-capable enemies open eligible unlocked doors. Stock bash-capable enemies can deliberately bash eligible locked doors during pursuit under the default enhanced door behavior. Mods can change the capabilities independently.
2. Bash retries use the approved combat cadence while pursuit and door eligibility remain. A missed actor strike never becomes a bash, and door changes before commitment are handled under COMBAT-016.
3. Stock tall enemies can use temporary clearance where the adjusted shape fits and recover their normal height when safe. A mod can make a large enemy remain blocked.
4. Occupancy rules hold during closing and resizing. Restore and repeated callbacks cannot duplicate committed door operations or bypass the saved attack interval.
