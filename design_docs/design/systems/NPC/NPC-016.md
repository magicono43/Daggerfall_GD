# NPC-016 — Ranged attacks and spell selection

Approval status **Approved — finalized v1**

The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-11 with “I approve”. See NPC-016-A01 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-016-interview-decisions).

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

Enemies choose attacks they can actually execute. Archers use finite ammunition, casters choose useful spells, and careful shooters hold fire when they see an ally in the way. A foe without a viable ranged attack can close, reposition or use another eligible action.

## Confirmed requirements

NPC-016-D01–D03 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-016-interview-decisions) confirm bow-first preference, spell usefulness checks, stock self-healing and self-buffing, and ally-aware aiming with Enhanced Combat AI lead.

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

In the local 1.1.1 snapshot, `Assets/Scripts/Game/EnemyMotor.cs#DoRangedAttack` checks ranged actions before touch spells and prioritizes the bow branch for bow-capable enemies. `CanCastRangedSpell` samples single-target or area-at-range spells and checks the selected spell against active effects and shot clearance. `CanCastTouchSpell` samples touch and caster-only spells in classic mode, or touch and caster-centered area spells in Enhanced Combat AI, where caster-only logic is a TODO. `EffectsAlreadyOnTarget` compares effects with active bundles. The source's bow readiness check tests capability and clearance. The recreation uses COMBAT-017 finite supply and checked release.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

- Evaluate one tactical opportunity at a time. Prefer an eligible bow with usable ammunition and a clear shot. Otherwise consider viable ranged spells. If neither route is viable, consider eligible close-range or self actions or reposition under NPC-013/015. A failed start roll settles that opportunity without trying another attack. Mods can replace the preference and fallback order.
- Filter offensive spells by delivery mode, actor capability, affordability, target or area applicability and expected useful effects under MAGIC. A spell with every useful effect already active and no meaningful approved refresh is skipped. A mixed spell may qualify if at least one effect offers a benefit. Choose uniformly among viable spells within the selected stock category.
- Stock AI can use authored touch, caster-centered area, ranged, and supported caster-only spells. Self-healing is eligible when the caster is injured and would recover health. Self-buffs are eligible during an active combat encounter when the caster lacks the applicable benefit. Consider self actions when no preferred ranged route is viable. Only use spells from the assigned loadout with supported AI purpose metadata. Exact priorities among eligible close-range and self actions remain tuning work.
- Check the intended shot path using the selected projectile's geometry. If a visible ally blocks the predicted path at selection, withhold that shot and consider another viable action. Recheck at release and apply normal cancellation if a visible ally blocks the shot then. Already released shots follow actual contact under COMBAT-017.
- Enhanced Combat AI can use bounded prediction from accepted observations under NPC-011. Otherwise aim at the current authorized target position. Retained pursuit memory alone cannot authorize default ranged fire. Check current geometry and intended actor at release. Reposition or hold when clearance is unavailable.
- Use COMBAT-017 start and release contracts. Use COMBAT-015 attack exclusivity, interruption and commitment. Close-range delivery and self-casts use the applicable COMBAT and MAGIC contracts. No tactical choice adds supplies, waives cost or bypasses an active attack.

## Formulas and parameters

COMBAT-017 stock ranged start uses distance strictly greater than 6 and less than 51.2 reference units, and horizontal facing within 22.5 degrees. Eligible opportunities use exact bow odds of 1/32 or ranged spell odds of 1/40 per 62.5 ms of eligible simulation. Keep those draws separate from tactical choice. Use configured touch, area and self eligibility with the owning delivery rules. Exact Godot geometry, lead bounds and starting ammunition by archetype remain integration details.

## Required data, inputs, outputs and events

Inputs include actor and target identity, observations, mode and capabilities, range and facing, shot geometry, visible ally positions, weapon and ammunition, assigned spells, cost and active effect queries, active attack and movement state, and current tactic profile. Output one eligible intent or a decision to wait, close or reposition. Admission and release publish their own retained results. Queries do not consume ammo, magicka or random draws.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- Empty ammo, insufficient magicka, unavailable spells or blocked paths can make another action viable at selection. Later source loss follows the retained action's cancellation rule.
- A rejected start or canceled release cannot substitute another target or action inside the operation. A fresh opportunity can choose another route.
- A hidden ally who is not known to block the shot can still be hit. Already released projectiles are independent. Area damage continues to use actual applicability and attribution.
- Missing profile data uses the owning system's recovery. A self-heal at full health or redundant buff cannot create a useful stock candidate solely because it is affordable.

## Configuration and balancing

Support validated preferences, ability and purpose classifications, spell utility thresholds, resource-empty fallback, lead and ally risk profiles. Keep finite stock supplies and approved release checks. Self-healing frequency and archetype ammo quantities affect encounter pacing and require content tuning.

## Save/load considerations

Restore the actor's inventory, magicka, assigned spells and active effects through their owners. Rebuild tactical eligibility from current observations. Apply COMBAT-015/017 rules for unfinished attacks, retained intervals and already released projectiles. Restore cannot duplicate a paid cast, consumed arrow or settled attack opportunity.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Keep tactical evaluation, selected intent, admission and delivery distinct. Retain selection and start outcomes for technical retry. Exact utility queries, actor profiles and path geometry remain integration choices.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A viable bow takes stock priority over a ranged spell. An empty or blocked bow can lead to a viable spell or another eligible action. A failed start roll produces no second attack attempt in that opportunity.
2. Spell selection checks cost and useful applicability. A wholly redundant spell is skipped. Stock AI can select eligible self-healing or self-buffing from its loadout.
3. A visible ally blocking the shot causes withholding. Enhanced lead uses accepted observations. Actual friendly contact after release follows COMBAT-017.
4. Attack exclusivity, finite arrows, costs and retained target validation hold through interruption and restore. Mods can vary supported tactics.
