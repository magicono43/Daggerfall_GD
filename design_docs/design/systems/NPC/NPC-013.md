# NPC-013 — Pursuit, detours and hazards

Approval status **Approved — finalized v1**

The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-11 with “I approve”. See NPC-013-A01 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-013-interview-decisions).

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

Enemies pursue through terrain suited to their movement abilities. Walls and ledges can slow ground enemies, while flyers can cross gaps and aquatic enemies can move vertically in water. A submerged floor remains usable by ground enemies. Breaking contact can end pursuit under NPC-011.

## Confirmed requirements

NPC-013-D01–D03 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-013-interview-decisions) confirm bounded local detours, extensible pathfinding, stock floor-walking underwater and no breath mechanic, and physical routing with normal hazard contact effects.

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

In the local 1.1.1 snapshot, `Assets/Scripts/Game/EnemyMotor.cs#GetDestination` uses a predicted target position, a detour point, or a search position derived from last-known observations. `AttemptMove`, `FindDetour`, `ObstacleCheck` and `FallCheck` test local obstruction and footing. Flying, levitating and aquatic movement bypasses the ground drop probe. `WaterMove` limits aquatic movement at the dungeon block's water level. These findings describe the inspected motor. The recreation's no-breath baseline and underwater floor-walking policy are confirmed design decisions.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

- Consume the authorized destination from NPC-011 or the current eligible combat movement intent. Attempt direct movement when traversable. Otherwise search for a bounded local detour. If none is available, wait and retry from reachable space. Stop pursuing when NPC-011 ends pursuit or NPC-008 invalidates the target. A detour or retry does not refresh contact.
- Ground enemies use supported floors, slopes and steps and avoid unsafe voluntary drops. Nonaquatic ground enemies can continue walking on traversable submerged floors, including slopes and steps. They cannot swim upward through open water or cross gaps by swimming. If the floor ends at an unsafe drop, use ordinary ground recovery. Apply the same ground clearance and support rules on either side of a shoreline.
- Aquatic enemies can move through suitable water, including vertically, and remain within supported water volumes by default. Flying or levitating enemies can use supported open space and cross gaps with body clearance. An active locomotion mode supplies the applicable geometry checks. Default enemies do not track breath or suffer breath depletion. Explicit mods can authorize amphibious movement, vertical swimming for other enemies, wall climbing, breath and associated effects, and other supported abilities.
- Base routing evaluates physical traversability and unsafe drops. Traversable damaging terrain can be entered. Apply hazards through their owning systems on actual contact, subject to normal protections and immunities. Mods can use recognized hazards, risk tolerance and applicable immunities to choose routes.
- Route planning uses authorized destinations and currently available geometry. Mods can provide more complex routes within the actor's capabilities and scope. Revalidate route segments at execution, including after geometry or locomotion changes. Door opening and bashing remain under NPC-014 and the approved combat and door rules.

## Formulas and parameters

No new damage or breath formula is selected. Configurable parameters include movement capability, supported surfaces and media, body clearance, slope and step limits, unsafe-drop threshold, detour search bounds and retry cadence. Exact Godot values remain tuning work. Pursuit timeout is owned by NPC-011.

## Required data, inputs, outputs and events

Inputs include actor and scope IDs, authorized destination and its precision, locomotion capabilities and active effects, current pose and body shape, loaded collision and navigation data, water boundaries, blockers and supported hazards. Outputs include movement intent, detour or wait status, blocked-route and door observations, and actual contact for hazard owners. A route query creates no contact effect.

## Cross-system interactions

NPC-008 and NPC-011 supply target eligibility, destination and expiry. NPC-014 handles doors. NPC-007 supplies compatible initial placement. LOC/WORLD own geometry and water context, MOVE owns shared movement and transition contracts, and MAGIC supplies levitation, immobilization and other effects. COMBAT owns attack authorization and movement commitments. Hazard owners resolve effects. CORE/SAVE and DATA/MOD govern simulation, restoration and validation.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- A clue across a wall, over a ledge or above a submerged ground enemy does not permit invalid movement. Wait, detour or end pursuit under the applicable owner rule.
- A door moving, geometry unloading or a locomotion effect expiring can invalidate the next segment. Stop safely and reevaluate. Restore or transition using the applicable movement owner's placement recovery if the current pose becomes unsupported.
- Crowded passages and repeated blocked probes use bounded work and retries. A failed route cannot grant fresh awareness, teleport the enemy, or make a failed attack become a door action.
- An underwater enemy without a breath profile receives no breath depletion. A modded breath profile uses explicit medium and effect eligibility, including restore and transitions.

## Configuration and balancing

Support archetype movement profiles and validated extensions for navigation, land and water transitions, climbing, optional breath and hazard-aware routing. Keep geometry and scope constraints applicable to richer pathfinding. Broader routing can make enemies harder to evade and increase planning cost.

## Save/load considerations

Preserve actor identity, usable world placement, owner-required effects and NPC-011 pursuit memory. Rebuild transient routes against ready geometry. Restore supported modded breath or traversal state through its owner. Loading grants no new target observation or hazard contact.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Keep pursuit intent, route planning, locomotion and hazard resolution distinct. Scope path requests and results to the current actor and world. Exact navigation representation, extension interfaces and physical thresholds remain integration choices.

## Acceptance criteria

1. An enemy approaches a reachable target directly and attempts a bounded detour around a blocker. If blocked, it waits and retries from reachable space. It stops at applicable pursuit expiry.
2. A stock ground enemy walks along a traversable submerged floor without breath depletion or vertical swimming. An aquatic enemy swims vertically within water and stays in that medium. Flying or levitating enemies use clear air routes.
3. Ground enemies avoid unsafe drops. Hazardous traversable ground can be entered under stock routing, with effects resolved from contact.
4. Mods can supply more complex pathfinding and supported amphibious movement, swimming, climbing, breath and hazard awareness. Execution still validates the applicable movement constraints.
5. A stale path or a newly blocked segment cannot move an enemy through invalid space. Reload preserves identity and pursuit state and rebuilds navigation against current geometry.

## Compatibility review and outstanding questions

The specification follows [NPC-011](NPC-011.md) pursuit expiry and observation precision, [NPC-007](NPC-007.md) movement-compatible placement, and [COMBAT-016](../COMBAT/COMBAT-016.md) deliberate door intent. NPC-014 retains door opening and bashing policy.

Exact Godot geometry thresholds, movement transitions, bounded retry cadence, hazard metadata and extension interfaces remain open integration details.
