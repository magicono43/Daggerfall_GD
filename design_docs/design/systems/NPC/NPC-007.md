# NPC-007 — Foe spawn placement and activation

Approval status **Approved — finalized v1**

The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-11 with “I approve”. See NPC-007-A01 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-007-interview-decisions).

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

Bring selected foes into the world at plausible, usable positions. Ambient enemies usually arrive unseen. Scripted encounters preserve their intended location. A few avoided ambient foes can remain in the area, with further ambient checks suspended when the area reaches capacity.

## Confirmed requirements

NPC-007-D01–D03 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-007-interview-decisions) confirm safe, normally unseen ambient placement within configurable distances, a rest visibility exception, authored markers with explicitly bounded fallback, and a moddable cap of three living ambient foes in the encounter area.

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

In the local 1.1.1 snapshot, `Assets/Scripts/Game/Utility/FoeSpawner.cs#PlaceFoeFreely` probes a direction near the player, searches downward for a floor and uses an overlap sphere for clearance. Its optional view check biases the probe by camera angle. `FinalizeFoe` aligns a ground foe or raises a flying foe and enables it. An unsuccessful probe leaves the foe pending for a later update. `Update` can raise an encounter event while work remains pending. `Game/Entities/PlayerEntity.cs#IntermittentEnemySpawn` uses minimum distances of 8 Unity units for dungeon requests and 10 for outdoor ambient requests. These values are reference measurements.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

- For an ambient request, test candidates within the configured minimum and maximum distance for its context, within the current encounter area and ready world geometry. Require clearance for the final foe shape, separation from occupants, and suitable support or movement space for its locomotion type. Reject candidates inside the player's view unless solid cover conceals the foe.
- While the player remains in the resting presentation, relax the view restriction. Continue to enforce distance, clearance, area and movement-space rules. Recheck visibility if that presentation ends before a placement otherwise eligible to continue commits.
- For scripted placement, try the authored marker first. Apply the script's declared placement and visibility policy. A fallback search requires explicit permission and must stay within the configured room or designated area. If no valid point exists, report failure to the script owner for retry or recovery.
- Use NPC-006's one pending ambient request by default and retain the selected foe through bounded placement retries. Cancel stale requests and close exhausted ones. Living ambient foes count toward the separate local cap even offscreen. Authored and quest foes use their own limits and still participate in physical clearance.
- Before activation, recheck scope, candidate clearance, actor readiness and capacity. Commit placement and actor registration once, then publish the applicable actor availability and encounter activation under CORE-020. Placement pending or failure leaves rest eligibility to CORE-019 and NPC-006.

## Formulas and parameters

Default ambient cap is three living ambient foes per encounter area. New ambient checks require the living count to be below that cap and space in the NPC-006 pending budget. A placement requires the final candidate distance to fall within its configured context band. Godot distances, distance measurement and candidate retry thresholds require tuning.

## Required data, inputs, outputs and events

Inputs include request, actor, session and encounter-area IDs, selected archetype, movement and collision shape, ready geometry, player view and rest presentation, local living and pending counts, and authored marker and fallback policy where applicable. Outcomes include committed placement, temporary blockage, exhausted search, invalid data, and stale or cancelled requests. Committed actor availability and encounter activation carry the scope and creation origin required by CORE-020.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- A candidate can become blocked or visible between selection and activation. Recheck the final pose and retry within the original request budget.
- In a tight room or unavailable stream region, preserve distance and clearance rules. Use authorized fallback or report failure. Ground and flying foes must pass checks appropriate to their final poses.
- A stale callback, repeated commit or restored request cannot publish a second actor. A pending request contributes to pending capacity until it commits or closes.
- Count offscreen or streamed-out foes while they remain living residents of the area. Committed death or an owner-authorized end to residency frees capacity. Lowering a cap suspends new checks until capacity is available.

## Configuration and balancing

Expose area or context caps, distance bands, visibility rules including the rest exception, and supported retry budgets and scripted fallback bounds. Validate ranges and profiles. Exact area boundaries, spacing and retry cadence are integration work.

## Save/load considerations

Rebuild counts from restored living ambient actor residency before admitting new requests. Restore settled placement and actor identity. Restore supported pending requests with the same selected foe and remaining budget, or close them under NPC-006 recovery policy. Restoration publishes availability with restore origin and does not replay fresh encounter activation or rest interruption.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Keep spawn requests and candidates separate from committed actors. Scope placement and registration to the current area and session. Validate the final physical pose before publication. Candidate sampling and registry mechanics remain implementation choices.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Ambient foes appear within the configured band at clear, usable positions and out of view or behind solid cover. Rest placement can use a visible direction during the resting presentation while retaining physical checks.
2. Scripted requests use valid markers first, search only within explicitly authorized fallback bounds, and report failure if no valid pose exists.
3. Three living ambient foes in the same encounter area suspend further ambient checks, including when foes are offscreen. A mod can change the cap. Quest and authored foes have separate limits.
4. A blocked ambient request retries the same foe within budget. Exhaustion and stale requests produce no activation. A successful request publishes the actor and applicable encounter activation once.
5. Restore reconstructs counts and actor availability without duplicate foes or a fresh activation event.
