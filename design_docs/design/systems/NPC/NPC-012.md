# NPC-012 — Language pacification

Approval status **Approved — finalized v1**

The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-11 with “I approve”. See NPC-012-A01 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-012-interview-decisions).

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

Let language proficiency defuse a hostile encounter automatically. A successful check makes the enemy leave the player and their allies alone. The player receives success feedback and can pass peacefully unless a qualifying hostile action or explicit owner event ends the protection.

## Confirmed requirements

NPC-012-D01–D03 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#npc-012-interview-decisions) confirm player and ally scope, resolution before first attack or alert, and moddable success feedback. [MAGIC-026](../MAGIC/MAGIC-026.md) already establishes automatic first detection, quest exclusions, the formula inputs and practice awards.

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

In the local 1.1.1 snapshot, `Assets/Scripts/Game/EnemySenses.cs#FixedUpdate` records first detection and automatically attempts pacification for nonquest monsters and class enemies with a mapped skill. Success clears the hostility flag, displays a localized message and tallies three skill uses. Failure tallies one except for Etiquette and Streetwise. `GetTargets` uses nonhostility in both directions between a pacified enemy and player allies. `Assets/Scripts/Game/Entities/EnemyEntity.cs#GetLanguageSkill` supplies the mapping and `Game/Formulas/FormulaHelper.cs#CalculateEnemyPacification` resolves the roll.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

- On the enemy's first accepted detection of the player, automatically evaluate the encounter once. Require an eligible hostile monster or class enemy with a declared language mapping. Quest-controlled enemies are excluded unless explicitly permitted by their quest. The first detection is recorded even when eligibility prevents the roll. Revisit, rediscovery, reload and later spell or skill changes do not open another default attempt.
- Capture live mapped skill, Personality, weapon state and applicable Comprehend Languages bonus. Settle the approved RULE-019 roll once and retain the outcome. Resolve the attempt before authorizing a new attack against the player or committing that observer's initial NPC-011 alert. A success prevents its initial contribution. Other observers' alert contributions remain intact.
- On success, establish lasting language nonhostility covering the player and eligible player allies. Preserve the enemy's team and unrelated combat relationships. Use current explicit ally membership so a mod-added companion can participate. Apply reciprocal proactive targeting restrictions between protected allies and this enemy.
- A qualifying hostile action from the player ends this language protection. A qualifying hostile action from an ally lifts language protection for that ally only, allowing local retaliation under NPC-008. Apply MAGIC-026's established qualifying break predicates, including accepted hostile effects and qualifying hits even if Shield prevents health loss. Preserve independent magical pacification and its separate scope. Explicit quest or NPC overrides need declared precedence.
- Revalidate pending attacks under COMBAT and MAGIC-026 when protection takes effect. Released attacks and committed outcomes retain their approved delivery and recovery behavior.
- Report three uses of the mapped skill on a committed success. On a failed roll report one use for a mapped creature language and zero for Etiquette or Streetwise. Send practice once through CHAR-012. Default feedback is a localized success message naming the enemy and skill when identifiable. Use generic feedback when identity would disclose a hidden actor. Failure is silent by default. Allow supported mods to suppress or replace the feedback.

## Formulas and parameters

Under RULE-019, raw score is `trunc(S/10) + trunc(P/5) + W + E` for Etiquette or Streetwise and `S + trunc(P/10) + W + E` for other mapped languages. `W` is +10 sheathed or −25 drawn, and `E` is the approved Comprehend Languages contribution. Clamp to 0–200 and pass if one uniform integer draw from 0–199 is less than the clamped score. Stock protection has no timed expiry.

## Required data, inputs, outputs and events

Inputs include stable player and enemy IDs, first accepted detection and scope, actor and quest eligibility, language mapping, captured formula inputs, ally relationships, committed hostile outcomes, and feedback policy. Outputs include eligibility and settled attempt result, language relationship change, affected attack and alert admission, practice report and optional presentation. A query or repeated event cannot authorize a new attempt.

## Cross-system interactions

NPC-008 owns eligibility and retaliation, NPC-009/010 provide accepted detection, and NPC-011 owns first-encounter memory and dungeon alert. RULE-019 owns the formula and RULE-003 owns draw retention. MAGIC-026 owns Comprehend Languages and magical protection. CHAR-012 accepts practice. QUEST supplies actor exceptions, COMBAT handles pending and released attacks, and UI presents feedback. CORE/SAVE and DATA/MOD own readiness, restoration and validation.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- A missing language mapping gives no stock attempt. A malformed required mapping or formula input uses explicit owner recovery.
- Pacification does not confer faction membership or follower control. An unavailable ally cannot receive or redirect an attack through a stale reference.
- A miss, fully resisted effect or incidental environmental outcome does not break protection by default. An ally's hostile action leaves the player's protection in effect.
- Feedback delivery failure cannot undo pacification or repeat the roll or practice. Handle partial commitment through retained outcome and owner receipts.

## Configuration and balancing

Support validated overrides for attempt eligibility and timing, quest exceptions, relationship scope and breaks, approved formula and practice policies, and feedback mode. Ally support consumes explicit relationships so a companion mod can use the feature without altering the base encounter flow.

## Save/load considerations

Persist the first encounter and attempt status, settled outcome, active language protection and applicable ally exceptions, and practice completion through stable actor IDs. Restore before new attack or alert eligibility is evaluated. Restore cannot replay feedback, award practice, or manufacture a new attempt.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Keep language and magical protection provenance explicit, separate from team membership. Use a checked resolution boundary for the first detection, attack admission and initial alert contribution. Schemas, owner interfaces and exact override precedence remain integration work.

## Acceptance criteria

1. An eligible first detection rolls once with RULE-019 inputs. Quest exclusions and later detection, spell changes or reload produce no automatic retry.
2. Success protects the player and eligible allies, prevents this observer's initial alert, and leaves unrelated combat and other alert contributions intact.
3. Qualifying player hostility ends language protection. Qualifying ally hostility lifts protection for that ally alone. Independent magical protection retains its own rules.
4. Practice follows 3 on success, 1 on eligible creature-language failure, and 0 on Etiquette or Streetwise failure. Replay cannot duplicate it.
5. Success feedback respects player knowledge and can be disabled or replaced by mods. Save/load preserves outcomes and scoped relationships.

## Compatibility review and outstanding questions

The specification preserves the first detection and practice rules in [MAGIC-026](../MAGIC/MAGIC-026.md) and the [RULE-019](../RULE/RULE-019.md) formula. The ally-inclusive scope applies to language success. Magical pacification retains its own caster-scoped default. [NPC-008](NPC-008.md) and [NPC-011](NPC-011.md) continue to own targeting and alert.

Exact ally classification, quest override precedence, presentation eligibility and persistence schema remain open integration details.
