# LAW-005 — Violence against civilians and guards

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-13 with “I approve”. See LAW-005-A01 and LAW-005-D01 through D03 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md).

## Gameplay purpose and intended experience

Make attacks on civilians and guards carry immediate legal risk while preserving a right to defend against an ongoing unlawful threat. The player can incur Assault without dealing damage. Murder depends on an actual death and its cause.

## Daggerfall Unity reference

In local DFU 1.1.1, `Assets/Scripts/Game/WeaponManager.cs#WeaponDamage` assigns Murder and requests guards after eligible ordinary mobile civilian contact. Peaceful guard contact assigns Assault and replaces the mobile representation before ordinary damage processing. `Game/Entities/DaggerfallEntityBehaviour.cs#HandleAttackFromSource` uses player source and victim state for additional Assault and Murder branches, including Murder for a dead city watch combatant. These observations come from static inspection.

## Confirmed rules and parameters

- Unauthorized hostile contact against a legally protected civilian or guard can qualify as Assault even if the subsequent hit resolves to zero damage. A swing that reaches nobody or a benign spell cannot qualify. COMBAT-018 supplies contact, injury and death facts separately and governs the ordinary civilian contact-kill shortcut and guard activation.
- Murder requires an authoritative confirmed unlawful death attributable to the player. Attributable harmful magic and poison can qualify. A blocked or prevented death supplies no Murder fact. Preserve source attribution through delayed effects and source absence. FACT-012 supplies the existing stock weights of five for a qualifying civilian murder and one for a qualifying city watch kill, once per deed regardless of detection or conviction.
- Stock qualifying violence is immediately detected where local law applies. No stock witness roll is required. Validated regional or modded profiles can change detection and guard response. LAW-001 applies filing, jurisdiction and suppression. MAGIC-029 preserves truthful incident facts and existing charges during ordinary beast-form charge suppression.
- Stock self-defense applies while the victim who initiated unlawful aggression still poses a threat. Lawful guard pursuit and retaliation provoked by the player do not qualify. Regional and modded policies can vary the exemption.

## Stock charge selection, guard response and self-defense timing

- Classify a single action that immediately kills as Murder when it qualifies. If an earlier action already established Assault and a later attributable death qualifies as Murder, retain both charge records under LAW-001. Repeated reporting of the same effect or death cannot create fresh charges. Stock court selects the most serious eligible charge. Mods can change classification or court selection.
- Request immediate guards for detected stock Assault or Murder when LAW authorizes deployment. Guard unavailability preserves the filed charge. NPC owns guard placement and scoped hostility. Conversion of an existing guard supplies no separate enforcement authorization.
- Evaluate self-defense at the player action that caused the outcome. Retain that context for delayed damage attributable to that action, including poison. A separately initiated attack after the threat ends gets a fresh evaluation. Surrender, withdrawal and renewed aggression require reliable NPC facts under the applicable policy.

## Required data, inputs and outputs

Preserve stable incident, operation, player, victim and cause identities, offense jurisdiction, commitment time, victim legal class, hostile contact, injury, prevention and confirmed death, aggression and threat context, harmful effect attribution, and detection and filing results. Inputs come from COMBAT, CHAR, NPC, MAGIC, QUEST and regional law. LAW returns classification, suppression or filing outcome and authorized enforcement requests. FACT and QUEST consume qualifying committed deeds and outcomes through their own rules. Concrete APIs remain open.

## Edge cases, configuration and persistence

- A miss that still makes valid hostile weapon contact with a peaceful guard can qualify as Assault. Mere broad-phase overlap or guard representation change does not establish contact. An unrelated actor, benign effect, or blocked death cannot fabricate a player murder. Quest protections and local legal protection are evaluated through their respective owners.
- Deduplicate harm, death, filing, deployment and deed handoffs by stable identity. Preserve completed outcomes and unfinished required handoffs at supported save points. Restore without new detection, legal effects or recruitment credit. NPC placement failure and optional presentation failures preserve authoritative violence outcomes. Target Godot 4.7.2. Schema, policy precedence, migration and recovery fixtures remain integration work.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Unauthorized hostile contact against a protected guard can file Assault at zero damage. A whiff and benign spell cannot. Guard identity survives combat activation.
2. An eligible civilian contact-kill can file Murder after confirmed death. Prevented death cannot. An attributable delayed poison death can qualify once. Verify a prior settled Assault remains recorded when a later outcome files Murder.
3. Immediate stock detection works without witnesses. An authorized response can request guards once. A region or mod can apply a different detection or response policy.
4. Defense against an ongoing unlawful aggressor qualifies. Lawful pursuit, provoked retaliation and a new attack after the threat ends do not qualify. Verify that delayed harm from a justified action retains the original context.
5. Suppression preserves truthful incidents and existing charges. Retrying or restoring cannot replay damage, death, charges, guard requests or guild credit.
