# LAW-016 — Legal state across transitions and saves

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-13 with “I approve”. See LAW-016-A01 and LAW-016-D01 through D03 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md).

## Gameplay purpose and intended experience

Preserve legal consequences and active pursuit across supported saves and travel. Give the player a clear reason when court temporarily blocks saving. Restore the same enforcement state without granting a fresh surrender offer or adding a guard wave.

## Daggerfall Unity reference

In local DFU 1.1.1, `Assets/Scripts/Game/Serialization/SerializablePlayer.cs` stores one charge, the surrender dialogue flag and regional records, and handles older uninitialized regional data. `Game/Utility/StartGameBehaviour.cs#StartFromClassicSave` separately imports regional and charge data. `Game/Entities/PlayerEntity.cs` clears the current charge on location exit and after fast travel. The inspected native player serializer contains no resumable court or prison session. These are static source observations. CORE-013 limits the recreation's scope to its own native Godot saves.

## Confirmed stock rules

1. Block manual saves, quicksaves and autosaves during unresolved surrender, forced evaluation and arrest handoff, and throughout court, prison and release. Keep the restriction until safe release and coherent control handoff. Provide a clear reason when a save is denied. Normal eligibility then resumes under the other save owners' rules.
2. At eligible saves, persist unresolved charges, legal history and filing order, regional standing, enforcement authority, warrants, banishment, and retained progress required by the legal feature owners. Preserve the active episode identity, ordinary-offer suppression, live responders through NPC, and valid pending civilian dispatch with its remaining active delay. Exiting the response area can cancel local dispatch under LAW-008. Escape and travel preserve unresolved charges in their filing jurisdiction under LAW-001.
3. Restore and validate against the current session, area and applicable authority. A valid pending dispatch resumes at its normal trigger using its remaining active seconds. Time outside the game does not consume the delay. Restore preserves existing responders and refusal state. Loading alone does not file charges, replay effects or authorize a new wave.
4. Require a validated migration when missing definitions affect enforceable legal state. If recovery is unavailable, identify the missing record or definition and preserve the current session. Closed historical records may be retained as inactive history with explicit notice. Inactive records carry no enforcement authority.

## Save admission, migration and restore

- Apply save admission to the captured game state, across all save entry points. A denied save leaves existing saves intact and does not queue an automatic retry. An autosave explicitly requested by a later eligible event follows the usual save flow.
- Preflight legal records and cross-owner links before replacing the session. Preserve stable identities, filing order, dispositions, applicable policy versions and recorded consequences during migration. An outstanding obligation or required unfinished effect needs enforceable-state handling even if its charge or case is marked closed.
- After validation, restore legal facts before enabling pursuit, input or gameplay notifications. Reconcile NPC responders by saved identity and cancel an obsolete dispatch under its owner rules. Resume only unfinished work through existing owner triggers. An invalid required responder link uses explicit recovery.

## Required data, inputs and outputs

Inputs include save or load request, current LAW phase and scope, travel and placement facts, save version and active content definitions. Required data includes stable character, jurisdiction, charge, case, warrant, restriction, episode, responder and dispatch IDs, filing order and dispositions, policy identities and versions, deadline or remaining active delay, and retained effect progress where required. Outputs include save admission or clear denial, validated restored state, migration or history notice, and load rejection or explicit recovery. No new court checkpoint timers or probability parameters are introduced.

LAW owns legal facts and save eligibility reasons. SAVE owns durable snapshots and migration. CORE preflights and replaces the session. NPC restores responders. TIME supplies clock progress and MOVE/WORLD supply current scope. COMBAT-019 preserves pending-hit behavior during unresolved arrest. Target Godot 4.7.2. Court outcome and history extensions use validated policies and native schema migration under DATA/MOD and SAVE.

## Edge cases and acceptance criteria

1. Manual save, quicksave and autosave are blocked during surrender, court, prison and incomplete release. A declined save preserves older saves. Safe release restores ordinary eligibility. A technical release failure retains the restriction.
2. Save during eligible active enforcement and restore the same responders, refusal state and remaining delay. Verify no fresh draw, prompt, charge or deployment. After leaving the response area, a cancelled dispatch stays cancelled while durable authority persists.
3. Cross a regional boundary and complete fast travel. Preserve unresolved charges in their filing jurisdiction. Apply destination eligibility under LAW-008. Restore without replaying normalization, default, payment, time advancement, healing or charge disposition.
4. Load a save with a missing required offense, jurisdiction or policy. An approved migration preserves enforcement and history. Without one, reject before replacing the current session. A closed record without outstanding effects can remain visible as inactive history with notice.
