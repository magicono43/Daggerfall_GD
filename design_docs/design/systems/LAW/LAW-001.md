# LAW-001 — Current charge and suppression

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-13 with “I approve”. See LAW-001-A01 and LAW-001-D01 through D07 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md).

## Gameplay purpose and player experience

Track accusations so the player can escape immediate pursuit while remaining answerable to the jurisdiction that filed them. Stock court focuses on the most serious eligible charge. A lesser offense cannot displace a more serious one. Regional and modded legal systems can handle the same set of charges differently.

## Daggerfall Unity reference

In the local DFU 1.1.1 snapshot, `Assets/Scripts/Game/Entities/PlayerEntity.cs#SetCrimeCommitted` replaces a single enum value, stores `None` during racial suppression and calls `OnCrimeUpdate` outside save loading. Periodic pursuit assigns Criminal Conspiracy directly. Location exit and completed fast travel clear the slot. `Game/Questing/Actions/SetPlayerCrime.cs` writes through the setter. `Game/UserInterfaceWindows/DaggerfallCourtWindow.cs` reads the slot for trial and clears it on prison release. These observations come from static inspection.

## Confirmed gameplay rules

1. Track multiple charges. At successful arrest commitment, select the most serious eligible charge for stock court and lock the selected charge into the case. An eligible charge filed before commitment can affect selection. Later charges cannot alter the committed selection under stock rules.
2. Rank eligible charges by explicit offense severity. Use the earliest filed charge on a tie. Regional profiles can override the ranking. Severity is independent of the size of any standing penalty.
3. On case resolution, close the other eligible charges from the same enforcement episode and jurisdiction and retain their history. Charges from other episodes or jurisdictions remain open. Mods can change which charges are heard and how remaining charges are disposed of.
4. Escape and travel can end immediate pursuit. Unresolved charges remain in the filing jurisdiction and can expose the player to enforcement on return. Mods can change this policy.
5. Beast actions ordinarily produce no new player charge. Preserve truthful incident records and existing charges under MAGIC-029. An existing warrant or banishment can authorize pursuit in beast form when the applicable policy establishes the player's identity. Regional or modded policies can vary this behavior. NPC threat reactions follow their own rules.
6. Scripted charges use ordinary LAW checks by default. Support explicit authored exceptions to charge suppression and moddable policy where possible. Regional and modded legal profiles can also change arrest and charge selection rules, including making surrender unavailable.

## Parameters and required data

The stock selection sorts eligible charges by descending configured severity and ascending filing order, then selects the first. The stock offense ranking and regional profile assignments remain content decisions. Configurable policies cover selection, disposition, travel retention, warrant recognition, surrender availability and scripted suppression exceptions.

The draft proposes stable charge, incident or request, jurisdiction, and enforcement episode identifiers. Keep the offense definition, filing order, disposition and source attribution. A committed case needs its jurisdiction, selected charges and the information required to determine which other charges it can close. Preserve actor, action and detection or recognition facts as applicable to the source. Exact data layout and event names remain open.

## Inputs, outputs and dependencies

- COMBAT and MOVE supply distinct action and outcome facts. ITEM supplies committed transfer, quantity and provenance facts. LAW decides which facts produce charges and which authorize enforcement.
- MAGIC supplies the relevant form and attribution. NPC supplies recognition or perception facts under the applicable rules and handles guard deployment when requested. Charge suppression and NPC threat reactions are separate outcomes.
- QUEST submits explicit scripted charge requests through LAW. The draft proposes returning the filing or suppression outcome so quest flow can handle it.
- LAW supplies enforcement eligibility to COMBAT-019. When arrest commits, LAW supplies the selected charges to court. The draft proposes publishing committed filing, arrest selection and disposition outcomes to observers. The surrender flow retains its existing deferred-hit and save rules.

## Edge cases, persistence and technical constraints

The following safeguards apply to v1.

- Use a stable filing sequence so charges filed at the same game time sort deterministically. A retry of the same filing operation should produce one filing. Distinct offense events may produce separate charges when eligible.
- A failed arrest or abandoned pursuit leaves unresolved charges available under the applicable policy. Case resolution closes eligible companion charges once. A charge outside the case's disposition scope stays open, including a later charge in the same jurisdiction when it falls outside that scope.
- Persist unresolved charges, retained legal history, filing order and any committed case state required at allowed save points. Restore state without filing charges again, replaying notifications or initiating pursuit solely because of load. Preserve COMBAT-019's restriction on ordinary saves during unresolved surrender or arrest handoff. Court save eligibility belongs to LAW-016.
- Validate custom policies and required offense or jurisdiction definitions. Surface an explicit recovery outcome when required saved definitions cannot be resolved. Preserve charge identity and disposition through recovery. Concrete migration and mod-removal behavior remains open.

## Acceptance criteria

1. File a serious charge and then a petty charge in one episode. Both are tracked and stock court selects the serious one. Equal-severity charges select the first filed, including after a permitted reload. A custom policy can select both.
2. Escape and travel away with unresolved charges. They remain filed in the original jurisdiction. Returning can expose the player to enforcement. A modded jurisdiction can use a different retention policy.
3. Resolve a stock case. Other eligible charges from the same episode close with history retained. Charges outside that scope remain open. A modded disposition can retain companion charges.
4. Transform with an unresolved charge and perform an ordinarily exempt beast action. Its incident remains available and the prior charge survives. Test recognized and unrecognized warrant pursuit separately. Verify ordinary scripted suppression and an authorized scripted exception.
5. File an eligible charge before successful arrest and confirm that it can affect selection. File a charge after commitment and verify the selected list remains stable. Verify a region with surrender disabled does not authorize a surrender offer.
6. Retry filing and case resolution and verify each commits once. Reload at an allowed save point and verify charge state and order without duplicate legal effects.
