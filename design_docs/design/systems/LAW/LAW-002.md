# LAW-002 — Forced entry and trespass

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-13 with “I approve”. See LAW-002-A01 and LAW-002-D01 through D05 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md).

## Gameplay purpose and player experience

Distinguish trying to defeat a lock, crossing after forcing it, and walking into a restricted space through an open route. A noisy bash can bring guards even if the door holds. Quieter stock methods have no random detection check, and authored restrictions can make particular places riskier.

## Daggerfall Unity reference

A failed pick returns after recording retry skill. Picks and Open have no equivalent bash roll in this path. `Internal/DaggerfallAction.cs#DoorText` can make enemies hostile at authored doors. ## Confirmed rules and parameters

| Committed action | Offense fact | Stock random detection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unauthorized bash against a lock, failed | Attempted breaking and entering | 10% at attempt resolution |
| Unauthorized bash against a lock, successful unlock | Attempted breaking and entering | No separate stock check at unlock |
| Unauthorized pick or Open used to defeat a lock | Attempted breaking and entering regardless of success | Disabled |
| Unauthorized crossing linked to a bash that defeated a lock | Breaking and entering | 10% at successful entry |
| Unauthorized crossing linked to a pick or Open that defeated a lock | Breaking and entering | Disabled |
| Unauthorized crossing through an open or unlocked route independent of a linked break-in | Trespass | Disabled |
| Ordinary activation of a locked entrance | None | Disabled |

The two bash checks use the approved RULE probability and retained-outcome contracts. They are separate from the physical bash success check. Mods and authored location policies may configure detection eligibility, odds and behavior, including checks for quiet methods and trespass. An authored interior hostility response can operate independently of civil filing.

When both attempt and completion are detected and chargeable, retain two charges linked to their respective facts. Stock court uses the worst eligible charge under LAW-001. Modded court can use both. Stock completed breaking and entering ranks above its corresponding attempt for this comparison. LAW-001 supplies jurisdictional retention, charge suppression, selection at arrest commitment and case disposition. Under MAGIC-029, ordinarily exempt beast actions still report truthful facts.

## Edge cases, configuration and persistence

Preserve an attempt when placement fails or the player walks away. Successful unlocking alone cannot create a completed-entry offense. Evaluate permission at the relevant attempt or crossing commitment. If permission is valid at crossing, file no crossing offense for that action. A prior attempt retains its own outcome. A bash against an already-unlocked entrance can produce noise. Crossing through it without permission follows the trespass rule unless linked to a qualifying lock defeat.

Use stable operation identities so repeated callbacks or recovery cannot reroll detection or duplicate filing. Save committed legal outcomes alongside the relevant durable lock and charge state. Preserve any unfinished mandatory handoff at a supported save boundary, or defer ordinary saving until it resolves under the owning specifications. Loading restores existing facts without creating a new attempt, crossing, detection roll or guard response. Modded probabilities and policies follow RULE and DATA validation. Engineering choices include event schemas, pending-state representation and profile migration.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A rejected ordinary locked-door click files nothing. An admitted failed unauthorized locked bash reports one attempt and checks 10% once under the stock policy.
2. Successful unauthorized bashing leaves the player outside. A subsequent linked unauthorized entry checks 10% once with greetings enabled or disabled. Cancelled or failed placement creates no completed offense.
3. Stock pick and Open report qualifying attempts and completed entries with random detection disabled. Unauthorized crossing independent of a qualifying lock defeat reports trespass with random detection disabled. Authored or modded policy can enable checks.
4. An authored interior hostility reaction can run without independently filing a charge. Valid scoped entry permission prevents an unauthorized crossing offense.
5. A policy that detects both attempt and completion can file both once. Stock court selects the worse eligible charge, and a modded policy can select both. Beast suppression preserves the underlying facts and existing charges.
6. Reload or technical retry preserves committed outcomes and produces no duplicate charge or detection roll. Audio settings do not affect authoritative results.
