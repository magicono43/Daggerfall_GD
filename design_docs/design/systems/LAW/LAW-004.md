# LAW-004 — Shoplifting and private property

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-13 with “I approve”. See LAW-004-A01 and LAW-004-D01 through D03 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md).

## Gameplay purpose and intended experience

Give taking protected goods consequences that reflect the setting. Stealing under a merchant's eye can fail before the player gets the goods. Household theft allows the player to reconsider and return untouched goods before settlement. After-hours shelf theft still involves access and ownership rules, including any separate break-in.

## Daggerfall Unity reference

Local DFU 1.1.1 `Assets/Scripts/Game/UserInterfaceWindows/DaggerfallTradeWindow.cs#DoSteal` checks detection before transfer, awards practice on either outcome and awards guild credit when undetected goods transfer. `DaggerfallInventoryWindow.cs#OnCloseWindow` checks its household theft basket at close. `AttemptPrivatePropertyTheft` awards guild credit before detection, requests guards if caught, and grants Pickpocket practice only if undetected. Its separate shelf close path compares collection-entry counts to credit recruitment. Static inspection establishes these code paths.

| Route | Detection and transfer | Legal and guild outcome |

## Stock filing, practice and settlement

- Household detection files Theft and requests guards under the same LAW permission and authorization checks as open-shop detection. An empty outstanding household batch settles without a roll or guild credit. A qualifying nonempty household batch earns one guild point once settled, including when detected, because its transfer stands. Count one qualifying closed-shelf deed per source interaction, using committed quantities.
- Retain DFU practice defaults of one raw Pickpocket practice point per resolved open-shop theft attempt and one per undetected nonempty household batch. Closed-shelf theft has no stock practice grant. Practice and deed eligibility are moddable.
- Opening or inspecting a container does not by itself file Theft. Apply MOVE-022's private-property prompt and allow explicitly authored inspection consequences independently. Required interaction teardown settles outstanding goods under the same rule as closing. A subsequent take after settlement begins a fresh batch of previously unsettled goods.

## Formulas, configuration and required data

RULE-010 resolves detection probability as `clamp(100 − S + Q + trunc(W) + N, 5, 95)` percent by default. Use live Pickpocket `S`, validated source quality `Q`, total evaluated weight `W` and authoritative stack count `N`. Capture coherent values when authorizing the check. Count only relevant accepted quantities for household detection. Use authoritative stack identities and normalization so UI display and callbacks cannot change basket burden. Rules, odds, detection timing and enforcement policies support validated regional and modded configuration. Exact weight units and stack normalization follow ITEM and RULE-010.

Retain stable actor, jurisdiction, source and item lineage, actual quantities and custody, permission and policy provenance, basket and operation identities, resolved detection and owner completion state. Track returned quantities and any irreversible use or onward movement relevant to household settlement. Preserve provenance across merges, splits and transfers to wagon or other containers.

## Edge cases, persistence and technical constraints

- Revalidate access, source, quantity, custody and destination capacity before an admitted check or transfer. Invalid, empty or cancelled requests do not become completed theft deeds. Retain outcomes across technical retries. A detected open-shop attempt leaves the goods with the shop. If an undetected transfer fails technically, preserve its outcome for recovery and award guild credit only when transfer commits.
- A partial return only removes the actual untouched quantity returned to its source. Consumption, transformation, sale, equip/use or onward custody must pass the settlement boundary before they can bypass the batch. Returning goods after settlement does not reverse settled consequences. A guard placement failure preserves any filed charge.
- Persist pending and committed basket facts at allowed save points so quitting, loading or leaving the scene cannot discard an unresolved required check or replay a result. Where a coherent state cannot be saved, defer ordinary saving through the owning save contract. Use one retained operation outcome across LAW, ITEM, CHAR, NPC and FACT. Target Godot 4.7.2. Concrete APIs and persistence schemas remain integration work.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Confirm a valid open-shop Steal basket. Detection prevents transfer and requests the permitted legal response once. Undetected transfer earns one qualifying guild point once committed.
2. Take multiple household items and return some untouched to the exact source. One check covers the remaining quantities. Returning all before settlement consumes no check. Trying to use or move those goods elsewhere settles theft first. Detection preserves committed transfers.
3. Take part of a stack from protected closed-shop shelves. The qualifying transfer contributes to one guild deed for the interaction and stock performs no random detection check. A configured mod can enable a check.
4. Inspect property, cancel a transfer, exceed capacity or lose the target. No successful theft is fabricated. Exercise practice on detected and undetected shop attempts and undetected household settlement.
5. Retry an interrupted transfer or settlement and reload at an allowed boundary. Preserve the actual quantities and retained detection outcome without duplicating practice, charges, guards or guild credit.
