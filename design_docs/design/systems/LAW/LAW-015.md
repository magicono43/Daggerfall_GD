# LAW-015 — Prison time and release

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-13 with “I approve”. See LAW-015-A01 and LAW-015-D01 through D03 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md).

## Gameplay purpose and intended experience

Make a prison term consume meaningful game time without requiring the player to watch the full countdown. Release returns the player safely to the world with the outcome and remaining legal obligations intact.

## Daggerfall Unity reference

In local DFU 1.1.1, `Assets/Scripts/Game/UserInterfaceWindows/DaggerfallCourtWindow.cs#UpdatePrisonScreen` advances the full term when its real-time countdown reaches zero and refills vitals. It suppresses encounters and normalization for that advance. `ReleaseFromPrison` advances four more hours, clears the charge, optionally repositions the player at a local entrance marker and clears enemies. These observations come from static inspection.

## Confirmed rules and parameters

- For a positive final ordinary sentence of `N` prison days, present a skippable countdown and commit `N × 1,440` game minutes through TIME once. Countdown pacing and skip affect presentation. Calendar deadlines, rentals and timed effects use their approved clock rules. Skip ambient encounters, periodic pursuit and legal and faction normalization for the sentence interval without catch-up.
- Finish ordinary sentence settlement under LAW-013 and apply LAW-010 recovery once when the fine and prison obligations are fully settled. Fine-only and zero-fine, zero-day guilty outcomes use LAW-013 settlement and receive no prison time.

| Stock release outcome | Additional game time | Vitals | Placement |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Positive prison term served | Four hours (240 minutes) | Full refill through CHAR | Validated jail exit or town gate in the sentencing jurisdiction, with safe local fallback |
| Ordinary guilty outcome without prison | Zero | Preserve current vitals | Safe court return anchor or validated local fallback |
| Acquittal or guild rescue | Zero | Preserve current vitals | Safe return under LAW-012/013 |
| Banishment | Zero | Full refill through CHAR | Validated local exit under LAW-014 |

Close the resolved case and eligible companion charges under LAW-001. End enforcement control and responder obligations scoped to that case. Preserve unrelated actors and obligations. Validate placement before returning control. If placement fails, retain settled sentence and release work for recovery.

## Release timing and recovery

- Treat the four-hour addition as release processing after a positive prison term. Skip ambient encounters and periodic pursuit for these minutes while custody remains active. Apply ordinary calendar and normalization rules for this separate interval. The prison interval retains its approved normalization exemption.
- Settle required time advances and owner effects before final placement and control release. Order vital refill after the advances. A placement retry resumes unfinished steps and cannot repeat time, payment, healing, reputation or charge disposition. Time advance alone supplies no rest or practice award.
- Use the validated arrest return anchor for a fine-only or zero-day guilty release. An unavailable anchor uses a validated local fallback or retains the handoff for recovery. No independent court exit is inferred for an authored execution outcome.

## Data, inputs, outputs and dependencies

Retain case and jurisdiction identities, committed disposition, payment status, prison length, policy version, countdown state where needed, clock progress, standing and vital results, owned control and responder scope, and validated release destination. LAW owns obligations and disposition. TIME advances the authoritative game clock. CHAR owns vitals, FACT and LAW apply eligible recovery, NPC handles scoped responders, and CORE/MOVE/WORLD coordinate control and placement. UI presents the countdown and outcome. Final release requires validated placement and settled required effects. Target Godot 4.7.2. Concrete APIs remain open.

## Edge cases, configuration and save/load

Validate nonnegative durations and safe clock arithmetic. Profiles may change presentation, processing time, placement and release effects through their owners. Handle an interrupted clock advance with retained owner progress. Admitted saves under LAW-016 preserve settled effects and any authorized pending state. Loading creates no fresh countdown completion, time advance, recovery or release reward. Save eligibility during court and prison remains LAW-016 scope.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Serving the same sentence with normal pacing and skip advances identical game time once. Calendar obligations follow their owner rules. Ambient encounters, pursuit and normalization skip the prison interval.
2. A positive prison release adds 240 minutes and refills vitals. Fine-only, acquittal and rescue add zero and preserve vitals. Banishment adds zero and refills vitals. An ordinary fine-only case receives eligible standing recovery once.
3. Release uses the validated destination, closes eligible case charges and ends case-scoped enforcement. Unrelated obligations and actors remain. Unavailable placement retains committed time and recovery without duplication.
4. Retry interrupted steps or restore from an allowed save boundary. Verify no extra clock advances, payment, healing, disposition or standing changes. A regional profile can specify a valid local fallback.
