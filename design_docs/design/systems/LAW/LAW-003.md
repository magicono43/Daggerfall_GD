# LAW-003 — Pickpocketing detection

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-13 with “I approve”. See LAW-003-A01 and LAW-003-D01 through D03 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md).

## Gameplay purpose and player experience

Make a failed civilian theft immediately consequential, while an enemy victim responds through hostility. A successful attempt may yield nothing. The Thieves Guild recognizes actual gains from qualifying theft.

## Daggerfall Unity reference

In the local DFU 1.1.1 snapshot, `Assets/Scripts/Game/PlayerActivate.cs#Pickpocket` uses a success roll. Failure through the townsperson path sets Pickpocketing and requests immediate guards. The enemy path turns the target against the player and can make area enemies hostile when the target had been peaceful. Success branches into a 67% chance to gain 1–6 gold or an empty result. The gold branch calls `Game/Entities/PlayerEntity.cs#TallyCrimeGuildRequirements` with one thieving point. These are static source observations.

## Confirmed rules and parameters

- For a committed attempt against an eligible stock townsperson, failure means detection with certainty. File Pickpocketing immediately when permitted under LAW-001 and request immediate deployment where the jurisdiction authorizes it. No additional stock detection roll occurs. A charge remains filed if guards are unavailable. Regional or modded policies may change criteria and timing, including delayed deployment.
- For a committed failure against an eligible humanoid enemy, request hostility for the target and any applicable declared response group under MOVE-024 and NPC policy. A formal charge requires an explicit authored or modded rule. Successful attempts leave existing hostility in effect and have no independent stock detection roll.
- Award one Thieves Guild point under FACT-012 when qualifying gold credit commits. Empty success and failure earn zero. Mods can count qualifying committed item theft once per deed under ITEM and FACT rules. Detection, filing, arrest and conviction are independent of recruitment credit.
- MOVE-024 and RULE-010 own attempt eligibility and success. Keep the approved skill-only `S` or eligible enemy `S + 5 × (L − T)` raw success chance with RULE-010 limits. The 67% find chance and uniform 1–6 gold apply after success. The approved one-attempt-per-persistent-target limit and default practice remain MOVE-024 rules.

## Data, inputs, outputs and interactions

MOVE supplies a stable attempt identity, responsible actor, explicit victim identity and category, jurisdiction and committed outcome. ITEM supplies the committed reward or transfer outcome. MAGIC supplies relevant attribution and suppression context under MAGIC-029. LAW applies the jurisdiction's detection, filing and enforcement policies. NPC owns hostility and guard deployment. FACT receives the settled qualifying deed. UI presents the outcome without affecting resolution. Retain policy provenance, resolved detection, charge references, and reward and reaction completion state where needed for recovery.

## Edge cases, configuration and persistence

- A missing, stale or ineligible target creates no valid attempt or legal consequence. A rejected repeat does not reroll detection. A previously hostile target remains hostile and group reactions require declared scope. Audio and UI settings have no bearing on detection.
- Preserve truthful attempt facts when LAW-001 suppression applies. Authorization for guard action, including any existing warrant, follows LAW policy. An unfulfilled guard request does not erase a committed charge. A technical failure of reward credit retains the original resolved reward for recovery. Recruitment credit waits for its successful commitment.
- Use the attempt identity across MOVE, LAW, NPC, ITEM and FACT to deduplicate consequences. Persist the spent attempt, unresolved owner work at supported save points, and committed outcomes as required for recovery. Load must not repeat charges, guard requests, hostility, rolls, gold or guild credit. Use validated definitions for modded eligibility, detection, filing, enforcement, group responses and recruitment.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A stock townsperson failure produces one detected result, one eligible charge, and one authorized immediate deployment request. If deployment is unavailable, the filed charge survives. A modded regional policy can delay deployment.
2. A stock eligible enemy failure follows declared hostility scope. A modded policy can additionally file a charge. An invalid or stale victim cannot trigger townsperson handling.
3. Qualifying committed gold earns one guild point. Empty success and failure earn none. Failed reward credit earns none until recovery commits it. Qualifying modded transfers count once per deed.
4. Retry, duplicate delivery and reload preserve the spent attempt and resolved outcomes without repeating consequences. Beast suppression preserves truthful incident facts and existing charges.
