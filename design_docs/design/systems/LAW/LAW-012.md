# LAW-012 — Guild rescue

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-13 with “I approve”. See LAW-012-A01 and LAW-012-D01 through D03 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md).

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

Let membership and rank in a crime guild occasionally secure release from court before the player pleads. High rank improves the odds. The intervention applies to the case and its eligible companion charges, while unrelated cases remain enforceable.

## Daggerfall Unity reference

In local DFU 1.1.1, `Assets/Scripts/Game/UserInterfaceWindows/DaggerfallCourtWindow.cs#HandleCourtLogic` calculates the base punishment, then checks the relevant crime guild before showing the plea. Active membership and a rank at least equal to a uniform draw from 0 through 19 yield rescue. The successful branch shows a guild message, fills vitals and invokes sentence reputation recovery. These observations come from static inspection.

## Stock rules and parameters

| Eligible guild | Selected charge |
| --- | --- |
| Dark Brotherhood | Assault or Murder |
| Thieves Guild | Attempted Breaking and Entering, Trespassing, Breaking and Entering, or Pickpocketing |

Theft is outside stock eligibility. Resolve the exact authorized membership and rank from the applicable membership set through FACT-009/011. A valid nonmember has no rescue check. Use the selected charge locked under LAW-001 and the retained base calculation under LAW-011. For eligible rank `r`, draw one uniform integer `u` from `[0,19]` and succeed if `u <= r`. Stock rank 0 has a 5% chance and rank 9 has a 50% chance.

Resolve the automatic check once before the plea, even if the base result is severe. Failure proceeds to the normal plea. Success automatically commits a distinct pre-plea rescue disposition and bypasses the fine, prison and severe outcome for this case. Close eligible companion charges under LAW-001 and retain legal history. Preserve unrelated charges, warrants and penalties under their own rules. Because no guilty outcome or served sentence occurred, stock applies neither a new LAW-010 loss nor sentence recovery. Preserve current vitals, including committed arrest health. Show which guild intervened.

## Modding and release integration

- Support stable guild and membership selectors for stock and custom guilds. Profiles can configure qualifying charges, jurisdiction, priority, rank odds, and release or recovery effects through validated owner operations. An extension can explicitly grant a vital refill. Penalties, standing and disposition effects must use the respective owner rules.
- For overlapping modded policies, select one eligible policy using explicit configured precedence and retain one decision per case by default. A policy that offers a choice or multiple checks requires explicit configuration. Multi-charge case eligibility and scope require a declared policy.
- Use the validated court return anchor or a safe authored alternative. Release this case's enforcement control and preserve unrelated actors. Prison-time advancement does not apply. Preserve a committed rescue on technical failure and retry only unfinished handoff effects.

## Required data, inputs and outputs

Retain case, selected charges, jurisdiction, guild and membership set, rank snapshot, policy identity and version, roll result, rescue disposition and release progress. FACT supplies the applicable membership and rank. RULE supplies the retained draw. LAW owns decision and charge disposition, CHAR owns any authorized vital change, CORE and MOVE coordinate release and placement, and UI presents the result. Target Godot 4.7.2. Concrete APIs remain open.

## Edge cases and persistence

Evaluate rescue only for a valid committed case. Required membership or policy data unavailable during resolution calls for recovery. A UI retry, duplicate request or supported save restore cannot redraw rescue or repeat committed effects. Persist captured inputs, the resolved decision and release progress where saving is allowed. LAW-016 defines court save eligibility. Missing required mod content follows validated migration or recovery.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Verify each stock guild's qualifying charges and that Theft is ineligible. Wrong or inactive membership does not qualify. Test rank 0 against draws 0 and 1, and rank 9 against draws 9 and 10.
2. Base calculation precedes rescue. A failed check opens the normal plea. A successful check bypasses ordinary and severe outcomes before the plea, closes eligible case charges, and preserves unrelated charges and current vitals.
3. Successful stock rescue applies no new standing loss, fiscal penalty or sentence recovery. A custom guild can supply configured eligibility, odds and approved release effects.
4. Retry and restore at an allowed boundary preserve the original result and commit each release effect once.

## Compatibility review and outstanding questions
