# LAW-011 — Penalty severity and fine calculation

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-13 with “I approve”. See LAW-011-A01 and LAW-011-D01 through D03 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md).

## Gameplay purpose and intended experience

Let the player understand what is at stake before entering a plea. A more serious offense generally carries a larger ordinary penalty, and negative local legal standing can increase it and expose the player to banishment. The court's initial calculation stays fixed through UI retries.

## Daggerfall Unity reference

In local DFU 1.1.1, `Assets/Scripts/Game/UserInterfaceWindows/DaggerfallCourtWindow.cs#HandleCourtLogic` evaluates two severity checks, calculates an offense-specific amount from the magnitude of legal standing, and rolls a fine or prison allocation for each unit. It caps the fine at available funds and converts full unpaid units to prison days. The inspected base selector supports banishment and ordinary punishment. The execution branch has no matching selection in that base logic. These observations come from static inspection.

## Stock rules and parameters

1. Use the charge selected at arrest under LAW-001 and capture the arrest jurisdiction's committed legal standing `R` when the court prepares the case, before any standing penalty from that case. Use `D = max(0, −R)` for both severity and the ordinary surcharge. Positive standing adds zero surcharge.
2. The severe outcome has probability `1 − (1 − A/100) × (1 − B/100)`, with `A = min(75, D)` and `B = min(75, floor(D/2))`. Evaluate independent RULE checks, smaller threshold first and short-circuit on success. At −20 the chance is 28%. Stock severe category is banishment. Authored alternatives require an explicit profile and LAW-014 support.
3. For the ordinary base sentence, calculate `amount = clamp(base + rate × D, minimum, maximum)` and `units = floor(amount/40)`. Each unit independently adds 40 gold or three prison days, with equal probability. A zero-unit result yields zero fine and zero days. Retain the resolution once per case through plea, UI recreation and technical retry.
4. For affordability, let `G` be the fine amount eligible funds can actually cover, capped at the assessed fine. Add `floor((assessed fine − G)/40)` prison days and use `G` as the capped fine. On an assessed 120-gold fine with 81 payable gold, the stock conversion adds zero days. The calculation moves no currency. Final validation and tender use ITEM-009. How pleas and later affordability settlement compose is a LAW-013 integration decision.
5. Before pleading, display the selected charge, possible ordinary fine and prison ranges, and whether severe punishment is possible. Label ordinary ranges as base and pre-plea. Indicate that fine and prison outcomes share the same units. Show the applicable unpaid-fine rule. Keep the exact retained base result internal until the flow calls for disclosure. Guild rescue, plea effects and execution of severe outcomes remain LAW-012 through LAW-014 responsibilities.

## Required data, inputs, outputs and integration

Retain case and charge identities, jurisdiction, standing snapshot, policy and version, offense parameters, retained random outcomes, ordinary allocation and severity, eligible funding context, and displayed range inputs. LAW owns case calculation. RULE owns validated randomness. ITEM and ECON supply eligible funding and commit any final payment. UI presents the case. LAW-010 applies standing changes on guilty outcomes. The calculation itself changes neither standing nor player inventory. Target Godot 4.7.2. Concrete APIs remain open.

## Configuration, edge cases and save/load

Jurisdiction and mod profiles may vary offense tables, severity thresholds, allocation odds and amounts, surcharge, unpaid conversion and disclosure. Validate arithmetic domains, bound roll counts and retain one authoritative base result. A preview consumes no random draws. Payment method or available funding can be revalidated at settlement while preserving the retained base allocation. An unavailable profile requires explicit recovery. Preserve captured inputs and outcomes at allowed case save boundaries under LAW-016. Reload or technical retry cannot reroll severity, allocate units again or debit funds twice.

## Acceptance criteria

1. At +20 standing, ordinary stock penalties match those at zero. At −20, verify surcharge and the 28% combined severe chance. Verify caps, zero units and the supported severe category.
2. Confirm 40 gold or three days per unit, floor rounding and the 120/81 affordability example. Preview and base resolution move no funds. A later payment failure preserves the original allocation.
3. Before pleading, show the charge, ordinary ranges and severe risk. Closing or recreating presentation, retrying or restoring at an allowed save point preserves the retained calculation.
4. Verify configured regional differences, invalid offense recovery and the administrative loan-default path. Subsequent guilty standing loss cannot reprice the captured base sentence.
