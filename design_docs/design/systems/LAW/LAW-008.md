# LAW-008 — Delayed pursuit and regional warrants

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-13 with “I approve”. See LAW-008-A01 and LAW-008-D01 through D03 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md).

## Gameplay purpose and player experience

Give the player a chance to evade a local dispatch while preserving regional exposure from outstanding warrants, low legal standing and banishment. Recognition by the watch can prompt immediate action. A civilian report gives the player a short window to leave the area.

## Daggerfall Unity reference

Local DFU 1.1.1 `Assets/Scripts/Game/Entities/PlayerEntity.cs#Update` makes separate elapsed-minute checks for low standing and banishment. A passing check writes Criminal Conspiracy before calling the pursuit path. Ambient encounters can stop later checks, and some time jumps suppress the loop. `SpawnCityGuards(false)` evaluates nearby observers and can schedule a 5–10 second civilian-report delay tied to the current location object. The guard branch can accept a raycast hit without confirming that it hit the player. These observations come from static inspection.

## Confirmed rules and parameters

- For each eligible game minute, stock regional legal standing strictly below −10 gives a 5% pursuit chance. Active banishment gives a separate 10% chance. A successful periodic check automatically files Criminal Conspiracy under the applicable LAW-001 rules. Threshold, odds, conditions and charge policy are moddable.
- An eligible watch NPC who identifies the player can activate an immediate response. An eligible civilian who identifies and reports the player triggers a moddable 5–10 active-second delay. Leaving the response area cancels pending local dispatch. The underlying warrant or banishment remains enforceable on return.
- Existing charges and enforceable warrants retain their jurisdiction under LAW-001. Pursuit in beast form requires identification under the applicable warrant or banishment policy. Ordinary beast-form charge suppression continues to apply. LAW-007 owns the placement and batch rules for a dispatched response.

## Stock travel, pursuit checks and dispatch

- Resume pursuit eligibility at the fast-travel destination. Skip route minutes, prison time and scripted absence for stock pursuit checks. Mods can enable route interceptions through supported travel rules. Stock warrant authority stays in the issuing jurisdiction, with explicit shared enforcement available to authored or modded policies.
- Evaluate both eligible periodic chances once at each eligible minute boundary. If both pass, record both grounds and settle one Criminal Conspiracy charge and one pursuit opportunity for that event. Filing precedes recognition and placement. Reuse an open Criminal Conspiracy charge for the same grounds in the same local enforcement episode. Immediate and delayed paths share the active response and do not start extra waves from duplicate reports. An enforceable warrant can also authorize pursuit on recognition without a fresh periodic conspiracy charge.
- Sample the civilian delay once as a uniform integer from 5 through 10 active seconds. Further reports do not restart it. A qualifying watch recognition may engage immediately and supersede an unfulfilled civilian dispatch. At expiry revalidate area, jurisdiction and pursuit authority. Use actual NPC identification facts, including unobstructed sight where visual recognition is required. An obstruction hit alone supplies no identification.

## Edge cases, configuration and persistence

- Keep periodic checks independent of ambient encounter random draws. Avoid applying route or suppressed minutes to the current scene on catch-up. Repeated callbacks and technical retry preserve resolved checks. Do not transfer pending dispatch to an unrelated location after scene replacement.
- Save pending dispatch identity and remaining active delay at supported boundaries. Restore against the current valid area and authority without rebuilding a fresh timer, replaying charges or duplicating guards. Time outside the game does not advance active dispatch. Expired or invalid authority ends the pending dispatch. Failed placement preserves valid charges and follows LAW-007 recovery.
- Validate regional or modded recognition rules, scoped warrant authority, chance parameters, delay bounds and travel hooks through DATA/MOD and RULE. Exact bounds beyond stock values remain owner integration.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Standing of −10 alone is ineligible. Below −10 and active banishment use their respective stock checks. Both passing in one minute settle one event. A successful periodic check files the stock conspiracy charge when permitted, even if no observer recognizes the player.
2. Eligible watch recognition can engage immediately. A civilian report schedules a retained delay. An obstructed visual check cannot establish recognition. Repeated reports cannot reset the timer or duplicate deployment.
3. Leaving cancels local dispatch. Returning can expose the player again under the preserved regional authority. Recognized beast-form pursuit and ordinary charge suppression follow LAW-001 and MAGIC-029.
4. Stock fast travel does not check skipped route minutes. Ordinary destination eligibility resumes. Verify an explicit modded route interception and a scoped cross-jurisdiction policy.
5. Reload during the civilian delay, retry placement, and process a catch-up window. Preserve valid outcomes without fresh rolls for completed checks or replayed filings and guards.
