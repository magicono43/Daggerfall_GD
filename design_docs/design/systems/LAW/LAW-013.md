# LAW-013 — Plea, debate and lie

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-13 with “I approve”. See LAW-013-A01 and LAW-013-D01 through D03 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md).

## Gameplay purpose and intended experience

Offer a predictable reduction for admitting guilt and a chance to avoid punishment through a defense. The player can weigh the case's possible punishment against their social skills and regional standing. The court retains the result once the player commits a choice.

## Daggerfall Unity reference

In local DFU 1.1.1, `Assets/Scripts/Game/UserInterfaceWindows/DaggerfallCourtWindow.cs#GuiltyNotGuilty_OnButtonClick` halves ordinary fines and days on Guilty and deducts the adjusted fine. `DebateLie_OnButtonClick` awards practice and checks acquittal using the chosen live skill, Personality and legal standing. Failed ordinary defense adjusts the fine, then transitions to sentencing without a matching debit in that branch. Acquittal fills vitals and invokes reputation recovery. These are static source observations.

## Confirmed stock rules and parameters

1. Once LAW-012 rescue has failed or been ruled ineligible, use the selected charge and retained LAW-011 base result. Guilty commits a guilty outcome. For an ordinary sentence, halve the original fine and prison days separately, rounding down. Stock plea discounts do not alter a severe outcome.
2. Not Guilty leads to Debate or Lie. Debate uses live Etiquette and Lie uses live Streetwise. At defense commitment, capture live skill `S`, live Personality `P`, and use the case's captured pre-penalty legal standing `R`. Stock acquittal chance is `clamp(R + floor((S + P)/2), 5, 95)%`. Award one raw practice point in the chosen skill once, regardless of verdict. Resolve one retained RULE check.
3. A failed defense commits a conviction. For an ordinary conviction draw one retained uniform integer `u` from 1 through 100. If `R + u < 25`, double the base fine. If `R + u > 75`, halve it and round down. At 25 through 75 keep the fine. Keep the base ordinary prison days until affordability conversion. Failed defense on a severe case proceeds to LAW-014 without the ordinary fine modifier.
4. For either ordinary guilty outcome, apply affordability to the adjusted fine. Cap it at payable eligible funds and add one prison day per full 40 gold unpaid. For example, a Guilty plea reduces 120 gold to 60. With 20 payable, settlement collects 20 gold and adds one day. Use the same ITEM-009 payment validation and collection for both guilty routes. LAW-010 standing loss commits once on the guilty outcome.
5. Acquittal closes the case and eligible companion charges under LAW-001. It preserves current vitals and applies no fine, prison, standing loss or sentence recovery. Unrelated obligations remain subject to their own rules. Once an ordinary guilty sentence is fully settled, apply LAW-010 recovery once, including fine-only sentences.

## Stock presentation and settlement

- Show each defense's labeled acquittal chance using the same resolved inputs as the check, refreshing estimates if live inputs change before commitment. Keep the retained base allocation internal before the plea under LAW-011. Explain final adjustments and the payable fine and prison term after the verdict.
- Confirm final funding through an explicit court tender profile under ITEM-009. Do not assume bank or wagon access. If a technical payment failure occurs, keep the verdict, modifiers and resolved sentence work for recovery. A valid funding change before final settlement may update affordability from the retained adjusted fine. After settlement commits, retries use the results already recorded.
- Treat a valid zero-fine, zero-day ordinary guilty result as fully settled when its guilty effects commit. Severe sentence completion and any recovery associated with it belong to LAW-014/015. Use an approved safe court return anchor for acquittal. Release only control owned by the case.

## Data, inputs, outputs and dependencies

Retain case, jurisdiction, selected charges, base sentence and severity, policy version, plea and defense IDs, captured standing and live inputs, verdict and modifier results, practice receipt, adjusted assessment, payment and time obligations, disposition and release status. LAW owns verdict and case settlement. RULE owns random resolution. CHAR supplies live skills and Personality and commits practice. ITEM/ECON validate and collect tender. TIME and LAW-015 coordinate prison. CORE/MOVE manage control and safe return, and UI presents choices. Target Godot 4.7.2. Concrete API and schema details remain open.

## Edge cases, configuration and save/load

Regional and modded profiles can configure plea adjustments, defense eligibility, skills, odds, practice, fine modifiers, and acquittal and sentence recovery effects through the responsible owners. Validate domains and arithmetic before resolution. Duplicate callbacks cannot submit a second plea, award practice twice, reroll or collect a second fine. Preserve pending effects and committed results at allowed court save boundaries under LAW-016. Recovery uses stable identities and resumes only unfinished work. Preview creates no practice or roll, and retries cannot replace the retained LAW-011 outcome.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Verify ordinary Guilty halves fine and days before affordability, including odd amounts and the 120/20 example. Verify severe Guilty bypasses ordinary discount and payment logic.
2. Verify Debate and Lie choose the correct live skill, use captured standing and live Personality, respect the 5–95% bounds, and award practice once. Verify failed ordinary defense at modifier values 24, 25, 75 and 76, including actual collection after conviction.
3. Acquittal preserves current vitals, closes eligible case charges and leaves unrelated obligations intact without legal or fiscal penalties. A settled fine-only or prison ordinary sentence applies recovery once. Partial settlement does not trigger recovery.
4. Test modded policy changes, unavailable tender, technical payment recovery, duplicate callbacks and restore at an authorized boundary. Verify no replay of payment, practice, verdict or standing.
