# LAW-007 — Immediate guard placement

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-13 with “I approve”. See LAW-007-A01 and LAW-007-D01 through D03 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md).

## Gameplay purpose and intended experience

Make immediate enforcement feel like nearby watch responding and reinforcements arriving through plausible routes. Keep the charge outstanding if the player escapes or the watch cannot reach the incident.

## Daggerfall Unity reference

In the local DFU 1.1.1 snapshot, `Assets/Scripts/Game/Entities/PlayerEntity.cs#SpawnCityGuards` skips dungeons and admits a batch when the relevant active count is at most five. Eligible interiors place two to five guards at an exterior door. Outdoors, the immediate path uses nearby pooled watch, can use some civilian positions behind the player, and falls back to a distant spawner if none were used. The batch admission check occurs before placement. These findings come from static inspection.

## Confirmed rules

- LAW authorizes immediate deployment. NPC first activates eligible nearby watch with stable identity and existing state under COMBAT-018. Reinforcements use safe entrances or off-screen positions with an approach route. Civilian positions may guide candidate selection while preserving civilian identity and unoccupied placement.
- Stock response aims for two to five guards per admitted batch. Use a pre-batch threshold of five active responding guards in the local enforcement area, inclusive. Existing responders count toward admission. A batch admitted at five may take the count above five. Mods can change the batch size, threshold, placement and reinforcement behavior.
- Deploy as many of the admitted responders as can safely join. End immediate placement when no valid placement or route remains. A filed charge stays open for later enforcement under LAW-001 and LAW-008. Stock dungeons exclude immediate city-watch deployment. Authored or regional policies may allow exceptions.

## Stock batch handling and placement recovery

- Sample the stock batch size uniformly from integers 2–5 once per admitted batch. Each new activation or reinforcement fills one slot. Guards already responding affect the threshold. Duplicate requests and new charges during the same active response reuse that response. A later distinct reinforcement request requires explicit policy authorization and its own threshold check.
- An entrance must offer safe placement and a feasible approach. Off-screen positions also require a feasible approach. If options run out after some responders commit, keep those responders and close remaining slots. A transient readiness failure may be recovered within the current request while eligibility holds. Once the request ends, renewed enforcement requires new authorization.

## Inputs, outputs and required data

Carry response, incident, enforcement episode, player, jurisdiction, area and policy identities, along with the authorized scope, current responder count, retained batch size, slot and guard identities, and placement or rejection outcomes. NPC supplies eligible watch and owns conversion, creation and activation. LOC/WORLD supply current geometry, entrances and route context. LAW records admitted, partially deployed, completed or unavailable outcomes. COMBAT-019 separately consumes LAW authorization for surrender. TIME and LAW-006 consume enforcement engagement under their rest interruption rules.

## Edge cases, configuration and persistence

An existing watch actor cannot fill two slots or be counted as a new reinforcement after conversion. Serialize threshold admission for concurrent requests in the same response area. Revalidate area, occupancy and route before activation. Preserve committed responders when another slot fails. A stale request cannot spawn guards after scope loss. At supported save points, retain committed actor and placement facts and any unfinished required work. Restore them without rerolling batch size or duplicating guards. Target Godot 4.7.2. Validate modded quantities and placement policies through DATA and MOD.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A valid request activates an eligible nearby watch actor with preserved identity before fresh reinforcements. Candidate placement leaves civilians intact and respects occupancy and routes.
2. A count of five admits a stock batch and can exceed five. A count above five blocks a new batch. Duplicate delivery and additional charges during an active response do not create extra waves. Modded settings can choose different limits.
3. An invalid route or occupied position cannot publish a guard. A partial response preserves successful placements. With no viable placement, the immediate request ends and filed charges remain open.
4. Stock dungeon requests do not deploy city watch. An authorized exception can use validated placement. Retry or reload retains prior outcomes without duplicating responders or rerolling the batch.
