# LAW-006 — Vagrancy and loan default

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-13 with “I approve”. See LAW-006-A01 and LAW-006-D01 through D03 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md).

## Gameplay purpose and intended experience

Give the player a clear choice before camping illegally in town. Collect overdue debt through the bank and make unpaid obligations affect standing in the region where they were incurred.

## Daggerfall Unity reference

In local DFU 1.1.1, `Assets/Scripts/Game/UserInterfaceWindows/DaggerfallRestWindow.cs#CanRest` can assign Vagrancy and request guards on an outdoor town rest request. The warning confirmation path can proceed into rest. With warnings disabled, the request can file a charge and stop. Denied indoor lodging follows another branch. `Game/LoanChecker.cs#CheckOverdueLoans` treats the loan as overdue when current game time exceeds its deadline. `OverdueLoan` attempts repayment from the regional account before marking a remaining debt as defaulted. `Game/Entities/PlayerEntity.cs#LowerRepForCrime` applies the regional and local people-faction losses. These findings come from static inspection.

## Confirmed rules and parameters

### Vagrancy

- Stock restricted outdoor town sleep requests display a warning. Cancellation carries no legal effect. Proceeding files Vagrancy where permitted and requests immediate guards where authorized. Disabling warnings preserves the consequence of the explicit rest request. Opening the rest interface is insufficient to file a charge.
- Once authorized enforcement engages, interrupt rest through the rest owner. Ordinary waiting and denied indoor sleep carry no stock Vagrancy charge. LAW-001 governs filing and suppression. Regional or modded policy can change the restricted areas and response.

### Loan default

- A loan becomes overdue when `now > due`, using authoritative game time. At the first eligible overdue check, collect `min(outstanding debt, available eligible account funds)` from the account in the loan region by default. Determine default from the committed remainder. Full first collection avoids default. Later eligible checks may collect newly available funds.
- On default apply −10 regional legal standing and a −5 local people-faction reputation adjustment once per loan. Track both effects even when the player's current location differs from the loan jurisdiction. The remainder stays payable. Later repayment clears remaining debt and ordinary standing recovery still applies. Default itself creates no active charge or immediate guard request. Collection sources, timing and penalties are moddable, including balance-based penalties.

## Rest and collection safeguards

- Revalidate rest eligibility, location and permission when the player proceeds. A stale warning or duplicate confirmation cannot file a charge. Preserve separate rest and enforcement outcomes. A guard placement failure leaves a filed charge intact. NPC and TIME own the engagement and interruption boundary.
- Evaluate default after a valid collection outcome, including a valid zero-funds result. A banking error requires recovery before determining the remainder. Key default and both standing effects to the stable loan identity, and resume only unsettled effects after a technical failure. Treat a later new loan in the same region as a distinct obligation.
- Treat default as an administrative standing event independent of ordinary charge suppression. Use explicit recovery if a required local faction mapping cannot be resolved. Preserve any committed collection, default or standing change during recovery.

## Required data, inputs, outputs and interactions

Rest needs request identity, rest mode, location and jurisdiction, local camping rule, permission and warning response, filing result, and enforcement state. MOVE/LOC supply current location and context. TIME owns rest eligibility and advancement, LAW owns legal response, NPC owns guard deployment, and UI presents the warning and outcome. Travel, lodging and guild grants supply context through their owners.

Debt needs loan identity, issuing region, accepted terms, due game time, outstanding amount, account source, and committed collection and default effects. TIME drives eligible overdue checks, ECON owns balances and collection, LAW owns legal standing, and FACT owns faction reputation. RULE-024 continues to govern accepted loan terms. Save pending obligations and committed effects at supported boundaries so a time jump, save or retry cannot repeat them. Restore the original debt terms and reconcile overdue work through the same authorized evaluation. Target Godot 4.7.2. Concrete APIs and persisted schemas remain open.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Confirm an eligible outdoor town rest request and receive one permitted charge and one authorized guard request. Cancel, open the rest interface, wait ordinarily or attempt ineligible indoor sleep and verify no Vagrancy is filed. With warnings disabled, a committed illegal rest request produces the same legal effect. Engaged enforcement interrupts rest.
2. At the exact due time, verify the loan is still payable without an overdue default. After the deadline, full collection avoids default. Partial or zero collection followed by a remaining debt records default once in the issuing region and applies both stock losses.
3. After default, collect later available funds without repeating penalties. Full repayment restores no lost standing directly. A distinct later loan can default independently. Default generates no active charge or immediate guards.
4. Retry partial work, advance time across a deadline, and reload at a supported save point. Verify no duplicate collection, penalties, charge or guard request. Test a regional override and a missing required faction mapping.
