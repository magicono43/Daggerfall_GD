# LAW-014 — Banishment and execution reachability

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-13 with “I approve”. See LAW-014-A01 and LAW-014-D01 through D03 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md).

## Gameplay purpose and intended experience

Make banishment a lasting regional consequence. The player leaves court near a local exit and can leave the region or risk renewed pursuit. Authored legal systems can impose different restrictions or select a severe outcome that uses the character death flow.

## Daggerfall Unity reference

In local DFU 1.1.1, `Assets/Scripts/Game/UserInterfaceWindows/DaggerfallCourtWindow.cs#HandleCourtLogic` sets the regional banishment bit, refills vitals and requests repositioning through the release path. `PositionPlayerAtLocationEntrance` chooses a local start marker on the current map pixel. The base selector does not choose execution. Its execution branch sets a separate regional bit and requests repositioning without invoking a player death operation. `Game/Entities/PlayerEntity.cs#Update` uses the banishment bit for its regional pursuit check. These observations come from static inspection.

## Confirmed rules and parameters

1. On a guilty outcome with retained stock banishment under LAW-011/013, apply the LAW-010 standing penalty once and impose a restriction in the sentencing region. Acquittal and LAW-012 rescue avert the retained severe result for that case. Resolve selected and eligible companion charges under LAW-001. Existing restrictions and unrelated obligations retain their own lifecycle.
2. Stock banishment has no automatic expiry. It survives travel, reload, and standing changes until an explicit pardon or removal. Entering or remaining in the region is possible. LAW-008 applies the stock 10% banishment check per eligible game minute and owns recognition, deployment, and destination-only stock fast-travel enforcement.
3. Complete stock banishment release at a validated local exit, such as a town gate, and refill vitals through CHAR. The region's banishment remains active. Stock banishment grants no LAW-010 sentence recovery. LAW-013's ordinary fine and prison flow does not apply. Mods may configure placement and release effects, including release at an appropriate regional border.
4. Stock court cannot select execution. A validated authored severe outcome may explicitly use the approved character death flow. An imported DFU execution flag is historical data and creates no new punishment on import or restore.

## Restriction, release and execution integration

- Use a stable active restriction for the player and sentencing jurisdiction. Repeat banishment in the same region updates its case history without stacking extra stock pursuit rolls. A pardon ends that restriction and cancels pending dispatch that depended solely on it. Other valid pursuit grounds and charge records follow their own rules.
- Prepare the release destination before publishing player control. If the preferred exit is unavailable, use a validated authored local fallback. If placement still fails, retain the case and restriction results for recovery. Clear only control and responder obligations scoped to the resolved case. Release timing belongs to LAW-015.
- For authored execution, retain the case, death request, and authoritative CHAR outcome. Submit the request once through CHAR and respect its configured prevention and resolution rules. An unresolved or prevented execution does not fabricate death or grant an ordinary release. An authored execution profile defines the continuation when death is prevented. Validate required policy and integration before admitting execution as a case outcome.

## Required data, inputs and outputs

Retain case, charge, actor and sentencing jurisdiction identities, severe category and policy version, restriction status and provenance, release destination and commitment, standing and vital effect progress, and any authored execution outcome. LAW receives committed verdict, placement readiness and owner results. It publishes authorized restriction changes, release or execution outcomes and information needed for pursuit. TIME owns any configured expiry or time advance. WORLD/LOC and MOVE validate exits, regional boundaries and placement. CHAR handles vitals and death. NPC consumes LAW-008 authorizations. Target Godot 4.7.2. Concrete APIs remain open.

## Configuration, edge cases and save/load

Validated profiles may configure expiry and pardon, regional entry and shared authority, placement and vital effects, and explicitly supported severe alternatives. Persist restriction state, historical flags and committed case effects at authorized save points under LAW-016. Restore without applying old restrictions as new punishment or repeating refills, placement, death requests or case effects. Validate legacy banishment conversion and preserve execution flags as history. Missing required jurisdiction, policy, or destination data follows migration or explicit recovery. Release and death flows resume only unfinished work.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A guilty stock severe case applies one standing penalty and activates banishment in the sentencing region. Acquittal and rescue avert that case's banishment. Repeated banishment grants no extra stock check.
2. A banished player can leave and return. Load and normalization preserve the restriction. LAW-008 applies the eligible 10% check in the sentencing region. Pardon stops pursuit based solely on that restriction.
3. Stock release refills vitals and places the player at a validated local exit. It grants no sentence recovery. Invalid placement retains the committed result without replaying effects. A supported profile can change placement or effects.
4. Stock execution cannot be selected. A legacy execution flag triggers no punishment on load. An authorized authored outcome submits one death request, and prevented death follows its declared continuation.
