# LAW-009 — Surrender and arrest handoff

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-13 with “I approve”. See LAW-009-A01 and LAW-009-D01 through D03 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md).

## Gameplay purpose and intended experience

Give the player a deliberate chance to surrender to authorized guards. After refusal, a potentially lethal guard hit may still lead to arrest depending on local legal standing. Successful surrender ends that pending hit and hands control to court.

## Daggerfall Unity reference

In local DFU 1.1.1, `Assets/Scripts/Game/EnemyAttack.cs` lowers reputation when opening the first qualifying surrender prompt. `Game/Entities/PlayerEntity.cs#SurrenderToCityGuards` sets health to one before some forced-surrender rejections. Voluntary surrender succeeds for living players. Forced surrender uses the regional standing thresholds below. `CourtWindow` requests an exterior transition for indoor arrests, marks the player arrested and posts the court UI request. These are static source observations.

## Confirmed rules and parameters

1. COMBAT-019 retains the first qualifying positive physical guard hit in an authorized enforcement episode before its mutations. Stock melee and arrows use the same eligibility rules. Showing the offer applies no standing penalty. Yes requests voluntary arrest. Stock voluntary surrender accepts for a living player with valid LAW authorization.
2. Explicit refusal or legitimate rejection resolves the retained hit once and suppresses further ordinary offers in the same episode. Brief loss of contact, changing guards and restoring a save preserve that suppression while the episode is active. Once LAW ends the pursuit, a newly authorized episode can offer again.
3. After refusal or legitimate rejection, a later qualifying guard hit with retained original physical damage at least equal to current living health requests forced surrender. Using legal standing in the enforcing jurisdiction, stock accepts with probability **0% below −20**, **50% from −20 through 0**, and **100% above 0**. Regional and modded policies may change acceptance or disable either route. Retain each resolved decision through retry under RULE and COMBAT-019.
4. Prepare a safe court return location within the arrest jurisdiction before commitment. Indoor arrests default to a validated nearby exterior point. Authored profiles can supply alternatives such as a courthouse or jail. At successful commitment, lock the selected court charges under LAW-001, set player health to one under the approved stock arrest-health rule, discard the deferred hit and hand control to court.
5. A legitimate rejection continues the retained hit through ordinary damage and CHAR prevention. Technical preparation or handoff failure preserves the pending operation for recovery. It cannot count as a refusal or spend another acceptance roll. Preserve the existing suspension and ordinary save restriction until a coherent outcome.

## Stock return placement and authorization recovery

- Use the safe current outdoor position as the default return anchor for an outdoor arrest. If it fails validation, use a valid authored local alternative or retain the handoff for recovery. A return anchor does not independently decide sentence release placement.
- Stock spells do not trigger the physical surrender flow. Explicit LAW/MAGIC policy may authorize a separate spell route. Active hostility and guard identity alone supply no arrest authority.
- Revalidate living player, enforcement authority, episode and court readiness before commitment. A genuinely expired authorization is a legitimate rejection when the retained hit remains valid. Route stale session or invalid hit context through owner recovery. Once arrest commits, repeated callbacks resume the court handoff without committing health or charges again.

## Required data, inputs and outputs

Carry stable player, session, guard, episode, attack and arrest request identities. Include enforcing jurisdiction, authorization, retained damage and original source, response type, sampled standing, accepted or rejected result, offer suppression, selected charge IDs, destination readiness and commitment state. COMBAT owns pending-hit calculation and continuation. LAW owns arrest eligibility, decision and case commitment. CHAR applies stock arrest health or ordinary damage outcomes. CORE and MOVE validate placement and hold control through handoff. UI presents the authorized decision and court. LAW-011 onward governs trial and sentence. Target Godot 4.7.2. Concrete APIs and data layout remain open.

## Edge cases, configuration and save/load

Serialize competing surrender requests through COMBAT-019. Preserve the originating hit and owner-scoped suspension through technical retry. Block ordinary saving during unresolved surrender, forced evaluation or arrest handoff. At supported save points, retain active episode suppression and committed arrest facts. Restore without reopening an old offer or repeating damage, acceptance checks or court commitment. LAW-016 governs court save eligibility. Validate policy thresholds, allowed routes and destination choices through DATA/MOD. Exact episode end criteria, simultaneous hit ordering, invalidation recovery and court integration fixtures remain follow-up work.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Test authorized voluntary acceptance across standing values. Test forced acceptance at −21, −20, 0 and 1, including equality of damage and health. Rejection leaves health unchanged before the same pending hit resumes.
2. Verify refusal or legitimate rejection suppresses ordinary offers across briefly lost contact, different guards and reload. Ending pursuit and authorizing a new episode can restore ordinary eligibility.
3. Commit an indoor arrest only after safe placement preparation and lock charges at commitment. A failed destination retains the hit, health and decision for recovery. A successful commit sets health to one and discards the deferred hit once.
4. Verify disabled routes, valid modded thresholds, stale callbacks and competing hits. Ordinary saves remain blocked during unresolved decision and handoff, and control stays suspended through successful transfer to court.
