# LAW-010 — Regional penalties and faction spillover

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-13 with “I approve”. See LAW-010-A01 and LAW-010-D01 through D03 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md).

## Gameplay purpose and intended experience

Give a guilty court outcome a lasting consequence in the responsible jurisdiction and among its people. Serving a sentence restores part of the loss. Ordinary game time gradually brings legal standing toward neutral.

## Daggerfall Unity reference

In local DFU 1.1.1, `Assets/Scripts/Game/Entities/PlayerEntity.cs#LowerRepForCrime` subtracts a listed regional loss and passes negative half to the local people faction with propagation. `EnemyAttack.cs` calls it when opening the surrender offer. `RaiseReputationForDoingSentence` uses half the listed loss minus one, then half that result for faction recovery. `NormalizeReputations` clamps legal standing to −100 through +100 and moves it one point toward zero on the 112-game-day schedule. These are static source observations.

## Confirmed rules and parameters

- Apply a stock offense penalty once when court commits a guilty plea or conviction for the selected charge. Apply no ordinary penalty solely for filing, arrest, offering surrender, or acquittal. Stock uses the DFU offense table. Regional or modded policies can change losses or define aggregation for multi-charge cases. The loan default rule in LAW-006 applies independently at default, once per loan.
- For listed loss `P`, stock requests a legal delta of `−P` and a local people-faction delta of `−floor(P/2)` through FACT-003. Record actual applied legal loss `L` and direct local faction loss `F` after owner bounds. Use the case jurisdiction and its local people faction. Keep this table separate from LAW-001 offense severity ranking.
- On eligible sentence completion, stock requests legal recovery `R = max(0, floor(L/2) − 1)` and local faction recovery `min(F, floor(R/2))`. Apply standing bounds to both commitments through their owners. Regional and modded profiles may change recovery.
- Every 112 game days, eligible regional legal standing moves one point toward zero. Rest and travel count. Prison sentence time skips covered boundaries without catch-up. Clamp each committed legal change to the stock range [−100, +100]. Schedule, step, bounds and regional participation are moddable. Normalization changes standing only and preserves warrant, charge and banishment records.

## Stock loss table

Values are DFU's configured regional legal loss before bounds. Faction losses follow the separate formula above.

| Offense | Loss | Offense | Loss |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| Attempted breaking and entering | 10 | Trespassing | 5 |
| Breaking and entering | 10 | Assault | 8 |
| Murder | 20 | Tax evasion | 10 |
| Criminal conspiracy | 2 | Vagrancy | 1 |
| Smuggling | 2 | Piracy | 2 |
| High treason | 75 | Pickpocketing | 2 |
| Theft | 8 | Treason | 36 |
| Loan default, separately governed by LAW-006 | 10 | | |

For example, Murder commonly applies −20 legal and −10 direct faction standing. With both losses fully applied, sentence completion requests +9 legal and +4 faction. At initial legal standing −98, the same conviction applies only two legal loss points. Its stock legal and faction recovery are both zero. Vagrancy also produces zero stock recovery.

## Calendar boundaries and faction recovery

- Process calendar boundaries strictly after the start through the destination of an eligible committed advance, matching the approved FACT-004 endpoint convention. Share the calendar boundary with faction normalization while each owner commits its own effects. Zero, backwards time and duplicate delivery add no normalization. Clamp a modded step at zero.
- Record faction recovery against the original case's direct local loss. Use a direct FACT change for that recovery. Separate relationship spillover follows its own standing rules. If a required faction mapping is missing, preserve the legal result and retain the missing faction effect for explicit recovery. Authored jurisdictions may explicitly opt out of spillover.

## Required data, inputs and outputs

Retain case, charge, jurisdiction, local faction, policy/version and committed outcome identities, requested and actual legal and faction losses, recovery state and calendar progress. LAW-001 supplies the selected charge, the court supplies guilty outcome and eligible sentence completion, LAW-006 supplies administrative default, TIME supplies committed advances, and FACT-002/003 owns faction changes and applicable propagation. Output committed legal deltas and separately tracked faction requests and outcomes. Court, pursuit and surrender consume current committed regional legal standing. Target Godot 4.7.2. Concrete APIs remain open.

## Edge cases, configuration and persistence

Persist standing, case loss and recovery records, normalization progress and any unfinished handoffs at supported save points. Recovery or reload cannot repeat a penalty, propagated effect or normalization boundary. Resolve jurisdiction from the originating case or loan even if the player moves. Validate modded intervals, bounds, loss and recovery rules. Preserve captured loss records across policy changes. Missing providers and partial owner failures require explicit recovery with already committed effects retained.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Filing, surrender offer, arrest and acquittal leave ordinary case standing unchanged. A committed guilty outcome applies the selected loss and faction effect once. LAW-006 default still applies its separate once-per-loan outcome.
2. Verify floor rounding, bounds, zero recovery for one-point loss, and the Murder examples above. Recovery affects the original jurisdiction and local faction and cannot replay on reload.
3. Compare one long eligible time advance with shorter advances over the same boundaries. Include an exact destination boundary, rest, travel and a prison advance. Verify no catch-up after prison time and no changes to warrants or banishment.
4. Test custom profiles, missing faction mapping, partial failure and recovery, and duplicate court or time notifications.
