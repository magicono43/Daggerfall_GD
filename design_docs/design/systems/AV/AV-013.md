# AV-013 — Cinematics, replacements and skip events

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-17 with “I approve”. See AV-013-A01 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#av-013-interview-decisions).

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

Play classic or replacement cinematics with correct framing and audio. A fresh button press skips an eligible cinematic by default. Players can choose dedicated Skip or Back for configurable cinematics. When playback ends, control passes cleanly to the appropriate menu, quest or gameplay context.

## Confirmed requirements

[AV-013-D01 and D02](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#av-013-interview-decisions) confirm any-button skip by default, configurable and moddable skip behavior, classified terminal results, classic VID support, multiple modern replacement formats and aspect-preserving framing. AV-013-A01 approves the complete specification.

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

In the local DFU 1.1.1 release snapshot, `Assets/Scripts/Game/UserInterfaceWindows/DaggerfallVidPlayerWindow.cs#Setup` tries a replacement before opening the classic 320×200 player. `Update` handles Back, enabled any-key skip and the backend's end condition through a shared finish and global end callback. These paths dispose the player and restore cursor handling. The static trace does not establish cleanup for all external removals or decoder failures.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

1. Accept a logical video ID and an owning flow. Resolve media under DATA-003/023, using a validated replacement when authorized by the owner and the original VID when no replacement is selected. Invalid selected contributions use explicit content recovery. Once playback is attempted, classify missing, unplayable or interrupted media according to its applicable lifecycle and the requesting owner's policy.
2. Support original VID and validated modern replacements. Launch targets for verification are WebM with VP8 or VP9 and Vorbis or Opus audio, and MP4 with H.264 and AAC audio. Accept video-only assets in supported profiles. Document verified container, codec, stream and platform combinations. Additional formats can be enabled through validated providers. An extension or container match alone does not establish support.
3. Fit the video inside the presentation area without cropping. Center it and preserve the authored display aspect, including the verified classic aspect correction where applicable. Use a shared audio/video timeline when audio exists. Cinematic composition belongs to its presentation scope and preserves HUD and input ownership.
4. Once the cinematic owns input, default to fresh keyboard, mouse or digital controller button presses for skip. Ignore held input, repeats, motion and analog drift. Allow a player to select dedicated Skip or Back on configurable in-game cinematics. Validate mod-authored skip profiles against the requesting flow. Resolve mandatory owner rules first, then an explicit applicable player preference, then the authored profile or stock default. Startup and death retain UI-008 and CORE-008 input rules. Consume the triggering activation and require fresh input in the next context.
5. Scope video, audio, input, cursor, HUD and ambience requests to the current attempt. World ambience is suppressed by default under AV-010, with authored keep or duck options where permitted. Use independent presentation time for clips allowed to run during world suspension. Release only the attempt's requests on every terminal path, including failure before playback and external cancellation. Restore effective state through UI and CORE.
6. Publish one terminal result per attempt after releasing owned resources and overrides. **Finished** means natural completion. **Skipped** means accepted skip input. **Failed** means a required playback dependency or the playback path failed. **Cancelled** means the owning scope explicitly withdrew or retired the attempt. Scope callbacks to the attempt and ignore late or duplicate callbacks. The owning flow defines what each result permits. Playback success alone cannot mutate quest or gameplay state.
7. Preserve startup's once-per-launch optional presentation and setting under UI-008. Preserve CORE-008's death-video scope, early skip and direct menu bypass if missing or unplayable, with diagnostic logging and no replacement death notice. A menu retry uses the recorded presentation outcome. Other cinematics follow their explicit owner continuation and save rules.

## Formulas, parameters and configuration

For an available area of width `W` and height `H` and valid authored display aspect `a`, fit width `min(W, H × a)` and derive height from the aspect. Center the resulting rectangle. Configurable inputs include profile ID, display aspect, compatible media types, audio gain, skip policy, ambience policy and validated recovery requirements. Exact timing tolerances and stall detection bounds remain open.

## Required data

Logical media and resolved provenance, requesting flow and attempt identities, validated stream metadata, presentation rectangle and aspect, effective skip and ambience policies, active view and ownership requests, playback state and terminal result.

## Inputs, outputs and events

- **Inputs** include a video request, profile and preferences, media readiness and playback status, deliberate skip input and owner cancellation.
- **Outputs** include synchronized presentation, classified terminal status and attributable diagnostics.
- **Events** identify playback start and exactly one terminal result per admitted attempt. Cleanup and continuation use the matching flow and attempt identities.

## Cross-system review

- **UI-008 and CORE-008/016** govern title and death behavior, input, presentation scopes and handoff. **DATA-001/003/023** govern optional startup media, replacements and validation.
- **AV-010** governs scoped ambience suppression and current-context resumption. **AV-012** and the applicable flow govern music scope.
- **QUEST and other requesters** own advancement and fallback decisions. They consume classified results under their existing commitment and persistence rules.

The specification follows the reviewed startup, death, media and ambience approval boundaries.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- Test skipping during preparation after input ownership, a natural end coinciding with skip, late decoder errors, external view removal and duplicate callbacks. Settle the accepted terminal outcome once.
- Test held input from a previous panel, analog drift and skip with a menu control under the pointer. The new context requires fresh input.
- Verify unavailable or mismatched codecs, corrupted streams, audio/video drift and unsupported aspect metadata. Follow the applicable owner recovery and release attempt-scoped requests.

## Save/load considerations

Persist the player skip preference as an application setting. Treat decoder state and exact playback position as transient unless a requesting owner defines an explicit supported save contract. Owners retain committed outcomes needed to avoid replay, including death presentation across menu retries. Restore uses the applicable flow and emits no synthetic completion.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Keep media resolution, playback and flow continuation distinct. Verify real decoding, synchronization and resource release on target platforms. Use attempt-scoped input and audio ownership across successful and unsuccessful playback.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Original VID and verified modern replacement profiles play with correct framing and synchronization. Invalid selected assets follow explicit recovery.
2. Eligible videos default to any fresh digital button. Configurable in-game videos honor dedicated input and validated mod policy. Held inputs and motion do not skip or activate the next context.
3. Finished, Skipped, Failed and Cancelled each settle once after cleanup. Duplicate and obsolete callbacks cannot advance the flow or clear another attempt's requests.
4. Default cinematic ambience suppression and authored keep or duck policies release properly and use current world context on return.
5. Optional startup skips or bypasses as approved. Failed death playback reaches the normal menu directly, with no substitute text, replay on menu retry or revived session.

## Outstanding questions
