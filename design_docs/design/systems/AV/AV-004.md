# AV-004 — Lighting, fog and underwater view

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-17 with “I approve”. See AV-004-A01 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#av-004-interview-decisions).

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

Make time, weather, shelter and immersion visually legible. A player diving during clear weather and surfacing during a storm sees the current storm atmosphere on return. Moving between blocks in one continuous pool preserves the underwater view. Graphics preferences and visual mods preserve the gameplay rules for detection, sunlight and breathing.

## Confirmed requirements

[AV-004-D01 and D02](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#av-004-interview-decisions) confirm atmosphere precedence, return to the current atmosphere, moddable parameters and the presentation/gameplay boundary. AV-004-A01 approves the complete specification.

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

In the local DFU 1.1.1 release snapshot, `Assets/Scripts/Game/SunlightManager.cs#Update` samples time-based light and sun angle. `WeatherManager.cs#SetFog` and `SetSunlightScale` configure weather fog, sunlight scaling and shadow strength. Building and dungeon handlers install local fog settings. `UnderwaterFog.cs#UpdateFog` derives a blend from camera height relative to dungeon water, applies dark green exponential fog and restores previously captured fog fields when the blend returns to zero. `Internal/DaggerfallSky.cs#SetSkyFogColor` also writes global fog color. These findings come from static inspection. See the [AV architecture review](../../../analysis/architecture/AV_ARCHITECTURE_REVIEW.md).

## Detailed rules and behaviors

1. Resolve base atmosphere for each supported view from a coherent snapshot of committed time, effective local context and weather. Exteriors use weather fog and TIME-007 daylight and lighting targets. Enclosed buildings and dungeons use their resolved local fog and lighting, including applicable authored area overrides. Partially exposed zones and exterior views follow TIME-006 and the authored scope. Use the AV-003 sky or horizon color only where the effective profile calls for it.
2. Compose underwater fog, tint and any declared light attenuation over the base atmosphere when the view is immersed in eligible depth-enabled water. Use validated local water extent and surface and the committed physical context. Anchor immersion evaluation to a stable view position derived from committed pose and posture. Cosmetic bob and interpolation cannot trigger transitions. Keep view immersion distinct from MOVE-014 breath and swimming classifications. Surface-only water does not supply an underwater view.
3. Continue resolving the base atmosphere while submerged. On surfacing, blend toward the current context, weather and lighting. A continuous pool seam or origin shift preserves immersion and applicable blend state. Changes between separate media or atmosphere volumes resolve using validated spatial precedence. Local light sources and material emission retain their owner state throughout.
4. Apply live weather and climate blending under TIME-006 and ordinary daylight changes under TIME-007. Mod profiles can declare finite blend durations, including immediate application. Retarget from the currently presented state when a new target arrives. On covered transitions, fast travel or native load, initialize the applicable destination lighting and fog before reveal, including an underwater view where applicable. Do not replay skipped transitions or entry effects.
5. Give AV one composition path per view with defined precedence. Scope overrides to their eligible area, water volume or view. Discard stale results on view, location or session changes. Rendered color, density, shadows and brightness cannot drive gameplay detection, targeting, sunlight exposure, breath or movement. Any gameplay visibility rule uses explicit inputs defined by its owner.
6. Accept validated mod profiles for colors, fog mode and density or range, ambient and directional light scales, underwater attenuation, selectors, local overrides and blend settings. Reject invalid selected contributions through DATA-003 recovery. For optional effects, use a declared compatible fallback or retain the applicable valid base presentation with a diagnostic. Missing authoritative context follows its owner recovery.

## Formulas, parameters and configuration

For a positive blend duration `d`, normalize progress as `clamp(elapsed presentation time / d, 0, 1)` and apply the declared curve. Zero duration applies the target immediately. Mode-specific interpolation and transitions between incompatible fog modes require a validated policy. Require finite values and valid ranges, including nonnegative density and ordered start and end distances where used. Exact stock values, validated immersion tolerances, attenuation curves and light composition limits require fixtures.

## Required data

Committed time and daylight schedule, effective weather and local atmosphere scope, view identity and activation, applicable sky or horizon color, resolved lighting and fog profiles with provenance, stable view position from pose and posture, depth-enabled water geometry and medium identity, local light and emission inputs, and declared settings and fallback policies.

## Inputs, outputs and events

- **Inputs** include committed environment, view, and water context changes, resolved profiles, local presentation inputs and graphics preferences.
- **Outputs** include effective per-view ambient and directional lighting, fog, underwater tint and attenuation, plus diagnostic and readiness results.
- **Events** report presentation readiness, failure or retirement for the applicable view. Gameplay water-entry feedback, time progression and detection results remain with their owners.

## Cross-system review

- **TIME-006/007/018** govern effective weather, interior precedence, daylight, exposure, live transitions and destination restore.
- **LOC-012 and MOVE-014** govern validated water extents, continuous seams, committed pose and physical water context. **CHAR-018** owns breath and recovery.
- **AV-001 and AV-003** supply material emission and sky/horizon inputs. **DATA-003** governs profile precedence, validation and recovery.

An authored exterior view can show outdoor conditions without changing gameplay exposure.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- Check weather changes while submerged, surfacing under a roof, and transitioning through adjacent or stacked water areas. Never apply water fog on a dry floor that shares horizontal coordinates with a pool.
- Test cosmetic camera bob close to the waterline, physically committed posture changes, continuous pool seams, and surface-only exterior water.
- Resolve simultaneous weather, local area, light and water changes from one applicable snapshot. Reject obsolete view results and diagnose unexpected or conflicting required context through its owner.

## Save/load considerations

Rebuild atmosphere and immersion presentation from restored committed state, resolved assets and active graphics preferences before reveal. Fog and blend progress are derived presentation state. Loading preserves authoritative breath and water context without introducing elapsed game time, synthetic splashes or repeated effects.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Keep atmosphere composition scoped to each eligible view. Share equivalent resources only where doing so preserves independent view settings. Validate appearance and transition costs in representative outdoor, indoor and underwater scenes.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Exterior and local indoor views use their applicable profiles. Eligible exterior views within an interior show their own context. Sky-driven fog color affects only profiles that request it.
2. An eligible depth-enabled view uses the underwater profile. After a storm starts during the dive, surfacing blends to the current storm atmosphere. Cosmetic camera bob, a continuous pool seam and surface-only water produce no false underwater transitions.
3. Mod profiles apply validated colors, fog, attenuation and blend settings. Invalid contributions follow explicit recovery.
4. Graphics and atmosphere changes preserve gameplay detection, targeting, exposure and breath results for the same authoritative inputs. Light and water presentation changes preserve underlying owner state.
5. Loading underwater and covered arrival into a different atmosphere reveal the correct destination view without a stale fog flash or replayed entry effects.

## Outstanding questions

- Verify stock colors, density/range, shadow and attenuation values against representative scenes. Establish immersion tolerances and profile mappings for special areas.
- Specify supported fog interpolation, how attenuation combines with local lights and material emission, and any renderer limits for simultaneous views.
