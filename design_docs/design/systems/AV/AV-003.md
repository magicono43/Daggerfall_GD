# AV-003 — Sky rendering and time context

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-16 with “I approve”. See AV-003-A01 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#av-003-interview-decisions).

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

Give outdoor exploration a recognizable horizon and readable changes in time, season and weather. The stock sky uses classic imagery, moves faster near dawn and dusk and retains the classic inclement-weather image at night. Mod profiles can supply different imagery and behavior.

## Confirmed requirements

[AV-003-D01 and D02](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#av-003-interview-decisions) confirm the classic imagery default, schedule-relative progression, moddable sky profiles and providers, and stock frame-zero night behavior for weather skies. AV-003-A01 approves the complete specification.

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

In the local DFU 1.1.1 release snapshot, `Assets/Scripts/Internal/DaggerfallSky.cs#ApplyTimeAndSpace` selects a climate sky base plus a seasonal or supported rain/snow variant. It samples a nonlinear daytime frame curve and selects frame zero at night. `LoadCurrentSky` uses the day-texture path when inclement weather suppresses clear-night imagery. `LoadVanillaNightSky` draws optional stars into clear palette regions of the night image. `DrawSky` renders the sky and `SetSkyFogColor` can set global fog color. The source skips sky updates above a time-scale threshold. These are static findings. See the [AV architecture review](../../../analysis/architecture/AV_ARCHITECTURE_REVIEW.md).

## Detailed rules and behaviors

1. Resolve an eligible view's sky from committed time, climate, season, weather, daylight schedule and local presentation context. For the stock profile, use the supported climate and season imagery in normal weather and the applicable rain or snow sky where the stock mapping selects one. Use the same resolved context for the visible sky and its reported horizon color.
2. During the stock daytime interval, sample the approved classic-style progression over the active TIME-007 daylight interval. Preserve faster change toward the ends and slower movement around midday. At night, use the corresponding clear-night imagery in normal weather, with optional stars. For the stock inclement-weather path, display frame zero of the selected weather sky throughout the night and suppress the clear-night image and its stars. This is a sky image rule. Local light levels continue to follow approved lighting policy.
3. Let validated mods add or override imagery, context selectors and precedence, curve and frame mapping, stars, night weather treatment and compatible custom sky providers. A mod can provide a dedicated storm-night sky or declare weather-dependent star visibility. Resolve precedence and required assets through DATA-003 and DATA-022. A custom provider consumes the same committed context and obeys the same readiness and lifecycle boundaries.
4. In live play, follow TIME-006 blend rules for weather and seamless climate changes. Retarget from the visible state and keep the current blend when the effective target is unchanged. Honor declared zero-duration changes. On fast travel, covered transitions and native load, prepare the destination sky directly at its committed context before reveal. Time jumps need only the resulting target. Sky resource work does not replay skipped frames or weather changes.
5. Use approved interior precedence. Ordinary enclosed views use their local presentation. An authored window, portal or exposed zone may request an eligible exterior sky through its local view context. A sky view does not confer gameplay exposure. Provide sky or horizon color as input to AV-004 fog composition, with final fog and underwater precedence belonging to that feature.

## Formulas, parameters and configuration

For stock daylight, evaluate normalized time as `u = clamp((local time − dawn) / (dusk − dawn), 0, 1)` using the resolved TIME-007 schedule. The profile maps `u` to the valid daytime frame range through its progression curve. The exact classic curve and frame boundaries require fixture verification. Night selection uses the authoritative day/night boundary. Mod profile inputs include selectors, image sequences, curves, star policy, permitted transitions and provider references.

## Required data

Validated sky profile and resolved resource provenance, texture or provider inputs, climate and season mappings, committed weather and time, resolved daylight schedule, view eligibility and activation identity, frame and horizon conventions, and declared asset requiredness.

## Inputs, outputs and events

- **Inputs** include committed time and context snapshots, area and view activation, graphics preferences, and provider or texture readiness.
- **Outputs** include the effective sky target, composited view and sky or horizon color for dependent presentation.
- **Events** report sky preparation, failure or retirement for the affected view and activation. Sky updates produce no authoritative time, weather or exposure events.

## Cross-system review

- **DATA-003/006/022** govern validated profiles, climate data, image compatibility and replacement recovery.
- **TIME-006/007/018** govern effective environment, live blending, schedule and restore order. Sky selection consumes these inputs without changing exposure or retained weather.
- **LOC/WORLD** provide local view context and readiness. **AV-004** owns final fog, lighting and underwater composition. **AV-005** owns retro viewport composition.

These rules follow the reviewed time and weather boundaries, including initialization before reveal and independence of gameplay sunlight.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- Resolve simultaneous weather, schedule and location changes from one applicable committed snapshot. Discard superseded work when an area, view or session changes.
- Test dawn and dusk endpoints, horizon seams, camera rotation, and mismatched replacement imagery. Validate frame bounds, compatible coverage and finite curve parameters before use.
- Apply explicit recovery for an invalid selected replacement or unavailable required sky dependency. Optional authored exterior cues may use their declared fallback or retain valid interior presentation with a diagnostic under TIME-006.

## Save/load considerations

Reconstruct the destination sky from restored time, retained weather, location, content and graphics preferences before reveal. Treat blend progress, sky textures and generated stars as derived presentation state. Keep any generated star pattern stable for the applicable sky profile across revisits and native restores.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Separate resolved sky profiles and view context from rendering resources. Scope simultaneous views independently where they require different context. Reuse equivalent resources and release retired textures and provider state. Coordinate sky color with AV-004 and support the approved view composition when AV-005 is defined.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Supported classic climate and season imagery appears in normal weather. Daytime progression follows the resolved daylight interval and changes faster near dawn and dusk than at midday.
2. Clear nights use the applicable night image and respect configured star visibility. A supported stock rain or snow sky uses its frame zero at night with clear-night stars suppressed. A valid mod can override either behavior.
3. An ordinary weather or climate transition blends according to the resolved profile and retargets coherently if interrupted. Native restore and covered arrivals reveal the destination sky without replaying skipped transitions.
4. An enclosed view retains local presentation. An eligible authored window or exposed zone can show the proper exterior sky without changing gameplay exposure.
5. A malformed selected replacement triggers explicit recovery. A missing optional exterior cue uses the declared fallback or retains the valid interior presentation with diagnostics.
