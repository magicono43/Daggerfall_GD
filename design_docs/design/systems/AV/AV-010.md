# AV-010 — Ambient loops, weather and spot effects

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-17 with “I approve”. See AV-010-A01 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#av-010-interview-decisions).

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

Make places recognizable through ambient sound. A storm can fade in rain while a nearby stream continues uninterrupted. Enclosed areas retain local ambience, with authored options for outside cues. Thunder can remain audible when the player reduces or disables lightning flashes.

## Confirmed requirements

[AV-010-D01 and D02](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#av-010-interview-decisions) confirm classic defaults, moddable ambient layers and spot cues, continuity for unchanged layers, cinematic ambience policy and lightning accessibility. AV-010-A01 approves the complete specification.

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

In the local DFU 1.1.1 release snapshot, `Assets/Scripts/Game/AmbientEffectsPlayer.cs#ApplyPresets` selects dungeon, storm and sunny-day spot cue sets. The component also handles rain and cricket loops, local and distant one-shots, and dedicated cemetery, water and lightning behavior. Some water cues consume shared DFRandom and Unity random draws. `AmbientEffectsPlayer_OnVideoStart` stops ambient loops and sets mute, and `AmbientEffectsPlayer_OnVideoEnd` clears mute. This is a static source review. Exact stock assets, mappings and rates need verification.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

1. Resolve eligible layers from a coherent snapshot of committed location, area, time of day, weather and applicable water or exposure context. Default to classic clips and recognizable contextual mappings. A validated profile declares whether layers coexist, override a named layer or form an exclusive group, with deterministic precedence. Use AV-009 routing, distance and optional muffling for playback.
2. Enclosed spaces use local ambience by default. Authored roof rain, distant thunder and other exterior cues can consume the retained outdoor weather through TIME-006 within their declared scope. An underwater presentation profile can select eligible cues through committed water context. Audible ambience leaves exposure and NPC hearing with their gameplay owners.
3. During ordinary weather and seamless location changes, crossfade changed layers using the resolved profile and continue unchanged eligible loops without restarting phase. Retarget an interrupted fade from its current audible state. Retire superseded sources. Covered travel, entry and native load prepare the destination from current context before reveal, without carrying obsolete source layers across the reveal.
4. Schedule optional spot effects through validated timing, selection and placement profiles using presentation-only randomness under RULE-003. Support local anchors and compatible distant playback. On pause, covered transition, load or retirement, suppress obsolete pending cues and avoid catch-up bursts. When eligible playback resumes, use a current-context schedule. Keep profiles for local drips, distant calls, cemetery effects, thunder and similar cues moddable.
5. When an eligible storm spot effect pairs thunder with lightning, use a common presentation occurrence and the profile's declared offsets. Apply AV-008 Flashing Effects to lightning, including mod effects. Reduced or Off leaves thunder eligible under audio settings and the profile. Ambient playback and lightning visuals do not drive gameplay noise, exposure or weather transitions.
6. Suppress world ambience by default for an active cinematic. A validated cinematic can keep or duck it. Scope each request to the owning playback lifetime. Apply the lowest gain among active requests alongside existing audio controls. Release only the ending request on finish, skip, failure or cancellation. When world ambience becomes audible again, resolve current context and avoid replaying suppressed one-shots.
7. Allow validated mod definitions to customize layers, loops, cue pools, timing and placement, transition durations, gain, priorities, coexistence and presentation alternates. Apply DATA-003/023 validation and recovery for invalid selected replacements. Use declared fallback or permitted silence for missing optional media, with diagnostics. Apply AV-008/009 playback priorities and budgets during contention.

## Formulas, parameters and configuration

For a layer with a positive blend duration, use normalized progress `clamp(elapsed presentation time / duration, 0, 1)` with its declared gain curve. Zero duration applies the destination gain immediately. Validate finite gains, fade and spot interval ranges, selection weights, supported placement rules and accessibility alternatives. Exact stock values and playback budgets require fixtures.

## Required data

Committed context and activation identity, resolved layer and spot profiles with provenance, logical media and anchor references, timing and blend policies, effective audio and flashing preferences, active cinematic requests, and fallback declarations.

## Inputs, outputs and events

- **Inputs** include committed environment and location changes, area readiness, cue eligibility and presentation time, audio and flashing preferences, and cinematic scope changes.
- **Outputs** include effective layers, transition gains, eligible spot cue requests, lightning and thunder presentation, and diagnostics.
- **Events** report applicable presentation readiness and playback lifecycle. Ambient events carry no gameplay authority.

## Cross-system review

- **TIME-006/018** govern live blending, enclosed context and preparation before reveal. **LOC/WORLD and MOVE-014** supply applicable area and water context.
- **AV-008/009** govern flashing accessibility, cue contention and clip playback. **AV-013** will own cinematic playback outcomes. Ambience consumes scoped cinematic state.
- **DATA-003/023, MOD and RULE-003** govern validated profiles, media recovery and independent presentation randomness.

The specification follows approved weather, spatial playback and accessibility rules.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- Test unchanged loops across weather changes, rapid retargeting, indoor transitions and weather that changes during a cinematic. Apply only results for the current context and activation.
- Handle skipped or failed cinematics and overlapping suppression requests without leaving ambience stuck muted. A stale completion cannot clear another active request.
- Check absent optional clips, competing spot events, pauses and time jumps. Never replay missed spots as a backlog.

## Save/load considerations

Rebuild layers and spot schedules from the restored location, retained weather and audio preferences. Loop phase and spot timers are derived presentation state on native load. Restore the destination mix before reveal and emit no historical spots or weather events.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Scope schedules, clips and transition resources to the active session and view or listener. Share immutable clips through AV-009 while keeping playback instances independent. Verify layer counts, fade costs and resource teardown on representative transitions.

## Acceptance criteria

1. An outdoor weather change crossfades eligible changed layers and leaves an unchanged stream loop continuous. Rapid retargeting starts from currently audible gains.
2. A stock enclosed area suppresses direct exterior ambience. A valid mod can add a roof rain or distant thunder cue without changing exposure.
3. Valid mods can add or replace layers and spot profiles, change coexistence and tune timing. Pause, loading and area changes do not create a backlog.
4. Cinematics suppress ambience by default and support authored keep or duck behavior. Finish, skip, failure and cancellation release the applicable request and resume from current context.
5. Flashing Effects controls storm lightning while thunder remains eligible. Missing optional clips use diagnosed recovery and leave gameplay hearing and weather unchanged.
