# AV-012 — Song replacements and MIDI synthesis

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-17 with “I approve”. See AV-012-A01 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#av-012-interview-decisions).

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

Play the classic soundtrack through MIDI synthesis and let players choose compatible instrument banks. Modders can replace songs with MIDI arrangements or recordings. Music transitions and track endings work consistently across playback paths.

## Confirmed requirements

[AV-012-D01 and D02](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#av-012-interview-decisions) confirm stock MIDI synthesis, validated MIDI and recorded replacements, selectable SF2 banks, independent backends, and explicit recovery. AV-012-A01 approves the complete specification.

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

In the local DFU 1.1.1 release snapshot, `Assets/Scripts/Internal/DaggerfallSongPlayer.cs#Play` initializes synthesis before checking for imported recordings. `InitSynth` uses the default or a configured SF2 bank, with an observed default-bank fallback for a missing custom bank file. The MIDI path can resolve replacement bytes. `OnAudioFilterRead` renders synthesizer samples, and update logic tracks playback and looping. These findings come from static inspection. Platform compatibility, transitions and timing have yet to be verified with playback fixtures.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

1. Receive AV-011's logical song request and resolve its media using DATA-003/023 precedence and provenance. Play stock music through a validated MIDI sequencer and default SoundFont. Accept validated mod MIDI and SF2 resources and recorded WAV and Ogg Vorbis assets within documented platform capabilities. An absent replacement selects the supported stock input. Invalid selected contributions use explicit recovery.
2. Choose playback from the resolved media type. Prepare recordings independently of any MIDI bank or synthesizer. MIDI playback requires a compatible validated bank and sequencer. Preserve declared timing, including MIDI tempo changes and supported loop metadata. Validate runtime capabilities before declaring readiness where practical.
3. Allow a player to select a validated mod SoundFont as an application preference. Capture the selected bank per playback instance and apply changes at the next admitted MIDI request. A missing optional saved bank reports the issue and selects the validated default. A present invalid selected bank uses DATA/MOD recovery. A failed or unavailable default produces a classified MIDI playback failure and leaves recorded playback eligible.
4. Give each admitted playback its own source identity, backend state, gain, cursor and lifecycle. Support simultaneous outgoing and incoming playback for AV-011 crossfades, including transitions across backend types. Prepare the incoming track before publishing the handoff. Retarget interrupted fades through AV-011 from the audible mix and retire obsolete instances. Shared decoded resources and bank data preserve independent playback state.
5. Report an attributable natural end or eligible loop boundary so AV-011 can resolve a pending outdoor switch. Support declared looping when music remains eligible. Distinguish cancellation, deliberate replacement and playback failure from a natural boundary. Ignore stale callbacks from retired playback. Backend restart or delayed readiness cannot trigger another playlist choice by itself.
6. Apply live music gain and applicable owner scope. Cinematic playback and music suspension follow their owning policies. When music resumes, honor the current AV-011 request. Preserve the current eligible track if preparation of its replacement fails. Otherwise apply declared compatible fallback or diagnosed silence under AV-011. Bound retries and diagnose the failing backend, song or bank. Playback state has no gameplay authority.

## Formulas, parameters and configuration

Use the selected score's timing or recording's presentation time as the playback timeline. A fade with duration `d > 0` samples its declared gain curve at `clamp(elapsed presentation time / d, 0, 1)`. Zero duration applies an immediate handoff. Validate format and channel limits, MIDI timing and loop ranges, bank capabilities, finite gains and fade values. Exact limits, fade curves and default bank identity require verification.

## Required data

Logical song and playback identities, resolved media and bank provenance, media backend and capability metadata, MIDI timing or recording duration and loop information, current gain, transitions and scope, and declared fallback policies.

## Inputs, outputs and events

- **Inputs** include logical track requests, media and bank selection, loop policy, mix and lifecycle requests, and playback capabilities.
- **Outputs** include prepared or active audio, independent playback state, and classified readiness and failure results.
- **Events** include readiness, natural end or eligible loop boundary, cancellation, retirement and failure with the applicable playback identity.

## Cross-system review

- **AV-011** selects tracks, determines when to switch and owns optional music recovery. AV-012 realizes those requests and reports usable boundaries.
- **DATA-003/023 and MOD** govern source resolution, validation and replacement recovery. An unavailable optional saved preference can use its declared default-bank fallback.
- **CORE and AV-013** own relevant application and cinematic scope. AV-010's ambient layers retain their separate playback policy.

The specification follows the approved music selection, media recovery and resource isolation rules.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- Test a recorded replacement with unavailable synthesis, missing optional saved banks, malformed selected banks and unsupported recordings. Distinguish absent assets from invalid contributions and backend failures.
- Exercise MIDI-to-recording, recording-to-MIDI and same-backend fades, including late readiness, failure and overlapping requests. Ignore ends from superseded instances.
- Verify tempo changes, timing, loop-boundary uniqueness, and music gain or pause changes during fades.

## Save/load considerations

Persist bank and mix selections as application preferences. Native game loading lets AV-011 reselect from restored context. Rebuild backend state from the effective preferences and track request. Exact playback positions, fades and synthesis buffers are transient.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Verify supported decoders and synthesis on target platforms. Keep media preparation outside real-time audio processing where practical. Include resolved asset, decoder settings and bank inputs in applicable cache keys. Measure resource use and uninterrupted playback for overlapping instances.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Stock MIDI plays with the validated default SF2. A valid mod MIDI and bank can produce a different arrangement or instrumentation.
2. A supported recorded replacement plays with MIDI initialization unavailable. Representative WAV and Ogg Vorbis fixtures verify declared platform support.
3. A missing optional saved bank reports the fallback. Invalid selected assets use explicit recovery. An optional playback failure preserves eligible music or uses declared fallback or diagnosed silence.
4. Both backends support track endings or loop boundaries and same- or cross-backend fades. Stale and duplicate notifications do not restart music or trigger an extra switch.
5. Loading reconstructs playback from AV-011's selection and effective preferences without replaying prior transitions.
