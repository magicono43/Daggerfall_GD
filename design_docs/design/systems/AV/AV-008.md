# AV-008 — Impact, missile and movement cues

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-17 with “I approve”. See AV-008-A01 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#av-008-interview-decisions).

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

Communicate projectile travel, resolved impacts, injury and traversal through recognizable visual and audio cues. Stock cues use classic sprites and sounds. Players can reduce flashing, disable blood effects and adjust sound or camera motion independently. Mods can replace stock cues and author new effects.

## Confirmed requirements

[AV-008-D01 and D02](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#av-008-interview-decisions) confirm normal blood and flashing by default, accessibility controls, moddable cues and presentation priorities. AV-008-A01 approves the complete specification.

In the local DFU 1.1.1 snapshot, `Assets/Scripts/Game/DaggerfallMissile.cs#UseSpellBillboardAnims`, `UpdateLight` and `PlayImpactSound` drive billboard, light and impact audio. `EnemyBlood.cs#ShowBloodSplash` spawns a one-shot blood billboard. `PlayerFootsteps.cs#FixedUpdate` selects movement clips from travel and surface context, and `Game/Player/HeadBobber.cs` supplies camera movement. ## Detailed rules and behaviors

1. Select attack feedback from committed contact and settled owner outcomes. Apply COMBAT-012's distinct policies for miss, parry or prevention, zero damage, physical injury, magical effects and death. Default physical blood and pain cues require actual settled physical health loss. Suppressed or unavailable feedback leaves damage, knockback, practice and detection intact.
2. Attach missile visuals to the authoritative projectile and follow its resolved trajectory and lifecycle. Support classic billboards and validated mod sprites, meshes, particles, lights, trails and clips. Use resolved contact for impact placement and choose the cue for the reported outcome. Expiration, interception and impact use their declared outcomes. Cosmetic offsets, visual culling and effect lifetime leave projectile collision and payload timing intact.
3. Map settled movement and surface context to footsteps, mounted and swimming cues, landings and splashes. Use MOVE-017's qualifying distance and gait rules, MOVE-010's landing classification and MOVE-014's genuine water entry. Apply the existing independent gait, mounted and landing camera controls and preserve their defaults. Avoid duplicate step, landing or splash feedback for the same resolved transition. Preserve valid independent outcomes.
4. Default **Blood Effects** to On and **Flashing Effects** to Normal. Blood Off suppresses cues tagged as blood, including mod splatters and blood particles. Offer Reduced and Off for rapid flashes. Reduced uses validated limits or a steady alternative where supplied. Off suppresses the flashing component and may retain an available steady cue. Keep persistent projectile identification where a compatible static or steady representation is available. Apply preferences to stock and mod presentations, including active cues after a setting changes. Sound and camera-motion settings retain separate controls.
5. Allow validated mods to add or replace blood, projectile and impact effects, footsteps and other movement cues. Profiles declare the applicable outcome, projectile or surface mapping, assets, priority, scope, accessibility tags and alternates, and fallback policy. A modded visual mapping cannot itself grant permission to emit a gameplay outcome. An approved gameplay owner may explicitly change its cue predicates. Use independent presentation randomness under RULE-003.
6. Deduplicate presentation deliveries by owner outcome and applicable cue identity. When valid cues compete for the same budget, favor distinct impacts, hard landings and water entry over repeated steps and decorative particles. Within equal priorities, use a stable tie policy. Cull or skip obsolete low-priority feedback, including catch-up after a stall. Keep authoritative noise and gameplay accounting independent of audio playback and render budgets.
7. For unavailable optional cue assets, use a declared compatible fallback or permitted omission or silence and diagnose the affected profile. Apply DATA recovery to invalid selected replacements or missing required resources. Pause gameplay-linked feedback with its simulation scope. Resume eligible ongoing feedback without replaying settled cues.

## Required data

Stable session, outcome, projectile and cue identities as applicable, committed contact or travel context, surface and impact outcome classification, resolved assets and provenance, eligible playback time, listener and view scope, effective accessibility and volume preferences, and declared requiredness.

## Inputs, outputs and events

- **Inputs** include committed projectile lifecycle, settled damage and contacts, resolved movement and surfaces, water and landing outcomes, pause and reset state, and preferences.
- **Outputs** include cosmetic projectile representations, impact visuals and sounds, movement cues and camera offsets, plus classified playback or failure diagnostics.
- **Events** consume owner notifications and may report presentation lifecycle. Playback cannot authorize a new hit, noise event, water entry or movement result.

- **MOVE-003/007/010/014/017** govern aim, stairs, landings, water entry and movement feedback. NPC gameplay hearing stays with its owner.

The specification follows approved outcome, camera and movement rules.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- Verify prevention after apparent contact, magic-only outcomes, lethal hits and repeated event delivery. A blood setting must never affect injury or knockback.
- Test overlapping missiles, contact next to a wall, stale projectile retirement, fast stair steps and continuous water seams. Preserve the meaningful event under presentation load.
- Check combined flashes from overlapping effects under Reduced and Off. Require a declared compatible alternate or suppress the affected optional flash if its reduced output cannot be validated.

## Save/load considerations

Persist accessibility and audio choices as application preferences. Reconstruct visuals for supported ongoing projectiles from restored owner state. Reset obsolete camera offsets and stride baselines on teleport or load under MOVE-017. Do not replay completed impact, injury, footstep or splash cues after restore.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Scope cue lifetimes and duplicate suppression to active operations and views. Validate supported sprite, mesh, particle, light and audio providers. Coordinate performance and fallback with AV-009 and verify that cosmetic camera transforms preserve authoritative aim.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Stock cues visibly and audibly respond to eligible settled outcomes at normal defaults. Prevention and zero physical loss produce no default physical blood.
2. Blood Off and Flashing Reduced or Off apply to both stock and validated mod cues. Available steady alternatives stay visible, and gameplay and independent audio settings are preserved.
3. A mod projectile sprite or 3D replacement follows the authoritative trajectory and uses the resolved impact. Mod blood, impact and footstep mappings work without changing their owner outcomes.
4. Under contention, distinct impacts, hard landings and genuine water entry take priority over competing repeat steps and decorative effects. Duplicate and obsolete notifications do not replay cues.
5. Failure, pause, teleport and native load preserve owner state and follow the approved reset or resumption rules without synthetic feedback.
