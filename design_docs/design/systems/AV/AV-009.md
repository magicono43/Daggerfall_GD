# AV-009 — Clip playback and spatial sound

**Approval status — Approved, finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-17 with “I approve”. See AV-009-A01 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#av-009-interview-decisions).

## Gameplay purpose and intended player experience

Communicate where sounds originate while keeping local action and interface cues clear. By default, world sounds become quieter with distance. Players can opt into muffling through walls and closed doors. With muffling off, distance and routing can be tested independently of obstruction.

## Confirmed requirements

[AV-009-D01 and D02](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md#av-009-interview-decisions) confirm stock routing, default distance-only falloff, opt-in muffling and validated mod overrides. AV-009-A01 approves the complete specification.

## Existing Daggerfall Unity behavior

In the local DFU 1.1.1 release snapshot, `Assets/Scripts/SoundReader.cs#GetAudioClip` resolves sound IDs or indices, caches clips and checks for replacements. `Internal/DaggerfallAudioSource.cs#PlayOneShot` accepts spatial blend and volume scale, and `PlayClipAtPoint` places one-shots at a world position. `SetSound` and `Apply` support on-demand, looping and proximity-based presets. Observed source settings include live sound volume updates. Exact attenuation varies with source configuration. This is a static source review.

## Detailed rules and behaviors

1. Resolve logical media through DATA-023. Keep reusable clip resources distinct from playback state. Each playback has its own gain, position or anchor, lifecycle and applicable mix preferences.
2. Route creatures, doors, impacts and other world cues through positional playback by default. Attach ongoing source sounds to their current anchor and locate contact sounds at the resolved contact. Route menus and explicit interface alerts through non-spatial playback. Use a stable listener-relative mix for the player's own footsteps, weapon swings and casts. Their resolved world impacts remain positional.
3. Apply validated distance falloff to positional playback. Default **World Sound Muffling** to Off. When enabled, eligible positional sounds also respond to validated wall and closed-door obstruction. Smooth changes in attenuation and filtering as the listener, source or obstruction moves. Turning muffling off restores the distance-only result. The setting gates both stock and mod cue profiles.
4. Support validated mod cue routing, clip substitutions, distance curves, muffling eligibility, strength and response settings. Define deterministic profile precedence. A cue can opt out of muffling. An unsupported optional obstruction input uses distance-only playback with an attributable diagnostic. Rendered audio has no authority over NPC hearing, detection or gameplay noise.
5. Support one-shots and owner-controlled loops. Treat pause, resume, retirement and scene changes according to the cue's declared owner scope. On contention, honor AV-008 priorities and apply bounded playback budgets. Stale or duplicate outcome deliveries cannot replay settled cues. The same clip can play simultaneously for distinct eligible outcomes.
6. Apply live gains and mutes to eligible active playback. For unavailable optional media, use a declared compatible fallback or permitted silence. Keep distinct diagnostics for media resolution and playback failure. DATA-023 governs invalid selected replacements and missing required media.

## Formulas, parameters and configuration

For positional playback, calculate gain from the cue gain, applicable mix gains and a validated distance function of listener-to-source distance. With muffling enabled and valid for the cue, add a bounded obstruction gain and filter response. With muffling off, use neutral obstruction settings. Validate finite gain, distance ranges, filter bounds and transition durations. Exact stock falloff curves, muffling values and playback budgets require fixtures.

## Required data

Logical cue and media IDs, resolved provenance, playback and owner outcome identities, world source/contact or listener-relative routing data, listener and session scope, distance and optional muffling profiles, current preferences, and optionality and fallback declarations.

## Inputs, outputs and events

- **Inputs** include owner cue requests, clip resolution, listener and emitter transforms, eligible obstruction context, preference changes and lifecycle requests.
- **Outputs** include attributable playback, effective routing and gain, and classified unavailable, bypassed or failed results.
- **Events** report applicable playback lifecycle and diagnostics. Authoritative hearing and gameplay outcomes come from their owners.

## Cross-system review

- **DATA-003/023 and MOD** govern media, profile validation, precedence and replacement recovery.
- **AV-008 and MOVE-017** govern priority, committed cue selection, pause behavior and separation from gameplay hearing. **UI** retains interface cue ownership.
- **AV-010/011 and CORE** supply ambient, music and application preference boundaries. Spatial clip playback does not select ambient programs or songs.

The specification preserves the reviewed cue priority, media recovery and gameplay ownership rules.

## Edge cases and failure conditions

- Check moving sources, doors changing state during playback, view or listener replacement, and two sources using the same clip. Retire obsolete spatial and filter state at scope changes.
- Test missing geometry and unavailable obstruction support with muffling enabled. Use the documented distance-only recovery while keeping eligible playback audible.
- Verify muted, inaudible and budget-dropped cues leave the same authoritative gameplay noise. A stalled frame cannot create a backlog of obsolete one-shots.

## Save/load considerations

Persist mix and muffling choices as application preferences. Reconstruct supported ongoing playback from the relevant owner's restored state. Transient clip positions, filters and settled one-shots are derived or retired on load. Do not replay completed impacts or footsteps.

## Technical constraints

Target Godot 4.7.2. Scope playback and obstruction work to current listeners, emitters and session. Avoid obstruction work when muffling is off. Validate pool reuse, filtering and update costs with representative rooms and simultaneous sources.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A fresh profile uses distance-only positional playback. A door or wall does not muffle a test source until the player enables the option.
2. Positional sources fall off and localize relative to the listener. Interface and designated player-local cues retain their expected routing. A player strike's impact sounds at the resolved contact.
3. Opt-in muffling responds smoothly to wall and door changes. A valid mod can tune falloff and muffling or opt a cue out. Disabling muffling restores the distance-only result for active playback.
4. Distinct instances of a clip keep independent state. Cue contention follows AV-008, optional failures follow diagnosed recovery and completed one-shots do not replay on load.
5. Routing, muting, falloff and muffling preserve authoritative gameplay hearing for identical owner inputs.
