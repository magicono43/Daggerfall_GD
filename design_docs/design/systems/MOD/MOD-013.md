# MOD-013 — Factions, guild services and interface overrides

**Approved — finalized v1.** The user approved Draft v1 on 2026-11-18.

## Purpose and intended player experience

Authors can add organizations, connect representatives to services from other packages, and customize supported screens. Players receive the correct services and entitlements for the representative they approach, even when a mod supplies the route or window.

## Daggerfall Unity reference

DFU 1.1.1 registers factions by numeric ID, guild implementations by group, and services through NPC mappings and custom callbacks. Its window factory lets a registration replace the implementation type for a window. See [FactionFile.cs](../../../../reference_material/daggerfall-unity-1.1.1-cve-2025/Assets/Scripts/API/FactionFile.cs#L710), [GuildManager.cs](../../../../reference_material/daggerfall-unity-1.1.1-cve-2025/Assets/Scripts/Game/Guilds/GuildManager.cs#L33), [Services.cs](../../../../reference_material/daggerfall-unity-1.1.1-cve-2025/Assets/Scripts/Game/Guilds/Services.cs#L202) and [UIWindowFactory.cs](../../../../reference_material/daggerfall-unity-1.1.1-cve-2025/Assets/Scripts/Game/UserInterfaceWindows/UIWindowFactory.cs#L116).

## Confirmed rules

1. Identify a new faction, guild or service by its domain, creator package ID and stable local key. Preserve target identity and provenance for explicit patches or replacements under DATA-003 and the owning domain.
2. A mod can map an authorized representative to another package's service by explicit stable reference and declared dependency. Validate the mapping, guild and variant, route and entitlement policy, and required handler before activation. Report missing or ambiguous required mappings.
3. Apply FACT-009 and FACT-014 on menu, shortcut and custom routes. Check the exact representative, guild or variant, membership and rank at opening and before payment or effects commit. Preserve visibility and discovery rules. The authorized service owner validates and settles consequential actions.
4. For an individual window, resolve the later valid replacement in enabled-mod order, then apply compatible targeted edits to that view under UI-003. Show the candidates and winner in pre-menu diagnostics. Identify the contributor and target when an edit is incompatible. Validate required actions and applicable navigation before activation.
5. Competing whole-system replacements use MOD-004's explicit provider choice. Window and service registrations follow the validated plan and application lock. Startup and recovery UI remain independently available. Runtime view failures follow UI-003, including explicit stock fallback where it safely serves the same request.

See MOD-013-D01 and D02 in the [gameplay decision log](../../GAMEPLAY_DECISIONS.md).

## Parameters, data, inputs and outputs

Inputs include package and domain IDs, representative and organization mappings, service routes and entitlement policies, declared dependencies, screen contracts, targeted edits, candidate order and owning interaction context. Outputs include resolved service mappings and views, provenance, validation diagnostics and owner-authorized service results. FACT and transaction owners define rank, price and other gameplay parameters. Concrete APIs and events remain open.

## Interactions and save/load

- [FACT-009](../FACT/FACT-009.md) resolves the exact organization and membership. [FACT-014](../FACT/FACT-014.md) governs visibility, access and consequential service checks.
- [UI-003](../UI/UI-003.md) governs replacements, targeted edits and recovery. [MOD-007](MOD-007.md) provides Content Diagnostics. [MOD-003](MOD-003.md), [MOD-004](MOD-004.md) and [MOD-005](MOD-005.md) govern dependencies, trust and registration.
- [DATA-003](../DATA/DATA-003.md) governs candidate validation and publication. Saves retain stable organization and gameplay references under owner rules. Loading checks required provider compatibility under [SAVE-017](../SAVE/SAVE-017.md) and uses the locked plan.

## Edge cases and technical constraints

- Equal local keys from different packages resolve independently. An unavailable required service provider surfaces a dependency or mapping failure.
- A shortcut to a concealed service follows its discovery gate. If entitlement changes while a replacement screen is open, commitment revalidates against the exact context.
- A replacement that removes an explicitly targeted control produces a diagnostic naming both contributions and the target. Route supported windows through the validated view selection so construction paths honor the chosen replacement.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Two packages can add organizations or services with equal local keys. A third can explicitly map a representative to one service through a declared dependency.
2. A custom service opens and commits only through the approved checks. Switching guild variants, losing rank or using a shortcut does not bypass authorization.
3. Two valid replacements for one window resolve by load order and expose provenance. An incompatible targeted edit is diagnosed before entry.
4. Competing replacements of the underlying service system require explicit provider selection. A failed window follows approved UI recovery without committing a service action.
