## Approved faction increments

The user approved this breakdown on 2026-11-18.

1. **Identity and standing.** FACT owns faction identity/hierarchy references, direct standing and approved normalization. Publish read-only reaction, rank and eligibility inputs. LAW retains legal authority and uses explicit standing handoffs.
2. **Membership.** Validate join/leave, rank changes, expulsion and crime-guild recruitment through scoped owner requests. Revalidate current qualifications at commitment and retain committed outcomes. Definition identity remains independent of guild display labels or variants.
3. **Benefits and persistence.** Return current rank/service/hall and reward entitlements. Service owners validate fulfillment and payment. Restore standing and membership facts without repeating join, promotion or reward operations.
