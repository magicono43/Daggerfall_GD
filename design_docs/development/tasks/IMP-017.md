## Approved inventory increments and identity

The user approved these increments and representation on 2026-11-18.

1. **Construction and identity.** Resolve immutable templates and material/construction rules through DATA/RULE. Distinguish definition, item and stack identities. New possessions and partial splits receive fresh identities. Ordinary modifications and transfers preserve identity under ITEM-001.
2. **Collections.** ITEM owns insertion, split, merge, removal, custody and transfer. Validate source/destination, quantities, stacking eligibility and admission before committing. Return surviving, new and retired identities explicitly. Ordinary rejection changes neither collection. Preserve committed facts and outstanding obligations on technical failure under ITEM-007.
3. **Capacity and tender.** Return approved carry/wagon admission and gold/credit denomination and tender outcomes. ECON owns quotes and transactions, while ITEM owns physical custody and instrument representation. Positive-weight admission while overweight follows ITEM-008 without introducing movement penalties.
4. **Persistence.** Capture typed identity, definition key, quantity/properties, custody and equipment references. Preserve allocator state and reject duplicate identity claims before publication. Restore actual possessions rather than re-running construction rewards.

Use a 64-bit increasing counter per category within the native save branch. Serialize counter and ID numeric components as decimal strings to preserve precision across JSON consumers. Reserve zero as an invalid sentinel, start allocation at one, and reject exhaustion rather than wrapping. Restore saved counters and validate that they cannot allocate an already retained ID. Retired IDs are not reused within that branch. Divergent save branches retain GAD-01's approved independent allocation policy.
