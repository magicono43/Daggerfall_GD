## Approved alternate-movement increments

The user approved this breakdown on 2026-11-18.

1. **Climb and edges.** Validate wall entry, climb/rappel motion and edge transitions under MOVE-011/012 and RULE-011. Preserve required velocity, support and fall history when the mode changes.
2. **Water and levitation.** Resolve submersion, swimming, buoyancy and magical traversal through the single MOVE authority. MAGIC supplies capability/lifetime. Effect expiry re-evaluates mode and clearance under the approved transition policy.
3. **Transport.** Validate mounts/carts and current exact ship entitlement before transition. ECON owns title/access and sale eligibility; MOVE/TIME own admitted boarding, visit and return context. Zero-duration ship boarding does not advance travel time.
4. **Recall.** MAGIC owns the anchor and authorized recall request. WORLD/LOC prepare the target and MOVE validates committed placement through the ordinary versioned frame and readiness contract.
