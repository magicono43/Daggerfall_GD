## Approved ranged increments

The user approved this breakdown on 2026-11-18.

1. **Bow lifecycle.** COMBAT owns admitted draw, cancellation, release and cooldown. ITEM commits ammunition under COMBAT-008's boundary. Give each admitted attack/shot its stable identity and retain committed costs/results.
2. **Flight and impact.** Capture COMBAT-009's required offensive context on release. Check the full authorized traveled segment against eligible blockers in approved order and resolve one blocking impact. Keep current defender context at its declared phase. Independent release persists under its owner policy after source loss.
3. **Enemy ranged intent.** NPC selects eligible ranged/spell intent under NPC-016. COMBAT/MAGIC validate commitment, allegiance, targeting and payload delivery. Infighting and source attribution follow COMBAT-017 rather than a separate damage implementation.

## Projectile fixture and integration

Use a pinned bow/ammunition source, a target behind a blocker, multiple actor colliders and a controlled enemy ranged archetype. Record draw/release/impact identities, ammo custody and offensive snapshot. IMP-019 supplies hit phases, IMP-033/058 spell delivery/admission and IMP-023/018 native equipment and projectile restore.

Saved in-flight state contains required pose/lifetime/source/payload facts under COMBAT-009. Recreating it cannot create a second enemy release or refund ammunition. Location departure, same-location rebase and source removal retain their distinct approved policies.

- Remove or change the bow/source after release. Verify the projectile uses its captured offensive context and approved absent-source mutation outcome.
- Sweep the projectile across the full step with a nearer wall and farther target. Verify nearest eligible blocking contact and one impact despite repeated actor colliders.
- Repeat impact callbacks and assert one damage/payload/ammunition recovery outcome. Test a shot expiring within a step against the declared contact/expiry order.
- Save and restore an in-flight projectile and a supported partial-impact obligation. Assert retained attribution and no double ammo, release or damage.
- Exercise enemy spell/ranged selection and infighting through real COMBAT/MAGIC owners with controlled allegiance and blocking geometry.
- Verify the accepted and rejected outcomes specified by the linked feature criteria; record which criteria are covered by owner tests and which require a milestone integration run.
