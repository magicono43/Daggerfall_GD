## Approved casting increments

The user approved this breakdown on 2026-11-18.

1. **Definitions and selection.** MAGIC validates registered effects/spells and classic conversion. UI-014 opens the selector through the F Cast Spell action. Inspection/highlighting has no Ready or Use effect. Explicit Ready captures stable spell/source context.
2. **Ready and affordability.** Validate authorization, current quote and applicable restrictions through MAGIC-003/RULE-018. Readying/cancellation is free. Preserve approved automatic self-target behavior and retained blocked selection. Revalidate at actual cast admission under the source-specific policy.
3. **Commitment.** Give casting a scoped identity, apply costs and release once under MAGIC-004, and retain actual outcomes through interruption or technical recovery. A stale input or callback cannot start a new cast. Independently released delivery follows its own lifetime.
4. **Delivery.** Touch, projectile and area routes use stable cast/component/target attribution and ordered geometry contact. Repeated colliders/contacts cannot repeat eligible payload admission. IMP-058 supplies actual defense/admission/lifetime and IMP-034 concrete effect consequences.

## Casting fixture and integration

Use pinned self-target, touch and projectile definitions plus an area-contact fixture. The initial payload receiver records delivery without authorizing missing real effects. Enabled production spells require valid registered effects and owner admission. UI-014's general readable-book behavior closes with IMP-064 and DATA-018.

IMP-018 closes source equipment, IMP-019 shared attack/collision outcome context, IMP-058 real bundle/defense and restore state, IMP-034 actual effects, IMP-047 crafted offers and IMP-041 enemy released delivery. Costs and action history remain owner-authoritative and distinct from service pricing.

## Acceptance

- Inspect and ready each fixture spell. Assert inspection consumes no cost/draw and ready/cancel preserves approved free preparation. Verify self-target automatic policy separately from touch/projectile activation.
- Reduce magicka or apply a blocking condition before cast admission and verify the current owner quote/restriction. Failed attempts cannot silently substitute another spell or repeat automatically.
- Interrupt before release and duplicate the same commitment callback. Assert costs, history and release obey the approved once-only phase boundary.
- Deliver touch, projectile and area contacts against a valid actor, obstruction and repeated colliders. Compare attribution and eligible payload count.
- Remove a source after an independent release and verify the route's approved source-loss policy. At IMP-058/034 replace the recording receiver with actual defenses/effects and compare resource and outcome state.
- At native restore integration, verify a readied spell cannot replay its old automatic attempt or held physical trigger.
- Verify the accepted and rejected outcomes specified by the linked feature criteria; record which criteria are covered by owner tests and which require a milestone integration run.
- Confirm that required handoff results reach the expected consumer and that rejected or obsolete input leaves committed state consistent under the approved failure policy.
