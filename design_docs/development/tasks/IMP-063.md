## Approved enforcement increments

The user approved this breakdown on 2026-11-18.

1. **Dispatch.** LAW authorizes deployment from current incident/warrant basis. NPC/MOVE perform normal guard archetype and placement validation. Failed placement returns checked failure without an active unplaced actor.
2. **Pursuit.** LAW owns immediate/delayed grounds and warrants under LAW-007/008. TIME delivers scoped occurrences. Preserve jurisdiction and cancel only obligations whose legal basis ended.
3. **Surrender/arrest.** Revalidate selected guard, combat and legal context under LAW-009/COMBAT-019. Commit one arrest handoff with retained case/outcome identity. IMP-044 consumes the committed case for court.

## Enforcement fixture and integration

Use a valid charge/warrant, blocked guard placement, expired/cancelled ground and a surrender choice pending while context changes. NPC actor state and LAW authorization remain distinct. Regional penalties/FACT spillover follow LAW-010 and the actual FACT interface.

IMP-043 supplies incidents, IMP-020 actual guards/combat intent, IMP-044 court, IMP-045 default incidents and IMP-023 persistence. The handoff cannot become a second charge or clear unrelated grounds.
