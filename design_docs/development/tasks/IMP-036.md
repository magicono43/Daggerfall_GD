## Acceptance

- For each grant-table career and qualifying/nonqualifying custom class, compare possessions, gold and spells with the approved table plus retained biography outcomes. Test item-based lighting both enabled and disabled.
- Start in the dungeon and exterior contexts. Verify valid facing/collision, initialized account/property/regional/weather state and zero gameplay advancement before readiness.
- Accept and decline tutorial separately. Assert the exact immediate queue and effective binding labels. Exterior instructions cannot require a dungeon exit. Later authored timers are not fast-forwarded.
- Fail optional tutorial and required main-story initialization separately. Verify warning/continuation versus failed-entry recovery. Retry required failure and assert no duplicate items, money, effects or quests.
- Reload the resulting native snapshot through IMP-038/023. Assert no fresh grants, tutorial offer or immediate queue replay beyond approved saved activity restoration.
- Verify the accepted and rejected outcomes specified by the linked feature criteria; record which criteria are covered by owner tests and which require a milestone integration run.
- Confirm that required handoff results reach the expected consumer and that rejected or obsolete input leaves committed state consistent under the approved failure policy.
