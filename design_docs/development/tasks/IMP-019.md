## Approved combat increments

The user approved this breakdown on 2026-11-18.

1. **Admission and timing.** COMBAT resolves readiness, equipped hand, accepted gesture/click input, wind-up, commitment and recovery under one scoped attack identity. Capture the approved authored timing profile and detect its gameplay marker through eligible simulation time.
2. **Reach and contact.** Resolve eligible contact and per-target order under COMBAT-005. Attribute contacts to stable actor/target/component identity. Multiple colliders and repeated marker delivery cannot create extra attempts. Revalidate eligibility at the approved phase boundaries.
3. **Ordered resolution.** Preserve the owner-defined phase sequence. Ordinary natural components settle numeric resolution, eligible poison/monster payloads, wear, primary health change and eligible reactive payloads under RULE-016. Strike enchantments integrate under COMBAT-011 with IMP-047. Apply actual mutations through ITEM, MAGIC and CHAR. Read current valid context before later components.
4. **Feedback.** AV receives committed weapon/impact results and follows the captured authored timeline. Rendered frame skipping cannot omit a gameplay marker, and duplicate cues cannot authorize another hit. Camera cosmetics remain separate from resolved aim.

## Attack and recovery handoff

Return distinguishable admission rejection, material rejection, miss, resolved zero damage, committed health/effect outcomes, interruption and technical failure with attack/component/target and session context. Preserve resolved draws and completed phases on recovery. Stop remaining work when source/target validity requires it; never retarget a different actor to finish the old attack.

Use a controlled actor with known stats and equipment initially. Record the precise phase trace, draw count and owner outcomes. ## Acceptance

- Drive a hit, miss and material rejection with controlled inputs and draws. Compare the phase trace, allowed draws, wear, fatigue/practice and applied health with the approved route.
- Interrupt before commitment and assert no later marker authorizes a hit. Skip a rendered frame across the marker, then deliver a duplicate marker; assert exactly one eligible attempt.
- Present multiple colliders for one actor and verify target deduplication. Test reach and obstruction around the declared geometry boundary.
- Break the source or kill the target during an earlier settled component. Preserve committed consequences and reject invalid remaining work without retargeting.
- Inject failure after an owner mutation and recover only outstanding phases with retained results. Feedback callbacks cannot repeat the mutation.
- Run the live IMP-020 encounter and inspect animation/impact synchronization in the separate rendered suite before M2 closure.
- Verify the accepted and rejected outcomes specified by the linked feature criteria; record which criteria are covered by owner tests and which require a milestone integration run.
- Confirm that required handoff results reach the expected consumer and that rejected or obsolete input leaves committed state consistent under the approved failure policy.
