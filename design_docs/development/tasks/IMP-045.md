## Approved banking increments

The user approved this breakdown on 2026-11-18.

1. **Accounts and instruments.** ECON extends the existing M3 projection from IMP-037 with regional deposits/withdrawals and credit operations. ITEM owns carried tender and physical credit custody. Validate exact selected branch, amount and fees before settlement.
2. **Loans and deadlines.** ECON owns admitted loans, repayment and reminders/default. TIME schedules due work under declared endpoint/precedence rules. Submit one attributable default incident to LAW and retain its receipt.
3. **Persistence.** Restore balances, instruments, obligations and supported settlement progress. Opening banking or loading a character cannot reset the initialized ledger or repeat payments, notices or default consequences.
