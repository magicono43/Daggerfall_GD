## Approved population increments

The user approved this breakdown on 2026-11-18.

1. **Outdoor civilians.** NPC owns population, pool leases, navigation and idling under NPC-001/002/003. Scene recycling expires its old lease and cannot make stale references address a new person. Retain owner-designated identity and assigned facts under the approved residency policy.
2. **Placed people and services.** LOC supplies stable placement and NPC binds the actor/service context under NPC-004 and LOC-007. Dialogue, quest and economy owners determine eligible interactions. Appearance and name are presentation rather than service identity.
3. **Names and property.** DATA supplies name banks and explicit generation inputs. NPC retains assigned names under DATA-017's versioned policy. LOC exposes furniture and container placement/property context while ITEM owns possessions and LAW/ECON/QUEST resolve permissions.

- Place visually similar NPCs with different stable service contexts. Verify each returns its own provider/placement identity independently of name or appearance.
- Re-enter the interior and verify owner-required actor/name/service facts remain stable. Inspecting or relocalizing names consumes no new generation draws.
- Query a furniture container's property context without granting access. At IMP-037/043 verify the real service and permission outcomes; at IMP-030 verify dialogue context matches the selected actor.
- Verify the accepted and rejected outcomes specified by the linked feature criteria; record which criteria are covered by owner tests and which require a milestone integration run.
