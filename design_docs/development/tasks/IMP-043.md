## Approved incident increments

The user approved this breakdown on 2026-11-18.

1. **Inputs.** MOVE, ITEM/ECON and COMBAT report authorized incident attempts and committed facts with actor, operation, property/location and attribution. Preserve the distinction between an undetected attempt, rejected action and detected incident.
2. **Evaluation.** LAW owns detection/charge/suppression and regional consequence policy under LAW-001 through LAW-006. Use approved RULE-010 draws where relevant and retain resolved outcomes for duplicate delivery.
3. **Integration.** Route actual pickpocket/shoplifting custody and civilian/guard attack facts through their owners. IMP-063 owns deployment/pursuit/arrest, IMP-044 court, and IMP-045 the real loan-default producer. A UI or scene transition cannot independently clear or recreate a charge.

## Incident fixture and handoff

Use a selected property/actor with controlled access, theft attempt, detection draw and a civilian attack. Return detected/undetected, suppressed, rejected or failed results under the approved incident-specific policy. Bind committed legal facts to the original jurisdiction even if presentation occurs after a transition.

An unchanged ordinary rejection cannot transfer goods or create the corresponding incident as a successful action. Approved attempt/detection incidents may still apply when the underlying theft fails, as specified by the owning feature. Validate each route's rules rather than treating every failed action alike.

- Change property/actor context before confirmation and reject stale authorization. A replacement container or actor cannot inherit the old permission.
- Repeat a committed incident identity and deliver its result after a location transition. Assert one original-jurisdiction legal outcome and no new charge from presentation.

- Verify the accepted and rejected outcomes specified by the linked feature criteria; record which criteria are covered by owner tests and which require a milestone integration run.
