## Approved record and coordinate handoff

The user approved these increments on 2026-11-18.

1. **Geographic records.** Decode region, location, terrain and climate data into immutable records with source provenance. Location identity survives a changed name or map position. Expose geographic membership separately from identity.
2. **Layouts.** Decode exterior and dungeon blocks with stable placement, door, marker and action references. Distinguish reusable block records from a placed occurrence. Reject unresolved required references with the source placement, referring record and target.
3. **Variants.** Resolve declared variant scope against the published content plan. Preserve retained record identities and validate explicit remapping before claiming compatibility with affected durable references. Required unresolved variants or mappings fail with owner context.

Preserve original source coordinate values and their declared units in decoded data. Use a single versioned conversion at the layout/physics boundary. Its interface takes source frame/unit identity and coordinates and returns coordinates in a named engine frame or an explicit unsupported-conversion result. Coordinate conversion cannot silently change identity or select a different marker.

IMP-013 and IMP-061 integrate placement and dungeon geometry. IMP-025/057 close geographic frame, origin and terrain behavior. Define and verify scale, orientation and precision through their geometry fixtures before physics acceptance. DATA does not independently choose motor-scale or placement correction constants.

## Constraints and readiness

Planning-ready for record decoding and the versioned conversion handoff confirmed by the user. Target Windows first on Godot 4.7.2. Execution waits for IMP-005 and authorization. Per-format schemas and numeric source values are pinned with their actual decoder fixtures. Downstream physics acceptance additionally requires the common scale/orientation vectors.

## Acceptance

- Pin a real location and block in the read-only ARENA2 fixture. Compare region/location identity, source coordinates, marker/door links and terrain/climate values against the selected source records.
- Rename and move an authored location while retaining its key and verify references still resolve to that identity. Report duplicate or ambiguous authored identities explicitly.
- Instantiate two occurrences of one block record and verify their placement-scoped identities differ while their shared immutable record remains unchanged.
- Remove a required referenced marker or door and verify referring/target diagnostics and failed readiness. Replace a variant with an invalid required link and verify no partial publication.
- Verify identity-preserving variant edits and an explicit mapping independently. A missing required mapping cannot pass compatibility.
- Round-trip representative source coordinates through the declared converter, recording frame, units and allowed tolerance. Joint physics fixtures verify orientation, scale and pose with IMP-013/025/061.
- Verify the accepted and rejected outcomes specified by the linked feature criteria; record which criteria are covered by owner tests and which require a milestone integration run.
- Confirm that required handoff results reach the expected consumer and that rejected or obsolete input leaves committed state consistent under the approved failure policy.
