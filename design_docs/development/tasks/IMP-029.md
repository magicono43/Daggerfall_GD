## Approved quest resource increments

The user approved this breakdown on 2026-11-18.

1. **Sites and markers.** QUEST selects eligible sites and binds stable location/layout/marker identities under QUEST-012/013/014. Preserve resolved selection across unload and technical retry. A missing required marker reports the selected site and resource rather than silently selecting another location.
2. **Resources and owners.** QUEST owns resource identity and relationships. NPC owns actor lifetime, ITEM possessions/custody and LOC placement. Bind representations to those owner-managed identities. Apply quest item reservation and disposition through ITEM-023/QUEST-016.
3. **Death and cleanup.** COMBAT submits confirmed foe death with scoped operation and quest resource identity. QUEST acknowledges the corresponding committed resource outcome once. Complete required corpse preparation only after the applicable obligations settle. Failed acknowledgment exposes explicit recovery and retains completed death work.

## Resource and death fixture

Use one quest site with a person, reserved item and foe. Unload the scene and reconstruct each representation against retained owner identities. Use an explicit pending/acknowledged/failed handoff for foe death. A failed request returns recovery immediately; pending work carries request identity and can be cancelled or retired with its scope. It cannot be treated as completed corpse readiness.

Declare a finite wait/retry budget and progress/failure signal for each asynchronous acknowledgment provider before registering it. A controlled provider fixture holds acknowledgment beyond that budget and verifies explicit recovery with retained death and custody facts. A configurable test budget can exercise exhaustion immediately; production values are recorded with the provider's evidence. Missing acknowledgment cannot become indefinite polling or synthetic success under COMBAT-020.

IMP-021 closes actual corpse custody and looting; IMP-030 actor/quest activation and journal presentation; IMP-038 durable bindings and pending receipts; IMP-031 later side effects and rewards. Quest completion or removal uses the approved owner cleanup policies and never implies arbitrary deletion of remaining possessions.

- Resolve the fixture's site and three resources. Remove a required marker and assert explicit failure naming the retained site and resource; retry cannot silently select a new site.
- Unload/re-enter and verify stable quest/owner identities with newly scoped scene handles. Deliver an expired handle and assert it cannot address a new pool occupant.
- Attempt an unauthorized transfer or cleanup of the reserved item. Verify the approved custody/disposition outcome with no duplicate or lost possession.
- Confirm foe death, hold acknowledgment pending, then complete it. Assert corpse readiness stays gated until required work settles. Repeat the same death signal and verify one quest resource outcome.
- Fail acknowledgment and retry with retained receipts. Assert no fresh loot, repeated death effects or duplicate corpse.
