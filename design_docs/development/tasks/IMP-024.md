## Approved room presentation increments

The user approved this breakdown on 2026-11-18.

1. **World presentation.** AV renders validated models/materials and animated billboards from IMP-008. Preserve stable placement and gameplay-owned collision/interaction. Read-only resources can be shared; per-instance changes remain isolated.
2. **View and HUD.** Compose world, retro resolution and post-processing under AV-005/006. Map UI interaction to the displayed HUD geometry and preserve readable labels under supported settings. Rendering cannot change gameplay aim independently of resolved facing.
3. **Local audio.** Each playback owns source identity, gain, spatial position, cursor and lifetime. Consume committed owner cues and apply CORE/AV scope and pause policies. Shared decoded clips cannot share playback progress.
