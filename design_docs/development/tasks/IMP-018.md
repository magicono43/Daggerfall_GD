## Approved equipment increments

The user approved this breakdown on 2026-11-18.

1. **Slots and admission.** ITEM owns equipped bindings and validates current possession, restrictions, slot selection and hand conflicts before mutation. UI-013 presents the committed arrangement and submits stable item/slot requests. Invalid or stale requests preserve the prior equipment state.
2. **Protection and powers.** Derive body-part protection from committed source identities. Coordinate bindings, protection and continuous-power reconciliation before dependent gameplay observes completion. Each source-bound power has its item and contribution identity. Preserve suppression, cooldowns and resource state on refresh and native restore.
3. **Condition and breakage.** Apply condition loss once for an authorized owner outcome. Resolve breakage and removal of affected contributions without replaying the transition. Independent released spells/projectiles retain their owning lifecycle. Preserve completed phases and outstanding recovery obligations on partial technical failure.

## Acceptance

- Attempt an eligible equip, an incompatible hand arrangement, a restriction failure and a stale item request. Compare committed slots/custody and assert unchanged state on ordinary rejection.
- Equip two contributing sources and remove one. Verify only its protection and powers are removed. Repeat notification and assert no accumulated bonus or repeated activation.
- Suppress one equipped power, refresh its item and restore its snapshot. Verify suppression and charges survive without fresh equip activation.
- Apply the same authorized condition outcome twice and assert one condition change/breakage result. Remove the broken source contribution without deleting an independently released attack.
- Inject a reconciliation failure after an actual partial commitment. Verify retained completed facts, gated dependent observation and recovery of outstanding phases only.
- At IMP-023/034/058 integration, restore bindings, armor and an active/suppressed effect combination and compare all authoritative and derived values.
- Verify the accepted and rejected outcomes specified by the linked feature criteria; record which criteria are covered by owner tests and which require a milestone integration run.
- Confirm that required handoff results reach the expected consumer and that rejected or obsolete input leaves committed state consistent under the approved failure policy.
