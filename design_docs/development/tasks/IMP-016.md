## Approved actor increments

The user approved this breakdown on 2026-11-18.

1. **Authoritative actor state.** CHAR owns permanent attributes and skills, progression facts and current vital pools. Expose read-only projections. Compute derived limits/modifiers through approved RULE formulas and owner context. Keep base data, live modifiers and current pools distinguishable.
2. **Vital mutations.** Accept scoped validated owner requests with actor and operation identity, requested change and attribution. Return applied changes and retained outcome under CHAR-018/023. COMBAT and MAGIC consume actual outcomes to continue their approved phases.
3. **Activity and collapse.** Consume committed MOVE activity for CHAR-020 fatigue and practice. Passive platform motion is classified under its approved activity rules. Resolve exhaustion through CHAR-022 with explicit collapse state and owner handoffs. Repeated updates do not restart an already committed collapse.
4. **Death and projections.** Resolve lethal intake and eligible prevention before committing death under CHAR-023. UI-006 reads owner projections. CORE performs death-return gating and navigation, with AV supplying classified playback. Presentation or repeated intake callbacks cannot repeat committed death consequences.

CHAR recomputes derived state after those sources are ready, then exposes coherent pools and limits under CHAR-018. SAVE coordinates completion.

IMP-018/034/058 close real equipment/effect combinations, IMP-032 live faction prevention, IMP-023 native restore and IMP-035 actual death cinematic handling.
