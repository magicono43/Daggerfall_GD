## Approved owner-restore increments

The user approved this breakdown on 2026-11-18.

1. **Payloads.** Capture quest/task/clock state, resource links, action/reward receipts, faction state and exploration through owner-defined versioned projections. Store them within the same IMP-022 generation bundle. The historical sidecar feature name does not select separate publication.
2. **References.** Validate required versions/providers and available links during preflight. Hydrate authoritative owner facts, register identities, then relink actors/items/sites/resources before exposing dependent readiness.
3. **Recovery and UI.** Preserve pending claims, technical recovery and one-time history. Resume only supported eligible outstanding obligations. Rebuild journal/choices from owner facts and preserve their scoped holds without replaying committed actions.

## Acceptance

- Save and restore the fixture's active tasks, clocks, resource identities and discovered map facts. Compare authoritative state and current scene bindings.
- Restore a completed one-time quest and repeat old start/accept callbacks. Assert no reactivation or duplicated effects.
- Restore committed money plus a pending item claim. Assert balances stay unchanged and only the supported outstanding item delivery resumes.
- Remove a required resource/provider or inject an incompatible payload. Verify preflight rejection where checkable, or scoped post-replacement recovery when geometry-dependent.
- Restore technical recovery and journal notice state without resuming suspended work implicitly or replaying the entry notice.
- Deliver duplicate relink/UI callbacks and assert one Session Restored outcome only after all required participants report readiness.
- Verify the accepted and rejected outcomes specified by the linked feature criteria; record which criteria are covered by owner tests and which require a milestone integration run.
- Confirm that required handoff results reach the expected consumer and that rejected or obsolete input leaves committed state consistent under the approved failure policy.
