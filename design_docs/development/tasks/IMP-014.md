- Verify a due instant at the start is excluded and one at the end is included. Zero elapsed time creates no crossing. Invalid, reversed and overflowing requests leave timestamp and remainder unchanged.
- Verify month/year crossings against TIME-001 fixtures and that restoring the same timestamp emits no advancement outcomes.
- Enqueue A/B at one instant and C dependent on A. Vary registration order and assert declared dependencies and stable independent order. Introduce a cycle and assert explicit failure.
- Set the per-pass budget to one and compare the final event trace, clock and owner state with an uninterrupted reference. Confirm no dependent player action becomes eligible between unsettled passes.
- Interrupt a longer advance at a known instant. Assert actual elapsed time equals committed endpoint minus start and no future occurrence applies. Retry outstanding required work without replaying committed effects.
- Compare equivalent bulk and chronological processing with the same fixture inputs. Query a holiday in two regions without clock or RNG mutation. At quest integration, verify expiry precedes the pending cutoff hand-in.
- Verify the accepted and rejected outcomes specified by the linked feature criteria; record which criteria are covered by owner tests and which require a milestone integration run.
- Confirm that required handoff results reach the expected consumer and that rejected or obsolete input leaves committed state consistent under the approved failure policy.
