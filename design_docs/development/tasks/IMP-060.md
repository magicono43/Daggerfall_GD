## Approved property increments

The user approved this breakdown on 2026-11-18.

1. **Houses.** ECON validates selected stable property, quote/payment, title and persistent storage entitlement. ITEM retains custody and WORLD/LOC the location records.
2. **Ships.** Bind title to the exact ship/destination. Check aboard and pending visit/return state and required contents before resale. Commit payment/title or payout/relinquishment coherently. MOVE/TIME own visit state and placement.
3. **Rooms.** Admit rental/extension against the selected room/provider and accepted terms. Expose committed entitlement and due instant to rest/access owners without making UI the authority.

## Property fixture and integration

Use a house and ship with stored possessions plus one room with a known expiry. Preserve required possessions and explicit relocation policy under ECON-013/014. A failed ordinary settlement changes no title or payment.

IMP-040 closes actual ship boarding/return and concurrent resale rejection. IMP-039 consumes room access. IMP-045/023 restore the same account/property participant and possessions. Querying entitlement cannot itself grant access or reset expiry.

- Sell an eligible property under the approved contents policy and assert one payout and relinquishment despite repeated confirmation.
- Rent/extend a room and compare entitlement immediately before/at expiry using TIME's boundary semantics.
- Restore deed, possessions and room rights through the actual participant. Assert no repeated payment, default ownership or expiry reset.
