## Approved offer and reward increments

The user approved this breakdown and initial M3 action set on 2026-11-18.

1. **Guild offers.** QUEST/FACT validate selected offer identity against current membership, rank, standing and progression requirements. Revalidate upon acceptance and return explicit eligible, rejected or failed results.
2. **Rewards.** Give each consequence entry a stable quest/run/entry identity and independent settlement record. Preserve declared entry order/dependencies. ITEM owns delivery/custody, ECON money and FACT standing/membership. Retain completed entries and eligible pending claims through interruption.
3. **Cross-owner actions.** Dispatch supported actions through explicit owner interfaces and retain their result. Validate the command/provider before publication as usable and check runtime context at execution. Technical failure does not become an authored failure penalty.

Initial M3 coverage includes item rewards, money, faction standing and guild eligibility. Legal effects close with IMP-043/044, travel with IMP-059, specialized magic with IMP-034/047/064, and custom providers with IMP-051. Maintain a per-command provider/acceptance table as those families are enabled. Required unsupported actions block the affected definition rather than silently succeeding.

## Settlement fixture

Use an eligible guild offer and an ordered reward bundle with money, standing and two items. Admit the first item and reject the second through destination capacity. Preserve committed facts and pending claim identity. Resume only the eligible pending work. An authored repeat uses a new run identity under QUEST-009.

IMP-038 closes durable entry receipts and claims; IMP-030 presents committed/pending outcomes. Required closing work remains governed by QUEST-011, including the approved handling of independent and dependent pending entries.

## Constraints and readiness

Planning-ready for the approved initial action set and named later integration gates. Target Windows first on Godot 4.7.2. Execution waits for IMP-029/030/032/037 and authorization. Define each additional command's mutation/failure contract before enabling its provider-backed production path.

## Acceptance

- Select an eligible guild offer, change membership/rank before acceptance and verify rejection with unchanged quest state. A fresh eligible selection can commit once.
- Commit the fixture reward's money/standing/first item, then leave the second item pending. Assert exact balances, standing, item identities and retained claim.
- Retry and reload the pending entry through IMP-038. Verify no repeated money/standing/first item and exactly one successful outstanding delivery.
- Run a legitimate authored repeat with a distinct run ID and verify its separately authorized consequences.
- Reject an unregistered required action and inject a provider failure after a committed effect. Assert retained receipts, owner recovery and no automatic authored penalty.
- At each named later provider gate, run the command table's success, ordinary rejection and interrupted/retry cases against the actual owner.
- Verify the accepted and rejected outcomes specified by the linked feature criteria; record which criteria are covered by owner tests and which require a milestone integration run.
- Confirm that required handoff results reach the expected consumer and that rejected or obsolete input leaves committed state consistent under the approved failure policy.

## Automated verification

Inject a settlement rejection and compare journal, balances, item IDs and standing. Replay a completed operation according to the approved action policy and assert the expected durable outcome for each verb. Use isolated fixtures under DEV-014 and record the inputs, expected outcomes and actual assertions.

The M3 gate covers offer, reward and action providers usable in the quest slice. Complete remaining verb coverage after M4 attack, magic, service, travel and law providers and M5 custom registration are integrated.

## Manual verification

Accept and complete a supported quest, force a reward or owner rejection, and inspect the journal, balances and standing. Record the fixture, build and observed outcome. Follow the linked feature’s explicit manual or rendered criteria where supplied.
