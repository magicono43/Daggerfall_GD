## Approved discovery increments

The user approved this breakdown on 2026-11-18.

1. **Discovery facts.** WORLD owns discovered locations/buildings and LOC owns explored local layout records under the approved scopes. Bind facts to stable identity and validated geographic/layout context. Context queries cannot discover or reveal places by themselves.
2. **Map projections and labels.** UI reads owner-provided map geometry, labels, navigation and notices. Preserve assigned building names and player-authored names under WORLD-015/RULE-027. Viewing, resizing or relocalizing a map cannot reroll assigned names or change discovery.
3. **Notes and markers.** Store annotations and teleporter markers in their owning character/location/layout scope under LOC-019. Keep authored text and marker identity separate from rendered scene objects.
4. **World persistence.** Capture and restore discovery and loose-object state through owner projections. Reconstruct existing identities and custody without generating duplicate possessions, announcing fresh discovery or reapplying edit actions.

- Drop a known possession, unload and restore. Assert one item identity/custody and the correct world pose without recreating loot.
- Save and reload exploration and map edits. Compare durable facts and rendered map projection, with no replayed discovery notices or generated names.
