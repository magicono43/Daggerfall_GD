## Approved travel increments

The user approved this breakdown on 2026-11-18.

1. **Destination/quote.** Bind stable destination and actual origin, route options, duration, entitlements, price and revision. UI selects and displays terms; TIME/ECON own route and payment meaning.
2. **Departure/advance.** Revalidate accepted terms and settle authorized departure costs. Advance through TIME's chronological/interruption contract with actual rest/travel effect and encounter policies. Retain committed elapsed consequences.
3. **Arrival/recovery.** Prepare destination geometry and validate MOVE placement before publishing arrival. Return explicit interrupted/failed/recovery outcomes and preserve settled costs and obligations under TIME-015/ECON-018.
