## Approved effect-family increments

The user approved this breakdown on 2026-11-18.

1. **Vitals.** Apply supported damage, healing, regeneration, fortification, drain and transfer through CHAR's authoritative request/result interface. Preserve requested, modified and actual applied amounts where their rules require those distinctions.
2. **Conditions.** Resolve poison, disease/cure, paralysis, silence and defense admission, progression and removal through their approved MAGIC lifecycles and TIME ordering. A blocked or rejected effect returns its explicit outcome.
3. **Equipped and passive sources.** Reconcile item powers, career/race grants and artifacts by stable source/contribution identity. Preserve suppression, charges, cooldown and completed facts on refresh or load. Remove only the affected source.
4. **Consumables and summoned possessions.** ITEM constructs supported objects and owns custody/consumption. MAGIC resolves authorized payloads and summoned duration. Expiry retires only the correct possession under ITEM-024 and source rules.

## Acceptance

- For each enabled family compare pinned immediate/timed values and actual applied CHAR results with its approved formula and eligibility rules.
- Apply two overlapping sources and remove one. Assert the other's contribution remains. Refresh and restore a suppressed source and verify no new activation, charges or cooldown reset.
- Progress poison/disease and a timed defense through due instants, interruption and removal/cure. Compare the retained lifecycle and committed outcomes, including duplicate callback handling.
- Consume a supported item through the owning request and verify one quantity/cost change and one admitted payload under its declared failure policy.
- Expire a summoned possession after transfer or equipment use where supported. Assert the correct stable identity retires once and unrelated possessions survive.
- At IMP-058/023 restore active/suppressed effects and item sources together. Compare required duration, source, pools and derived values without replayed effects.
- Verify the accepted and rejected outcomes specified by the linked feature criteria; record which criteria are covered by owner tests and which require a milestone integration run.
- Confirm that required handoff results reach the expected consumer and that rejected or obsolete input leaves committed state consistent under the approved failure policy.
