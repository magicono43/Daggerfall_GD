## Approved loot and corpse increments

The user approved this breakdown on 2026-11-18.

1. **Assigned loot.** Generate loot only at an authorized owner boundary under ITEM-020. Retain assigned results and source context. Corpse preparation, refresh, retry and native restoration do not generate new loot.
2. **Corpse preparation.** For a confirmed death prepare one retained corpse identity within the death location scope. Wait for required death/quest obligations, settle equipment/source removal and ITEM custody transfer, then publish loot readiness. Preserve committed custody and outstanding obligations on failure.
3. **Target and retrieve.** MOVE resolves interaction target and mode, with HUD input consumption and owner dispatch under MOVE-018/019/022. ITEM revalidates selected container/item, exact quantity, access and destination capacity before transfer. LAW and QUEST retain property and resource authority.

## Custody handoff and integration

A possession has one authoritative custody at every committed boundary. A pending corpse is inaccessible as finalized loot. Return prepared, pending-required-work, ready, rejected-access or failed-with-recovery with death/corpse/item and session identities. Repeated callbacks reconcile the same corpse operation and cannot allocate new items or publish multiple containers for the same death.

M2 verifies an ordinary enemy corpse and retrieval through IMP-017/019/020. IMP-029 closes quest death acknowledgment, reservation and quest cleanup policies. IMP-043 closes actual property incidents. IMP-023 closes native reconstruction and custody receipts. Preserve the required permission checks before enabling the corresponding live context.

## Acceptance

- Assign a known loot outcome, confirm death and repeat its notification. Assert one corpse identity, identical assigned items and zero rerolls during retry or inspection.
- Inject transfer failure after a committed custody change. Verify every possession exists once, inaccessible pending loot stays gated, and recovery resumes outstanding transfer only.
- Select a stack and exceed destination capacity. Assert unchanged source/destination, then accept a valid quantity and compare surviving/new/retired identities.
- Move or remove the selected target before confirmation. Verify a stale selection cannot retrieve a replacement item or unrelated container.
- At IMP-023 reload, compare corpse identity, assigned loot and custody against the saved state. At IMP-029 test required quest acknowledgment and protected item access before exposing the corpse.
- Verify the accepted and rejected outcomes specified by the linked feature criteria; record which criteria are covered by owner tests and which require a milestone integration run.
- Confirm that required handoff results reach the expected consumer and that rejected or obsolete input leaves committed state consistent under the approved failure policy.
