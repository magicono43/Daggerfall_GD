## Approved crafting and strike increments

The user approved this breakdown on 2026-11-18.

1. **Spell/potion authoring.** MAGIC validates supported spell components and recipes, exposes the exact product and quote and requests owner costs/output on confirmation. Preview has no mutation or live random effect.
2. **Enchantment creation.** Validate exact item identity, eligibility, capacity, powers and charges. ITEM owns the possession and mutable state; MAGIC owns admitted enchantment definitions and effects. Retain accepted terms and require revalidation before commitment.
3. **Strike effects.** COMBAT runs effects in explicit declared order under COMBAT-011. Each declares its eligibility, damage basis and cost/counting unit. MAGIC/ITEM/CHAR apply consequences. Preserve completed effects/costs if a later phase fails and recover outstanding work only.

## Crafting and strike fixture

Use one supported spell recipe, potion recipe and eligible/ineligible item pair plus a strike list with mixed physical/magical components. Pin costs, capacity and per-effect inputs/results. A whole-operation replacement and a numeric adjustment retain distinct hook contracts.

IMP-046 closes paid-service routing against the same product/quote interface. IMP-018/034 closes actual source effects and charges, IMP-019 ordered hit phases and IMP-023/058 supported persistent obligations. Do not expose a crafted product whose required effect/provider cannot validate.
