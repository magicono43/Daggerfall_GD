## Approved conversation increments

The user approved this breakdown on 2026-11-18.

1. **Context.** NPC owns speaker identity, tone/reaction inputs, knowledge and topic context. Bind the conversation to the selected actor and current scope. Appearance, localized name and recycled scene handles cannot substitute for that identity.
2. **Text and choices.** DATA-016 expands supported macros from explicit owner context. UI presents structured messages and stable answer IDs. Rendering, highlighting and language refresh perform no owner mutation, random generation or implicit acceptance.
3. **Offers and authored dialogue.** QUEST owns social-offer eligibility and acceptance while NPC supplies speaker context. Capture stable offer and variant identity and revalidate at commitment. Invalid or stale selection returns the owner's reason rather than accepting a replacement offer.
4. **Journal and restoration.** UI displays committed journal, authored responses and technical recovery facts. Restore NPC/QUEST authoritative state and supported pending UI through their contracts. A load or reopening cannot replay accepted choices or advance a suspended quest.

## Conversation fixture and handoff

Use two visually similar actors with distinct knowledge/service/quest context. Supply one valid social offer and one whose eligibility changes while the choice is open. Bind answers to explicit request/offer/actor identity. Preserve meaningful localization structure and pending state across presentation refresh.

IMP-031 closes actual rewards and guild offer consumers, IMP-038 durable conversation/quest state, IMP-053 recovery inspection, and IMP-036 actual opening presentations. DATA-016 macro expansion closes IMP-007's production text integration. Service choices route to their domain owner rather than implementing payment or eligibility in the talk view.

## Acceptance

- Open conversations with the two fixture actors and compare speaker, topics, knowledge and offer identity independently of appearance.
- Invalidate the selected offer before confirmation. Verify rejected acceptance preserves the relevant owner state and never accepts a different candidate.
- Switch locale while a choice is pending. Assert stable answer/offer identity, focus and selected choice with zero additional RNG draws or committed effects.
- Expand a supported macro twice from the same explicit context and compare output and unchanged owner state. Malformed required context returns an explicit diagnostic.
- Accept a valid offer once, reopen the actor and compare journal/authored response facts. Repeat the old confirmation and assert no duplicate quest acceptance.
- At IMP-038 reload after acceptance and during an approved pending/recovery state. Verify restored facts and scoped UI without replaying prompts or effects.
- Verify the accepted and rejected outcomes specified by the linked feature criteria; record which criteria are covered by owner tests and which require a milestone integration run.
- Confirm that required handoff results reach the expected consumer and that rejected or obsolete input leaves committed state consistent under the approved failure policy.
