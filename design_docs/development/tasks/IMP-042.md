## Approved mechanism increments

The user approved this breakdown on 2026-11-18.

1. **Doors and locks.** LOC owns authoritative lock state and motion. MOVE routes stable target/context requests. RULE lockpicking, COMBAT bashing and MAGIC utility requests use their declared eligibility and mutation contracts rather than directly changing scene state.
2. **Triggers and chains.** Validate authored links and action parameters before activation. Resolve accepted triggers and ordered actions with stable request/record identities and explicit failure outcomes under LOC-015. Preserve required completed work during recovery.
3. **Geometry and traps.** LOC coordinates authored moving geometry and puzzle/trap state. MOVE owns actor collision/support consequences, CHAR/COMBAT damage and WORLD/MOVE teleport placement. Publish committed outcomes and retain supported action progress for SAVE.
