## Agreed delivery scope

The user approved these three increments on 2026-11-18.

1. A project-owned, versioned nonclassic generator with documented independent seed derivation and unbiased integer range mapping. Pin reference vectors. Preserve the separately specified opt-in classic generator and its numeric behavior.
2. Verify purpose isolation, fresh initialization after successful load, retained assigned outcomes and owner-designated pending results. Reuse resolved results on technical retry under the owning operation contract.
3. Define each formula hook's inputs, outputs, allowed mutations, random-source use and failure behavior. Verify registry selection using controlled providers. Connect executable providers through IMP-050/051.

## Interfaces and verification boundaries

The caller supplies the declared purpose/session or classic generation operation and receives a checked draw or retained resolution. A draw in one purpose cannot advance another source. Classic vectors use the inspected RULE-002 source and explicitly supported range mapping. Nonclassic bounded draws use unbiased mapping. RULE-001 clamps the computed chance to 0–100 and consumes one logical percentile draw even at the extremes.

The registry takes candidate hook declarations and eligible provider registrations in resolved MOD order. Verify the last valid replacement wins, duplicate same-provider replacements and incompatible signatures fail preflight, and an invalid contribution cannot publish. Invoke the selected provider once under its declared contract. A runtime callback error enters owner recovery without selecting another provider. Keep the registry locked for the application run.

The first registry fixture uses a declared pure integer calculation hook, built-in output and two controlled replacement providers with known outputs. A thrown callback and a detectable invalid result must report the selected provider and failure without another invocation or built-in fallback. Actual gameplay hooks integrate with their owning features and IMP-051.
