## Approved paid-service increments

The user approved these independently verifiable groups on 2026-11-18.

1. **Repair/identification.** Capture exact item, provider, quote and destination/custody. ITEM owns condition, identification and item state. ECON owns paid job, due-time and collection settlement. Revalidate selected identity and access before commit.
2. **Training/temple.** CHAR, FACT and MAGIC validate skill training, donations, treatment and blessing eligibility/effects. ECON coordinates accepted payment and required fulfillment under each route's policy. UI submits the selected terms.
3. **Magic commerce.** MAGIC/ITEM expose authorized spell, potion and enchanted-item offers and capacity/definition checks. ECON binds price, payment and delivery. Crafting and effect behavior remain with IMP-047/034.

## Service matrix and integration

For each route record provider eligibility, selected target, accepted price/revision, required payment/custody changes, fulfilled outcome and supported pending/recovery state. Use pinned valid and invalid fixtures per group. Ordinary rejection changes no settlement facts. Technical partial commitment retains receipts and remaining obligation under the route's approved behavior.

IMP-045 supplies ledger/instruments and persistence, IMP-060 actual property/lodging entitlements, IMP-039 training/progression, IMP-034 effects, and IMP-047 crafted product definitions. Complete each provider-backed route's success and failure checks before exposing it to players.

## Constraints and readiness

Planning-ready for the approved three service groups. Target Windows first on Godot 4.7.2. Execution waits for IMP-034/037/039/040/045/047/060 and authorization. Pin actual prices, timing and eligibility vectors from approved owner specifications.

## Acceptance

- For each service group accept one pinned valid quote and compare payment, target/item state and fulfillment against its owner expectations.
- Invalidate eligibility, target, price or destination before confirmation. Verify unchanged rejection or revised terms requiring fresh confirmation.
- Inject failure after payment or custody commitment. Assert receipts preserve completed work and only outstanding fulfillment resumes.
- Repeat confirmation and reload supported pending jobs/claims. Verify one charge, one delivered effect/product and correct retained due time.
- Collect a completed repair and inspect an identified item through the production UI. Verify selected identity and no substitute item.
- Integrate actual training, temple and magical product owners and compare entitlement, resource and persistence outcomes for each enabled route.
- Verify the accepted and rejected outcomes specified by the linked feature criteria; record which criteria are covered by owner tests and which require a milestone integration run.
- Confirm that required handoff results reach the expected consumer and that rejected or obsolete input leaves committed state consistent under the approved failure policy.
