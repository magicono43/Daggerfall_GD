## Approved creation increments

The user approved this breakdown on 2026-11-18.

1. **Draft and navigation.** CHAR owns the creation draft and validated selections. UI owns page navigation, editing and focus. Preserve choices on back/forward navigation under the approved dependency/invalidation rules. Final confirmation submits one immutable validated draft through CORE's session request.
2. **Race and career.** Resolve races, preset classes, questionnaire results and custom careers from IMP-009 definitions. Validate skill tiers, attributes and advantage/advancement tradeoffs under CHAR-002/003/004/005. Retain stable definition identity independently of displayed text.
3. **Biography and starting rolls.** Keep selected biography and resolved outcomes in the draft, with explicit provenance for effects already represented in allocations. Revisit pages without redrawing retained results. Explicit authorized rerolls use the owning feature's rules. Validate allocation budgets and bounds.
4. **Presentation and handoff.** Name, portrait and reflex choice feed the same draft and final review. CORE receives the selected draft identity and complete validated contents. IMP-036 applies actual grants, placement and opening content. UI-008's startup/death cinematic and title outcomes close with IMP-002/035/036 and CHAR-023's actor/death flow under IMP-016.

## Creation fixture and mutation contract

Use one preset and one custom-career fixture with pinned definition values, biography inputs and controlled rolls. Record the draft before and after page revisits, upstream changes and final submission. Each fixture declares which dependent selections become invalid and which retained facts remain. Preserve original source rules in CHAR-001 through CHAR-010.

Draft inspection and presentation can change draft state only through deliberate edits. They do not allocate live possessions, grant live spell membership, apply actor effects or start quests. Record represented biography effects so IMP-036 can apply remaining obligations exactly once. A rejected or stale final submission cannot create a second session.
