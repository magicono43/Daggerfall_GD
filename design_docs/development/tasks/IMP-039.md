## Approved progression and rest increments

The user approved this breakdown on 2026-11-18.

1. **Practice and increases.** CHAR admits practice from authorized committed actions under CHAR-012 and resolves periodic increases/mastery under CHAR-013/RULE-007. Preserve attempt identity and owner-approved timing and limits.
2. **Levels and rewards.** CHAR owns eligibility, earned outcomes and pending allocation under CHAR-014/015. UI-012 edits and confirms supported allocations without rerolling or granting rewards on refresh.
3. **Rest.** TIME coordinates selected timed rest, heal-until or loiter after CORE threat query and owner-validated bed/access admission. CHAR supplies recovery under CHAR-021/RULE-008. UI paces the view without advancing time independently.
4. **Interrupt and restore.** Stop advancement at the committed interruption instant and preserve applied recovery/progression. Capture and restore only supported ongoing states with retained rewards and required operation facts.
