## Approved trade increments

The user approved this breakdown on 2026-11-18.

1. **Initial state.** ECON creates the character's account/property baseline once through the M3 initializer below. Native restore hydrates the existing projection.
2. **Merchant access and stock.** Resolve merchant service, eligible stock and goods by stable identity. ITEM owns custody and stack properties. ECON owns commerce eligibility and stock/price context. Preserve household/property distinctions under ITEM-021.
3. **Quotes.** Bind basket quantities, source/destination, accepted price, tender and offer revision. Revalidate access, stock, funds and capacity at settlement. Material changes return revised terms for explicit confirmation.
4. **Settlement.** Commit debit or payout, stock change and item delivery as one exchange outcome. Ordinary rejection changes none of them. Technical failure retains committed facts and outstanding recovery. Publish completion and practice once.

## Acceptance

- Initialize two character baselines and compare distinct zero-balance/no-property projections. Repeat fresh-entry callbacks and verify an initialized ledger cannot be reset.
- Quote a pinned merchant basket with known goods, quantities, tender and destination. Compare prices with the approved RULE/ECON vectors.
- Change stock, price, funds, access and capacity between quote and confirmation in separate cases. Verify unchanged ordinary rejection or revised terms requiring confirmation.
- Commit purchase and sale, including credit payout where supported. Compare conserved item quantities/custody and money value and one practice outcome.
- Inject failure after a committed mutation and resume with retained receipts. Repeat the original confirmation and verify no duplicate debit, delivery, stock depletion or practice.
- At IMP-036 verify required initial economy failure blocks entry. At IMP-045 restore the same baseline after real account activity without reinitialization.
- Verify the accepted and rejected outcomes specified by the linked feature criteria; record which criteria are covered by owner tests and which require a milestone integration run.
- Confirm that required handoff results reach the expected consumer and that rejected or obsolete input leaves committed state consistent under the approved failure policy.
