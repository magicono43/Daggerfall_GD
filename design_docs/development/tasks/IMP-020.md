## Approved enemy increments

The user approved this breakdown on 2026-11-18.

1. **Archetype and placement.** Construct the actor from validated definitions and progression context. Validate placement against ready geometry through the production MOVE/LOC seam. Register and activate only after successful placement. Reject unsuitable or obstructed placement without leaving an active actor.
2. **Sensing and target intent.** NPC owns allegiance, sight/hearing observations, stealth/illusion queries, alert and pursuit memory. Return explicit observation and intent state. Target selection alone cannot commit a combat outcome.
3. **Movement and positioning.** Consume the approved actor motor and route/hazard context. Maintain valid movement and melee positioning. Submit door open/bash requests to their owner. IMP-042 closes real door/lock mutation and linked action checks.
4. **Attack commitment.** NPC submits actor/source/target intent. COMBAT admits and captures target and timing under COMBAT-015/016. Revalidate facing, range, source and actor state at the simulation commitment marker. Preserve approved cooldown/interval results and distinguish ordinary miss/cancel from technical failure.
