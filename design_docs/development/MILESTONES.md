The [backlog](TASK_BACKLOG.md) lists the work associated with each integration gate. A milestone passes when its required scenarios pass in the target build and the evidence is recorded. Feature criteria outside the slice stay open until the named integration work runs. The [second audit](PRE_IMPLEMENTATION_AUDIT_2.md) confirms the revised boundaries. ## M2

**Playable room and native save.** Use the approved DEV-014 fixture approach with an interior or dungeon room, a controllable actor, one supported opponent, a loot outcome and a native save. Drive setup through production scene, placement, attack and persistence requests. Verify input handoff at pause, movement/collision, attack commitment, loot conservation, successful restore and rejected load preserving the active session. Run enemy placement/combat and restore preservation through the headless gate where applicable. Run rendered checks for reach, timing, scene transitions and thumbnail agreement where required. Keep content and test state isolated.

## M3

**New character, settlement and quest.** Exercise approved creation, required starting grants, optional tutorial and main-story opening in both approved start contexts. Traverse a settled area and quest site, talk to a relevant actor, fight or obtain a resource, settle a supported reward and restore the quest, discovered world and associated state. Verify the quest fault/retry scenario and prove previously committed effects occur once. Inject missing required placement and opening records. Exercise terrain seams and restore gates in the rendered run. Enable cross-owner quest actions as each owner contract and outcome is integrated.

## M4

**Broader RPG loops.** Verify progression, interruption and rest across clock boundaries. Exercise ranged combat, alternate traversal, spells and crafting, representative legal cases, banking deadlines and service transactions. Run travel and arrest-release placement failures and test save/resume over relevant time boundaries. Close provider-dependent cases deferred from M2/M3 and record any remaining release-scope gaps.

## M5

**Extensibility and release tooling.** Exercise exported restricted and trusted mod paths once feasibility and selected boundaries are evidenced. Verify configuration ordering, extension cleanup and save compatibility. Run validator parity checks for inspector/export, world edits, localization changes and packaging. Validate gameplay with approved reference data and permitted packages. Close the full linked acceptance matrix or record a user-approved scope disposition. Conditional capabilities retain their specification gates.
