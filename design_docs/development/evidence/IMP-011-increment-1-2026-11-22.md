## Concrete delivery contract

Capacity is **256 logical edges** (a complete tap uses two). Edge 257 cancels all
unfinished input, reports pending/delivered counts and the rejected edge, and
requires fresh presses. Already-returned outcomes remain separate; delivered
unfinished hold IDs are reported through cancellation, and completed holds are
not retroactively cancelled. No cancellation is sent through ordinary release.
The fixture both delivers a full 256-edge batch and invalidates it with edge 257.

Each physics delivery drains only its entry batch, with sequence cutoff and
CORE eligibility/context/epoch checks between callbacks. Reentrant input waits
for another update even when a targeted disconnect removes intervening edges.
Consumers receive detached original identities and own gameplay validation,
rejection and consequences. Rejection consumes intent without cooldown retry.
`context_current(edge)` supports revalidation before owner mutation. Shortcut
selection also checks that epoch after gameplay callbacks, preventing a transient
focus out/in from reviving an earlier candidate in the same window/session.
Godot's serialized event-arrival order is the physical-event tie-break; shortcut
contenders use the separately declared UI priority.

Input selection is locked on main-down. Modifier changes cannot switch plain and
chord actions mid-hold; sides are explicit. GUI gets first refusal, while ups are
observed even if GUI consumes them. Reserved recovery has an input-only bridge
hold, so no physics update can deliver gameplay between recovery capture and UI
navigation. Multiple recovery contributors aggregate independently of gameplay.

Only one explicitly selected controller contributes. Active means last accepted
gameplay input, excluding deadzone noise and GUI-consumed analog input. Selected
device removal cancels affected holds; an idle removal preserves unrelated
keyboard work. Active removal adds its own session-scoped CORE hold. Reconnect
alone never clears it; stock Continue choices release only that token. Actual
held hardware buttons/triggers are quarantined on selection/reconnect. Late
events from a disconnected device are rejected. Focus return reconciles missing
physical ups without logical release. Trigger thresholds are 0.55/0.45.

The stock shortcut priority and quicksave/quickload conflict policy are retained.
One winning unavailable owner reports unavailable, with no fallback or invented
gameplay window/transaction. Cast Spell routes to spellbook intent and never
casts. F7 uses CORE's existing HUD preference. Run/sneak/crouch, combat, console,
screenshot and other action effects remain their future providers' work.
