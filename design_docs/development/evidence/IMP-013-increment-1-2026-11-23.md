## Implemented production slice

The normal session shell composes `location_assembly.gd`, specializing the accepted
dungeon owner rather than introducing another session, content, physical-pose or
dungeon authority. Its source hooks share isolated staging, atomic publication,
original CORE tickets, cancellation, retirement, retained outcomes and activation.
The original preparation ticket and activation scope/version remain distinct.
Stock gameplay stays unavailable; a controlled CORE provider qualifies lifecycle
seams only. Real DATA, media, resource, geometry and Jolt providers are exercised.

RMB assembly includes the actual exterior model inventory, required ground,
selected building interior models and closed action doors. It separates exterior
occurrence, building parent and interior-instance transforms. Native fixtures cover
snapped, free/rotated and repeated/reordered exterior occurrences and a separately
placed native interior. Definition queries work before activation and use sorted
owner-maintained classification indexes. Live building, static-plane entrance,
action-door and marker metadata is detached and scoped to content/layout/activation.
Unqualified named-building/service and MAPS door-pool associations remain pending.

Static hits use actual Jolt shape ownership and transformed triangles. Coincident
facades produce an ambiguous no-entrance result; an exact authored selector resolves
its declared region. No hit dispatches traversal. Source entry roles and explicit
authored multiple roles retain interior scope. A marker at zero is valid. Rest is
not automatically promoted to entry. Missing required records/selectors, missing
resources and unavailable owner bindings reject without replacing committed geometry.

See the [owner contract](../../../main_project/scripts/loc/LOCATION_ASSEMBLY.md).
Optional billboard/scenery presentation and person/population/action activation
are retained metadata or pending owners, not silently certified gameplay geometry.
Native restoration here is compatible fresh-process geometry reconstruction only.
