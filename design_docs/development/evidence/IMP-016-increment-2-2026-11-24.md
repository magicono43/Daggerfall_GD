## Implemented scope and policy

The same composed `core.actors` CHAR authority accepts versioned owner intake,
retains detached applied outcomes and supports indexed technical redelivery and
explicit continuation acknowledgment. CORE binds eligible real provider Objects
through trusted readiness declarations, with scope-local consumer handles.
Arbitrary labels, UI/DATA/MOVE and presentation do not confer intake authority.
No second registry, lifecycle, pose owner, gameplay expression language, executable
loader, new dependency or balance hook was introduced.

The [production contract](../../../main_project/scripts/char/VITAL_INTAKE.md)
defines exact request/result schemas, units, bounded arithmetic, consumer
eligibility, lookup-before-revision ordering, commitment and failure protocol,
retention limits and reconstruction. Spend is sufficient-or-unchanged and permits
exact zero; damage/drain and heal/recover saturate only valid amounts. Generic
fill excludes breath. Requested physical amount, actual loss, separately attributed
magical intake and deferred lethal ownership are never conflated.

Lethal health intake uses the explicitly authorized **unavailable, unchanged
rejection** option. Increment 4 must supply CHAR-023 before lethal intake can
finalize prevention or death. There is no terminal-zero kill, prevention roll,
revival, free cast, partial spending or invented stricter training rule. Existing
reconstructed zero health blocks healing/fill. Fatigue crossings retain a pending
transition, distinct from collapse, with CHAR-owned CORE simulation/input/save
holds. A coordinator owns and preserves its own continuation hold across failure;
it cannot release CHAR or unrelated holds.

Positive-to-zero crossings receive one retained transition each, including source
maximum clamps; repeated zero and zero maxima produce no additional crossing.
Recovery above zero rearms the next crossing. Source removal and capacity increase
never refill, erase receipts or synthesize healing. Pure formulas, source facts,
base health and progression retained_outcomes remain separate from pool writes.

The accepted v1 baseline record remains supported. Explicit actor-state-v2 adds a
separate bounded vital-retention-v1 ledger and checked pending/acknowledged state.
Quiescent reconstruction validates numerical results, complete identities,
attribution, unique commit revisions, depletion coverage, source receipts and
pending counters; unsupported pending outcomes and ledger erasure fail. It does
not replay a debit, notification, consequence, grant or RNG draw. This is not
native SAVE or GAD-01 cross-session persistent identity migration.
