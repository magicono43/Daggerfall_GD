## Implemented behavior

DATA validates immutable `dungeon-arrivals-v1` declarations through ordinary
shipped/MOD/loose publication. LOC prepares indexed explicit landing/route/return
bindings with its existing assembly stage. It validates source role, effective
block, exact record pose/frame and classic payload SHA, then transforms anchors
and unit facing through the existing 0.025-metre geometry frame. Repeated block
occurrences, reordered records, distinct/shared landings and doorway/return IDs
retain their proper scope. There is no first-Start/Enter, horizontal-block,
unrelated-origin or exterior-start fallback.

An original `arrival={route,kind,doorway}` request binds placement handoff to that
route. Omission is inspection/reconstruction only. General routes expose their
authored default/backups in order; named/opening routes cannot switch targets.
Approach selection is explicitly unsupported until an authored policy exists.
Access is checked for every candidate and after placement. Controlled MOVE
responses distinguish exact/corrected/rejected/pending/unavailable, with no LOC
actor dimensions, correction radius, floor snap or search. Anchor and actor-pose
claims remain separate. Real consumers must satisfy all other owner gates.

Handoffs carry original requester/session/content/preparation/CORE identities,
effective layout/geometry/context/marker versions and the separate activation
session. Their receipt includes activation scope, so an old isolated-world result
cannot qualify after activation. Callback checks reject changed scope, route,
ticket, frame or identity. Wrong-ticket cancellation, late required failure,
duplicate intake, activation/retirement and content withdrawal preserve existing
ownership. Failed/cancelled preparation preserves the previous valid context.
Point reads use prepared indexes and detached narrow projections.

Return bindings resolve actual published exterior layout/site/placement context.
Nonempty declared record references must exist in that context, but record
existence does not certify a static doorway. `doorway_binding_pending`, exterior
geometry/traversal and WORLD geography gates remain explicit. Classic static-door
association and executable return traversal are not invented.

Native restoration uses saved authoritative pose/state through SAVE/MOVE and
stable `binding` references. Reconstructing a marker index selects no arrival and
emits no entry events. Fresh-process checks are compatible reconstruction seams,
not a native SAVE implementation or replay of opening/doorway effects.
