Resolved textures reach real assembled meshes through AV. The final
`dungeon-atlas-pixel-uv-v1` profile caches one immutable atlas definition per
model/palette/preparation and creates independent mutable atlas textures/materials
per occurrence. One immutable mesh surface preserves triangle/normal/UV tuples,
with per-triangle binding indices in UV2. Sampling repeats within each selected
atlas region using original source dimensions, with nearest filtering and checked
opaque/cutout/blended behavior. Immutable shaders/mesh/shape prototypes remain
shared; collision, scoped records, links and owner state are not remapped.
Selected missing/invalid assets fail explicitly. A narrow source-backed decoder
extension supports the exact TEXTURE.000/001 solid-swatch profile.

compatibility receipts. Context-only root alternatives are accepted with unchanged
structural placements/assembly. Structural/block/record migration remains
`provider_pending`; generic DATA receipts never certify SAVE or gameplay meaning.
Resource staging deferred by the LOC-only DATA path is explicitly unvalidated
until LOC stages every required resource and checks geometry/environment.

sound zero is absence10000; otherwise native height=-8*sound; magnitude supplies
castle metadata. Conflicts fail. LOC converts through the accepted0.025m frame,
requires source/block/pose/SHA matches, and checks declared native extents against

free surfaces and clipped submerged cells, separate equal-height pools, explicit
priority, dry bridges/floors and stacked rooms. Joining witnesses stay inside the
liquid cells. Unproved required membership, including a higher-priority context,

cells/anchors for complex curved or occluded spaces; it is not a fluid solver.
